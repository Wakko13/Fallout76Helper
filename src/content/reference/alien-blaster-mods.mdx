For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how to acquire the Alien
Blaster itself, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

Despite being an extremely rare novelty weapon, the Alien Blaster does have a real, if small, mod
pool covering Barrel, Grip, Sights, and Magazine — including the notable **Fusion Magazine** mod,
which converts it away from its scarce unique ammo onto common Fusion Cells at the cost of damage
and capacity.

## Barrel mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short Barrel | Standard | — | Not documented |
| Long Barrel | Superior range and sighted accuracy, better recoil, poor hip-fire accuracy | Not documented | Not documented |

## Magazine mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Magazine | Standard — fires the Alien Blaster's own unique, very-limited-supply ammo | — | Not documented |
| Fusion Magazine | Reduces damage and ammo capacity; switches ammo to Fusion Cells (common ammo, easy resupply) | Not documented | Not documented |

The Fusion Magazine is the mod most players actually care about on this weapon: it trades the Alien
Blaster's headline high damage for the ability to keep firing it indefinitely on an ammo type you can
actually buy and carry in bulk, rather than hoarding a handful of unique rounds.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

As with the Gamma Gun, this page is more incomplete than most others in this series, and that
reflects the source data rather than a shortcut taken here: Fextralife's Alien Blaster page and every
individual mod sub-page fetched for it (Short Barrel, Long Barrel, Sharpshooter's Grip, Standard
Sights, Fusion Magazine) consistently show "??" for perk requirement, accuracy/weight change, and
crafting materials — only the plain-text effect description is filled in, and even that is missing
for a few rows (e.g. the exact wording of the Standard Grip and Standard Barrel/Sight entries beyond
"Standard"). Attempts to cross-check against fallout.wiki and fallout.fandom.com for this weapon
returned errors during this research session, so the materials gap couldn't be closed from an
alternate source. If exact recipes matter for planning around this weapon, check the in-game crafting
menu directly. Given how rare the weapon and its ammo are in the first place, most players will only
ever interact with the Fusion Magazine conversion — that's the one worth confirming in-game before
committing scarce alien tech to a Weapons Workbench.
