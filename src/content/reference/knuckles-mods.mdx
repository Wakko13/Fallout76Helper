For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Knuckles sit
relative to the rest of the unarmed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

As the cheapest, earliest unarmed weapon to craft, the Knuckles carry a correspondingly simple mod
slot — four options covering the usual bleed/pierce/exceptional spread.

## General mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sharp | Targets bleed; better damage; Weight +20% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 2x Adhesive, 4x Steel |
| Spiked | Armor piercing; better damage; Damage +25%; Weight +10% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 1x Adhesive, 2x Steel, 2x Screw |
| Puncturing | Armor piercing; superior damage; Weight +30% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 3x Adhesive, 4x Steel, 2x Screw |
| Bladed | Targets bleed; exceptional damage; Weight +50% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 3x Adhesive, 2x Leather, 6x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's table was clean and consistent for this weapon — a uniform Makeshift Warrior 2 gate
and full materials for all four mods. The one caveat that applies across this whole wave: Patch 60
("Gone Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting melee weapons and
mods entirely, so treat Makeshift Warrior 2 as the historically-documented requirement rather than
a guaranteed current block.
