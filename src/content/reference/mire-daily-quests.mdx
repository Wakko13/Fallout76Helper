This is a roster of The Mire's repeatable quest-log content — quests picked up from a
specific NPC, terminal, or item that reset on a timer, as distinct from the region's
public events (covered in
[Ash Heap and The Mire public events](../ash-heap-and-mire-public-events/)) and from
Daily Ops. For how the daily/drop/repeatable system works in general, see the
[daily and repeatable quests overview](../daily-and-repeatable-quests-overview/).

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Idle Explosives** (Daily) | Boomer, the damaged bomb-disposal Protectron at Southern Belle Motel | Speak with Boomer, then travel to a randomly-assigned location and collect the inert bombs found there (no need to return to Boomer — the quest completes on picking up the last one) | Adhesive, aluminum, Blast Mine plans, random ammo/aid, and a chance at legendary gear |
| **Play Time** (Daily) | Chloe, at Berkeley Springs | Complete one randomly-assigned task for Chloe — examples include collecting science samples or flowers, attending a "playdate," or retrieving data | Radaway, a Pyrolyzer (modded Flamer), random ammo/aid/plan/armor/mod/weapon, and a legendary chance |
| **Queen of the Hunt** (Daily) | Shelby O'Rourke's terminal at the Hunter's Shack | Check three marked locations, track down and defeat whichever cryptid spawns, then extract and deposit a tissue sample at the Hunter's Shack analyzer | A Cryptid perk card, ~300 XP, random ammo/aid/plan/armor/mod/weapon, and a legendary chance |
| **Waste Not** (Daily) | Maintenance terminal at a Red Rocket station | Read the terminal's "Alerts" entry to reveal a nearby Protectron patrol area, destroy the Red Rocket Protectrons there for three Red Rocket cores, then return and deposit them | Copper, rubber, random ammo/aid/plan/armor/mod/weapon, and a legendary chance |

Idle Explosives, Play Time, and Waste Not are also three of the random quest/event
sources for the **Assault Rifle** plan — see
[Rifles and carbines](../rifles-and-carbines/) for the full list of ways to unlock it.
Queen of the Hunt is also the reliable, quest-gated way to summon a **Vengeful
Mothman** (and roughly a 1-in-6 chance at a Grafton Monster) rather than hunting the
wild open-world version — see [Cryptid locations](../cryptid-locations/) for the
full cryptid-hunting picture.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Waste Not's trigger location is a regional oddity.** Multiple sources describe it
  starting at "Red Rocket Mega Stop," but that named location is documented elsewhere
  on this site (and by several wikis) as sitting in the **Savage Divide**, not The
  Mire — the walkthrough detail of hunting Protectrons "north of Freddy Fear's House
  of Scares" points the same way. It's included here because that's how this site's
  existing [Rifles and carbines](../rifles-and-carbines/) entry and this research
  task grouped it (alongside Idle Explosives and Play Time, both solidly Mire-based),
  but if you go looking for it and find yourself in Savage Divide rather than The
  Mire, that's likely why — treat the regional placement as unresolved rather than
  re-explained away.
- Play Time's task pool ("collect flowers," "retrieve data," etc.) wasn't fully
  enumerated by any source consulted — treat the examples above as illustrative, not
  a complete list.
- Exact reward tables, XP values, and legendary drop chances are community-tallied
  and can shift with balance patches.
