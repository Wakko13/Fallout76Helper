This is the index for "which quest gave me that" across the main storyline and the Enclave arc it
eventually opens up. For the full mechanics of each system, see
[power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/) and
[nuke launch codes basics](../../faq/nuclear-launch-codes-basics/) — this page only tracks which quest
hands you which reward, and links out for the how-it-works detail.

## Opening main quest

**Reclamation Day** is the very first quest (leaving Vault 76). It hands out starter consumables, a
few perk cards, and CAMP basics based on choices you make along the way, but it does **not** hand out
any unique named weapon or armor — that's a common misconception. Your first power armor (almost
always a bare, non-level-locked frame) tends to come from exploring nearby locations rather than from
a quest reward; see the power armor basics page for details.

Later, the main-quest-adjacent **The New Arrivals** has you name a new Nuka-Cola flavor at a
manufacturing terminal — see [Nuka-Cola mixing](../nuka-cola-mixing/) for what that actually gets you
(cosmetic naming only, no gameplay difference between the three name options).

## The Enclave arc

The Enclave storyline is a linear chain gating access to the Whitespring bunker, faction rank, and
eventually nuke launching. Rough order:

| Quest | Prerequisite | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bunker Buster | Reach the Abandoned Waste Dump | Opens the Enclave storyline / Whitespring bunker access; no notable unique item confirmed |
| One of Us | Bunker Buster | Urban Operative underarmor; a "Photo Frame – The Enclave" CAMP decoration |
| Officer on Deck | One of Us | **Last Bastion** — a unique urban scout armor chest piece (guaranteed +5% accuracy with ballistic weapons legendary effect); Enclave Officer outfit; also the quest that lets you start earning Commendations toward **General** rank |
| (General rank, 10 Commendations) | Officer on Deck completed | Access to the bunker's military wing; **X-01 power armor plans**, claimed from the Archival Terminal's Schematic Archive once you hit General |
| I Am Become Death | Officer on Deck / General rank | Final Enclave quest; unlocks actual nuke launching via MODUS — see the nuke codes page for the full launch process |

Commendations toward General come from killing Legendary enemies, completing Extermination
Operations, Robot Patrol missions, and Resource Drop tasks — not from a single quest turn-in.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The "Bunker Buster" item listed by some community wikis as a *reward* of the "One of Us" quest is
almost certainly a naming collision with the "Bunker Buster" *quest* earlier in the chain — treat that
specific line item as unconfirmed rather than a real unique weapon called Bunker Buster. Whether
"Officer on Deck" itself directly grants General rank, or merely unlocks the Commendation grind that
leads to it, is described inconsistently across sources; this page treats commendation-earning as
unlocked by the quest rather than the quest itself instantly granting the rank. Exact wording and
placement of "Reclamation Day" rewards (which perk cards, which starter items) vary by which
S.P.E.C.I.A.L./perk choices you made at creation, so don't expect an identical loadout to someone
else's.
