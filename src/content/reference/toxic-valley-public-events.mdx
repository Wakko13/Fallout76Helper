Toxic Valley's public event roster on the general rotation (see
[event scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/) for how that rotation works) —
with one important caveat: **two of the seven events historically associated with
this region are currently disabled**, not just rare. See the note at the bottom
before planning around them.

## Currently active

| Event | Trigger location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grafton Day** | Random spot in Toxic Valley (Grafton's south gate) | The mayor kicks off a "parade" that spawns a Grafton Monster plus Eyebots, Protectrons, and Mister Handies; "Watch the Parade" becomes "Kill the Grafton Monster" once you land a hit | Standard event reward pool, chance at legendary gear |
| **Manhunt** | Grafton Dam (enter via the left sewer pipe on the building's east end) | Fight through Mad Dog Malone's super-mutant gang to find and subdue Mad Dog Malone himself | Standard event reward pool |
| **Protest March** | Grafton | Defend against three waves of rioters — Scorched carrying protest signs in waves 1 and 3, Mr. Handy/Protectron robots in wave 2 — while nearby super mutants may also join in from the rooftops | Standard event reward pool |
| **Patrol Duty** | Eastern Regional Penitentiary | Escort a prison-guard robot on one of two patrol routes (Cell Blocks A/B + yard, or exercise yard → solitary → Cell Block D) | Chance at legendary weapon/armor |
| **Swarm of Suitors** | Pioneer Scout Camp, Grafton Lake (reach the lake's central island to start it) | Stop three waves of mirelurks/mirelurk hunters from reaching the island before time runs out | 1–3 mirelurk eggs plus Treasury Notes (a Mirelurk Queen spawns and rewards drop sharply if the waves reach the island) |

## Disabled (Patch 66, "The Backwoods," March 2026)

| Event | Former trigger location | What it involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Dogwood Die Off** | Becker Farm (also involved Graninger Farm and Smith Farm aerosolizers) | Repair and refill three farm aerosolizers with junk fertilizer to save Grafton's bleach dogwoods |
| **Jail Break** (a.k.a. "Jailbreak") | Eastern Regional Penitentiary | Repair rooftop turrets and survive five waves to stop Mad Dog Malone's gang from breaking him out |

Bethesda's official patch notes for The Backwoods state plainly: *"Jail Break and
Dogwood Die Off, have been disabled. These two events were going to take much more
time and effort to bring up to the quality we would like so we have made the
decision to disable them for now."* Note that Jail Break's Mad Dog Malone
break-out setup and the still-active **Manhunt** (subduing Mad Dog Malone at
Grafton Dam) are two distinct events, not duplicates — Manhunt remains live.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Bethesda didn't give a re-enable date for Dogwood Die Off or Jail Break as of this
writing (August 2026, five months after the disabling patch) — check current patch
notes before assuming either has returned. If Grafton Monster strategy is what
you're after, the fuller write-up lives in
[notable bosses and encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/) rather than
here.
