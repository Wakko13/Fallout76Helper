For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Baseball Bat
sits relative to the rest of the one-handed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

The Baseball Bat has one Blade/Head mod slot, but that slot holds an unusually long ladder of
eleven mutually exclusive options — plain bleed/pierce variants, a fire-adding Heated Coil, a
Nuclear-Material-hungry Rocket line, and combo mods that stack multiple effects at once.

## Blade/Head mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chain-wrapped | Superior damage (learned via a ~15% chance when scrapping certain bats) | — | 1x Adhesive, 1x Steel |
| Sharp | Targets bleed; better damage | Makeshift Warrior | 1x Adhesive, 4x Steel |
| Spiked | Armor Penetration +16% | Makeshift Warrior | 1x Adhesive, 1x Screw, 3x Steel |
| Barbed | Armor Penetration +10%; targets bleed | Makeshift Warrior | 1x Adhesive, 3x Steel |
| Bladed | Targets bleed; superior damage | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 1x Adhesive, 1x Oil, 7x Steel |
| Heated Coil | Damage +25%; adds fire damage | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 1x Adhesive, 2x Asbestos, 1x Circuitry, 3x Copper |
| Rocket | Superior damage | Makeshift Warrior | 2x Adhesive, 1x Circuitry, 2x Nuclear Material, 7x Steel |
| Bladed Rocket | Targets bleed; superior damage | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 3x Adhesive, 5x Aluminum, 1x Circuitry, 2x Nuclear Material, 2x Oil |
| Puncturing Rocket | Armor Penetration +22%; superior damage | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 3x Adhesive, 4x Aluminum, 1x Circuitry, 2x Nuclear Material |
| Heated Rocket | Adds fire damage; superior damage | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 4x Adhesive, 5x Aluminum, 1x Circuitry, 3x Copper, 3x Nuclear Material |
| Searing Puncturing Rocket | Damage +20%; Fire Damage +10; Armor Penetration +15%; Extra Limb Damage +25%; Chance to Cripple +20% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 5x Adhesive, 6x Aluminum, 2x Circuitry, 5x Copper, 4x Nuclear Material |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This entry needed cross-referencing beyond Fextralife, whose Baseball Bat page only listed mod
names without effects, perks, or materials for most rows — the numbers above lean on search-indexed
snippets from the fandom mirror (direct fetches of fallout.fandom.com/fallout.wiki returned
402/403 this session, a known limitation). The exact Makeshift Warrior **rank** for Sharp, Spiked,
Barbed, and the base Rocket mod wasn't pinned down precisely (shown as unranked "Makeshift Warrior"
above); the Bladed/Heated Coil/Rocket-combo family consistently required rank 2 across sources.
Separately, Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting
melee weapons and mods entirely — plan knowledge alone is said to suffice now — so treat every perk
column here as the historically-documented requirement rather than a guaranteed current block.
Heated Coil's damage effect also had a conflicting alternate reading in one source ("Damage -40%,
Weight +10%, Fire Damage +36%") that looked like it may have been copy-pasted from a firearm mod
template rather than describing this weapon; the "+25% damage, adds fire damage" version above came
from a more weapon-specific source and is the one to trust first, but verify in-game if precision
matters for a build.
