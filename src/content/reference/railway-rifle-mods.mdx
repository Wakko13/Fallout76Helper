## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The two sources didn't fully agree with each other: Fextralife's populated table only showed Standard
and Automatic Piston for Receiver (no Shotgun Receiver or any Prime variant, and no Barrel or Muzzle
section at all), while Nukes & Dragons' game-data pull shows six receiver variants and adds a Barrel
and Muzzle slot. The Shotgun Receiver in particular is a real, well-known Railway Rifle option in
community builds (it turns the weapon into a short-range one-shot-kill spike shotgun), so its absence
from Fextralife's table is very likely a documentation gap on their end rather than the mod not
existing — but its materials and perk gate are genuinely unconfirmed by any source checked here. The
same caveat applies to both Recon Scopes' exact tracking/accuracy effect text, which Fextralife
described qualitatively ("Tracks targets... superior sighted accuracy") without exact numbers — the
number shown for Short Recon Scope above is inferred from the equivalent non-tracking Short Scope and
should be treated as approximate. As always, exact stat values and material counts shift with balance
patches.
