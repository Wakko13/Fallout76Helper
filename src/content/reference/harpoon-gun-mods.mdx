## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Every "Not documented" materials cell here is deliberate rather than an oversight: a search pass
did turn up specific-looking numbers (e.g. "1x Gear, 2x Oil, 2x Screw, 2x Spring, 4x Steel" for
Standard Harpoon) attributed to a perk gate of "Gun Nut" rank — but Gun Nut is a Fallout 4 perk
name, not a Fallout 76 one (76 uses "Gunsmith"), and Fallout 4's Far Harbor DLC has its own,
differently-modded Harpoon Gun. That's a strong signal of cross-game contamination in the search
results rather than genuine Fallout 76 data, so those figures are excluded here rather than
reproduced. Muzzle only has one confirmed option (No Muzzle) on either source — it's unclear
whether that's genuinely the weapon's only muzzle option or an incomplete table; treat it as
unconfirmed either way. As with the rest of this series, verify materials and perk gates in-game or
against a live wiki before farming toward a specific Harpoon Gun build.
