This entry is scoped to apparel with a **real in-game mechanical effect** (radiation
resistance, underwater breathing, a stealth field, a stat bonus) or a **notable
non-cash-shop acquisition** (a quest reward, a raid drop, a faction-gated purchase).
Purely cosmetic Atomic Shop outfits — the ones with no in-game find/craft/quest path
and no mechanical effect — are intentionally out of scope for this knowledge base;
this is about finding things in the game, not the cash shop catalog.

## Radiation protection

| Item | Effect | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hazmat suit | 1,000 Radiation Resistance plus a hidden 98% radiation damage reduction — the standard answer to "how do I stand in a rad zone." No other armor or clothing can be worn over it. | World loot: reliably found at **Poseidon Energy Plant WV-06** (south of Vault 76, in an exterior room on a pile of boxes — an easy early grab), **Emmett Mountain Disposal Site**, **Federal Disposal Field HZ-21**, and **West Tek Research Center** (damaged variants). Plans have also been made purchasable from select robot vendors since patch 1.2.3.9, letting you craft your own once learned. |
| Chinese stealth armor | Same radiation protection as a Hazmat suit (1,000 RR / 98% reduction) **plus** a modulating stealth field that makes you much harder to spot while crouching (similar to a Stealth Boy) and 90% fall-damage reduction | Plans for the armor and helmet are a reward from **"Invisible Ties,"** a Settlers-faction quest. Alternatively purchasable from Samuel for Gold Bullion once that currency system is unlocked. |

## Underwater / waterborne hazards

| Item | Effect | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cave diving suit | Underwater breathing, immunity to water radiation and disease, protection from airborne hazards, 1,000 Radiation Resistance | Found in a chest during the **"Supplying Demands"** quest at Kerwood Mine, overlooking the flooded section. Unique — if you don't grab it during the quest, there's no other way to get it. |
| Diver suit | Same category of effects as the Cave diving suit (underwater breathing, water radiation/disease immunity, airborne hazard protection) but lower Radiation Resistance (250) | World find — check wiki listings for current spawn locations; less exclusive than the Cave diving suit. |
| Marine Wetsuit | Underarmor that protects against waterborne hazards; worn underneath other armor pieces rather than replacing them | Plan found at armor-plan spawns in the Mire and Cranberry Bog; also a rare (~3%) drop from Mirelurk Queens. |

## Vault jumpsuits (underarmor with mod slots)

Vault jumpsuits are worn as underarmor beneath your actual armor pieces and, once
modded with Vault-Tec Underarmor Linings, add small amounts of Damage/Energy/Radiation
Resistance — a minor but real mechanical layer, not just a reskin.

| Item | Notes | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vault 76 jumpsuit | The default starting jumpsuit | Given automatically during **"Reclamation Day"** (the game's opening quest); the Plan can also drop from creatures, containers, treasure maps, and armor-plan spawns across most regions if you need a spare/clean copy later. |
| Vault-Tec jumpsuit | Rare regional variant | Rarely obtained from daily quests/events in The Forest, or via its plan from the Overseer's Cache in Grafton. |

## Faction disguises required for quest access

A handful of outfits aren't just flavor — the game checks what you're wearing and
treats you as hostile-to-enemies or friendly-to-NPCs based on it:

| Item | Effect | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cultist's garments | Required to move through Old Crimora Mines without the cultists turning hostile — take it off (or never put it on) and you're recognized as a Vault 63 agent and attacked | Found in a small shack right of the mine entrance at Old Crimora Mines, as part of the **"Oldest Trick in the Book"** main quest introduced with the Skyline Valley update. |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Cave diving suit's "unique, no other source" status and the Diver suit's exact
Radiation Resistance figure are wiki-sourced and worth a quick sanity check if a patch
has touched underwater content recently. Hazmat suit plan availability at robot vendors
is patch-dependent (post-1.2.3.9) and vendor stock rotates, so treat "buy the plan" as
"check regularly" rather than guaranteed. Faction-disguise mechanics like the Old
Crimora Mines cultist check are newer (Skyline Valley) content and more likely to shift
with hotfixes than older systems.
