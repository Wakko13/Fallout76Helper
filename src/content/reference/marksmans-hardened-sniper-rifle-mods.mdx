## What's actually known

Fextralife's dedicated page for the Marksman's Hardened Sniper Rifle has essentially no populated
data — its perk requirement, minimum level, crafting restrictions, location, and mod section are all
placeholder ("??") text rather than real values, and it doesn't have its own entry in Nukes & Dragons'
weapon database (which does cover ordinary weapons like the Combat Rifle in detail). That's a genuinely
thin/unreliable source situation, not a case of the weapon having zero mods by design.

Community reports (Steam discussions) describe players successfully attaching accuracy- and
damage-oriented mods to Marksman-branded sniper rifles, which is consistent with this weapon sharing
the standard **Combat Rifle** mod pool — [Rifles and carbines](../rifles-and-carbines/) already
describes it as "a unique Combat Rifle variant." If that relationship holds, its receiver, barrel,
stock, magazine, sight, and muzzle options would match the Combat Rifle's own mod-slot mods; check the
Combat Rifle's mod-table entry in this same reference section for that full breakdown once it's
published, since duplicating an unconfirmed table here risks getting weapon-specific details wrong.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Everything on this page beyond "the wiki source is thin" is inference rather than a confirmed fact:
whether this weapon truly shares the Combat Rifle's exact mod pool 1:1, whether it comes with any
mods pre-attached that can't be removed, and its current in-game acquisition method (also flagged as
unresolved on the [Rifles and carbines](../rifles-and-carbines/) overview) are all worth checking
against a freshly-edited wiki page before planning a build or a farming route around this specific
weapon. This is one of the weaker-sourced pages in this batch — a short, honest page felt more useful
than inventing a mod table that can't be verified.
