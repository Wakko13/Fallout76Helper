For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Flamer sits
relative to its Ultracite-fueled cousin the Cremator, see [Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/).

Fextralife's page confirms the Flamer has four mod slots — Tank, Barrel, Propellant Tank, and
Nozzle — but its own effect/materials table came back unpopulated (placeholder "??" perk text
throughout). The effect numbers below are Nukes & Dragons' percentage figures; materials are
filled in only where a targeted search of individual Fandom mod-item pages turned up a Fallout
76-specific (not Fallout 4) crafting cost.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

No perk gate came through for any Flamer mod on any source checked — that's a real gap, not a
"Standard mod, no perk" situation, since several of these (Vaporization Nozzle in particular) read
as clearly late-tier mods that should sit behind a Gunsmith rank. A Fandom search did turn up
Fallout 4-specific Napalm Tank materials (4x Adhesive, 8x Aluminum, 2x Copper, 3x Screw), but that's
a different game's crafting cost for a similarly-named mod, not Fallout 76 data, so it's
deliberately omitted here rather than reused. Materials for the Tank, Barrel, and Propellant Tank
slots weren't found on any Fallout 76-specific source in the time available — treat every
"Not documented" cell as a prompt to verify in-game or against a live wiki before farming toward a
specific loadout.
