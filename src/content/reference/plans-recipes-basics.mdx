**Scope note:** this covers the mechanics of the plan/recipe system and a couple of
concrete highlights. It is deliberately not a full database of every plan and where
to get it — that's a much larger, faster-decaying reference than fits one entry.
Check FalloutBuilds or the Fallout Wiki plan-category pages for a specific item.

## How it works

- **Plans** unlock the ability to craft weapon/armor mods, camp building pieces, and
  similar non-consumable recipes. **Recipes** (a separate but same-mechanic category)
  unlock food/drink/chem crafting.
- Picking one up does **not** auto-learn it. It sits in your inventory as an item you
  can read (learn) at will — or drop for another player. This is the basis for plan
  trading: if you already know a plan and find a duplicate, drop it for a teammate
  instead of scrapping it.
- Once learned, a plan/recipe is a **permanent, character-wide unlock** — it shows a
  "(Known)" tag if you encounter another copy, and you never need the physical item
  again to craft that thing.
- Learned plans/recipes are per-character, not account-wide, and are not something
  you can currently transfer after learning (only before, as an unlearned item).

## Where they come from

- **World pickups** — containers, workbenches, and points of interest across
  Appalachia; some are fixed spawns, others are randomized from a pool.
- **Vendors** — most notably **Minerva**, a traveling gold-bullion vendor who rotates
  through a set of plan lists (including a periodic "Big Sale") and moves between four
  fixed locations (Foundation, The Crater, Fort Atlas, Whitespring Resort) on a weekly
  cycle. The **Vault 79 gold bullion vendor** (unlocked after the Wastelanders
  main questline) sells a large fixed catalog of gold-bullion plans.
- **Quest rewards** — many faction and main-story quests hand out plans directly,
  including some not obtainable any other way.
- **Event/Daily Ops rewards** — Daily Ops and certain public events reward plans,
  sometimes at higher completion tiers only (e.g. Elder-rank Daily Ops).
- **Workshops** — capturing or successfully defending a workshop can grant camp
  construction plans (walls, siren towers, and other functional structures) the
  first time you claim it.

## Concrete highlights

- **Gold bullion plans** (Minerva / Vault 79 vendor) cover some of the strongest mod
  plans in the game — e.g. the plasma caster's pulse/true capacitor mods and the
  Gauss pistol's hardened receiver — and are otherwise very hard to get any other way.
- **Workshop-only plans** (advanced walls, tower components, siren towers) are only
  granted the first time you personally claim a workshop, which surprises a lot of
  players who assume all camp plans come from loot or vendors.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Vendor rotation cadence (Minerva's location schedule, "Big Sale" timing) and exact
plan-to-vendor mappings shift with game updates more than most mechanics in this
entry — treat the "how it works" section as durable and the specific vendor examples
as needing a freshness check against FalloutBuilds/Minerva tracker before relying on
them for a specific plan hunt.
