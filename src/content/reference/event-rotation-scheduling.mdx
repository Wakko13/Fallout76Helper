**This is the highest-staleness category in this knowledge base.** Anything naming a
specific event, date, or "currently running" content will be wrong within days.
What follows is the underlying mechanism — durable — plus where to look up what's
actually live right now.

## Public events

A random public event spawns automatically roughly **every 20 real-time minutes**
somewhere in Appalachia, visible as a map notification any nearby (or fast-traveling)
player can join — no signup, no server-wide requirement to have a full team.

**Mutated Public Events** are a recurring variant (roughly every other week, skipped
if it collides with a seasonal/limited-time event) where public events spawn with
extra enemy mutations and better rewards; while active, a mutated event is guaranteed
at the top of every hour rather than the normal random ~20-minute cadence.

## Daily Ops

Short, replayable instanced missions (best done as a team) with their own daily
reset, a rotating enemy/mutation combination, and a completion-time-based reward
tier — full "Elder" rank rewards require finishing fast (historically under 8
minutes) and are capped once per day per character. Ops themselves run on a
recurring but non-daily *availability* schedule (roughly every other weekend
historically) — check the calendar for the current cadence rather than assuming
they're always up.

## Seasonal & weekend events

- **Seasonal events** tie to real-world holidays or recurring in-game dates (e.g. a
  Halloween-themed event, a winter holiday event), run for roughly one to two weeks,
  and offer a prize pool that's only obtainable during that window.
- **Weekend events** (Thursday through Monday) don't add unique content — they're
  bonus multiplier weekends (double XP, double Gold Bullion, increased currency from
  a specific activity, etc.) layered on top of whatever's already running.
- Both are scheduled in advance by Bethesda and published as a **Community
  Calendar** covering the upcoming weeks/season.

## Where to check the current live schedule

- **fallout.bethesda.net** — official news posts, including Community Calendar
  announcements, directly from Bethesda.
- **FalloutBuilds.com/fo76/events/** — third-party tracker that stays current with
  the published calendar and adds context/explanation.
- In-game: the map and event log show what's active/upcoming for your world right
  now, which is more reliable than any written page for "what's live this exact
  minute."

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Specific numeric details here (the ~20-minute public event timer, Daily Ops'
availability cadence, the Elder-rank time threshold) are pulled from community
write-ups rather than a single authoritative patch-note source, and Bethesda has
adjusted Daily Ops availability rules before — verify against a current source if
precision matters (e.g. you're timing a farming session around it).
