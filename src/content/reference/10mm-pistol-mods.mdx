## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Sight and Muzzle tables above are effect-only — Fextralife's 10mm Pistol page didn't return
materials or perk gates for any sight or muzzle mod on this pass (unlike the Pipe weapons and .44
Pistol pages, where at least some sight/muzzle rows had full data). Several Barrel and Receiver rows
are similarly effect-only where marked "Not documented." The **Prime Receiver's perk gate** is worth
independently verifying — Fextralife listed "Gunsmith 2" for it here, but the Pipe Pistol's
equivalent Prime Receiver on this same source is gated at Gunsmith 5, which is the more commonly
cited requirement for Prime receivers across Fallout 76 weapons; this may be a data-entry
inconsistency on the wiki rather than a real per-weapon difference. As always, exact numeric stat
values and material counts shift with balance patches, so treat the above as a strong starting point
rather than a guaranteed-current recipe.
