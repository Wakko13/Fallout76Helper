For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Super Sledge
sits relative to the Sledgehammer and the rest of the two-handed lineup, see
[Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

Despite the Super Sledge's status as one of the best per-hit damage dealers in the game, its mod
depth is genuinely thin — only one mod is documented as actually obtainable and craftable.

## Head mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heating Coil | Adds energy damage; Weight 3 | Makeshift Warrior 4 | 3x Asbestos, 4x Adhesive, 3x Circuitry, 7x Copper |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's Super Sledge page also lists a **Stun Pack** mod ("Adds Electrical damage, and chance
to stun") with no perk or materials filled in. Cross-checking against a search-indexed fandom
source suggests Stun Pack is leftover Fallout 4 content (it exists there on the baton, Revolutionary
Sword, and Super Sledge) that **was never actually implemented as craftable in Fallout 76** — it
isn't listed here as a real mod option for that reason, but flag it if you spot it in-game, since
that would directly contradict this reading. If the Super Sledge really only has one functional
mod, that's a genuinely thin mod pool for a signature weapon — matching this wave's instruction to
say so plainly rather than pad the table. Separately, Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025)
reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting melee weapons and mods entirely, so treat
Makeshift Warrior 4 as the historically-documented requirement rather than a guaranteed current
block.
