For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how T-60 stacks up against
the other five models and where to find one, see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/). For the
full functional-mod pool T-60 draws from and the perk-requirement caveats common to every power
armor set, see [T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) — T-60's torso/leg/arm/
helmet mod names matched T-45's list exactly.

As with every power armor set, there is **no Sturdy/Hardened-style resistance-tier ladder** for
T-60 — its paint list (below) is the closest equivalent, and most paints are cosmetic-only or grant
SPECIAL points rather than resistance.

## Torso mods (incl. Jet Pack)

Same ten-mod pool as T-45. Jet Pack and (partially) Optimized Servos materials were confirmed
directly for T-60 and are noticeably higher than T-45's, consistent with T-60 being a mid-tier
model above T-45/T-51.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Assembly | Action Point regen +6 | Not documented | Not documented for T-60 specifically |
| Stealth Boy | Activates stealth field while crouched | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Motion-Assist Servos | Strength +2 | Not documented | Not documented for T-60 specifically |
| Kinetic Dynamo | Restores AP equal to 20% of damage taken | Not documented | Not documented for T-60 specifically |
| Reactive Plates | Reflects 50% of melee damage back on the attacker | Not documented | Not documented for T-60 specifically |
| Tesla Coils | Deals continuous Energy damage to nearby enemies | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Medic Pump | Auto-uses a Stimpak when health drops below 50% | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Blood Cleanser | -50% chance to get addicted when consuming chems | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Emergency Protocols | Below 20% health: +25% move speed, -50% damage taken | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Jet Pack | Jet-assisted boost while jumping; Max AP -1, Weight +20% | Not documented | Confirmed: 6x Adhesive, 9x Aluminum, 8x Asbestos, 10x Nuclear Material |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **The Brotherhood-of-Steel rank paint names (Initiate/Knight/Sergeant/Captain/Officer/Sentinel/
  Paladin/Elder) were surfaced as a flat list with no effect data at all** — it's not confirmed
  whether any of them grant a SPECIAL bonus the way Hot Rod/Military/Minutemen paint do on other
  sets, or whether they're purely cosmetic rank insignia. Treat every "Cosmetic" entry above as an
  assumption based on the general power armor paint pattern, not a confirmed per-mod description.
- **Optimized Servos materials came from a search-engine summary**, not a direct per-mod page fetch
  (unlike T-60's Jet Pack, which was fetched directly) — slightly lower confidence than the Jet Pack
  row.
- **Perk requirements are entirely undocumented for T-60** specifically — see
  [T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) Uncertain section for the aggregated
  (unverified) Science!/Armorer rank claims that plausibly extend to T-60 by the same functional-mod
  family.
- **Whether the "Distant Thunder" event still drops T-60 plans** is called out as community-reported
  and worth a sanity check in [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/) — doesn't affect mods
  specifically, but is relevant if you're trying to unlock T-60 crafting at all before modding it.
- **Base T-60 torso stats** (context, not a mod): 554 Damage Resistance / 460 Energy Resistance / 607
  Radiation Resistance at level 50, plus a listed 200 Carry Capacity figure that — as with T-51 —
  may already reflect a Calibrated Shocks leg mod rather than a base-piece stat; per Nukes & Dragons.
