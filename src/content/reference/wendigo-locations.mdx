This entry covers regular wild Wendigos found roaming Appalachia. For the scripted
Wendigo Colossus fight (Earle Williams' transformed boss form in the "A Colossal
Problem" public event, which also spawns waves of regular Wendigos as adds), see
[Notable repeatable boss encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/) instead.

## Reliable spawns

- [**Wendigo Cave**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Wendigo_Cave) (Savage Divide) — the namesake location and a dependable multi-spawn
  cave, worth the trip on its own.
- [**Monongah Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Monongah_Mine) — another consistently Wendigo-populated cave/mine interior.
- [**Big B's Rest Stop**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Big_B's_Rest_Stop) (southern Mire, near Camp Venture) — one Wendigo reliably
  spawns inside the rest stop building.
- [**Haven Church**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Haven_Church) (center of the Mire) — a Wendigo frequently spawns inside alongside
  multiple Ghouls, so expect a mixed fight.
- [**Hawke's Refuge**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Hawke's_Refuge) — a chance for a Wendigo to spawn in a small side cavern off the
  main cave.

## Chance spawns and convenient challenge spots

- [**Freddy Fear's House of Scares**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Freddy_Fear's_House_of_Scares), [**KMAX Transmission**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/KMAX_Transmission), and [**Appalachian
  Antiques**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Appalachian_Antiques) each carry roughly a 1-in-3 chance of a single Wendigo spawn — useful
  as secondary stops if your main location comes up empty.
- If you only need one kill for a seasonal or daily challenge, [**Lewisburg**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Lewisburg) and the
  [**Pylon Ambush Site**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Pylon_Ambush_Site) are called out specifically because their Wendigo spawns sit
  close to a fast-travel point, minimizing travel time.
- Wendigos can also appear in **Appalachian blast zones** (irradiated areas created by
  nuke detonations), though this isn't a fixed farming spot.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact spawn odds at the "chance" locations (roughly 1/3) are community-sourced
estimates, not official numbers, and any of these can be affected by server state or
recent nuke activity in the area. If you're chasing a specific Wendigo variant (e.g.
for a unique drop), verify current behavior against a recent guide since Bethesda has
adjusted spawn tables in past updates.
