For general faction background (Crater vs. Foundation, reputation ranks), see
[factions and reputation](../factions-and-reputation/). This page indexes the Wastelanders-era Raiders
and Settlers story quests specifically — where the unique gear actually comes from.

## Pre-Wastelanders: Key to the Past

Before the Raiders/Settlers reputation system even existed, an earlier questline through Rose at
Pleasant Valley Ski Resort ends with **Key to the Past**, whose reward is a **full, ready-to-wear
Raider Power Armor set** (no crafting required) plus a Missile Launcher plan. This is *not* the same
content as the Vault 79 heist arc below — it's an earlier, separate Raider-flavored storyline. See
[power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/) for how this fits into getting your first suit.

## The shared opening: The New Arrivals

Both the Raiders and Settlers stories share the same opening main quests before you pick a side:
**The New Arrivals** (level 20+; also the quest where you name a Nuka-Cola flavor — see
[Nuka-Cola mixing](../nuka-cola-mixing/)) leads into **Overseer Overseen**, after which the story
branches based on who you choose to help.

## Raiders arc (Crater)

| Quest | Prerequisite | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fun and Games | Raiders branch, mid-arc | A prototype "Steel Mill" T-51b power armor helmet — conditional on being cooperative with Ra-Ra in dialogue during the quest |
| Buried Treasure | Fun and Games | **Slug Buster**, a unique plasma rifle — conditional on letting Ra-Ra help during the Vault 79 break-in; community reports say it's technically obtainable even on a Settlers-leaning save, but it's far more reliable through the Raiders path |
| Siding with Crater | Buried Treasure | Locks in the Raiders ending |
| Secrets Revealed | Siding with Crater | Raiders-side heist finale; opens the Vault 79 gold room (1,000 Gold Bullion, the currency spent with Samuel/Smiley and other faction vendors) |

## Settlers arc (Foundation)

| Quest | Prerequisite | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trade Secrets | Settlers branch, mid-arc | Sets up the Foundation side of the Vault 79 plan |
| Invisible Ties | Trade Secrets | **Chinese Stealth Armor and helmet plans** — a guaranteed reward on this quest (also purchasable later from Samuel at Foundation for Gold Bullion if you miss it) |
| Duty Calls | Invisible Ties | Continues the Foundation side of the heist prep |
| Siding With Foundation | Duty Calls | Locks in the Settlers ending |
| Division of Wealth | Siding With Foundation | Continues toward the finale |
| All That Glitters | Division of Wealth | Settlers-side heist finale (this is the quest behind the "A Golden Future" achievement/trophy) |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact quest immediately before "Fun and Games" and "Trade Secrets" (i.e., the full blow-by-blow of
the early Raiders/Settlers branch before the questlines given here) wasn't fully pinned down — treat
the tables above as "confirmed waypoints with reliable rewards," not an exhaustive step-by-step. The
Slug Buster's availability to Settlers-aligned characters is disputed in community discussion — some
players report it requires a Raiders-flagged save specifically. Whether "Siding with Crater" /
"Siding With Foundation" grant any item themselves (versus just being the choice-lock quest) wasn't
confirmed; they're listed here mainly as sequence markers.
