For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Gauss Minigun's
Gold Bullion acquisition cost compares to the rest of the heavy-weapon family, see
[Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/). Fextralife's own Gauss Minigun page returned a 404 on every
slug variant tried this session, so this table is built from Nukes & Dragons' database plus
targeted Fandom item-page searches.

**Important naming correction:** unlike the Gauss Rifle, Gauss Pistol, and Gauss Shotgun (which
all use a "Capacitor" slot with the Standard/Quick/Large/Swift/Perforating/Piercing/Stinging/Prime
naming family — see [Gauss Rifle mod options](../gauss-rifle-mods/)), the Gauss Minigun's
equivalent slot is genuinely named **Tesla Coil**, with its own distinct option names (Dynamo,
Capacitor, and a Prime Capacitor). Every source checked for this page agrees on that — it is not a
scrape error.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Tri Barrel row is the shakiest entry on this page: the Fandom page that supplied its materials
and perk gate covers the Tri Barrel mod shared by name between the regular Minigun and the Gauss
Minigun, and its "learn method" text referenced a **Plan: Minigun tri barrel** rather than a
Gauss-specific plan name — that's a real risk this page is showing the base Minigun's Tri Barrel
cost rather than the Gauss Minigun's own. The Penta Barrel row, by contrast, cited a
Gauss-Minigun-specific plan name ("Plan: Gauss minigun penta barrel") and is more trustworthy. No
materials or perk gate were found for Gunner Sights, No Tesla Coil, Tesla Coil Dynamo, or Prime
Capacitor on any source checked — the Prime Capacitor's plan-drop gate is inferred from the
existence of "Plan: Prime Gauss minigun capacitor" as a named item rather than confirmed
crafting-menu text. Treat every "Not documented" or "unconfirmed" cell as a prompt to verify
in-game or against a live wiki before farming toward a specific Gauss Minigun build.
