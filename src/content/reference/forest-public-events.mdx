Nine public events spawn on the general public-event rotation in The Forest — see
[event scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/) for how that rotation actually
works. This entry is the roster: what each one is and where it triggers, not when
it's currently live.

| Event | Trigger location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Back on the Beat** | Morgantown, at the Responders' liquor-store HQ | Power up the Protectron "Steelheart," then escort him to all three Responder safe rooms and defend him for 60 seconds while he tests an alarm | Access to Responder stash rooms in Morgantown |
| **Collision Course** | South end of Morgantown Airport, past the runway gates | Activate a mortar within 4 minutes to fire a flare/siren, then survive five waves of Scorched | A Cargobot flies in and drops a Government Aid Drop |
| **Death Blossoms** | Random Forest location (radiant spawn) | Defend five blossoming mutated corpse flowers for 7 minutes from creatures drawn in by the smell | Corpse Flower Seeds, plus the usual legendary-item chance |
| **Feed the People** | Mama Dolce's Food Processing | Activate the food-processing terminal, gather soy chunks/beef stock/veggie mix, load the hopper, then alternate defending and repairing 3 consoles until the timer runs out | Canned goods/food rewards |
| **Fertile Soil** | Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center, Flatwoods (south of Overseer's camp) | Kill three rogue Mister Handy supervisors (Wellington, Chattingham, Danforth), then use the mainframe terminal to reset the farm robots' targeting | Fertilizer, plus ongoing fertilizer drops at owned nearby workshops (Tyler County Dirt Track, Sunshine Meadows Industrial Farm, Billings Homestead, Dabney Homestead) |
| **Leader of the Pack** | Near Aaronholt Homestead, Tyler County Fairgrounds, or Transmission Station 1AT-U03 | Kill the three legendary wolf-pack leaders to draw out the "big bad wolf" | Raw wolf meat and a shot at rare apparel |
| **Project Beanstalk** | Silva Homestead barn (Arktos Pharma terminal) | Escort Pharmabot JD-7E to three crop-field stops (Alpha/Beta/Gamma) and protect it for a minute at each while it sprays pesticide, fending off stingwings and mole rats | Standard event reward pool (chance at legendary gear) |
| **The Path to Enlightenment** | Landview Lighthouse | Kill fireflies and radtoads for bioluminescent fluid, then deposit it into the lighthouse lamp within a 30-minute window to summon the Wise Mothman | Interact with the Wise Mothman for a temporary "Wisdom of the Mothman" perk |
| **Tea Time** | The Giant Teapot, north of the Power Plant | Start the boilers, then defend three pipes (main pipe behind the teapot, a secondary near the Red Rocket station, and the water-tower pipe) across 3 creature waves | Completion reward plus the usual legendary chance |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact fluid amount for The Path to Enlightenment varies between sources (30
units in some write-ups, 50 in others) — likely a value that's been tuned across
patches, so don't be surprised if it doesn't match exactly. Community forum threads
have periodically questioned whether Back on the Beat still spawns reliably, but no
patch note documents it being disabled — treat that as a "may feel rare," not
"removed." None of these nine appear on Bethesda's list of currently disabled
events (that list, as of the March 2026 "Backwoods" update, only covers Jail Break
and Dogwood Die Off in Toxic Valley — see
[Toxic Valley's public events](../toxic-valley-public-events/)). If a boss/mechanic
here overlaps a fight covered in more depth elsewhere, check
[notable bosses and encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/) first.
