**A source note up front:** Fextralife's Cryolator page confirms three mod categories exist (Barrel,
Stock, Sights) but its table came back with no populated mod rows — consistent with the page's own
"??" markers elsewhere. The table below instead comes from Nukes & Dragons' game-data-driven database,
which uses **percentage-based** effect values, breaks the mod slots out slightly differently (Scope,
Grip, Magazine, Muzzle, Barrel — five slots rather than Fextralife's three), and does not list crafting
materials or perk gates for any row.

## Barrel mods

The Cryolator's barrel slot is unusual: alongside a plain damage-boosting barrel, several options
switch the weapon from its normal freezing stream to firing physical cryogenic capsules instead.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |

| Standard Barrel | Standard | — | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

No crafting materials or perk gates were found for any Cryolator mod on either source checked —
Fextralife's table was effectively empty and Nukes & Dragons doesn't publish materials at all, so every
"Not documented" cell above is a genuine gap rather than an oversight. The three cryogenic-capsule
barrel options (Blasted, Polar Lobber, Crystallizing) only came through with a one-line qualitative
effect each ("shoots cryogenic capsules") and no numeric stat deltas — it's unclear from the sources
checked whether these are craftable standard mods or tied to unique/event-drop Cryolator variants,
so treat their availability as unconfirmed until checked against a live wiki. If you're chasing a
specific Cryolator build, verify exact numbers and any Science!/Gunsmith gate in-game before farming
materials.
