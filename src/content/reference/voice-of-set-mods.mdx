For general modding mechanics (Weapons Workbench, Gunsmith perk, mod plans), see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Voice of Set sits among
the other sidearms, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

Nothing to build a table out of here: Fextralife's page states plainly that the Voice of Set has **no mod
slots** — its "Mods" section reads "None." As a unique reward tied to the Mistress of Mystery gear line
(obtained during the Prototypical Problems quest and crafted at the Order of Mysteries' fabricator, per the
[Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/) overview), it ships as a fixed-stat collector's weapon rather
than a base gun you customize at a Weapons Workbench. Its roughly-20%-higher base damage over a standard .44
Pistol is baked in rather than something you add via mods.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This is a short, accurate answer rather than a thin one: the source is explicit and unambiguous about the
weapon having zero mod slots, so there's nothing here likely to have been missed. The one thing worth
double-checking against a current wiki page is whether a later update ever added moddability to unique
quest weapons like this one — unlikely, but unique-weapon mod support has occasionally changed across patches
for other games in the series.
