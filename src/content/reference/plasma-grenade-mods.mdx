## No mod slots

The Plasma Grenade genuinely has **no weapon mods** — Fextralife's entry lists the mod section as
unfilled/not applicable, and nukesdragons' interactive weapon database (which does surface mod
configuration options for moddable guns) shows none for it either. It's crafted at a **Tinker's
Workbench**, requiring the Demolition Expert 4 and Science 1 perks, from the Plan: Plasma Grenade —
there's no separate Weapons-Workbench mod tree layered on top of that craft. What you build is a
fixed 200 ballistic + 200 energy damage explosive with no Receiver/Barrel/Sight-equivalent slots.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- **Demolition Expert** still scales its damage as a perk-card multiplier — see
  [Grenades, mines, and thrown explosives](../explosives-and-thrown/) for the current rank breakdown.
  That's independent of the (absent) weapon-mod system.
- Legendary Plasma Grenades exist as a separate legendary-star system, unrelated to weapon mods.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's Plasma Grenade page has several other placeholder fields (exact crafting materials,
drop locations) beyond mods, so this is a thin-documentation source rather than one that explicitly
rules mods out with a citation. The consistent absence across both Fextralife and nukesdragons, and
the same pattern holding for every other grenade/mine checked in this wave, makes "no mods" the safe
read — but a quick in-game Weapons Workbench check would remove any remaining doubt.
