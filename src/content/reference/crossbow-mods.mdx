For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how to acquire the Crossbow
itself, see [Rifles and carbines](../rifles-and-carbines/).

**A source discrepancy worth flagging up front:** Fextralife's Crossbow page states its mod section is
"None." That conflicts with Nukes & Dragons' game-data-driven weapon database, which lists a working
Frame slot and a Scope slot, and with The Fallout Wiki's separate plan pages for Cryo, Explosive, and
Poisoned crossbow frame mods — all of which independently confirm those mods exist and are player
learnable. The table below follows Nukes & Dragons and the confirmed plan pages rather than
Fextralife's "None," since multiple independent sources agree the mods are real; treat Fextralife's
Crossbow page as simply out of date on this point rather than authoritative.

## Frame mods (Receiver)

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Frame | Standard | — | Not documented |
| Explosive Frame | Damage -40%, weight +10%; bolts explode on impact | Learned via **Plan: Explosive crossbow frame** (vendor-sold); crafting perk not documented | Not documented |
| Cryo Frame | Damage -40%, weight +10%, cryo damage +60% | Learned via **Plan: Cryo crossbow frame** (vendor-sold); crafting perk not documented | Not documented |
| Flaming/Heated Frame | Damage -40%, weight +10%, fire damage +36% | Plan requirement not confirmed for this specific frame name | Not documented |
| Plasma Frame | Damage -40%, weight +10%, energy damage +60% | Plan requirement not confirmed for this specific frame name | Not documented |
| Poisoned Frame | Damage -40%, weight +10%, poison damage +36% | Learned via **Plan: Poisoned crossbow frame** (vendor-sold); crafting perk not documented | Not documented |
| Prime Frame | Damage to Scorched +65% | Not documented | Not documented |

## Sight mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No Sights | Standard | — | — |
| Iron Sights | No stat change listed | — | Not documented |
| Glow Sights | Weight +5%, AP cost -20% | — | Not documented |

There is no separate Limb, String, Stock, Barrel, or Magazine slot documented for the Crossbow in
either source — the mod pool appears to be just the two categories above.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This page is the messiest sourcing situation among this batch: Fextralife directly contradicts two
other independent sources on whether the Crossbow has mods at all. The elemental frame names
themselves ("Flaming" vs. "Heated") are inconsistently labeled between Nukes & Dragons' UI text and
the plan-name conventions used elsewhere on the wikis, so those two rows in particular are worth a
live in-game check if you're hunting for a specific plan. No crafting-material lists were found for
any row on either side. If you're chasing an elemental-bolt stealth build, confirm current frame
availability and materials against a live wiki before planning a farming route — this is exactly the
kind of page where a future patch or a wiki correction could change the picture.
