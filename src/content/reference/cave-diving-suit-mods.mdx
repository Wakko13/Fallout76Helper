## No mod slots

The Cave diving suit is a **body suit** in the same category as the Hazmat Suit and Chinese
stealth armor — one item filling every armor-related slot at once, not a standard five-piece set.
Sources describing it are explicit that it **cannot be scrapped, sold, or dropped**, and that body
suits as a category cannot take legendary effects; no mod table for it appears on any source
checked this session. Combined with its unique, single-copy acquisition (a chest during "Supplying
Demands" at Kerwood Mine — miss it and it's gone for that character), there's no real avenue for
modding it even if a mod slot existed: what's in the chest is permanently what you get.

Its fixed effects — underwater breathing, immunity to water radiation and disease, protection from
airborne hazards, and 1,000 Radiation Resistance — are the complete package, with no lining or
material tier to upgrade them.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

As with the Hazmat Suit and Chinese stealth armor, no source found this session states "no mods"
for the Cave diving suit in explicit terms the way the Black Powder Pistol's Fextralife page does
— this is inferred from its body-suit classification and the absence of any mod table anywhere
it's documented. The suit's "no other way to get it" uniqueness is itself wiki-sourced (per the
existing [Functional attire](../functional-attire/) entry's own uncertainty note) and worth a
sanity check if underwater content has been touched by a recent patch.
