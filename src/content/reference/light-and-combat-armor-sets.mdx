For the mod-slot system these armors share (Cushioned, Deep Pocketed, Dense, Sturdy/Hardened
material tiers, etc.), see
[weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For legendary star
effects any of these can roll, see
[legendary effects overview](../legendary-effects-overview/). This entry covers the sets
themselves and how to acquire each — not individual piece/material combinations.

## Low tier — early game

| Set | Profile | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leather armor | Highest **Energy Resistance** of any body armor, weak Damage Resistance | Plan found very early via world loot/containers; among the easiest plans to pick up in the first few hours. |
| Metal armor | Opposite of Leather — strong **Damage Resistance**, weak Energy Resistance | Also an early, common plan; found in containers and off early human enemies. |

Both are viable to mix-and-match (e.g., Metal legs/arms with Leather chest) since resistances
are tracked per body slot, not as an all-or-nothing set bonus.

## Mid tier

| Set | Profile | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combat armor | Balanced Damage + Energy Resistance, arguably the most mod-flexible set in the game | Plan drops from vendors (Brotherhood/Responders vendor bots rotate stock roughly every 24 hours), world containers, and military-adjacent locations; also a rare find at spots like the Rangers District Office. No quest requirement. |
| Robot armor | Balanced resistance with some radiation resistance built in; visually themed after in-game robots | Plan/world-spawn armor found in containers, off boss drops, and treasure maps concentrated in Savage Divide, Cranberry Bog, Ash Heap, and the Mire (the Mire specifically: Camp Venture, Harpers Ferry, Treetops, KMAX Transmission, Sunday Brothers' cabin, Mosstown, Dagger's Den). |
| Trapper armor | Balanced resistance, themed around Appalachian trappers/scavengers | Plans found in containers/world spawns across Toxic Valley, Savage Divide, Cranberry Bog, Ash Heap, and the Mire; also awarded from **Daily Ops** (level 15-50) and as a reward from the **"Retirement Plan"** quest. Not sold by any fixed vendor. |

## Upper-mid tier — built-in radiation resistance

| Set | Profile | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marine armor (Marine Combat Armor) | Comparable to a heavy Combat Armor build, slightly behind it on raw resistance, but the only dedicated non-power combat armor with **innate radiation resistance**. Comes in level-35 and level-45 grades. | Plans are **not sold by any vendor** — found only in world containers/armor-plan spawns in the Cranberry Bog, Ash Heap, and the Mire. Farming usually means looping those regions' plan-spawn containers. |

## Endgame — Gold Bullion vendor

| Set | Profile | How to acquire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secret Service armor | Fully upgraded, the **highest radiation resistance of any non-power armor set**, with damage resistance only slightly below Metal armor; one of just three non-power sets whose chest piece can take a Jet Pack mod | Requires unlocking the Gold Bullion currency system first (finish **"Secrets Revealed"** for the Raiders or **"All That Glitters"** for the Settlers), then buy the plans from **Reginald "Regs" Stone** in Vault 79 for 5,900 Gold Bullion total. Each crafted piece rolls a random legendary effect, so expect to craft multiples to land the roll you want. |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Vendor plan availability (Combat armor especially) rotates and is inconsistent —
treat "buy it from a vendor" as "check regularly," not a guaranteed source. Marine
armor plan drop rates from world containers are community-reported, not from an
official drop table. Gold Bullion prices (Secret Service armor's 5,900) have shifted
with balance patches before, so confirm the current cost in-game before saving up.
