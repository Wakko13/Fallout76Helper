than a separate rifle-conversion stock the way Pipe weapons work.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's 10mm SMG page returned a notably smaller mod list than the 10mm Pistol's — no Sight or
Muzzle section at all, and only one Magazine entry (the game's own wiki elsewhere mentions a
"Drum Magazine" plan for this weapon that was introduced in a Nuclear Winter-era update, which
doesn't appear on the page fetched here; it may exist but wasn't documented on this pass). Materials
for the Receiver/Stock/Barrel tables above came through cleanly and consistently, so those are more
trustworthy than the apparent gaps elsewhere on the page. As always, exact numeric bonuses and
material costs shift with balance patches — treat the figures above as a strong starting point, and
double check for a Drum Magazine or Sight/Muzzle option in-game before assuming this list is
complete.
