For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how Hellcat stacks up
against the other five endgame models and how to unlock it (auto-granted during "The Catalyst" in
Steel Reign — no plan drop or vendor purchase exists), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).

Hellcat shares the same four-slot functional-mod pool (torso/legs/arms/helmet) documented in
[T-45's mod file](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) — every mod name and effect below matches T-45's
one-for-one, with no Hellcat-exclusive functional mod found on any source checked. What
distinguishes Hellcat is an **inherent, un-modded set bonus** (see below) and a helmet pool that
appears to be **missing Rad Scrubbers**, present on T-45/X-01 but absent from every later power
armor line checked in this initiative so far.

## Set bonus

Wearing all six Hellcat pieces grants **-2% ballistic damage taken per piece, for -12% total at a
full set** — applied before the armor's damage-resistance calculation, so it's particularly strong
against armor-piercing ammunition. This is an inherent trait of the Hellcat pieces themselves, not
a craftable mod — nothing to install at a Power Armor Station for it.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Gold Bullion "costs" listed by The Duchess Flame for every Hellcat mod (200-3000 each) are
  likely plan-purchase prices from a vendor like Regs, not Power Armor Station crafting-material
  costs** — they don't match the Adhesive/Aluminum/Rubber/etc. material pattern confirmed for T-45
  in part 1, and Hellcat's own acquisition path (auto-unlocked during "The Catalyst," no
  vendor/plan-drop path per `power-armor-sets.mdx`) makes a Gold Bullion vendor origin questionable
  for the base armor, though individual mod plans could plausibly still route through Regs. Treat
  every material/cost figure in that source as unverified against a second independent source.
- **Rad Scrubbers' absence from Hellcat's helmet pool is inferred from omission**, not a direct
  "this mod doesn't exist for Hellcat" statement — a broader search confirmed Rad Scrubbers is
  compatible with Excavator, Raider, T-45, T-51b, T-60, Ultracite, and X-01, notably **not** listing
  Hellcat, Union, T-65, Strangler Heart, or Vulcan. Worth a quick in-game check at a Power Armor
  Station before assuming it's unavailable.
- **Core Assembly's Action Point figure is inconsistent across sources**: T-45's file (part 1)
  states a flat "+6" AP regen; The Duchess Flame's Hellcat-specific page states "+6% AP
  regeneration." These could describe the same underlying value differently, or one source is
  simply wrong — not resolved here.
- **No Hellcat-exclusive functional mod (torso/leg/arm/helmet) was found on any source** — unlike
  Strangler Heart (Welded Rebar) or Vulcan (Reflection Assembly, Gimbal Bracers, Ultracite Bracers,
  Elastic Servos), Hellcat appears to rely entirely on the shared base pool plus its inherent
  ballistic-reduction set bonus. Worth a second check in case a raid/event-exclusive Hellcat mod
  exists that wasn't surfaced by this session's searches.
- **Materials for every mod on this page were not found on any reachable source** — same gap as
  most of T-45's pool; Fextralife's per-mod Hellcat pages weren't reachable this session and
  fallout.wiki/fallout.fandom.com returned 403 on every direct fetch attempt.
