For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how to acquire The Dragon
itself, see [Rifles and carbines](../rifles-and-carbines/).

## No weapon mod slots

The Dragon genuinely has **no weapon mod slots** — Nukes & Dragons' database entry lists only
legendary-star mod slots for it (the separate legendary-effect system, covered below), with no
Receiver, Barrel, Stock, Sight, or Muzzle category at all. This wasn't always the case: community
reports indicate The Dragon could previously take a Large Bayonet like its base Black Powder Rifle,
but that option was removed from The Dragon specifically in a past balance update — any copies
obtained with a bayonet already attached reportedly had it stripped off. The regular (non-unique)
Black Powder Rifle can still take a Large Bayonet; The Dragon cannot.

What you loot or craft is what you fire: its identity is the four-barrel single-trigger volley and
slow reload, not a build-around modding platform.

## Two things still worth knowing

- It can still take a **legendary effect** (1, 2, or 3 legendary stars via Legendary Cores/Modules at
  a weapon workbench) if you find or craft a legendary copy — that's the separate legendary-effect
  system, independent of the weapon-mod slots this page is about. See
  [Legendary effects & crafting](../legendary-effects-overview/) for how that system works in general.
- Condition/repair and ammo (.50 caliber balls, four consumed per shot across the barrels) still apply
  normally; "no mod slots" only means the weapon-mod system doesn't apply here, not that the weapon is
  otherwise unmodifiable in every sense.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The bayonet-removal history above is based on community reporting (forum/Steam discussion threads)
rather than a patch-note citation pulled directly for this page, so treat the "why" as reliable but the
exact patch/date as unconfirmed. As with any unique weapon, it's worth a quick current-wiki check
before assuming this is permanent if a future update reintroduces mod slots for it.
