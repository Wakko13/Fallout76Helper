## The point pool

You distribute an initial pool of points across the seven stats at character
creation, then gain roughly one more SPECIAL point per level-up through level 50. By
level 50 you have **56 total SPECIAL points** to allocate, spread across a max of 15
per individual stat. After level 50 you stop gaining new points but can still
reshuffle one point per level-up (see the respec FAQ entry for details).

Your current value in a stat isn't just a flat bonus — it's also the point budget you
have to equip perk cards tied to that stat (see the perk-card-slots FAQ entry).

## What each stat governs

| Stat | Governs |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | Carry weight; melee weapon damage |
| Perception | Awareness of nearby enemies/detecting stealth; V.A.T.S. weapon accuracy |
| Endurance | Total Health; Action Point drain while sprinting; disease resistance |
| Charisma | Sharing perk cards with teammates; rewards from group/team missions; vendor prices |
| Intelligence | Terminal hacking; condition/durability of crafted items; scrapping returns |
| Agility | Action Points available in V.A.T.S.; sneaking effectiveness |
| Luck | Critical Hit recharge rate; condition/durability of looted items |

This is the broad-strokes version — each stat also gates a specific tree of perk
cards (e.g., higher Intelligence unlocks better crafting/chem perks, higher Luck
unlocks better looting/crit perks) that matter more for build-crafting than the raw
per-point numbers above. Once the `perks` reference category has entries, cross-link
standout cards per stat here.
