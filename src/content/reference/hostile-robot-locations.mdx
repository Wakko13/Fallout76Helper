This covers hostile robots you fight out in the world — not player-placed CAMP
defenses. Most of Appalachia's robots (Protectrons, Mister Handies, etc.) are only
passive-until-provoked rather than aggressive on sight, so these are locations where
you can reliably pick a fight, not places that will attack you unprompted.

## Protectrons and turrets

- [**The Whitespring Bunker**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Whitespring_Bunker) — a dense cluster of robot types under one roof: several
  Protectrons (including medical-variant ones), Mister Handies, Mister Gutsies, Miss
  Nannies, and Whitespring Turrets. All are passive by default and turn hostile only
  if you provoke them (or fail the ID-card/behavior checks that gate entry), which
  makes this a good "start a fight on your terms" spot.
- Hostile Protectron variants tend to show up in locations that were originally built
  for heavy security — airports and large pre-War towns — with specific reports at
  [**Beckley**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Beckley) and [**Relay tower HG-B7-09**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Relay_Tower_HG-B7-09).
- [**Watoga Underground**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Watoga_Underground) — turrets here have been reprogrammed by the Blood Eagles and
  fight alongside their human Raiders, useful if you want a mixed robot/human
  encounter in one stop.

## Assaultrons

- [**Site Alpha**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Site_Alpha), [**Site Bravo**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Site_Bravo), and [**Site Charlie**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Site_Charlie) (the three pre-War nuclear missile
  silos) — each houses a strong military robot roster including Assaultrons,
  Mister Handies/Gutsies, Eyebots, and at least one Sentry Bot in the storage area.
  Access requires becoming a U.S. Army General via the "Officer on Deck" quest line
  plus a Nuclear Keycard, so this is an endgame stop rather than an early one.
- The upper floor of the hardware store in [**Clarksburg**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Clarksburg).
- Patrolling the perimeter walkway just west of [**Watoga Station**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Watoga_Station).

## Sentry Bots

- Eastern **Watoga Station**, extending out toward [**Sunrise Field**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Sunrise_Field) and **Thunder
  Mountain Substation** — a favorite general-purpose area for reliable Sentry Bot
  encounters.
- **The Whitespring Bunker / Whitespring Resort** — though the main building's Sentry
  Bots tend to run level 60+, so this is a tougher stop.
- [**98 NAR Regional**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/98_NAR_Regional) — at least one Sentry Bot guards the loot here.
- The parking lot of [**Garrahan Mining Headquarters**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Garrahan_Mining_Headquarters).
- [**Dyer Chemical**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Dyer_Chemical) (the Mire) — a Sentry Bot commonly patrols the path on the right
  side of the main building.

## Mister Handy / Mister Gutsy

- [**Watoga**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Watoga) — eight Mister Handies bearing the Watoga corporate emblem patrol in and
  around the town; killing them triggers more to spawn from robot fabricator pods, so
  this location can effectively be farmed repeatedly in one visit.
- [**Vault-Tec University**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Vault-Tec_University) — a Mister Handy in one of the classrooms.
- The [**Charleston Capitol Building**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Charleston_Capitol_Building), during the "Recruitment Blues" quest area,
  where Mister Handies staff the in-universe Charleston DMV.
- Mister Gutsy (the tougher military variant) shows up alongside Assaultrons at the
  missile **Sites Alpha/Bravo/Charlie** above rather than at a dedicated civilian
  location.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Robot factions in Fallout 76 are frequently passive-until-attacked rather than
aggressive by default, so "location" here generally means "where you can find one to
pick a fight with," not "will attack on sight." Site Alpha/Bravo/Charlie access
requirements (Officer on Deck, Nuclear Keycards) are quest-gated and may have changed
across seasons — verify current unlock steps before planning a run there.
