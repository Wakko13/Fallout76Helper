For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how X-01 stacks up
against the other five endgame models and how to unlock it (Archival Terminal at Enclave General
rank), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).

X-01 shares the same four-slot functional-mod pool (torso/legs/arms/helmet) documented in
[T-45's mod file](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) — every mod name and effect below matches T-45's
one-for-one. Unlike the five other endgame sets in this wave, X-01's helmet and leg pools appear to
retain the **older-generation mods** (Rad Scrubbers on the helmet, Explosive Vent on the legs) that
Union/T-65/Hellcat/Strangler Heart/Vulcan seem to have dropped in favor of Cooling Vent — see
Uncertain. No X-01-exclusive functional mod was found, and no full-set bonus was found either.

## No set bonus found

A dedicated search turned up no unique X-01 full-set bonus (unlike Union's Poison
Resist/Carry Weight, Vulcan's fusion-core/AP bonus, or Strangler Heart's acid damage) — X-01 gets
only the universal power armor full-set benefit (42% damage reduction / 90% radiation damage
reduction) that any complete power armor set provides.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **X-01's leg pool showing Explosive Vent instead of Cooling Vent is a genuine structural
  difference from Union/T-65/Hellcat/Strangler Heart/Vulcan (all five confirmed via Nukes & Dragons
  to have Cooling Vent instead), and matches T-45's own leg pool (which also lists Explosive Vent,
  no Cooling Vent)** — this reads as an "older armor generation" pattern (T-45/X-01) vs. "newer
  generation" pattern (Union onward), but a broader search also confirmed Cooling Vent plans exist
  for T-45/T-51b/T-60 specifically, meaning **T-45's mods file (part 1) likely under-documented its
  own leg pool** — Cooling Vent is probably available on T-45 too and simply wasn't found by that
  session's sources. Whether X-01 also secretly has a Cooling Vent option (and Explosive Vent was
  simply what the fetched page happened to show) isn't confirmed either way.
- **X-01 torso base stats** (for context, not a mod): 504 Damage Resistance / 607 Energy Resistance
  / 607 Radiation Resistance at level 50, per Nukes & Dragons.
- **"No X-01 set bonus" rests on a single aggregated web-search summary**, not a primary
  patch-notes citation — worth an in-game check, though it's consistent with X-01 being described
  everywhere as a well-rounded "classic prototype" suit rather than a bonus-chasing pick.
- **Targeting HUD's presence on X-01's helmet pool is inferred, not directly confirmed** — the
  X-01-specific Fextralife fetch listed Internal Database, Recon Sensors, Sensor Array, VATS Matrix
  Overlay, and Headlamp, but not Targeting HUD; it's included here on the assumption that a helmet
  mod present on T-45/Hellcat/Vulcan is also present on X-01, but that assumption isn't verified.
- **Materials for every mod on this page were not found on any reachable source**, matching the gap
  in T-45's file — fallout.wiki/fallout.fandom.com returned 403 on every direct fetch attempt this
  session, and per-mod Fextralife pages for X-01 weren't individually checked beyond the main armor
  page.
