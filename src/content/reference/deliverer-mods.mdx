For general modding mechanics (Weapons Workbench, Gunsmith perk, mod plans), see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Deliverer sits among
the other sidearms, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

The Deliverer uses the same mod slot layout as a standard 10mm-family pistol — receiver, barrel, grip,
magazine, and sights — plus one fixed suppressor slot (its signature built-in silencer). The table below
lists every mod option and its effect exactly as documented. **Crafting material costs and perk requirements
are not filled in on Fextralife for any Deliverer mod** — both the shared overview page and every individual
mod sub-page (checked directly) show the materials/perk columns as unknown. Treat the "Materials" column
below as a known gap rather than an omission on this site's part.

| Standard Receiver | Standard | Not documented | Not documented |
| Light Frame Receiver | Lighter weight, reduced damage | Not documented | Not documented |
| Heavy Frame Receiver | Heavier weight, improved damage | Not documented | Not documented |
| Calibrated Receiver | Better critical shot damage | Not documented | Not documented |
| Hardened Receiver | Better damage | Not documented | Not documented |
| Hair Trigger Receiver | More sensitive trigger, better rate of fire | Not documented | Not documented |
| Powerful Receiver | Superior damage | Not documented | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This is the weakest-sourced page of the batch on materials specifically: Fextralife's shared "Deliverer Mods"
table and every individual mod sub-page checked (Standard Receiver, Calibrated Receiver, Comfort Grip, Extended
Barrel) all leave the perk-requirement and crafting-material columns unfilled ("??" on the source pages) even
though the mod names and effect descriptions are populated. Since the Deliverer is a 10mm-frame weapon, its
real material costs are likely close to (though not guaranteed identical to) the equivalent 10mm Pistol mods —
worth cross-checking an in-game crafting menu or a current wiki page directly before planning a build around a
specific Deliverer mod.
