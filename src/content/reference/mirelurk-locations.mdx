Mirelurks (mutated hermit-crab-like creatures) cluster near water — lakes, rivers,
and marshland — and their nests reliably mix in tougher **Mirelurk King** and
**Mirelurk Queen** variants alongside the regular Softshell, Diseased, and
Steelshell types. Queens in particular hit hard with an AoE screech and are worth
knowing the location of in advance rather than stumbling into one underleveled.

## Densest farms (with guaranteed Queens)

- **Spruce Knob Lake** (southern Appalachia, south of Spruce Knob) — around nine
  regular mirelurks plus one Mirelurk King and one Mirelurk Queen, including
  Softshell and Diseased variants. One of the single best mirelurk farms in the
  game for sheer volume.
- **Highland Marsh** (eastern Appalachia, east of Vault 94) — described as having
  the most mirelurks findable in one spot, plus a guaranteed Mirelurk Queen,
  Softshell and Steelshell mirelurks, and mirelurk eggs to loot.
- **Toxic Dried Lakebed** (northeast of Vault 76, near Wavy Willard's Water Park
  and north of Clarksburg) — around six regular mirelurks plus one Mirelurk
  Queen.

## Other reliable spots

- **Sunrise Field** — four mirelurks, two Mirelurk Kings, and one Mirelurk Queen.
- **Mac's Farm** — four mirelurks and one each of King and Queen.
- **Quarry X3** — three mirelurks plus one King and one Queen, close to Big Bend
  Tunnel, which itself has a smaller mirelurk family just outside the tunnel
  entrance.
- **Ohio River Adventures** (western Appalachia, along the river) — a mixed group
  of mirelurks alongside human enemies, useful if you're farming the area for
  other loot anyway.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Spawn counts (Kings/Queens per site) come from community farming surveys, not
official data, and Bethesda has added new mirelurk spawn zones with map
expansions before (e.g. the Skyline Valley update added new documented spots) —
so this list may be missing newer locations added after this entry was written.
Treat Queen/King presence at a given site as "usually there," not guaranteed on
every visit.
