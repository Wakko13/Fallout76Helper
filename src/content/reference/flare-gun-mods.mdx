For general modding mechanics (Weapons Workbench, Gunsmith perk, mod plans), see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Flare Gun sits among
the other sidearms, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

Nothing to build a table out of here: Fextralife's page states plainly that the Flare Gun has **no mod
slots** — its "Mods" section reads "None." This tracks with its role described on the overview page: it's
mainly a visual-signal utility item rather than a combat weapon, so there's no receiver/barrel/sights
progression to speak of. It can technically still be fired at enemies, but there's no way to customize it
for that role.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The source is explicit and unambiguous about zero mod slots, so there's little to double-check on the mod
question itself. If you're relying on the Flare Gun for anything beyond its signaling role, it's worth
confirming current damage/ammo numbers against a live wiki page, since this page doesn't cover base stats —
only mods, which don't exist for this item.
