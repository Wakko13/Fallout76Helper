perk gates for any row. Treat this page as lower-confidence on exact numbers than the standard
Laser Rifle/Pistol page, though the mod *names* and general shape of each slot should be reliable.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This is the thinnest-sourced page in this batch: only one usable source was found at all, it uses a
different effect-value convention (percentages) than most of this site's other mod tables (flat
numbers), and it supplies **no crafting materials or perk gates whatsoever**. Several rows are also
collapsed — Nukes & Dragons groups near-duplicate Short/Long/Automatic/Sniper Barrel variants (4 each)
without breaking out the individual per-variant numbers in the summary this page was built from. If
you're farming for a specific Ultracite build, treat the mod *names* here as reliable (they line up with
the standard Laser Rifle's naming scheme) but verify exact percentages, materials, and any Science!/
Gunsmith gating against a live wiki or in-game before committing resources. fallout.wiki's dedicated
page for this weapon returned HTTP 402 to direct fetches this session — a known limitation, not a sign
the page doesn't exist — and may have better data if you can reach it directly.
