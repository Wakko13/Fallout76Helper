For how power armor actually works — chassis vs. armor pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting,
repair stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). This entry is only
about which models exist, roughly how they stack up, and where each one comes from.

## Plan quick-reference

- **T-45 / T-51 / T-60** — No plan exists; these are pure world-loot frames.
- **Excavator** — Plan: reward from the "Miner Miracles" quest.
- **Raider power armor** — No plan needed; a full ready-to-wear set is handed to you by "Key to the Past."
- **Ultracite power armor** — Free set from "Belly of the Beast"; extra piece Plans unlock afterward from Taggerdy's terminal.
- **Hellcat** — No plan drop exists; crafting knowledge auto-unlocks during "The Catalyst."
- **Union** — Plan: bought from Giuseppe Della Ripa (Whitespring Resort) with Stamps.
- **T-65** — Plan: bought from Regs (Vault 79) with Gold Bullion, or occasionally Minerva's stock.
- **X-01** — Plan: unlocked via the Archival Terminal at Enclave General rank.
- **Strangler Heart** — Plan: Vault 94 raid drop, or bought from Regs.
- **Vulcan** — Plan: Gleaming Depths raid drop.

## Early/world-found frames

| Model | Tier | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T-45 | Entry-level | World loot only — no plan, no quest. Frames with 2-3 pieces attached spawn at Vertibird crash sites, Emergency Service rooftops, High School rooftops, and reliably around **Watoga Municipal Center**. Very common near Vault 76 in the first hour or two. |
| T-51 | Slightly above T-45 | Same deal — pure world loot, no plan/quest path. Look around Central Watoga: Grafton Dam, ARKTOS Pharma, Point Pleasant, Charleston Fire Department. |
| T-60 | Mid-tier | World loot (Cranberry Bog, the Mire) plus a chance from the **Distant Thunder** public event. No dedicated quest reward. |

These three are the ones you'll wear before level 20-25 just from exploring — there's no
scripted "here's your first full suit" quest for any of them.

## Quest-reward sets

| Model | Tier | Quest / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excavator | Mining/utility (bonus carry weight + damage resist while mining, extra plant material yield) | Plans from the **"Miner Miracles"** questline at Garrahan Mining Headquarters (level 25 quest; craftable at level 25/35/45). |
| Raider power armor | Mid-tier, raider-aesthetic | Full ready-to-wear set (no crafting needed) handed to you by Rose on completing **"Key to the Past"**, the closing quest of the initial Raiders faction line (after Signal Strength → Flavors of Mayhem). |
| Ultracite power armor | High-tier, strong radiation resistance | A complete set is handed to you free for completing **"Belly of the Beast"** (via "Defiance Has Fallen" from Abbie's Bunker) at Fort Defiance. Crafting plans for extra pieces unlock afterward from Taggerdy's terminal on Fort Defiance's top floor; crafting requires level 50. |
| Hellcat | S-tier, high all-around resistance | No plan drop or purchase exists — crafting knowledge for every piece is unlocked automatically during **"The Catalyst"**, late in the Steel Reign questline (requires finishing Steel Dawn first). Non-tradable. |

The Excavator and Raider entries here match what's already stated in
[power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/) — Excavator from Miner Miracles,
Raider armor from Key to the Past.

## Faction-currency / vendor sets

| Model | Tier | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Union power armor | S-tier, well-rounded | Plans bought from **Giuseppe Della Ripa**, the Expeditions vendor at the Whitespring Resort (near Bubbles, the Nuka-Cola vendor), using **Stamps** earned from Expeditions (The Pitt, Atlantic City). Priced per-piece; community pricing has dropped over time as more Expeditions launched. |
| T-65 | S-tier, best physical/electrical/radiation resistance in slot | Plans bought from **Regs** inside Vault 79 using **Gold Bullion**, gated behind finishing "All That Glitters" (Settlers) or "Secrets Revealed" (Raiders) to unlock the Gold Bullion system. A handful of T-65 plans occasionally rotate through Minerva's weekly stock with no quest prerequisite. |
| X-01 | High-tier, classic "prototype" suit | Requires joining the **Enclave** and reaching the **General** rank (finish "Officer on Deck," then bank 10 Commendations). At General rank, read the Prototype Power Armor Schematic on the Archival Terminal in the Whitespring Bunker's Military Wing to unlock all plans. |

## Raid-locked sets

| Model | Tier | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strangler Heart | A-tier — an Ultracite-armor variant with much higher radiation resistance and an acid-damage set bonus | Plans drop from the **Vault 94** raid, or can be bought from Regs in Vault 79 for Gold Bullion. |
| Vulcan | S-tier — set bonus grants +25% fusion core duration and +25% AP refresh rate | Plans drop from the **Gleaming Depths** raid (roughly 5% per stage / ~22% per full clear); plans are non-tradable and stop dropping once you've learned them all, then resume once new mod plans are needed. |

## Rough tier snapshot

Community consensus (FalloutBuilds and similar tier-list sources, current as of this
writing) puts **Hellcat, T-65, Excavator, Union, X-01, and Vulcan** at the top (S-tier),
with **Ultracite, Raider, and Strangler Heart** just behind (A-tier), and the free
early frames (T-45/T-51/T-60) well below both — fine to level in, not what you want at
level 50 endgame.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact Stamp/Gold Bullion prices for Union and T-65 plans have changed via balance
patches more than once (Union in particular was reported as high as several thousand
Stamps at launch and has since been reduced) — verify current pricing in-game before
budgeting for it. Tier-list placement (S vs. A) is community opinion, not an official
Bethesda ranking, and shifts with each balance pass. Whether "Distant Thunder" still
drops T-60 plans as of the current patch is community-reported and worth a quick sanity
check if you're farming that event specifically for the plans.
