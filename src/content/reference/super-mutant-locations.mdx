Super mutants are one of the best all-around targets in Appalachia for XP and
legendary farming, since they show up in large, dense packs at fixed sites rather
than as scattered singles. They're almost always paired with **Mutant Hounds**
(and occasionally Mutant Floaters) at the same locations. This is regular wild
super mutant activity, not the Eviction Notice public event, which is covered
separately in [Notable repeatable boss encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/).

## Largest concentrations

- [**West Tek Research Center**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/West_Tek_Research_Center_(Fallout_76)) (Savage Divide) — around 12 super mutants outside
  plus roughly 29 more inside, for over 40 total, with 4-12 Mutant Hounds mixed
  in. The single biggest super mutant farm in the game and also a reliable
  Glowing Ghoul spot.
- [**Grafton Steel**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Grafton_Steel) (Toxic Valley) — reported at around 38 super mutants plus 8
  Mutant Hounds, rivaling West Tek for sheer density.

## Reliable mid-size farms

- [**The Thorn**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Thorn_(Fallout_76)) (Cranberry Bog) — the raider/super mutant base in the bog, with
  mutants spawning in the mud trenches and along the stream banks.
- [**New Gad**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/New_Gad) (Forest region) — 19 super mutants plus 3 Mutant Hounds.
- [**Huntersville**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Huntersville) (Savage Divide) — 11 super mutants plus 3 Mutant Hounds.
- [**Wade Airport**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Wade_Airport) (Forest region) — 8 outside and 16 more if you clear the
  workshop, for 24 total.
- [**Abandoned Bog Town**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Abandoned_Bog_Town) (Cranberry Bog) — 7 in town plus 15 in the attached
  workshop, 22 total.
- [**Eastern Regional Penitentiary**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Eastern_Regional_Penitentiary) (Toxic Valley) — 15 super mutants, 3 Mutant
  Hounds.
- [**National Isolated Radio Array**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/National_Isolated_Radio_Array) and [**Sunnytop Ski Lanes**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Sunnytop_Ski_Lanes) (Savage Divide) —
  11 and 13 super mutants respectively, both with heavy Mutant Hound support (up
  to 8 at Sunnytop).

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Spawn counts above are aggregated from community location surveys, not official
data, and total headcounts (especially the West Tek/Grafton Steel "40+" figures)
assume you fully clear both exterior and interior areas — don't expect to see
that many super mutants at once without doing so. Big Bend Tunnel is sometimes
listed as a joint super mutant/mole miner site; treat it as a secondary stop
rather than a primary super mutant farm.
