Radstags are the six-legged, two-headed irradiated deer of Appalachia — ordinary
wildlife rather than a combat-challenge creature type, hunted mainly for
**Radstag Meat** and **Radstag Hide** (hide breaks down into Leather for
armor/clothing crafting). The two-headed body plan is standard for the species,
not a rare mutation — every Radstag you see has it. If you're after Radstag as a
SPECIAL-boosting food ingredient, note that plain Grilled Radstag does **not**
grant a SPECIAL bonus (it gives +20 Carry Weight instead) — see
[SPECIAL-boosting foods](../special-boosting-foods/) for the dishes that
actually do.

## Best farming spots

- [**Thomas Farm**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Thomas_Farm) (unmarked location near Dent & Sons Construction, Savage
  Divide) — a barn area with roughly 6 Radstags, often including Albino
  variants.
- [**Seneca Rocks Visitor Center**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Seneca_Rocks_Visitor_Center) (Savage Divide) — southwest of the load-in
  point along the cliffside; one of the densest spawns in the game, with
  reports of 4-8 Radstags including both Albino and Glowing variants.

## Other reliable spots

- [**Skullbone Vantage**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Skullbone_Vantage) — north of the map marker, a herd that can include up
  to four Albino Radstags.
- The pond between [**Whitespring Resort**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Whitespring_Resort) and [**Safe 'n Clean Disposal**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Safe_'n_Clean_Disposal) — 2-3
  Radstags.
- North of [**The Whitespring Golf Club**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Whitespring_Golf_Club) — 2 Radstags.
- [**Gauley Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Gauley_Mine) (The Forest) — variable spawns near the train tracks by the
  STOP signs.
- [**Black Bear Lodge**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Black_Bear_Lodge) (Toxic Valley) — several spawns to the west.
- [**Point Pleasant**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Point_Pleasant) — several Radstags during the Mothman Equinox event.

## Variants

Community trackers list up to a dozen Radstag color/mutation variants
(Erratic, Albino, Rabid, Devolved, Glowing, Sickly, Observant, and more), but
most are cosmetic reskins of the same creature. The two worth specifically
hunting out:

- **Glowing Radstag** — drops Glowing Blood, Glowing Meat, and Nuclear
  Material in addition to normal drops; reliably found near Seneca Rocks
  Visitor Center.
- **Sickly Radstag** — drops Fragrant Venison alongside nuclear waste and
  glowing materials.

Albino, Rabid, and Sickly variants are considered the rarer spawns.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact spawn counts ("6 at Thomas Farm," "4-8 at Seneca Rocks") come from
community tallies rather than a fixed official spawn table and can vary by
visit or after patches. The full list of ~12 cosmetic variants isn't
consistently documented across sources — treat only Glowing and Sickly as
mechanically distinct (different drops) and the rest as visual flavor.
