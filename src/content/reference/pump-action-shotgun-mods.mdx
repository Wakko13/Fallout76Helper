## Sights mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Sights | Standard | — | 1x Adhesive, 3x Steel |
| Suppressor | Silences the weapon, Accuracy -30, improved recoil/stability | Gunsmith 3 | 3x Adhesive, 4x Aluminum, 5x Plastic, 2x Screw |

Unlike the Combat Rifle and Combat Shotgun, the Pump Action Shotgun's sights slot on Fextralife has
no Reflex Sight or Scope options at all — just Standard Sights and the Suppressor, which is filed
under this same slot rather than a separate Muzzle category. There's also no dedicated Muzzle
category (Compensator, Bayonet, etc.) documented for this weapon.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Speedy Receiver and True Receiver rows list identical effect text (Fire Rate +1, Accuracy +6) on
Fextralife despite being distinct, differently-gated mods (True has no perk requirement, Speedy needs
Gunsmith 3) — that's very likely a copy-paste error on the source page rather than the mods genuinely
being redundant; worth an in-game check if the distinction matters for your build. The thin sights
slot (no scope/reflex options) is consistent with the Pump Action Shotgun's short-range, hip-fire-lean
identity, but it's worth confirming in-game that a zoom option genuinely isn't available rather than
just missing from this source. As always, exact numeric stat values and material counts shift with
balance patches, so treat the above as a strong starting point rather than a guaranteed-current recipe.
