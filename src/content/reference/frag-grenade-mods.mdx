## No mod slots

The Frag Grenade genuinely has **no weapon mods** — Fextralife's entry for it lists the mod section
as unfilled/not applicable, and it doesn't appear in any mod-tree database alongside guns and melee
weapons. Grenades and mines in Fallout 76 are crafted at a **Tinker's Workbench** from a plan, not the
Weapons Workbench used for firearms and blades, and that plan produces a finished item with no
separate Receiver/Barrel/Sight-style mod tree attached to it. What you craft (or loot) is what you
throw.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- The **Demolition Expert** perk (Intelligence tree) still scales its damage — see
  [Grenades, mines, and thrown explosives](../explosives-and-thrown/) for the current rank breakdown.
  That's a perk-card damage multiplier, not a weapon mod, so it applies without any Weapons Workbench
  interaction.
- Legendary Frag Grenades (with a legendary star and effect) do exist as a separate system from
  weapon mods, the same way legendary effects apply to guns regardless of their mod slots.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's own Frag Grenade page has several placeholder ("??") fields beyond just mods — location
and improvement-perk fields are also unfilled on that source, so treat this as a case of thin
documentation rather than a page that positively rules mods out with citations. That said, the absence
lines up with every other grenade and mine checked for this same wave, and with how the crafting
system for this whole category works, so it's very likely permanent rather than a documentation gap.
Worth a quick in-game check at a Weapons Workbench if you want to rule it out with full certainty.
