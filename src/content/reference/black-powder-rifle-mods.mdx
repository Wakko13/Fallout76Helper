## Muzzle mods

The Black Powder Rifle's mod pool is a single slot with a single option — everything else about the
weapon (its damage, reload animation, limb-damage bonus) is baked into the base weapon rather than
modded on.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No Muzzle | Standard | — | — |
| Large Bayonet | Range -12%, bash damage +75%, weight +13% | Not documented for this weapon specifically | Not documented for this weapon specifically |

There is no Receiver, Barrel, Stock, Magazine, or Sight slot for this weapon — both sources checked
agree the mod section is limited to the muzzle/bayonet option above.

## Two things still worth knowing

- It can still take a **legendary effect** (the separate legendary-star system that applies to almost
  all weapons regardless of mod slots) if you find or craft a legendary copy — that's independent of
  the weapon-mod system this page is about.
- Condition/repair and ammo (.50 caliber balls) still apply normally; a thin mod pool only means the
  weapon-mod slots are limited, not that the weapon is otherwise unmodifiable in every sense.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Neither source checked lists exact crafting materials or a perk gate for the Large Bayonet on this
specific weapon. Comparable bayonet mods on other rifles range from cheap (Hunting Rifle: 1x Adhesive,
1x Screw, Gunsmith 2) to slightly pricier (Pipe weapons: 1x Adhesive, 1x Screw, 6x Steel, Gunsmith 1),
so expect something in that ballpark, but verify in-game before assuming an exact recipe. As always,
exact numbers shift with balance patches.
