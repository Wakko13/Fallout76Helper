## The major factions

| Faction | Base | What they're about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settlers | Foundation (built on the former Spruce Knob Workshop) | Wastelanders-era survivors trying to rebuild civilization peacefully; trade, farming, cooperation |
| Raiders | Crater (the converted Crashed Space Station) | Wastelanders-era survivors who took the opposite path — extortion, scavenging, might-makes-right |
| Brotherhood of Steel | Fort Atlas | The First Expeditionary Force sent to reclaim Appalachian tech; runs its own internal rank structure (Knights/Scribes up to Paladins), separate from the Settlers/Raiders-style reputation track |
| Enclave | Whitespring Bunker / related sites | Remnant pre-war government faction reintroduced through its own questline; access and standing are tied to completing that storyline |
| Crater / Foundation vendors | (see above) | Not factions themselves, but the settlements — their vendor stock is what your Settlers/Raiders reputation actually unlocks |
| Blue Ridge Caravan Company | Traveling — no fixed base | Traders/caravaneers who move goods across Appalachia; source of Gold Bullion vendor content and connected to Minerva's rotating stock (see `vendors-and-legendary-scrip.mdx`) |

Settlers and Raiders are mutually exclusive in practice: story choices in the
Wastelanders questline push you toward favoring one over the other, and actions that
raise reputation with one often lower it with the rival faction.

## How reputation is gained and lost

- **Quests** — main questline missions for a faction are the biggest reputation
  sources, especially early on.
- **Daily/repeatable quests and events** — once you've cleared the main storyline,
  faction-specific dailies (available from faction quest-givers) are the ongoing way
  to keep climbing.
- **Aligned actions** — helping a faction's interests (or its allies) in the world
  generally nudges reputation up; working against them (siding with their rivals,
  killing their NPCs) pulls it down.

Your current standing with the Settlers and Raiders is visible from the social menu
as a rank icon next to your name — click it to see your numeric progress toward the
next rank.

## What reputation unlocks

- **Vendor access** — each faction's shop expands its available stock (including
  faction-exclusive plans and recipes) as your rank climbs from Neutral through
  Friendly toward Ally.
- **Cosmetics and faction-themed items** — outfits, paint jobs, and other
  faction-flavored cosmetic unlocks are gated behind specific rank thresholds.
- **Quest/dialogue access** — higher-ranked characters get treated differently in
  faction dialogue and unlock additional quest content tied to that faction's
  storyline.
- **Brotherhood of Steel and Enclave** — these don't use the same
  Hostile-to-Ally reputation ladder as Settlers/Raiders; standing is instead tied to
  progress through their respective questlines and internal ranks, which unlock
  faction gear, plans, and (for BoS) rank-specific titles.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact reputation point thresholds per rank (and whether they've been rebalanced
since Wastelanders launched) aren't consistently documented across sources — treat
"Neutral → Friendly → Ally" as the reliable progression shape, not the specific point
totals. The Brotherhood of Steel's internal rank structure and the Enclave's
reputation mechanics specifically are thinner in community documentation than the
Settlers/Raiders system — verify against current patch notes if you're relying on a
specific unlock.
