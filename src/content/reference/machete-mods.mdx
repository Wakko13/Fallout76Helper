For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Machete sits
relative to the rest of the one-handed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

Like most one-handed blades, the Machete has exactly one mod category — a Blade slot with two
mutually exclusive damage-type options. There's no grip, guard, or handle slot to speak of.

## Blade mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sacrificial Blade | Targets bleed and are poisoned; exceptional damage; Weight +20% | Makeshift Warrior | 1x Fiberglass, 1x Adhesive, 2x Steel |
| Serrated Blade | Targets bleed; exceptional damage; Weight +20% | Makeshift Warrior | 1x Adhesive, 2x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife doesn't list a specific Makeshift Warrior **rank** for either blade mod, just the perk
name — worth confirming in-game if you're planning a perk budget around it. Separately, community
sources report that Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025) removed the hard perk-gate on crafting
melee weapons and their mods entirely — you now only need to have learned the plan, not have the
perk equipped — so treat "Makeshift Warrior" here as the historically-documented requirement rather
than a guaranteed current hard block. As always, exact material counts can drift with balance
patches.
