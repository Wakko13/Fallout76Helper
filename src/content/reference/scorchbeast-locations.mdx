This entry is about regular wild Scorchbeasts you can hunt in the open world for
challenges, meat, or XP. For the scripted 30-minute Scorchbeast Queen event fight, see
[Notable repeatable boss encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/) instead
— the Queen is a distinct, much tougher unique encounter, not just a big Scorchbeast.

## Where they spawn

Scorchbeasts are tied to **fissure sites** — the cracked, glowing radiation vents
scattered across the map — and the **Cranberry Bog** region has the Prime fissure site
plus the highest overall fissure density, which is why it's the best general region
for hunting them. Active fissure sites spawn a Scorchbeast roughly **every 18
minutes**, so a fissure with nobody else farming it is a reliable, repeating source
rather than a one-time kill.

Named/notable fissure sites in the Bog:

- [**Fissure site Alpha**](https://fallout-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Fissure_Site_Alpha) — southeast of Survey Camp Alpha.
- [**Fissure site Epsilon**](https://fallout-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Fissure_Site_Epsilon) — south of Firebase Major.
- [**Fissure site Prime**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Fissure_Site_Prime) — south-southeast of drop site V9, northwest of Forward
  Station Delta, southwest of the Glassed Cavern. This is the "main" fissure tied to
  the Scorchbeast Queen encounter, but it also produces regular Scorchbeasts between
  Queen events.

Unmarked fissure sites with confirmed Scorchbeast patrols:

- Northeast of [**Ranger Lookout**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Ranger_Lookout) / southeast of [**Creekside Sundew Grove**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Creekside_Sundew_Grove) — the
  Scorchbeast here routinely patrols out toward [**Bootlegger's Shack**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Bootlegger's_Shack) too.
- North-northwest of [**Appalachian Antiques**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Appalachian_Antiques), just south of a monorail support with a
  car on top — patrols Appalachian Antiques and the surrounding area.
- Next to the third monorail pylon north of [**Pylon V-13**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Pylon_V-13), on the line that ran to
  the RobCo Research Center.

## General tips

Because the Cranberry Bog is Appalachia's highest-radiation region overall, expect
heavy Rad resistance/Rad-X needs regardless of which fissure you camp — the region's
Scorched population is also elevated for the same reason, so fissure camping usually
means fighting through ground Scorched as well as the airborne Scorchbeast.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The 18-minute respawn timer and specific unmarked-fissure coordinates are
community-measured rather than official numbers and can vary by fissure or after
balance patches. Fissure site activity in general can also be affected by nearby
server events (e.g. an active Scorchbeast Queen fight at Prime), so a "dead" fissure
might just be between spawns.
