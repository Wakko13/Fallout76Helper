For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Sledgehammer
sits relative to the Super Sledge and the rest of the two-handed lineup, see
[Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

The Sledgehammer has one Head mod slot with six options — cripple/limb-damage variants layered
with bleed, armor penetration, and a Nuclear-Material-hungry Rocket line, similar in spirit to the
Baseball Bat's mod ladder but with a blunt-damage flavor.

## Head mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sharp | Targets bleed; chance to cripple; extra limb damage; Weight +20% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 6x Adhesive, 3x Lead, 5x Steel, 3x Wood, 8x Concrete, 2x Oil |
| Heavy Spiked | Armor piercing; chance to cripple; extra limb damage; Weight +10% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 8x Concrete, 6x Adhesive, 5x Steel, 3x Lead, 3x Wood |
| Heavy Rocket | Chance to cripple; extra limb damage; exceptional damage; Weight +30% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 1x Circuitry, 2x Nuclear Material, 8x Adhesive, 5x Steel, 8x Concrete, 3x Lead |
| Heavy Spiked Rocket | Chance to cripple; extra limb damage; superb damage; Weight +10% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 9x Adhesive, 8x Steel, 8x Concrete, 2x Nuclear Material, 2x Circuitry |
| Heavy Sharp Rocket | Targets bleed; chance to cripple; extra limb damage; superb damage; Weight +20% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 2x Nuclear Material, 2x Circuitry, 9x Steel, 10x Adhesive, 8x Concrete |
| Heavy Searing Sharp Rocket | Adds fire damage; targets bleed; chance to cripple; extra limb damage; Weight +20% | Makeshift Warrior 2 | 12x Aluminum, 12x Adhesive, 5x Circuitry, 6x Nuclear Material, 8x Concrete, 3x Lead |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's table gave a consistent Makeshift Warrior 2 gate and full materials for all six
mods, which is unusually clean for this wave — but as with the rest of this batch, Patch 60 ("Gone
Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting melee weapons and mods
entirely, so treat Makeshift Warrior 2 as the historically-documented requirement rather than a
guaranteed current block. No plain "Standard Head" baseline row was listed with its own materials
(the base weapon presumably needs no mod to use), which matches how the unmodified state is handled
across this series.
