For general modding mechanics (Weapons Workbench, Gunsmith perk, mod plans), see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Enclave Plasma Pistol
sits among the other sidearms, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

This weapon breaks the usual mod pattern, and that's the single most important thing to know about it: there
is **no base crafting plan for the gun itself**, and — with one exception — **no craftable plans for its
mods either**. The only mod you can learn and build at a Weapons Workbench is the **Prime Capacitor**, via a
separate plan that's a rare Scorchbeast/Scorchbeast Queen drop. Every other receiver/capacitor, barrel,
stock/grip, scope, and cosmetic skin option below can only be acquired already attached to a looted or
event-reward Enclave Plasma Gun, or extremely rarely as a standalone loose mod item (MODUS vendor rolls,
specific vendor bots, or Cranberry Bog world spawns) — never crafted from raw materials by choice.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The mod *names* above are reasonably well attested across the sources checked, but individual effect
numbers and exact material lists are not — this weapon's mods largely aren't meant to be crafted from raw
materials in the first place, which is the real story here more than any missing table cell. The Prime
Capacitor's exact material list in particular is community-reported rather than confirmed on the primary
wiki source (Fextralife's own page left it blank); treat the material list above as "the ingredient
categories you'll likely need" rather than an exact recipe, and verify at a Weapons Workbench once you've
learned the plan. If you're chasing a specific non-Prime mod, the practical path is farming Enclave "Lost
Connection" events and MODUS vendor rolls for a weapon that already has it, not trying to craft it — per Nuka
Knights, event-drop weapons have roughly a 30% chance of coming with non-standard mods already attached, while
loose-mod drop rates from vendors and world spawns are reported in the fraction-of-a-percent range.
