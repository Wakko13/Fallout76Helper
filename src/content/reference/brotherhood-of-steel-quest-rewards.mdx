For general faction background (Fort Atlas, the Knight/Scribe/Paladin rank structure), see
[factions and reputation](../factions-and-reputation/) — this page is just the quest → reward index
for the Brotherhood's two-part story arc.

## Steel Dawn (the Brotherhood's return to Appalachia)

| Quest | Prerequisite | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defiance Has Fallen | Opening BoS quest | Plan: Gatling Gun; BoS Fatigues outfit; Plan: Protective Lining (BoS Underarmor) |
| Back to Basic | Defiance Has Fallen | Soldier's Certificate |
| Recruitment Blues | Back to Basic | Ultracite Laser Pistol; BoS Field Scribe Uniform |
| Belly of the Beast | Recruitment Blues | **Ultracite Power Armor (full set)**; BoS Soldier's Suit — this is the Steel Dawn finale |

## Steel Reign (the concluding chapter)

| Quest | Prerequisite | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Knight's Penance | Steel Dawn completed | Opening Steel Reign quest; no unique item independently confirmed |
| Missing Persons | A Knight's Penance | Not independently confirmed |
| Out of the Blue | Missing Persons | Not independently confirmed |
| A Satisfied Conscience | Out of the Blue | Not independently confirmed |
| The Catalyst | A Satisfied Conscience | **Hellcat Power Armor plans** — the Steel Reign (and full Brotherhood storyline) finale reward, and the quest that unlocks crafting the set |

Steel Reign's launch also introduced legendary crafting (turning found gear legendary at a workbench)
as an update-wide system rather than a reward tied to one specific quest step — don't expect a single
"legendary crafting station" item to drop from a quest turn-in.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Individual rewards for "Missing Persons," "Out of the Blue," and "A Satisfied Conscience" were not
independently confirmed during this research — sources consistently describe The Catalyst as the
quest that hands over Hellcat plans, but the mid-chapter rewards for the three quests between it and
"A Knight's Penance" weren't clearly documented. Treat those three as "probably minor/cosmetic
rewards" rather than assuming they're empty. Note that **T-65 power armor is a Gold Bullion vendor
purchase, not a Brotherhood quest reward** — it's easy to mix the two up since both are Brotherhood-
flavored gear.
