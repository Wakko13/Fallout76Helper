Radtoads are large mutated toads found mainly around water in **The Forest**
and **Toxic Valley**. They're not a huge combat threat individually but do have
a long-range tongue grab, close-range bite, and lay explosive egg mines when
cornered, so don't treat them as completely passive. Kills yield Radtoad Legs,
Radtoad Eggs, and general junk; rarer spawns can drop Glowing Blood, Glowing
Meat, Nuclear Waste, or Prime Meat.

## Best farming spots

- [**Grafton Dam**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Grafton_Dam) (Toxic Valley) — around 5 Radtoads near the water's edge and
  on the dam itself, the densest reliable spot.
- [**The Sludge Hole**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Sludge_Hole) (near Wendigo Cave) — 2-3 Radtoads, a good low-level
  option.

## Other spots

- [**Beckwith Farm**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Beckwith_Farm) — chance spawns.
- Around [**Grafton**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Grafton) generally — chance spawns beyond the dam itself.

## Event spawns

- **Fasnacht Day** (Helvetia) — Radtoads appear as part of the event's creature
  waves.
- **The Path to Enlightenment** (Landview Lighthouse) — includes a wave of
  Radtoads.
- **Tea Time** (The Giant Teapot) — Radtoads can appear alongside the event's
  other pest spawns.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Spawn counts at Grafton Dam and The Sludge Hole are community-tallied rather
than an official fixed number, and can vary between visits or server
instances. The conditions for rarer Glowing/Prime Meat variant spawns aren't
well documented — treat them as a bonus rather than something you can farm
for directly.
