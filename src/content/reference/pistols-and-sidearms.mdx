## The sidearms

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **10mm Pistol** | 10mm rounds | Plan sold by Responders vendor bots (Church bot in Flatwoods, Grafton Station/trading post) once you're level 5+; small chance from Forest daily quests/events | Cheapest, most ammo-efficient starter pistol — the default "first gun" most characters pick up |
| **.44 Pistol** (revolver) | .44 round | Plan sold by Responders/Raider vendor bots (Flatwoods, Grafton, Pleasant Valley Ski Resort) at level 5+; low chance from Forest/Toxic Valley quests, Patrol Duty, Daily Ops (level 5-49) | High per-shot damage, strong VATS-crit revolver — core weapon for Gunslinger-perk crit builds; base for uniques like The Fixer and Steel Dawn's Fact Finder |
| **Black Powder Pistol** | Black powder | Plan only sold by Flintlock at The Whitespring Resort, level 10+; the weapon itself can also be found as loot northwest of Helvetia, from Vertibot airdrops, or off Scorched | Slow-reloading, hard-hitting frontier-style pistol tied to the Free States questline; a flavor/collector pick more than a meta one |
| **10mm Submachine Gun** (10mm SMG) | 10mm rounds | Plan is a guaranteed reward from the **Cold Case** quest (Miss Annie, Wavy Willard's Water Park); also sold by vendors at level 10+, world spawns in Forest/Toxic Valley, 10% from Patrol Duty, low chance Daily Ops (10-49) | Pistol-framed but **automatic fire** — falls under automatic-weapon perk cards (Commando-style) rather than the semi-auto Gunslinger tree despite the small frame; ammo-hungry but strong sustained close-range DPS |
| **Enclave Plasma Pistol** | Plasma cartridges | Not craftable from any base plan — obtained as loot or a reward (~30% chance) from Enclave "Lost Connection" events; only its **Prime receiver** mod has a learnable plan, and that's a rare Scorchbeast/Scorchbeast Queen drop | Enclave-exclusive energy sidearm; the scarcity itself is the appeal — a loot-hunt trophy weapon rather than something you reliably farm a plan for |
| **Syringer** | Syringer ammo (canisters loaded with darts — Bloodleaf, Cloaker, Health, and others) | Plan comes from Rose's "Someone to Talk To" questline at Top of the World; most dart types are learned by scrapping found syringer rounds; the special Karma dart is unlocked via the **Flavors of Mayhem** quest | Support/utility weapon, not a damage dealer — darts apply status effects (healing, cloaking, poison, radiation) to yourself, allies, or enemies |

## Why the 10mm SMG isn't a "Gunslinger" gun

It's easy to assume anything pistol-sized falls under the same perk cards as the 10mm Pistol or .44, but Fallout
76 splits weapon perks mainly by **fire mode**, not frame size: non-automatic (semi-auto/revolver) one-handed
guns lean on Gunslinger-style Agility cards, while automatic-fire weapons — including the 10mm SMG despite its
pistol grip — lean on Commando-style Agility cards instead. Worth knowing before building a perk loadout around
"pistols" as a single category.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact vendor stock (which specific vendor bots carry which plan) and event-reward drop chances shift with
patches and aren't officially documented — the vendor/level gates above are consistently reported across
community sources but treat them as "where to start looking," not guarantees. The Enclave Plasma Pistol's total
unavailability from any craftable base plan is also a common source of confusion in community threads; double
check against a current wiki page before assuming it's still true.
