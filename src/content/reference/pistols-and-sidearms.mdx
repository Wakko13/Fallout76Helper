## Pipe weapons — the actual starter gun

Before any of the sidearms below, this is what most characters are really holding first. **Pipe
weapons** (also just called **Pipe Guns**) are the one weapon family known from the very start —
no Plan needed, craftable at a Weapons Workbench from level 1 with common early junk (steel,
adhesive, screws, oil). There's no separate "recipe" to find; if you can reach a workbench, you
can build one.

| Variant | Ammo | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pipe Pistol** | .38 Round | The base single-shot version; a receiver mod converts it to full-auto (**Pipe Auto Pistol**), which shifts it onto Commando-style perks instead of Gunslinger-style ones — same fire-mode split as the 10mm SMG below. |
| **Pipe Revolver** | .38 Round by default; a receiver mod can rechamber it to .45 or .308 | Higher per-shot damage than the base Pipe Pistol, closer in role to the .44 Pistol further down this table. |
| **Pipe Bolt-Action** | .308 Round | Slower, harder-hitting single-shot variant. |
| **Pipe Rifle** / **Pipe Bolt-Action Rifle** | Same as their pistol counterpart | Attaching a stock mod to a Pipe Pistol or Pipe Bolt-Action turns it into the rifle-class version — same gun, longer effective range, still built at a Weapons Workbench. |

Condition degrades faster on pipe weapons than on machined guns, which the **Licensed Plumber**
perk (Intelligence) offsets. They're a "get you through the first several levels" weapon family —
worth outgrowing once the plans below are affordable, but there's no shame in a Pipe Revolver
build early on.

## The sidearms

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **10mm Pistol** | 10mm rounds | Plan sold by Responders vendor bots (Church bot in Flatwoods, Grafton Station/trading post) once you're level 5+; small chance from Forest daily quests/events | Cheapest, most ammo-efficient starter pistol — the default "first gun" most characters pick up |
| **.44 Pistol** (revolver) | .44 round | Plan sold by Responders/Raider vendor bots (Flatwoods, Grafton, Pleasant Valley Ski Resort) at level 5+; low chance from Forest/Toxic Valley quests, Patrol Duty, Daily Ops (level 5-49) | High per-shot damage, strong VATS-crit revolver — core weapon for Gunslinger-perk crit builds; base for uniques like The Fixer and Steel Dawn's Fact Finder |
| **Black Powder Pistol** | Black powder | Plan only sold by Flintlock at The Whitespring Resort, level 10+; the weapon itself can also be found as loot northwest of Helvetia, from Vertibot airdrops, or off Scorched | Slow-reloading, hard-hitting frontier-style pistol tied to the Free States questline; a flavor/collector pick more than a meta one |
| **10mm Submachine Gun** (10mm SMG) | 10mm rounds | Plan is a guaranteed reward from the **Cold Case** quest (Miss Annie, Wavy Willard's Water Park); also sold by vendors at level 10+, world spawns in Forest/Toxic Valley, 10% from Patrol Duty, low chance Daily Ops (10-49) | Pistol-framed but **automatic fire** — falls under automatic-weapon perk cards (Commando-style) rather than the semi-auto Gunslinger tree despite the small frame; ammo-hungry but strong sustained close-range DPS |
| **Enclave Plasma Pistol** | Plasma cartridges | Not craftable from any base plan — obtained as loot or a reward (~30% chance) from Enclave "Lost Connection" events; only its **Prime receiver** mod has a learnable plan, and that's a rare Scorchbeast/Scorchbeast Queen drop | Enclave-exclusive energy sidearm; the scarcity itself is the appeal — a loot-hunt trophy weapon rather than something you reliably farm a plan for |
| **Syringer** | Syringer ammo (canisters loaded with darts — Bloodleaf, Cloaker, Health, and others) | Plan comes from Rose's "Someone to Talk To" questline at Top of the World; most dart types are learned by scrapping found syringer rounds; the special Karma dart is unlocked via the **Flavors of Mayhem** quest | Support/utility weapon, not a damage dealer — darts apply status effects (healing, cloaking, poison, radiation) to yourself, allies, or enemies |
| **Gamma Gun** | Gamma cells | Plan has a 21% chance to be sold by wandering vendor Grahm (Monongah Power Plant area, level 20+); also a low-chance reward from the Radiation Rumble event (1.25-2.5% at higher milestones), holiday event gifts, and random vendor bots at level 20+ | Deals more damage the more radiated the target already is, and irradiates *you* slightly with each shot — a build-around weapon that pairs with Rad Resistance/radiation-immune perks rather than a general-purpose pick |
| **Alien Blaster** | Alien blaster ammo (unique, very limited resupply) | Not craftable from a base plan under normal play — found in a broken safe submerged in a toxic pond between Graninger Farm and Phillippi Battlefield Cemetery, or dropped/plan-rewarded (~low chance) by the **Invaders From Beyond** limited-time event; a separate plan converting it to fire fusion cells is found southeast of Black Mountain Ordnance Works | Extremely rare, high-damage novelty sidearm — the ammo scarcity means it's a "break glass in emergency" weapon rather than a main loadout piece |

## Unique and specialty pistols

A handful more one-handed guns exist as their own distinct weapons rather than variants of the
ones above:

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Single Action Revolver** | .45 round | Craftable via Plan like the main sidearms above (not quest-locked) | Hits slightly harder than the .44 Pistol with good accuracy, but a low fire rate and very few available mods — a narrower, more specialized version of the .44 rather than a strict upgrade |
| **Deliverer** | 10mm rounds | A compact, suppressed 10mm pistol; specific current in-game acquisition wasn't pinned down with confidence during this research — verify against a live wiki before planning a run around it | Built-in suppressor makes it a natural stealth/sneak-attack pick over the standard 10mm Pistol |
| **Voice of Set** | .44 round | Obtained during the **Prototypical Problems** quest, crafted at the Order of Mysteries' fabricator as part of the Mistress of Mystery gear line | Deals roughly 20% more base damage than a standard .44 Pistol; a themed collector's weapon tied to the Mistress of Mystery questline |
| **Zeta Gun** | Its own unique ammo | Alien-themed rare weapon; specific current acquisition wasn't pinned down with confidence during this research — verify against a live wiki before planning a run around it | Novelty/collector sidearm in the same spirit as the Alien Blaster |
| **Flare Gun** | Flare gun ammo | Plan sold by Responders vendor bots from level 1 | Not really a combat weapon — mainly used to launch a flare (visual signal/utility), though it can technically be fired at enemies |

## Why the 10mm SMG isn't a "Gunslinger" gun

It's easy to assume anything pistol-sized falls under the same perk cards as the 10mm Pistol or .44, but Fallout
76 splits weapon perks mainly by **fire mode**, not frame size: non-automatic (semi-auto/revolver) one-handed
guns lean on Gunslinger-style Agility cards, while automatic-fire weapons — including the 10mm SMG despite its
pistol grip — lean on Commando-style Agility cards instead. Worth knowing before building a perk loadout around
"pistols" as a single category.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact vendor stock (which specific vendor bots carry which plan) and event-reward drop chances shift with
patches and aren't officially documented — the vendor/level gates above are consistently reported across
community sources but treat them as "where to start looking," not guarantees. The Enclave Plasma Pistol's total
unavailability from any craftable base plan is also a common source of confusion in community threads; double
check against a current wiki page before assuming it's still true. The Gamma Gun's exact vendor/event odds and
the Alien Blaster's precise safe location are community-reported rather than officially documented — treat both
as "start here," and note the Alien Blaster's toxic-pond safe is an Easter egg location, not a guaranteed loot
table entry, so it's worth a wiki screenshot check before making the trip. The Deliverer's and Zeta Gun's
current acquisition methods are the weakest-sourced entries on this page — the underlying weapons are real,
but this research couldn't confidently pin down how you actually get one right now, so verify against a
current wiki page before planning around either.
