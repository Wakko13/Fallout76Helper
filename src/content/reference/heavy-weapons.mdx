Heavy weapons live on the Strength SPECIAL tree and are built around big single-target or
area damage at the cost of weight, AP drain, and (for most of the family) burning through ammo
fast. For how the standard mod slots on any of these actually work once you've got one, see
[Standard weapon and armor mods](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/) — this entry is about
which weapons exist in the family and how to get them, not the mod mechanic itself.

## The lineup

| Weapon | Ammo/fuel | How to acquire | Notable mod or niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minigun | 5mm rounds | World drop from super mutant overlords and similar high-tier enemies; found at locations like Camp Venture, the ATLAS Observatory, and Harpers Ferry | Highest sustained rate of fire in the family; the ammo hog of the group at high fire-rate settings |
| Gatling Gun | 5mm rounds | Short version guaranteed from the "Defiance Has Fallen" quest (with ~200 5mm Rounds); can also drop from a wrathwing cultist enemy at Lucky Hole Mine; plan otherwise a rare event/quest/world-spawn reward | Slower fire rate than the Minigun but more damage per shot and better accuracy — the more ammo-efficient of the two 5mm miniguns |
| Fat Man | Mini nukes | Plan is not sold by any fixed vendor — low-chance reward from Wastelanders-era quests, "Novice of Mysteries," Cranberry Bog daily/repeatable content, and a ~4.55% chance from completing "Missing Persons" | One-shots most non-boss enemies; mini nuke ammo is scarce and mostly player-crafted or bought from other players, so this is an "emergency button" weapon more than a main loadout piece |
| Missile Launcher | Missiles | Guaranteed reward from the "Key to the Past" quest; low chance from "Personal Matters"; low-chance world/event rewards in Ash Heap and The Mire | The Hellstorm variant is a separate plan, given free on completing the Brotherhood of Steel "Disarming Discovery" quest at Fort Atlas, and also appears in the Daily Ops loot pool |
| Auto Grenade Launcher | 40mm grenades | Sold by Raider vendor bots (level 40+, including at Pleasant Valley Ski Resort) and the Brotherhood vendor bot Phoenix at Watoga Shopping Plaza (level 40+); also a reward from several Ash Heap/Cranberry Bog/Mire events | Full-auto answer to the M79 below — much faster grenade delivery, at the cost of accuracy and ammo consumption |
| Broadsider | Cannonballs | Plan has a low chance from the "Missing Persons" quest reward pool, Daily Ops (level 25-49), and Supplying Demands (search-for-sunken-valuables task); always in vendor stock at level 25+, or buyable from Grahm for 1,008 caps | Explosive heavy pistol-format weapon that fires literal cannonballs — a niche pick, but cheap cannonball ammo and a distinctive one-handed heavy-weapon playstyle |
| M79 Grenade Launcher | 40mm grenades | Always in vendor stock at level 15+; world spawns/containers/treasure maps in Toxic Valley and Savage Divide; low-chance Daily Ops and event reward | Single-shot, more accurate and controllable than the Auto Grenade Launcher — a lighter-weight explosive option |
| Flamer | Flamer fuel | Always in vendor stock at level 30+ (Free States bots at Whitespring, Grahm, Watoga Station bot); low-chance reward from Grahm's Meat-Cook event; world spawns in Savage Divide, Ash Heap, The Mire, Cranberry Bog | Constant close-range damage-over-time; fuel drains fast, so it lives or dies on your fuel supply |
| Cremator | Ultracite-adjacent "fuel" ammo (energy-classed despite being a flame launcher) | Plan sold by Mortimer at the Crater for 750 Gold Bullion (requires Raider reputation); previously also earnable free via the Season 16 Scoreboard | Fires an arcing fireball that leaves a lingering burn patch — strong crowd-control niche, but Science! and higher Gunsmith-adjacent perks matter for full mod access |

## Ammo is the real bottleneck

Every weapon in this family (outside the mini-nuke-fed Fat Man, which has its own scarcity
problem) eats ammunition fast enough that a heavy-gunner build lives or dies on ammo supply.
This site's [ammo crafting and conversion](../../faq/ammo-crafting-and-conversion/) entry covers
building 5mm/40mm/fuel-type ammo at a Tinker's Workbench and converting surplus calibers through
the Ammo Converter, and [lead farming](../../faq/lead-farming/) covers the raw-material choke
point (lead) that most ballistic ammo recipes share. Strength-tree cards like Bandolier
(ammo carry-weight reduction) also matter more for this family than almost any other weapon type
— see [Perk essentials](../perks-essentials/) for the general new-character perk picture.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Plan drop sources for most of these weapons are RNG-weighted event/quest/vendor pools that
Bethesda doesn't publish exact odds for — community-reported percentages (e.g. Broadsider's
4.55% from Missing Persons) come from datamined tables and can drift after balance patches, so
treat them as "roughly this likely," not guaranteed math. The Cremator's free Season 16
Scoreboard route was a limited-time offer tied to that season and will not be available to
characters who missed it; the Gold Bullion vendor purchase is the durable path going forward.
Exact fuel-per-shot and ammo-per-shot costs for the Flamer/Cremator/Auto Grenade Launcher shift
with weapon mods (bigger tanks/magazines change consumption), so treat any specific "X ammo per
shot" number as mod-dependent rather than fixed.
