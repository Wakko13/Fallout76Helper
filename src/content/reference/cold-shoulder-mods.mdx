## What makes Cold Shoulder different from a normal Double-Barrel Shotgun

Cold Shoulder isn't a plain Double-Barrel Shotgun with a legendary star rolled on it — it comes with
three effects **permanently baked in and locked**, which cannot be re-rolled or removed the way normal
legendary effects can:

| Built-in effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Quad | Quadruples ammo capacity — a capacity tier otherwise unavailable on this weapon by normal means |
| Paranormal | +50% damage to cryptid-type enemies |
| Cryo | Overrides the projectile to deal a flat ~75-77 cryo damage per source, plus a slow effect on hit, replacing the shotgun's normal damage type |

Because the weapon already carries three unique perks in these slots, **you cannot apply additional
legendary effects to it** — there's no open legendary-star slot to fill.

## Normal weapon mod slots

Nukes & Dragons' database states that Cold Shoulder's ordinary weapon-mod pool (Receiver, Barrel,
Grip, Scope, and a Muzzle slot) is **identical to the standard Double-Barrel Shotgun's** — no mods are
removed or restricted on the unique version. See
[Double-Barrel Shotgun mod options](../double-barrel-shotgun-mods/) for that full table, including the
receiver/barrel/stock/sight options with crafting materials (from Fextralife) and the separate,
lower-confidence note on a possible Muzzle slot (from Nukes & Dragons, which is also the only source
for the "identical pool" claim on this page).

## Acquisition

Cold Shoulder was originally a Season 12 ("Rip Daring and the Cryptid Hunt") scoreboard reward, and is
now purchasable with **Stamps** from the vendor Giuseppe Della Ripa at Whitespring Refuge — see
[Shotguns](../shotguns/) for the fuller acquisition writeup, since that page already covers this in
detail and this entry shouldn't duplicate it.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's own Cold Shoulder page returned a 404 this session (the URL slug couldn't be resolved),
so the "identical mod pool to the base Double-Barrel" claim rests on Nukes & Dragons alone rather than
being cross-confirmed — and Nukes & Dragons itself disagrees with Fextralife about how many mods the
base Double-Barrel Shotgun even has (see that page's Uncertain section). The exact cryo-damage number
also varies slightly between sources (75 vs. 77 flat damage) and neither fallout.wiki nor
fallout.fandom.com were reachable this session to help resolve either discrepancy (a known limitation,
not a sign the pages don't exist). If you're specifically counting on a particular mod being available
on Cold Shoulder rather than assumed-identical-to-base, confirm at a Weapons Workbench in-game before
farming materials for it.
