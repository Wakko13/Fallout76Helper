For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how Vulcan stacks up
against the other five endgame models and how to earn its plans (Gleaming Depths raid drop, ~5%
per stage / ~22% per full clear, non-tradable), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).

Vulcan shares the same four-slot functional-mod pool (torso/legs/arms/helmet) documented in
[T-45's mod file](../t-45-power-armor-mods/), **plus four raid-exclusive mods** found on no other
set in this wave — all four drop as separate plans from Gleaming Depths encounters and are
non-tradable. This is the mod-richest of the six sets checked. It also has an inherent full-set
bonus, confirmed here as **not** requiring any mod to be installed.

## Set bonus (inherent, not a mod)

Wearing all six Vulcan pieces grants **+25% Fusion Core duration and +25% Action Point regen
rate**. Multiple independent sources (Nukes & Dragons character-build stats, The Duchess Flame's
modding guide, GameRant) confirm this is an **inherent trait of the six matched Vulcan pieces
themselves** — nothing to craft or install at a Power Armor Station for it, matching how Union's
and Strangler Heart's set bonuses work. `power-armor-sets.mdx` (already on this site) describes the
second half of the bonus as "+25% AP refresh," which one GameRant source phrased as "25% decrease
in VATS AP cost" — these are very likely the same underlying effect described three different ways
by three sources, not three different bonuses, but the exact mechanic (passive AP regen rate vs.
VATS-specific AP cost) isn't nailed down definitively — see Uncertain.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **The exact wording of the second half of Vulcan's set bonus differs across sources** —
  `power-armor-sets.mdx` says "+25% AP refresh rate," a Nukes & Dragons character-build page and
  The Duchess Flame's guide both say "+25% AP Regen Rate," and one GameRant source says "25%
  decrease in VATS AP cost." These read as the same effect described loosely by different writers,
  but a genuine "general AP regen" vs. "VATS-specific AP cost reduction" distinction would matter a
  lot for build planning — worth confirming exactly which one it is in-game before relying on it.
- **All four Vulcan-exclusive mods (Reflection Assembly, Gimbal Bracers, Ultracite Bracers, Elastic
  Servos) are confirmed to exist and to be Gleaming Depths raid-exclusive drops** (Fallout Wiki
  plan pages), but their **materials weren't found on any reachable source** — only their effects
  and drop mechanism are confirmed.
- **Gimbal Bracers' effect is described inconsistently between sources**: Fallout Wiki says
  "reduces weapon's cone of fire by 50%"; Nukes & Dragons phrased it as "Scoped Accuracy +0.15,
  Hip-Fire Accuracy +0.15" — likely the same underlying stat represented two different ways (a flat
  accuracy value vs. a cone-of-fire percentage), not independently confirmed to be identical.
- **"33 total mod plans" for Vulcan was mentioned by one aggregated web-search summary** but not
  independently verified by counting the tables above (which total roughly 11 torso + 6 leg + 6 arm
  + 6 helmet = 29 misc mods, before counting per-piece legendary mod slots that would push the
  total higher) — the two counting methods aren't reconciled here.
- **Rad Scrubbers' absence from Vulcan's helmet pool is inferred from omission**, not a direct
  "this mod doesn't exist for Vulcan" statement — a broader search confirmed Rad Scrubbers is
  compatible with Excavator, Raider, T-45, T-51b, T-60, Ultracite, and X-01, notably **not**
  Vulcan.
- **Whether Reflection Assembly and Reactive Plates are mutually exclusive torso-slot picks (like
  the rest of the torso pool) or can stack was not directly confirmed** — treated here as an
  alternate torso-slot option consistent with the "one mod per slot" mechanic established in the
  T-45 file.
- **Materials for every mod on this page — base-pool and Vulcan-exclusive alike — were not found on
  any reachable source.** Fextralife's per-mod Vulcan pages weren't reachable this session (direct
  URL guesses 404'd) and fallout.wiki/fallout.fandom.com returned 403 on every direct fetch attempt,
  so all Vulcan-exclusive mod details above came from WebSearch summaries of those wiki pages
  rather than a direct fetch — cross-check in-game before budgeting materials.
