## No mod slots

Unlike every other weapon in this mod-table series, the Black Powder Pistol genuinely has **no
weapon mods at all** — Fextralife's mod table for it lists the mod section as "None." There's no
Receiver, Barrel, Grip, Sight, Magazine, or Muzzle slot to attach anything to at a Weapons Workbench;
what you loot or craft is what you fire. This lines up with its flavor as a Free-States-questline
frontier weapon rather than a min-max combat pick — its identity is the slow reload and hard-hitting
black-powder mechanic itself, not a build-around modding platform.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- It can still take a **legendary effect** (the separate legendary-star system that applies to almost
  all weapons regardless of mod slots) if you find or craft a legendary copy — that's independent of
  the weapon-mod system this page is about.
- Condition/repair and ammo (black powder) still apply normally; "no mods" only means the weapon-mod
  slots are absent, not that the weapon is otherwise unmodifiable in every sense.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This is a case where the short page is the accurate page — there was no mod table to omit or
summarize. That said, weapon mod availability has occasionally been added to previously mod-less
weapons in past Fallout 76 updates, so it's worth a quick current-wiki check before assuming this is
permanent if a future patch is a plausible factor in your reading of this page.
