Yao guai (mutated black bears) are one of the harder creatures to reliably farm
in Appalachia — they have noticeably fewer fixed spawn points than ghouls,
Scorched, or super mutants, so the handful of guaranteed locations below matter
more than usual. They're also the best near-guaranteed source of **Loose Screws**,
a crafting material that's otherwise annoying to find, which is the main reason
players specifically hunt them out.

## Guaranteed spawns

- **Mountainside Bed & Breakfast** (Savage Divide) — the only location with
  **two** yao guai guaranteed at once; follow the tracks north from the B&B to
  find them. This is the closest thing yao guai have to a proper farm route.
- **Dolly Sods Wilderness** (Savage Divide) — one guaranteed yao guai.
- **Site Alpha** (Savage Divide) — one guaranteed yao guai.

## Looser secondary spots

Yao guai are sometimes (not reliably) found at **Graninger Farm**, the garden
area between the **Whitespring Resort** and **Whitespring Golf Club**, the
**Pumpkin House**, **Seneca Rocks Visitor Center**, and **Berkeley Springs West**.
These are worth a look if you're already passing through, but shouldn't be the
basis of a dedicated farming loop.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Because yao guai have so few confirmed guaranteed spawns compared to other
creature types, community lists disagree somewhat on which secondary locations
are reliable versus just "sometimes." Treat the Mountainside B&B / Dolly Sods /
Site Alpha trio as the dependable core route and everything else as opportunistic.
