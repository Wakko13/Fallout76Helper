## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife names the top-tier receiver "Prime Automatic Receiver" and gives it a flat "+3 Damage"
figure, while Nukes & Dragons lists a plain "Prime Receiver" (plus separate Prime .308/.45
variants) at "+65% Damage to Scorched" — these read as the same mod described two different ways
(a small always-on damage bump vs. a large conditional one) rather than confirmably-reconciled
numbers; both are presented together on that row rather than picking one. Only the Standard and
Prime Automatic receivers have confirmed materials and a perk gate (both from Fextralife); every
other mod across all five slots has no materials or perk data on either source checked. The three
caliber-converting receivers (.308 and .45 variants) change the weapon's ammo type entirely, which
is a bigger commitment than a stat-tweak mod — confirm ammo availability before committing to one.
