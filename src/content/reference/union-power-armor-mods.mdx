For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how Union stacks up
against the other five endgame models and how to buy its plans (Giuseppe Della Ripa at Whitespring
Resort, for Stamps), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).

Union shares the same four-slot functional-mod pool (torso/legs/arms/helmet) documented in
[T-45's mod file](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) — every mod name and effect below matches T-45's
one-for-one, with no Union-exclusive functional mod found on any source checked. What
distinguishes Union is an **inherent, un-modded set bonus** (see below) and a helmet pool that
appears to be **missing Rad Scrubbers**, present on T-45/X-01 but absent from every later power
armor line checked in this initiative so far.

## Set bonus

Wearing all six Union pieces grants **+150 Poison Resistance and +75 Carry Weight** — an inherent
trait of the Union pieces themselves, not a craftable mod. The Poison Resistance bonus is popular
enough that it's commonly cited as freeing up a legendary-perk-card slot otherwise spent on Funky
Duds.

## Paint / material tiers

Same shared cosmetic-paint system as T-45 — see
[T-45's Paint / material tiers table](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) for the full mod list (Hot Rod,
Military Paint, Minutemen Paint, Vault-Tec Paint, Titanium Plating, Lead Plating, Prism Shielding,

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Rad Scrubbers' absence from Union's helmet pool is inferred from omission**, not a direct
  "this mod doesn't exist for Union" statement — a broader search confirmed Rad Scrubbers is
  compatible with Excavator, Raider, T-45, T-51b, T-60, Ultracite, and X-01, notably **not** listing
  Union, Hellcat, T-65, Strangler Heart, or Vulcan. Worth a quick in-game check at a Power Armor
  Station before assuming it's unavailable.
- **No Union-exclusive functional mod (torso/leg/arm/helmet) was found on any source** — unlike
  Strangler Heart (Welded Rebar) or Vulcan (Reflection Assembly, Gimbal Bracers, Ultracite Bracers,
  Elastic Servos), Union appears to rely entirely on the shared base pool plus its inherent
  Poison-Resist/Carry-Weight set bonus.
- **Whether Union's Jet Pack (labeled distinctly as "Union Jet Pack" on Nukes & Dragons) differs
  mechanically from the base Jet Pack mod at all was not confirmed** — every effect figure found
  matches T-45's Jet Pack exactly; the distinct label is presumed to be per-model naming only, not
  a different effect.
- **Materials for every mod on this page were not found on any reachable source** — same gap as
  most of T-45's pool; Fextralife's per-mod Union pages weren't reachable this session (direct URL
  guesses 404'd) and fallout.wiki/fallout.fandom.com returned 403 on every direct fetch attempt.
- **Union's exact Stamp price per plan has changed via balance patches** (per `power-armor-sets.mdx`,
  reported as high as several thousand Stamps at launch, reduced since) — not relevant to mods
  specifically, but worth remembering if budgeting Stamps for mod plans too.
