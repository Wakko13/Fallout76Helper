**Scope note:** the full legendary effect list runs into the hundreds of
combinations across weapons, armor, and power armor, and changes with balance
patches. This entry explains the system and lists effects that come up repeatedly in
current build discussions — it is not, and isn't trying to be, an exhaustive
effect database.

## How star tiers work

- A legendary item shows 1-3 stars in its name, one per legendary effect ("mod")
  slotted into it.
- The **1st-star effect** is a "prefix" mod and is what shows in the item's display
  name (e.g. "Bloodied", "Anti-Armor"). 2nd and 3rd star effects generally don't
  change the displayed name.
- A 4th tier of legendary mods exists but is only obtainable as a reward from
  individual sections of the Gleaming Depths raid — it isn't part of the normal
  crafting pool.

## How crafting/rerolling actually works (post-Legendary Crafting overhaul)

- Legendary Cores were removed and converted to Legendary Scrip; crafting now costs
  only **Legendary Modules**, obtained mainly as public-event rewards (amount varies
  by event) and treated as a lightweight junk item in your inventory.
- Rolling stars on an item at a workbench costs **5 Legendary Modules for the 1st
  star, 10 for the 2nd, 15 for the 3rd** — but which specific effect you get is
  otherwise random unless you spend further resources (below).
- To pick a **specific** effect rather than rolling randomly, you need a matching
  **Legendary Mod** (obtained by scrapping legendary gear at a workbench, or crafted
  yourself from Legendary Modules) plus **Legendary Scrip**, at an escalating cost
  per change (roughly 10/15/25/45/70 scrip for your first several changes on an item,
  climbing toward ~1,000 scrip by the 20th change on the same item).
- Manually choosing an effect this way makes that item **untradeable and
  undroppable** (it can still go in your stash box) — a real tradeoff if you were
  planning to trade or sell the result.

## Effects that come up a lot in current build discussions

Treat this list as a snapshot, not a ranking — "best" is playstyle-dependent and
shifts with balance patches.

**Weapons (1st star):**
- **Bloodied** — big damage bonus scaling as your HP drops low; highest ceiling but
  fragile without heavy damage/health mitigation to compensate.
- **Anti-Armor** — bonus damage that ignores a portion of target armor; strong
  general-purpose pick against high-armor enemies.
- **Vampire's** — heals you based on damage dealt; popular on automatic weapons for
  sustain-heavy builds.

**Weapons (2nd/3rd star):**
- **VATS-related AP cost reduction** — frequently picked for VATS-centric builds
  since it directly extends how many VATS hits you can chain.

**Armor:**
- **Overeater's** (power armor) — damage reduction that scales with how full your
  hunger/thirst meters are, up to a meaningful total across a full set.
- **Sentinel's** / **Cavalier's** — chance-based damage reduction while standing
  still / while sprinting, respectively; often paired for defensive builds.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Legendary system mechanics (module costs, scrip curve, what counts as "meta") have
changed multiple times via major updates — cross-check current numbers against a
freshly-updated wiki page before treating the crafting costs above as exact, and
don't assume the "commonly sought" list above is still current a few patches from
now.
