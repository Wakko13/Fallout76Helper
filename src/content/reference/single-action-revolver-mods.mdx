For general modding mechanics (Weapons Workbench, Gunsmith perk, mod plans), see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Single Action Revolver
sits among the other sidearms, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

As the overview page notes, this revolver really does have a narrow mod pool compared to the .44 Pistol —
Fextralife's table lists only receiver, barrel, and grip slots, with no magazine, sights, or muzzle options
at all.

## Receiver

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Receiver | Standard | Not specified on source | 1x Oil, 2x Screw, 6x Steel |
| Prime Receiver | Greatly increases weapon damage, with bonus damage to Scorchbeasts/Scorched; requires Ultracite ammo variant | Learned from the separate "Plan: Prime single action revolver receiver" (Scorchbeast Queen drop, low-chance regular Scorchbeast drop, or a rare Supplying Demands quest reward) | Not specified on source |

## Barrel

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Barrel | Standard | Not specified on source | Not specified on source |
| Long Barrel | Superior range and sighted accuracy; better recoil; poor hip-fire accuracy | Not specified on source | 3x Adhesive, 4x Screw, 11x Steel |

## Grip

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Grip | Standard | Not specified on source | 4x Screw, 3x Wood |
| Ivory Grip | Improved recoil and hip-fire accuracy (Accuracy +1, Value +13) | Not specified on source | 2x Adhesive, 4x Fiberglass, 3x Screw |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Prime Receiver is documented as its own separate plan (a Scorchbeast Queen / Scorchbeast / Supplying
Demands drop, same acquisition pattern as other weapons' Prime receivers) but Fextralife's own weapon page
doesn't list its exact crafting materials — expect it to follow the same "Ultracite + high-tier flux + common
junk" pattern seen on other Prime receivers, but verify before farming for it. Perk requirements weren't
specified for any mod on the source page, which is unusual — most one-handed gun mods gate behind a Gunsmith
tier, so don't assume these are perk-free without checking in-game. As always, exact effect numbers and
material costs shift with balance patches.
