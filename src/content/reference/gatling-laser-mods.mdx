For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For the Gatling Laser's role as
a sustained-fire heavy energy weapon, its fusion-cell ammo, and how it compares to the rest of the
energy-weapon family, see [Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/).

**A source note up front:** Fextralife's Gatling Laser page lists a shorter Receiver lineup (Standard,
True, Refined Beta Wave Tuner, Severe Beta Wave Tuner) with crafting materials attached, while Nukes &
Dragons' database lists several more Receiver options (Calibrated, True Gatling Laser Receiver, Gamma
Wave Emitter, Vicious, plus the same two Beta Wave Tuners and a Prime Receiver) using percentage-based
values and no materials. The table below merges both — rows with materials came from Fextralife, rows
marked "Not documented" are Nukes & Dragons-only additions. The two sources also disagree on where
**Beam Focuser** lives: Fextralife files it under Sights, Nukes & Dragons files it under Muzzle. It's
listed under Muzzle below (matching the dedicated Muzzle slot Nukes & Dragons documents for this
weapon), with the conflict flagged again at the bottom.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This page merges two sources that disagree in both effect-value format (Fextralife's flat numbers vs.
Nukes & Dragons' percentages) and, for one mod, category placement (Beam Focuser as Sights vs. Muzzle)
— both readings are shown rather than picking one arbitrarily. Perk requirements weren't confirmed for
any individual mod on either source; expect the usual Gunsmith-rank plus Science! gate implied by the
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/) overview, but verify the exact rank in-game before committing
materials. Materials for the Nukes & Dragons-only Receiver additions (Calibrated, True Gatling Laser
Receiver, Gamma Wave Emitter, Vicious, Prime) and the two extra Barrel options (Aligned Long Barrel,
Stabilized Charging Barrels) weren't found anywhere reachable this session.
