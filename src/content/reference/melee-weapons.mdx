Melee and unarmed weapons share the Strength SPECIAL tree's damage cards but split into
distinct handling classes — one-handed, two-handed, and unarmed — that each pull from
different perk cards (Iron Fist for unarmed, separate bladed/blunt cards for one- and
two-handed). For how mod slots work on any of these (grips, blade/head materials), see
[Standard weapon and armor mods](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/).

## One-handed

| Weapon | Ammo/fuel | How to acquire | Notable mod or niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Machete | None (melee) | Not crafted from a plan — found as loot near Vault 76 at the start of the game, and later given by Lacey Drummond/Isela Mejia during Wastelanders content | Fast, low-commitment starter blade; good early pick before you've farmed better plans |
| Baseball Bat | None (melee) | Requires Plan: Baseball Bat, crafted at a Weapons Workbench once learned | Blunt damage type; common early-mid game blunt option |
| Board | None (melee) | Common early scrap-tier find/craft, widely available from junk-tier loot | Bottom-of-the-ladder blunt weapon — mostly a placeholder until you have better plans |
| Pole Hook | None (melee) | Crafted at a Weapons Workbench once the plan is known | ~50% longer reach than most one-handed melee weapons, useful for hit-and-retreat play |

## Two-handed

| Weapon | Ammo/fuel | How to acquire | Notable mod or niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sledgehammer | None (melee) | Common craftable/found blunt two-hander, available from an early plan | Slower and less polished than the Super Sledge, but cheap to build and repair early on |
| Super Sledge | None (melee) | Plan not fixed at any single vendor — ~21% chance to be sold by Grahm the traveling vendor at level 30+, plus a low chance from Daily Ops (level 30-49) | High per-hit damage and long reach; can drop most common enemies in one or two swings, at the cost of slow swing speed and heavy weight |

## Unarmed

| Weapon | Ammo/fuel | How to acquire | Notable mod or niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Knuckles | None (melee) | Plan sold by Raider vendor bots starting at level 1 | Cheapest, earliest unarmed option to get crafting |
| Power Fist | None (melee) | Plan sold by the Brotherhood of Steel vendor bot Phoenix at Watoga Shopping Plaza (level 30+); crafting itself needs Crafting Level 30 and Makeshift Warrior rank 5 (Intelligence) | Mechanized punch weapon with a distinct high-damage swing; the backbone unarmed pick once you're past Knuckles |
| Deathclaw Gauntlet | None (melee) | Not sold by any vendor — plan drops from mutated event reward packs, level-3 safes in the Ash Heap (the three at Pleasant Hills Cemetery are a reliable farm spot), treasure maps, and Ash Heap world spawns | Highest unarmed ceiling in the family — a literal deathclaw hand mounted as a weapon; worth the farm for a dedicated unarmed build |

## Unique / named melee weapons

| Weapon | Ammo/fuel | How to acquire | Notable mod or niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shishkebab | Flamer fuel (for the burn effect on top of the blade) | Plan drop pool includes several Ash Heap/Cranberry Bog/Mire events (Census Violence, Monster Mash, Line in the Sand, Distant Thunder, Surface to Air, AWOL Armaments), Grahm's Meat-Cook event, and a ~21% chance to be sold by Grahm at level 35+ | Machete blade rigged to a fuel line for a fire-damage-over-time bonus on every hit; the signature "sword that's also a flamethrower" unique |
| Death Tambo | None (melee) | Obtainable from Rose's questline at Top of the World; plan otherwise found via world spawns/containers in Savage Divide and The Mire, and a low chance from Daily Ops (level 20-49); sometimes found at the Sons of Dane compound alongside the game's other "musical weapons" | A spiked, weaponized tambourine — cosmetic novelty pick as much as a combat one, but scales like any other blunt one-hander |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Plan drop-rate percentages quoted above (Super Sledge/Shishkebab's ~21% Grahm chance, event
reward odds) come from community datamining, not official patch notes, and can drift as loot
tables get rebalanced — treat them as rough likelihood, not guaranteed math. The Board's exact
acquisition path is thin in current sources; if it matters for a specific build, verify against
a fresh wiki check rather than relying on this entry alone. "Musical weapons" (Death Tambo and
its siblings) are a small, patch-added weapon subset whose spawn locations have shifted before
and could shift again.
