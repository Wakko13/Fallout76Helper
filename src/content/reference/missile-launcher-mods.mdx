For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Missile Launcher
fits into the rest of the heavy-weapon family, plus the separate Hellstorm variant plan, see
[Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/).

Fextralife and Nukes & Dragons organize these slots slightly differently (Fextralife splits
"Melee" and "Stability" into two slots; Nukes & Dragons treats Bayonet and Stabilizer as
competing options within one Muzzle slot). This page follows the Nukes & Dragons structure since
a bayonet and a barrel stabilizer occupying the same physical mount point is the more plausible
reading — flagged below in case you find otherwise in-game.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Whether Bayonet and Stabilizer genuinely share a single Muzzle slot (this page's structure, from
Nukes & Dragons) or occupy two separate slots as Fextralife's page layout implies is not fully
settled between the two sources — Fextralife listed them as slots 4 and 5 ("Melee" and
"Stability") with no note that they're mutually exclusive, which would mean you could theoretically
run both stabilized recoil and a bayonet at once. If your build depends on that distinction, check
in-game rather than trusting either source's slot layout blindly. The Triple Barrel and Night
Vision Scope have no perk-gate or materials data on either source checked, and the Quad Barrel's
perk requirement was explicitly listed as "None" on Fextralife despite being a clear upgrade tier —
worth a sanity check given every other non-standard mod on this weapon needs Gunsmith 5.
