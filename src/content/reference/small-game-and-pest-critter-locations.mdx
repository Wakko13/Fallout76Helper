None of the animals below have a farming story strong enough for their own
page, so they're grouped here. The first group is harmless small game hunted
purely as food-crafting ingredients (see
[SPECIAL-boosting foods](../special-boosting-foods/) for what some of their
meat cooks into — Crispy Squirrel Bits, Fox Jerky, Ground Mole Rat, Awesome
Opossum Bacon, Mutt Chops, Dog Meat Steak, and Cat Meat Steak are all built
from critters covered on this site). The second group is small hostile pests
worth a quick mention even though they're not a serious combat challenge.

## Small game (Squirrel, Rabbit, Fox, Beaver, Opossum, Cat, Chicken)

These seven spawn as a shared, randomized "critter pool" — the same handful of
world spawn points roll between Squirrel, Rabbit, Fox, Beaver, Opossum, Cat,
and Chicken (and occasionally Radstag or Frog), so you generally hunt them as
a group rather than targeting one species specifically. Appalachia has roughly
775 individual critter spawn points in total. Reliable clusters:

- **Whitespring Resort area** — the small hill south of the entrance, and the
  strip between the resort and Whitespring Station.
- **Overseer's Camp** — across the river, and south of the hillside camp.
- **Carleton Mine** (Toxic Valley) — multiple spawns around the nearby pond and
  near an Aerosolizer.
- **Grafton Station** (Toxic Valley) — near a pond with a rusty car.
- **Woods Estate** (Toxic Valley) — along roads and near vehicles.
- **Gilman Lumber Mill** (The Forest) — in fields, near the docks, and around
  jeeps in the muddy areas.
- **Marigold Pavilion** (The Forest) — around rock ledges and a Mothman
  shrine.
- **Charleston Train Station** (The Forest) — the field with the hay rolls.
- **Camden Park** and **Abandoned Mine Shaft 6** (Ash Heap) — around crashed
  vehicles and along roads/tracks.
- **Twin Lakes** and **Lake Eloise** (Savage Divide) — around the water's
  edge.
- **Drop Site G3** (Cranberry Bog) — near trees and a phosphate deposit vein.

**Chicken** specifically also shows up outside **Foundation's** walls in the
garden area. Private or low-population server instances tend to have better
critter spawns than a busy/old server, since these are small, easily-tapped
spawn points.

## Small hostile pests (Tick, Rad Ants)

Not "wildlife" to hunt for meat — these are small hostile nuisances worth
knowing the spawn spots for.

- **Tick** — drops Tick Blood Sac, an Antiseptic-scrapping ingredient; see
  [Antiseptic farming](../../faq/antiseptic-farming/) for the full farming
  writeup. Reliable spots include the unmarked shack west of Big B's Rest
  Stop (up to 8), Moonshiner's Shack (~4), Lake Eloise (~4), and Spruce Knob
  Campground (~3), plus chance spawns at Dolly Sods Wilderness, Point
  Pleasant, Kerwood Mine, Gorge Junkyard, Hunter's Shack, and Kanawha County
  Cemetery.
- **Rad Ants** — irradiated ants (Forager, Soldier, and Glowing variants, plus
  larger "Overgrown" versions at higher levels) that drop Ant Meat and carry
  heavy radiation resistance. Found at the Investigator's Cabin, the bottom
  level of The Top of the World, Appalachian Antiques, the unmarked cemetery
  near Hopewell Cave, outside Lucky Hole Mine, Fort Defiance, Watoga High
  School, and Thunder Mountain Substation TM-02 (often already fighting
  whatever else spawns there). Also appear during the **Tea Time** public
  event at The Giant Teapot.

**Bee Swarm** is not an independent spawn — swarms are released by **Honey
Beasts** when threatened, not a standalone critter with their own nest. See
[Insect and swarm-pest locations](../insect-swarm-locations/) for Honey Beast
spawn spots.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact critter spawn-point counts ("775 total") and per-site species mix come
from community tallies rather than an official list, and which of the seven
species rolls at a given spot is randomized per visit — treat the locations
above as "good critter pools," not guaranteed-species farms. Rad Ant location
lists vary somewhat between sources; treat the ones above as a reliable
starting set rather than exhaustive.
