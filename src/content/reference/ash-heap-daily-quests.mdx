This is a roster of the Ash Heap's repeatable quest-log content — quests you pick up
from a specific NPC, terminal, or item and that reset on a timer, as distinct from
the region's public events (covered in
[Ash Heap and The Mire public events](../ash-heap-and-mire-public-events/)) and from
Daily Ops. For how the daily/drop/repeatable system works in general — reset timers,
where they show up in the Pip-Boy — see the
[daily and repeatable quests overview](../daily-and-repeatable-quests-overview/).

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lost and Found** (Drop) | No NPC — pick up a key dropped by any Mole Miner, mostly encountered in Ash Heap mines | Use the key to open a matching randomly-placed chest nearby; the wrong chest just means killing another Mole Miner for a fresh key | Random ammo, aid items, and junk materials |
| **Earth Mover** (Repeatable) | Claim and hold the Mount Blair workshop | Find or craft four Ignition Cores, install them in the Rockhound bucket-wheel excavator's reactors, then defend it from roughly 30 minutes of enemy waves while it deposits ore | Random ore/ammo/aid, a random plan/armor/mod/weapon, and a chance at a legendary — also one of the random reward sources for the **Plasma Grenade** and **Gatling Laser** plans (see [Explosives and thrown weapons](../explosives-and-thrown/) and [Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/)) |
| **Mistaken Identity** (Daily) | Camden Park — auto-triggers on first visit; report to the Camden Park security Protectron to "clock in" | Don the park uniform and calibrate three carnival attractions in turn: **The Chow Line**, **Lucky Mucker**, and **Dross Toss** (see breakdown below) | Mr. Fuzzy Tokens (spendable at the company store for the Mr. Fuzzy outfit/helmet), plus random ammo/aid/plan/armor/mod/weapon and a legendary chance |
| **Lucky Strike** (Repeatable) | Buy a Miner's, Excavator's, or Prospector's map from a U-Mine-It! vending machine (the one at Kanawha Nuka-Cola Plant, in The Forest, is the commonly-cited source) | Equip the map from the Pip-Boy notes to mark a random dig site — usually but not exclusively in Ash Heap's mining areas — then find the dirt mound and interact with it to excavate (no pick or shovel needed; crouching helps spot the low-visibility dig hitbox) | Metal ores including black titanium ore, plus random ammo and aid items |

## Camden Park's three mini-games

Mistaken Identity's three calibration tasks are effectively their own mini
quest-within-a-quest, and each is documented individually on community wikis:

| Mini-game | Task |
| --- | --- |
| The Chow Line | Eat six Moldy Hot Dogs within 60 seconds |
| Lucky Mucker | Talk to Zeke at the Lucky Mucker booth, grab the coal bucket, then load coal into four wheelbarrows scattered around the park inside the time limit |
| Dross Toss | Throw the quest-specific "Dross" item into old tires around the park before time runs out |

All three were confirmed current and active across multiple independent sources
(Fextralife, Fallout Wiki/Fandom, GamesHedge, SegmentNext) during this research, so
they're included here with confidence rather than as a maybe.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Lucky Strike's regional home is genuinely ambiguous.** The map that starts it is
  sold at a U-Mine-It! machine in The Forest (Kanawha Nuka-Cola Plant), but
  SegmentNext lists the quest itself under Ash Heap, and its ore-excavation theme
  fits Ash Heap's mining identity — it's included here on that basis, but the actual
  dig site it sends you to can land in more than one region. Treat it as
  "Ash-Heap-flavored" rather than strictly Ash-Heap-exclusive.
- Whether Lucky Strike shows up under the Pip-Boy's "DAILY" tab (like the other three
  entries here, which are explicitly documented as daily-reset) or is a plain
  repeatable without a fixed reset timer wasn't consistently stated across sources.
- Exact reward tables and drop chances (legendary odds, Mr. Fuzzy Token counts, etc.)
  are community-tallied and can shift with balance patches.
