For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Minigun sits
relative to the rest of the heavy-weapon family, see [Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/).

The Minigun's mod pool is narrower than a standard two-handed rifle: three slots total (Barrel,
Sights, Muzzle), with no Receiver or Magazine slot — the belt-fed 5mm ammo isn't modded through a
magazine option the way box-fed rifles are. Fextralife's table only had populated effect text for
the Standard and Accelerated barrels plus the Shredder muzzle mod; the rest of the numeric values
below come from Nukes & Dragons' percentage-based database, which doesn't publish crafting
materials or perk gates at all.

## Barrel mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Barrel | Standard | — | 2x Adhesive, 5x Gear, 4x Oil, 6x Screw, 5x Spring, 17x Steel |
| Accelerated Barrel | Fire Rate +15%, Weight +25%, Range -25%, Recoil -15%, Sighted Transition -15% | Not documented | 4x Adhesive, 10x Gear, 5x Oil, 7x Screw, 6x Spring, 18x Steel |
| Tri Barrel | Fire Rate -20%, Range +50%, Weight +43%, Spread (hip-fire) +20%, Recoil +36%, AP Cost +30%, Sighted Transition +6%, Damage +25% | Not documented | Not documented |
| Prime Barrel | Damage to Scorched +65%; switches the weapon to Ultracite 5mm ammunition | Learned via **Plan: Prime minigun barrel** (rare drop) | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's own table text for the Gunner Sight and Shredder rows both read "Superior bash.
Inferior range" — that's almost certainly a copy/paste or table-scraping error on Fextralife's
side (it's the stock description used for bayonet-style melee mods on other weapons, not a sight
or muzzle attachment), so this page uses Nukes & Dragons' numeric percentage effects for those two
rows instead, which read as far more plausible for a sight and a muzzle brake respectively. Only
the Shredder's perk gate (Gunsmith 5) and materials came through clean on Fextralife; the
Accelerated Barrel's materials are Fextralife-sourced but its perk gate was never listed by either
source, and the Tri Barrel and Prime Barrel have no materials or perk data on either source
checked — the Prime Barrel's plan-drop gate is inferred from the existence of "Plan: Prime minigun
barrel" as a named item rather than confirmed crafting-menu text. Treat any "Not documented" cell
as a prompt to verify in-game or against a live wiki before farming toward it.
