For how underarmor lining mods work as a category — including why this item doesn't get them —
see [Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the jumpsuit is
acquired (rare daily-quest/event drop in The Forest, or its plan from the Overseer's Cache in
Grafton), see [Functional attire](../functional-attire/).

## No mod slots — this is a different item from the Vault-Tec University Jumpsuit

This is the most important thing to get right about this page: **"Vault-Tec Jumpsuit" is not the
same item as the "Vault-Tec University Jumpsuit."** The University Jumpsuit is confirmed
underarmor in Nukes & Dragons' database and does carry the Vault-Tec Underarmor Lining mod slot
(see [Vault 76 Jumpsuit mod options](../vault-76-jumpsuit-mods/) for that lining table, which
applies equally to it). The plain "Vault-Tec Jumpsuit" — the light-blue jumpsuit from the Grafton
Overseer's Cache described in [Functional attire](../functional-attire/) — does **not** appear in
that underarmor list at all.

Fextralife's page for it and a fandom summary both describe it as a cosmetic **Outfit** worn "on
top of" any equipped armor and underarmor, explicitly framed as promoting player individuality
rather than as a stat-bearing piece — the same category as the hundreds of other cosmetic
full-body outfits in the game, none of which carry mod slots. No mod table exists for it on any
source checked.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The existing [Functional attire](../functional-attire/) overview page groups "Vault 76 jumpsuit"
and "Vault-Tec jumpsuit" together under "Vault jumpsuits (underarmor with mod slots)," describing
both as moddable underarmor. Based on this session's research, that framing is likely accurate for
the Vault 76 Jumpsuit but **not** for the Vault-Tec Jumpsuit specifically — the item that actually
shares the Vault-Tec lining group with Vault 76 is the differently-named Vault-Tec *University*
Jumpsuit. This is a naming collision that's easy to get wrong (three very similarly named items:
Vault 76 Jumpsuit, Vault-Tec Jumpsuit, Vault-Tec University Jumpsuit), and the overview page may be
worth a follow-up correction. The "Outfit vs. underarmor" classification itself rests on
Fextralife's stub-page boilerplate plus a search-engine summary of a blocked fandom page rather
than a directly-loaded, fully detailed source, so a quick in-game check (try applying a lining mod
to it at an Armor Workbench) would be the most definitive way to settle this.
