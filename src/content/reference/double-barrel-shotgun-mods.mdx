For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how to acquire the
Double-Barrel Shotgun itself, and for the unique **Cold Shoulder** variant built on this same frame,
see [Shotguns](../shotguns/) and [Cold Shoulder mod options](../cold-shoulder-mods/).

**A source discrepancy worth flagging up front:** Fextralife's table for this weapon is genuinely
short — only 4 Receiver options, 4 Barrel options, 2 Stock options, and 2 Sight options, with no
Muzzle category at all. Nukes & Dragons' game-data-driven database disagrees substantially: it shows
a 5-slot system (Receiver, Barrel, Grip, Scope, **Muzzle**) with far more entries per slot (11
receivers, 12 barrels, 7 grips, 4 scopes, 3 muzzle options) and percentage-based effect values instead
of Fextralife's flat numbers. The two sources don't line up on mod names or counts closely enough to
merge into one table with confidence, and Nukes & Dragons doesn't list crafting materials or perk
gates at all. The table below follows Fextralife since it's the only source with materials data;
the note further down covers what Nukes & Dragons adds.

No perk-gated mods appear anywhere in Fextralife's table for this weapon — every row above is
available with no Gunsmith requirement, which tracks with the Double-Barrel's identity as a simple,
early-accessible break-action weapon rather than a deep modding platform.

## What Nukes & Dragons adds: a possible Muzzle slot

Nukes & Dragons independently lists a Muzzle slot that Fextralife doesn't document at all:

| Mod | Effect (percentage-based, per Nukes & Dragons) | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No Muzzle | Standard | — | Not documented |
| Muzzle Brake | Range -12%, Weight +10%, Recoil -15% | Not documented | Not documented |
| Spiked Muzzle Brake | Range -12%, Weight +25%, Recoil -15%, Bash Damage +25% | Not documented | Not documented |

Treat this table as lower-confidence than the four above — it's the only source claiming this slot
exists, has no crafting materials, and uses a different effect-value convention than the rest of this
page.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This is the messiest sourcing situation in this batch of shotguns. Nukes & Dragons' full listing
implies a much larger true mod pool for Receiver, Barrel, and Stock too (e.g. Hardened, Refined,
Vicious, Severe, and Scorched/Prime-killer receivers; multiple Aligned/True/Stabilized barrel
variants beyond the four Fextralife shows) — but without materials or perk gates for those extra
rows, adding them here would mean inventing crafting costs, which the source data doesn't support.
Both fallout.wiki and fallout.fandom.com returned blocked responses this session (a known limitation,
not a sign the pages don't exist), so a third independent source to break the tie wasn't available.
If you're planning a specific build around this weapon, especially one relying on a Muzzle mod or a
receiver/barrel option not listed above, verify against a live wiki or in-game before farming
materials for it.
