Neither of these is a major faction with a reputation track like Settlers/Raiders or an internal rank
ladder like the Brotherhood — see [factions and reputation](../factions-and-reputation/) for how they
fit into the wider faction picture. Both are worth running through mainly for CAMP plans and cosmetics
rather than combat gear.

## Free States

Started at **Abbie's Bunker**, north of The Mire, where a recorded message from Abbie kicks off a
short chain about a Free States hunting party gone missing.

| Quest | Prerequisite | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early Warnings | Start at Abbie's Bunker | Plan: Shadowed Combat Armor Arms |
| Reassembly Required | Early Warnings | Plan: Nuke Mine (CAMP defense trap) |
| Coming to Fruition | Reassembly Required | Plan: Tesla Arc Trap; Plan: Flamethrower Trap |
| The Missing Link | Coming to Fruition | No unique reward independently confirmed |
| Tracking Unknowns | Related side quest, Free States lore | **Suppressor's Pyrolyzer** — a unique flamer with a built-in legendary effect; Prototype Hazmat Suit |

## Blue Ridge Caravan Company (Costa Business)

A set of **seven one-off "daily" quests** (added in the Once in a Blue Moon update), started by
talking to **Vinny Costa** at the Big Bend Tunnel east entrance in the Cranberry Bog. Each deals with
a different crew member's personal problem, and only one Costa Business quest can be turned in per
day (same reset timer as Daily Ops) — so running the full chain takes about a week even once
unlocked.

| Quest | Crew member | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Costa Business: Kieran | Kieran Kennedy | Plan: Blue Ridge Toy Truck |
| Costa Business: Eugenie | Eugenie | Plan: Blue Ridge Themed Stash Box |
| Costa Business: Rudy | Rudy Fernandez | Plan: Clean Blue Ridge Sleeping Bag; Rudy's Canned Pozole recipe (+1 Luck, +2 Charisma for 10 min) |
| Costa Business: Libby | Libby Wen | Plan: Blue Ridge Guardsman Paint |
| Costa Business: Herschel | Herschel Klein | Plan: Blue Ridge Scout Helmet |
| Costa Business: Vera | Vera Thornberg | Requires The Bucket List completed and a camera with film — reward not independently confirmed |
| Costa Business: Carver | Carver Timmerman | Finale of the chain — reward not independently confirmed |

Separately, the **Riding Shotgun** activity (escorting Blue Ridge brahmin caravans through Big Bend
Tunnel, also started via Vinny Costa) has a chance at the Blue Ridge Caravan outfit, mask, flag plan,
and backpack plan — it's a repeatable activity rather than a one-time quest, so treat those as "random
drop chance," not a guaranteed reward.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Vera and Carver Costa Business rewards weren't confirmed by any source consulted here — if you're
specifically farming the set, expect to just find out in-game. "Tracking Unknowns" is grouped here
under Free States because of its lore tie-in (a lost Free States hunting party), but it wasn't
confirmed to share a quest-giver or unlock chain with Early Warnings/Reassembly Required/Coming to
Fruition/The Missing Link — it may be a standalone side quest rather than a literal continuation of
that four-quest sequence. Exact prerequisite level and full walkthrough detail for both questlines are thin in general web
coverage compared to the main/Enclave and Raiders/Settlers arcs.
