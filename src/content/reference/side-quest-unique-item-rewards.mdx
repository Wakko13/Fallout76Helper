These don't belong to a faction reputation track or a multi-part story arc — each is a standalone
quest (or short chain) worth doing specifically for one notable item. Two other site pages already
cover quest-gated CAMP/crafting unlocks that fit this same "one-off, not faction-tied" pattern:
[fermenting and alcohol](../fermenting-and-alcohol/) for the Brewing Station/Fermenter via **Wasted on
Nukashine**, and [Nuka-Cola mixing](../nuka-cola-mixing/) for the custom flavor-naming reward on **The
New Arrivals**. This page adds the ones those don't cover.

## Miner Miracles — Excavator power armor

**Miner Miracles**, found at Garrahan Mining Headquarters, requires **character level 25**. Completing
it awards the **Excavator power armor schematics** (plans for the full mining-themed set, which trades
combat stats for mining yield and carry weight bonuses) plus a Power Armor Station plan. See
[power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/) for how this fits alongside Raider power armor
(Key to the Past) as one of the two guaranteed full quest-reward suits in the game.

## The Order of the Tadpole — random legendary armor piece

A Wild Appalachia-era side quest chain: collect three Tadpole Badges (Kindness, Helpfulness, Bravery
and Growth challenges) as a Pioneer Scout, then turn them in to Scout Leader Jaggy. Completion rewards
200 caps, Pioneer Scout crafting plans, a Pioneer Scout backpack plan, and — notably — **a random
legendary armor piece**, making it one of the few quests that hands out guaranteed legendary gear
rather than a specific named item.

## Cold Case — Perfect Storm

A side quest unlocked via the "Contact Miss Annie" miscellaneous objective at Wavy Willard's Water
Park. Completing it rewards **Perfect Storm**, a unique modded 10mm submachine gun whose level scales
to your character at the time you finish the quest (so it stays useful even if you're not high-level
yet).

## The Motherlode — worth a look, not a guaranteed unique

A Hornwright Industrial HQ side quest. Confirmed rewards are Ultracite Ore and other assorted ore from
the Docking Station Dispenser, plus a random weapon. Some players report a power armor suit sitting
next to the dispenser as well, but this reads as a nearby loot spawn rather than a scripted, guaranteed
quest reward — don't go in expecting a specific named suit.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Order of the Tadpole's "random legendary armor piece" reward means the *slot and legendary effect*
you get are RNG, not a fixed item — don't expect to target a specific piece by running this quest.
Whether Cold Case's level-scaling applies only at first completion or also affects Perfect Storm's
stats afterward wasn't confirmed. The power armor reportedly found near The Motherlode's reward
dispenser is the least confirmed claim on this page — treat it as "commonly reported," not fact, until
you've verified it in-game.
