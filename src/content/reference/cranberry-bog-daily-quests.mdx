This is a roster of Cranberry Bog's repeatable quest-log content — quests picked up
from a specific NPC, terminal, or item that reset on a timer, as distinct from the
region's public events (covered in
[Cranberry Bog public events](../cranberry-bog-public-events/)) and from Daily Ops.
For how the daily/drop/repeatable system works in general, see the
[daily and repeatable quests overview](../daily-and-repeatable-quests-overview/).

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cop a Squatter** (Daily) | The Mister Gutsy police chief, upstairs via elevator at Watoga Emergency Services | Travel to a randomly-assigned location and clear out hostile "squatters" — the enemy type varies (Blood Eagles, cultists, feral ghouls, Mirelurks, Scorched, or super mutants) | 600 XP, 70 caps, 8 legendary scrip, random loot, and a chance at legendary weapons/armor |
| **Request Government Air Drop** (Drop) | No NPC — pick up a "U.S. Government supply requisition" holotape, found as random loot on enemies or in containers | Take the holotape to a relay tower's emergency broadcast terminal (often robot-guarded) and upload it to call in a supply drop, then retrieve the crate (which also draws nearby wildlife) | Random ammo and aid items from the crate itself |
| **Forbidden Knowledge** (Daily) | Available after completing the "Defiance Has Fallen" main-quest step, tied to Fort Defiance | Deposit Technical Data into a small red storage hatch in a Camp Venture basement to seal away a technical schematic | A Brotherhood of Steel underarmor mod, random ammo/aid, and a chance at BoS-themed apparel (Bomber Jacket, Science Scribe Outfit, T-60 BoS Initiate paint, etc.) |
| **Heart of the Enemy** (Daily/Drop — see note below) | "Mission 099-01 Orders," found on Senior Knight Wilson's terminal desk (Fort Defiance, 4th floor) after completing "Recruitment Blues" | Power up an Automated Research Terminal at Vault-Tec University, then retrieve viable Scorchbeast DNA — any dead Scorchbeast works, including ones killed by other players, though Cranberry Bog is the practical place to find one — and run it through the Automated Centrifuge back at the University | Random ammo/weapons/aid, a shot at a Gatling Laser, random plans/armor/mods/weapons, and a legendary chance |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Heart of the Enemy's region is genuinely disputed between sources**, and this
  entry makes an independent call rather than resolving it. Its quest-giver terminal
  and turn-in point (Vault-Tec University, the Automated Centrifuge) sit in **The
  Forest**, which is why some trackers file it there. But its actual objective —
  finding a dead Scorchbeast for DNA — points players specifically at **Cranberry
  Bog**, where Scorchbeasts are far more common, and both GamesHedge and SegmentNext's
  region-by-region breakdowns list it under Cranberry Bog. It's included here on that
  basis; if the parallel Forest-region catalog on this site also lists it, that's the
  same source disagreement showing up on both sides, not an error.
- Sources also disagree on whether Heart of the Enemy is a "Daily" (Fextralife, which
  says it resets every 24 hours) or a "Drop" quest (VGR's own article title calls it
  a Drop quest) — the Recruitment Blues/terminal-note prerequisite makes it read more
  like a gated daily than a pure random-item drop, but this wasn't fully resolved
  across sources.
- Request Government Air Drop is repeatable in practice (new holotapes keep spawning
  as loot) but isn't a fixed-NPC daily with its own reset timer the way Cop a Squatter
  and Forbidden Knowledge are — Fextralife files it as a side quest rather than under
  the Pip-Boy's "DAILY" tab, consistent with it being holotape-triggered like Lost and
  Found and Idle Explosives elsewhere in this catalog.
- Exact reward tables and legendary drop chances are community-tallied and can shift
  with balance patches.
