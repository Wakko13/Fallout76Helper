This entry covers the general CAMP crop-farming and water-purification mechanic. If you specifically want the
adhesive-from-crops pipeline (corn + mutfruit + tato → Vegetable Starch), see
[adhesive farming](../../faq/adhesive-farming/) instead — that FAQ only touches farming as one step in a
crafting-materials loop, not the mechanic itself.

## Growing crops at your CAMP

Planting doesn't need a special "planter" object — you plant seeds directly onto open dirt/soil ground within
your CAMP's build area:

1. Have the seed (or a harvestable plant item) in your inventory.
2. Open Build Mode, go to the **Food** category, and place the crop on a patch of dirt (not concrete/flooring —
   it needs bare soil).
3. Optionally add **fertilizer** to a planted crop to speed up its regrowth cycle and boost how much it yields
   per harvest — the exact percentage bump isn't officially documented, so treat it as "worthwhile if you have
   spare fertilizer" rather than a precise multiplier.
4. Harvest repeatedly — planted crops regrow on a timer rather than being consumed outright, similar to wild
   plants in the world.

**Known plantable crops** include Corn, Mutfruit, Tato, Melon, Gourd, Carrot, Razorgrain, Blight (Soot Flower),
and Bloodleaf, among others — coverage varies by what seeds you've picked up. Corn, Mutfruit, and Tato are the
three most farmed because together they feed the Vegetable Starch → adhesive loop referenced above, but any of
them work fine as plain food crops on their own.

### Where seeds come from

- **Harvesting the wild plant directly** — picking a crop out in the world (farms, gardens, roadside patches)
  has a chance to give you a seed alongside the harvested item, not just the item itself.
- **Wildlife Refuges** and similar points of interest scattered around the map often contain rarer seed types.
- **Raid/event/quest rewards** occasionally include seed packets for less common crops.
- Once you have one seed of a type, you're set — planting and harvesting your own CAMP crop is a renewable
  source of more of the same seed, so you never need to keep re-finding it in the wild.

### Crops attract attacks

Planted crops make your CAMP a more attractive target for random attack events — hostile mobs that spawn to
raid your CAMP will prioritize destroying crops (and other placed objects) over just wandering past, so a
farming CAMP benefits more than most from actually having some defenses up.

## Water purification

A **Water Purifier** is a placeable CAMP/workshop object (plan required, not default-known) that continuously
turns a water source into **Purified Water** — the safest, highest-value drinking water in the game, restoring
25 HP and roughly 25% of your Water meter per can with no disease risk. There are three tiers:

| Type | Output | Power cost | Storage cap | Placement | Plan location |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 12/hour | 8 | 2 | Does **not** need to be in water — good for hill/mountain CAMPs | Tyler County Dirt Track (workshop capture) |
| Normal/Standard | 24/hour | 4 | 3 | Must be placed in a water source | Charleston Capitol Building (Overseer's Cache) |
| Industrial | 60/hour | 10 | 5 | Must be placed in a water source | Camp McClintock (Overseer's Cache) |

Because storage caps are small relative to hourly output (especially the Industrial purifier's 60/hour vs. a
5-can cap), you need to empty it regularly to actually capture the full output rather than letting production
go to waste once the internal storage fills.

### Aqua Boy/Aqua Girl isn't a "safe drinking water" perk

It's a common assumption that the Endurance perk **Aquaboy/Aquagirl** makes drinking dirty or contaminated water
safe — it doesn't. What it actually does is let you swim without taking radiation damage or needing to breathe,
i.e. it's a swimming/underwater perk. It does **not** prevent radiation or disease from scooping water out of a
pump or body of water and drinking it directly (neither does Lead Belly, which covers food/drink radiation
generally but not this specific interaction the way people sometimes expect). If you want genuinely safe
drinking water without a purifier, boiling water at a Cooking Station or buying/looting Purified Water directly
are the reliable options — a purifier just automates the supply.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact percentage boost fertilizer gives to crop yield/regrowth speed isn't in any official Bethesda source
found during research — community guides give inconsistent numbers (ranging loosely from ~20-50%) depending on
crop type, so treat "fertilizer helps, use it when you have spare" as the safe takeaway rather than a specific
percentage. Purifier output/storage numbers above match current community wiki data but, like other placeable
stats in this collection, are subject to drift after balance patches.
