For what actually happens to your character while addicted to alcohol (the penalty stacking, withdrawal, and
how to cure it with Addictol or the Tasty Radscorpion Egg Omelette), see
[chems and consumables basics](../../faq/chems-and-consumables-basics/) — alcohol shares that same addiction
system with chems, it isn't a separate mechanic. This entry covers the Fermenter itself and what you can
actually brew with it.

## Unlocking the Brewing Station and Fermenter

Both are gated behind a single quest, not available from the start:

1. Interact with a **Pi Mu party poster** — found at Vault-Tec University, Morgantown, various bus stops and
   train stations, or purchasable from the Atomic Shop to place yourself — to start **Wasted on Nukashine**.
2. Completing it awards the `Brewing station` plan, the `Fermenter` plan, one Nukashine, 150 caps, and 500 XP,
   and permanently unlocks the brewing/distilling crafting menu.
3. After that, you can occasionally speak to **Biv** (once per day) to learn additional recipes through
   further experimentation.

Both objects can be built in a regular CAMP or claimed Workshop; **neither can be placed inside a CAMP
Shelter.**

## What the Fermenter does

The Brewing Station is where you actually craft a drink from raw ingredients — the Fermenter's job is purely to
age what you've already crafted:

- A freshly-crafted alcoholic drink starts in its **Fermentable** state — largely undrinkable/weak, and it has
  to sit and age before it's good.
- Left in your inventory or stash, a drink takes about **60 minutes** to age from Fermentable to **Fresh**, then
  roughly another **120 minutes** to age from Fresh to **Vintage** (for the handful of drinks that support it) —
  three hours total.
- **Placing the drink in a built Fermenter roughly halves both stages** — about 30 minutes to Fresh, then
  another 60 minutes to Vintage, call it 90 minutes total. A Fermenter has **10 slots**, so you can age multiple
  drinks at once.
- Progress is visible on the item's condition bar; the aging process consumes the current-stage item and
  replaces it with the next stage when it finishes — you don't get to keep the earlier stage as a separate item.
- **Fresh** is drinkable and gives the drink's normal effect; **Vintage** keeps the positive effect (often
  boosted or extended) while dropping or softening the negative side-effect that stage.

Mixed drinks (ones built from another crafted alcohol as an ingredient, like Hard Lemonade or White Russian)
skip the Fermentable stage entirely and are ready to drink as soon as crafted — no aging needed.

## Vintage: which drinks actually get it

Only five drinks currently have a Vintage tier: **Nukashine**, **Lead Champagne**, **Firecracker Whiskey**,
**Tick Blood Tequila**, and **Toxic Gin**. Everything else brewed at the Brewing Station tops out at Fresh.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact fermentation timers (60/120 minutes without a Fermenter, roughly halved with one) and the specific
Vintage-eligible list are pulled from community guides rather than an official patch note, and sources
disagreed on this point — one guide claimed Firecracker Whiskey was designed to have a Vintage tier but never
actually shipped, while another currently lists it as obtainable; this entry goes with the "obtainable" version
but treat it as worth re-checking in-game if you're specifically farming for it. Individual drink effect
numbers (buff magnitude and duration) also come from community-maintained tables rather than official
Bethesda documentation and may drift with balance patches.
