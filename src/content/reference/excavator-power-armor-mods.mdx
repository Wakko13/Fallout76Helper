For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how Excavator stacks up
against the other five models and how to unlock it (Miner Miracles questline reward, craftable at
level 25/35/45), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/). For the full functional-mod pool
Excavator mostly shares and the perk-requirement caveats common to every power armor set, see
[T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/).

As with every power armor set, there is **no Sturdy/Hardened-style resistance-tier ladder** for
Excavator — no paint options with material data were found for it at all (see Uncertain).

## The mining bonus isn't a mod

Before the mod tables: Excavator's signature feature — **wearing the Excavator torso quadruples the
ore yield from mining most ore veins** (aluminum, iron, lead, copper, silver, black titanium, coal,
crystal, gold; Ultracite Ore and Uranium Ore are excluded) — is an **inherent property of the torso
piece itself**, not something you select from a mod slot. It stacks with the separate +100 Carry
Weight the full set grants. Neither bonus appears in the tables below because neither is a mod in
the schema sense used across this initiative — they're baseline stats of the piece.

## Torso mods (incl. Jet Pack)

Same nine-to-ten-mod family as T-45, with one confirmed substitution: **Welded Rebar replaces
Reactive Plates** on Excavator (and, per the same source pattern, on Raider and Ultracite too) — a
slightly stronger version of the same melee-reflect effect. Excavator's Jet Pack materials matched
T-45's exactly.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Assembly | Action Point regen +6 | Not documented | Not documented for Excavator specifically |
| Motion-Assist Servos | Strength +2 | Not documented | Not documented for Excavator specifically |
| Kinetic Dynamo | Restores AP equal to 20% of damage taken | Not documented | Not documented for Excavator specifically |
| Welded Rebar | Reflects 55% of melee damage back on the attacker (replaces Reactive Plates on this set) | Reported: Armorer rank 1 (one source described this as Raider-exclusive, but it was also seen listed for Excavator and Ultracite — see Uncertain) | Not documented for Excavator specifically |
| Tesla Coils | Deals continuous Energy damage to nearby enemies | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Medic Pump | Auto-uses a Stimpak when health drops below 50% | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Blood Cleanser | -50% chance to get addicted when consuming chems | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Emergency Protocols | Below 20% health: +25% move speed, -50% damage taken | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Jet Pack | Jet-assisted boost while jumping; Max AP -1, Weight +20% | Not documented | Confirmed: 4x Adhesive, 6x Aluminum, 5x Asbestos, 7x Nuclear Material |

Note: unlike T-45/T-51/T-60/Ultracite, **Stealth Boy did not appear in Excavator's fetched torso mod
list** — this may just be an artifact of the source not showing a complete list rather than a real
exclusion; not confirmed either way.

## Helmet mods

Same named pool as T-45 (Sensor Array, Internal Database, Targeting HUD, Recon Sensors, VATS Matrix
Overlay, Rad Scrubbers, Headlamp variants) — confirmed present on Excavator's Fextralife page, no
materials or perk data found for this set specifically.

## Paint / material tiers

No Excavator-specific functional paint (Hot Rod/Military/Minutemen-style SPECIAL-bonus paint) was
found on the sources checked. Search results turned up a list of purely cosmetic Atomic-Shop-style
skins (Aqua, Arktos Pharma, Armor Ace, Atomic Onslaught, Blood Raider, Blue Camo, Coal Dust, and
more) rather than the functional resistance/SPECIAL paint family documented for T-45/T-51/T-60/
Ultracite — these read as separately-sold cosmetic reskins, not part of the crafted mod system, so
they're intentionally not tabled here as "mods."

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Welded Rebar's perk requirement and whether it's actually Raider-exclusive is unresolved.** One
  aggregated search result described "Welded rebar requires Armorer 1" and called it Raider-only,
  but this file's own Nukes & Dragons fetch of the Excavator torso page listed Welded Rebar directly
  in Excavator's mod list (not Reactive Plates) — and the same was true for Ultracite. Either the
  "Raider-only" claim is wrong, or Excavator/Ultracite have their own separately-plan-gated Welded
  Rebar variant with the same name. Not resolved with the sources available this session.
- **Calibrated Shocks materials come from an aggregated search summary, not a direct per-mod page
  fetch** — lower confidence than the T-60 Jet Pack row in the T-60 file, for example. The 17-each
  pattern here is plausible relative to T-45's confirmed lower numbers and T-51's reported 35-each,
  since Excavator sits at a similar level bracket to T-45, but it wasn't independently verified.
- **Stealth Boy's absence from the fetched torso list** could be a genuine Excavator exclusion or
  just an incomplete source — flagged rather than assumed either way.
- **Perk requirements are otherwise entirely undocumented for Excavator** — see
  [T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) Uncertain section for the aggregated
  (unverified) Science!/Armorer rank claims for the shared mod family.
- **Base Excavator torso stats** (context, not a mod, level-45 craftable tier): 411 Damage Resistance
  / 411 Energy Resistance / 280 Radiation Resistance, 140 Carry Weight bonus listed at the piece
  level (possibly overlapping with the set's +100 Carry Weight bonus rather than additive to it —
  not confirmed which), per Nukes & Dragons.
