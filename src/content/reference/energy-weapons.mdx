For the general mechanics of weapon mod slots (receiver, barrel, sights) and where mod plans come from, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/) — this entry is about which energy weapons
exist, how to get each one, and the mod options specific to the energy-weapon family. For legendary star
effects, see [Legendary effects & crafting](../legendary-effects-overview/). Most energy-weapon mods (and the
Gatling Laser/Plasma, Gauss line) are gated behind the **Science!** perk in addition to whatever Gunsmith rank
the base weapon needs.

## Ammo types matter here more than in ballistic weapon families

Unlike ballistic guns, which mostly differ by caliber, energy weapons split into genuinely different ammo
families that don't cross over:

- **Fusion cells** — power laser weapons (Laser Rifle/Pistol, Ultracite Laser, Gatling Laser).
- **Plasma cartridges** — power plasma weapons (Plasma Rifle/Pistol, Gatling Plasma) and the Enclave Plasma line
  (covered in [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/)).
- **2mm electromagnetic cartridges** ("Gauss rounds") — power the Gauss Rifle, Gauss Pistol, and Gauss Shotgun.
- **Cryo cells** — power the Cryolator specifically; a separate ammo pool from the other three.

The **Batteries Included** perk card reduces carry weight for fusion cells/plasma cartridges specifically —
worth knowing if you're juggling multiple energy-weapon types and constantly overweight.

## The weapons

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Laser Rifle / Laser Pistol** | Fusion cells | Plan not sold by any vendor — comes from Forest/Toxic Valley world spawns (e.g. Charleston Herald, Clarksburg gun shop), containers, treasure maps, low chance from Missing Persons, Daily Ops (5-49), and Patrol Duty | The most common, cheapest-to-feed energy weapon family; receiver mod lets you choose automatic vs. non-automatic fire, so it flexes between Rifleman- and Commando-style builds |
| **Ultracite Laser Rifle** | Ultracite fusion cells (also accepts standard fusion cells) | Plan sold by Brotherhood vendor bots (Whitespring Resort, Vendor Bot Phoenix in Watoga) at level 30+, usually 3,000+ caps; low chance from Personal Matters and Savage Divide/Ash Heap dailies | Brotherhood-flavored upgrade to the base laser rifle with stronger base stats; popular "off-meta but strong" pick since it's less commonly built around than the standard laser rifle |
| **Plasma Rifle / Plasma Pistol** | Plasma cartridges | Plan sold by Brotherhood/Enclave vendors — Vendor Bot Phoenix (Watoga, level 15+), MODUS at the Whitespring Bunker terminal, Brotherhood vendor at Whitespring Resort; low chance Daily Ops (15-49), Toxic Valley/Savage Divide world spawns | Heavier per-shot damage than laser at the cost of a slower rate of fire and pricier ammo; a strong high-damage pick once you can keep it fed. A **"Plasma Sniper Rifle"** isn't a separate weapon — it's just a Plasma Rifle with a sniper barrel mod attached. |
| **Gauss Rifle** | 2mm electromagnetic cartridges | Plan sold by MODUS in the Enclave bunker at level 35+; low chance Daily Ops (35-49); world spawns/creature drops in Savage Divide, Ash Heap, The Mire, Cranberry Bog | Charge-based: holding the trigger builds capacitor charge, with damage scaling up to roughly 3x base at full charge — a build-around-the-charge-mechanic sniper/burst weapon rather than a spray-and-pray one |
| **Gauss Pistol** | 2mm electromagnetic cartridges | Plan purchasable for Gold Bullion from Regs (Settlers/Raiders, post-Wastelanders main quest) or from Minerva before finishing Wastelanders, ~250 Gold Bullion; requires Science 1 to craft | Same charge-shot mechanic as the Gauss Rifle in a one-handed frame — a favorite for VATS-crit Gunslinger-adjacent energy builds |
| **Gauss Shotgun** | 2mm electromagnetic cartridges | Plan purchasable for 500 Gold Bullion from Samuel at Foundation (requires max Settlers "Ally" reputation, post-Wastelanders); also appears in Minerva's rotating stock | Charge-and-release shotgun blast with the same capacitor mechanic as other Gauss weapons; widely considered one of the strongest shotguns in the game once charged |
| **Cryolator** | Cryo cells | Plan sold by Free States vendor bots at level 25+, or found as a random recipe spawn in Savage Divide; low chance Daily Ops (25-49); also dropped by various high-tier creatures (Deathclaws, Fog Crawlers, Grafton Monsters, Mothmen, Scorchbeasts, Wendigos) | A short-range flamer-style stream weapon that deals cryo damage and slows/freezes targets instead of burning them — crowd-control utility more than a primary damage dealer |
| **Gatling Laser** | Fusion cells | Plan sold by Brotherhood vendor bot Phoenix at Watoga Shopping Plaza (level 25+) and MODUS at the Whitespring Bunker terminal; also a random reward from several quests/events (Personal Matters, Earth Mover, Lode Baring, Breach and Clear, A Mine of Her Own, Battle Bot); Savage Divide world spawns | Sustained-fire heavy energy weapon — high total DPS at the cost of very fast fusion-cell consumption; usually a Heavy Gunner-adjacent build rather than Rifleman/Commando |
| **Gatling Plasma** | Plasma cartridges | Plan sold by MODUS at the Whitespring Bunker terminal; also from Savage Divide/Ash Heap/Mire/Cranberry Bog containers, treasure maps, world spawns; low chance Daily Ops (30-49) | Same heavy-weapon niche as the Gatling Laser but plasma's higher per-hit damage trades off against pricier, heavier ammo |
| **Tesla Rifle** | Fusion cells | Plan sold by the vendor bot at Watoga Shopping Plaza or the Brotherhood vendor at Whitespring Mall, roughly 2,376 caps; requires Science! to mod | Fires a bolt that deals full damage to the first target and then chains to nearby enemies at reduced damage (65% to the first chain target, ~42% to the second) — a wide, shotgun-like aim radius makes it a crowd-damage pick rather than a single-target one; the **Vault 63 Bertha Tesla Rifle** is a notable unique variant from the Skyline Valley update |
| **Salvaged Assaultron Head** | Built-in charge (reload to recharge, up to 5 charges) | Found on a shelf in the Pioneer Scout Camp archery range, or occasionally drops from defeated Assaultrons | Unique held weapon (not modded like the others) that fires a focused beam — damage scales with how many times you've reloaded/charged it before firing, up to the 5-charge cap |

Two energy-weapon names that turn out to be worth a closer look before you chase them: **Institute
Laser Pistol/Rifle** are real, obtainable Fallout 76 items despite the very Fallout-4-coded name —
functionally close cousins of the standard Laser Pistol/Rifle rather than a wholly different
weapon. The **Laser Musket**, by contrast, is genuinely **not obtainable through normal play** —
it exists in the game's files (left over from the modding-system rework) but was never released;
if you see one offered for trade or purchase, that's a strong signal of a hacked/duped item, not a
legitimate rare find.

## Gauss-specific mods: capacitors

Beyond the standard receiver/barrel/sight slots covered in the mod-basics FAQ, Gauss weapons have a family of
capacitor mods that other energy weapons don't: a **Prime Capacitor** adds a flat damage bonus (with an extra
bonus specifically against Scorched/Scorchbeasts), and a **Calibrated Capacitor** boosts critical-hit damage
instead. These stack on top of the charge-shot mechanic rather than replacing it.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact vendor stock, Gold Bullion prices, and quest/event reward tables shift with patches and aren't officially
documented — treat the routes above as "where to start looking" rather than a guarantee for any specific
weapon's plan. Tesla Rifle's chain-lightning percentages and the Salvaged Assaultron Head's exact
per-charge damage scaling are community-tested rather than officially published — treat the numbers
as directionally right.
