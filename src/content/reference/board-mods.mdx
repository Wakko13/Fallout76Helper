For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Board sits
relative to the rest of the one-handed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

Matching its bottom-of-the-ladder scrap-tier status, the Board has one mod slot with three
options — all sharing the same "nail-a-thing-to-a-plank" theme.

## Spike/Blade mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spiked | Armor piercing; better damage; Damage +25%; Weight +10% | Makeshift Warrior 1 | 3x Adhesive, 4x Screw, 2x Spring, 5x Steel |
| Puncturing | Armor piercing; superior damage; Damage +30% | Not documented | 1x Adhesive, 2x Steel |
| Bladed | Targets bleed; exceptional damage; Weight +50% | Makeshift Warrior 1 | 3x Adhesive, 4x Screw, 2x Spring, 5x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The **Puncturing** row's materials cell (1x Adhesive, 2x Steel) looked implausibly cheap next to
Spiked and Bladed on the same weapon, and its perk requirement was blank on Fextralife — this
smells like the recurring Fextralife data-quality issue of a truncated or mismatched cell rather
than a genuinely cheaper mod; verify in-game before relying on it. Fextralife's raw table also
showed a duplicated "2x Spring" entry on the Bladed row, which has been collapsed to a single
2x Spring line here on the assumption it was a copy-paste artifact, not a real double-cost.
Separately, Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting
melee weapons and mods entirely, so treat the Makeshift Warrior 1 requirements above as historical
rather than a guaranteed current block.
