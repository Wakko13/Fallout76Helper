None of these five have one single standout farming spot the way a boss does — they're
ambient pests scattered across Appalachia — but each has a handful of locations where
they cluster densely enough to be worth a deliberate visit.

## Bloatflies

- **Aaronholt Homestead** (The Forest) — generally cited as the most reliable single
  Bloatfly spot.
- **New River Gorge Resort** and the bridge to its west — around 9 Bloatflies.
- The unmarked cave/campground northeast of **Freddy Fear's House of Scares** — around
  6 Bloatflies, often alongside a few Honey Beasts.
- Smaller clusters (roughly 3 each) north of **Mire's Eye**, east of **The Trading
  Post**, and northwest of **Research Site Saxony**.
- Also present at Camp McClintock, Graninger Farm, Kanawha County Cemetery, Kiddie
  Corner Cabins, Southhampton Estate, and Sylvie & Sons Logging Camp.

## Bloodbugs

- **Appalachian Antiques**, **Bootlegger's Shack**, **Camp McClintock**, **Dent &
  Sons Construction**, **Kanawha County Cemetery**, **Southhampton Estate**, and
  **Sylvie & Sons Logging Camp** are the wiki-listed reliable spawn locations.
- **Aaronholt Homestead** again comes up as a mixed spawn point (Bloodbugs, Bloatflies,
  Radroaches, Ticks, or Bee Swarms).
- Public events **Campfire Tales** (Camp Adams) and **Tea Time** (the Giant Teapot)
  both spawn Bloodbugs as part of their waves.
- Generally more common near water and vegetation, e.g. around Grafton and Toxic
  Larry's Meat 'n Go in Toxic Valley.

## Stingwings

- **White Maintenance Building** (Savage Divide) — the most reliable spot, with 3
  fixed spawns.
- **Bleeding Kate's Grindhouse** — another consistently populated location.
- The pond southeast of **Big B's Rest Stop** — 3-4 Stingwings.
- **Beckwith Farm** and **Old Mold Quarry** — 1-2 each, near water/quarry edges.
- Also reported northeast of **Treetops** and near an unmarked water tower south of
  **Meadow Breeze Storage Depot**.

## Cave Crickets

- **Lucky Hole Mine** (Savage Divide) — spawns throughout the mine's lower levels.
- **The Deep** (Ash Heap) — spawns throughout the cave system, most common in lower
  levels.
- **Uncanny Caverns** (Cranberry Bog) — same pattern, densest deeper in.
- Caves just outside **Tanagra Town** (the Mire), and a small cave west of Watoga.
- They spawn in packs and are the standard farming target for Waste Acid (4-8 per
  kill), so any of the above cave systems works for bulk acid runs.

## Honey Beasts

- South of **Middle Mountain Pitstop**, facing Solomon's pond — generally the most
  consistent spot, with up to three spawning.
- **The Freak Show** (lower Savage Divide) — potential for three.
- **Treetops** (southeast of Sunday Brothers' Cabin, west of the Valley Galleria).
- A cave northeast of **Freddy Fear's House of Scares**.
- Occasional appearances at Seneca Rocks Visitor Center and the garden strip between
  the Whitespring Resort and Whitespring Golf Club.
- Also turn up in several public events: Fasnacht Day, Irrational Fear, Primal Cuts
  (Savage Divide), Project Paradise, and Spin the Wheel.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Spawn counts and "reliable" tags for all five creature types come from community wiki
tallies built up over many play sessions, not an official spawn table — actual numbers
per visit can vary, and pest-type spawns in particular seem to have some randomness
baked in (e.g. Aaronholt Homestead's mixed pest pool). Treat this as "go here first,"
not a guarantee.
