None of these five have one single standout farming spot the way a boss does — they're
ambient pests scattered across Appalachia — but each has a handful of locations where
they cluster densely enough to be worth a deliberate visit.

## Bloatflies

- [**Aaronholt Homestead**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Aaronholt_Homestead) (The Forest) — generally cited as the most reliable single
  Bloatfly spot.
- [**New River Gorge Resort**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/New_River_Gorge_Resort) and the bridge to its west — around 9 Bloatflies.
- The unmarked cave/campground northeast of [**Freddy Fear's House of Scares**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Freddy_Fear's_House_of_Scares) — around
  6 Bloatflies, often alongside a few Honey Beasts.
- Smaller clusters (roughly 3 each) north of [**Mire's Eye**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Mire's_Eye), east of [**The Trading
  Post**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Trading_Post), and northwest of [**Research Site Saxony**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Research_Site_Saxony).
- Also present at Camp McClintock, Graninger Farm, Kanawha County Cemetery, Kiddie
  Corner Cabins, Southhampton Estate, and Sylvie & Sons Logging Camp.

## Bloodbugs

- [**Appalachian Antiques**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Appalachian_Antiques), [**Bootlegger's Shack**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Bootlegger's_Shack), [**Camp McClintock**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Camp_McClintock), [**Dent &
  Sons Construction**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Dent_&_Sons_Construction), [**Kanawha County Cemetery**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Kanawha_County_Cemetery), [**Southhampton Estate**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Southhampton_Estate), and
  [**Sylvie & Sons Logging Camp**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Sylvie_&_Sons_Logging_Camp) are the wiki-listed reliable spawn locations.
- **Aaronholt Homestead** again comes up as a mixed spawn point (Bloodbugs, Bloatflies,
  Radroaches, Ticks, or Bee Swarms).
- Public events **Campfire Tales** (Camp Adams) and **Tea Time** (the Giant Teapot)
  both spawn Bloodbugs as part of their waves.
- Generally more common near water and vegetation, e.g. around Grafton and Toxic
  Larry's Meat 'n Go in Toxic Valley.

## Stingwings

- **White Maintenance Building** (Savage Divide) — the most reliable spot, with 3
  fixed spawns.
- [**Bleeding Kate's Grindhouse**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Bleeding_Kate's_Grindhouse) — another consistently populated location.
- The pond southeast of [**Big B's Rest Stop**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Big_B's_Rest_Stop) — 3-4 Stingwings.
- [**Beckwith Farm**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Beckwith_Farm) and [**Old Mold Quarry**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Old_Mold_Quarry) — 1-2 each, near water/quarry edges.
- Also reported northeast of [**Treetops**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Treetops) and near an unmarked water tower south of
  [**Meadow Breeze Storage Depot**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Meadow_Breeze_Storage_Depot).

## Cave Crickets

- [**Lucky Hole Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Lucky_Hole_Mine) (Savage Divide) — spawns throughout the mine's lower levels.
- [**The Deep**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Deep) (Ash Heap) — spawns throughout the cave system, most common in lower
  levels.
- [**Uncanny Caverns**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Uncanny_Caverns) (Cranberry Bog) — same pattern, densest deeper in.
- Caves just outside [**Tanagra Town**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Tanagra_Town) (the Mire), and a small cave west of Watoga.
- They spawn in packs and are the standard farming target for Waste Acid (4-8 per
  kill), so any of the above cave systems works for bulk acid runs.

## Honey Beasts

- South of [**Middle Mountain Pitstop**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Middle_Mountain_Pitstop), facing Solomon's pond — generally the most
  consistent spot, with up to three spawning.
- [**The Freak Show**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Freak_Show) (lower Savage Divide) — potential for three.
- **Treetops** (southeast of Sunday Brothers' Cabin, west of the Valley Galleria).
- A cave northeast of **Freddy Fear's House of Scares**.
- Occasional appearances at Seneca Rocks Visitor Center and the garden strip between
  the Whitespring Resort and Whitespring Golf Club.
- Also turn up in several public events: Fasnacht Day, Irrational Fear, Primal Cuts
  (Savage Divide), Project Paradise, and Spin the Wheel.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Spawn counts and "reliable" tags for all five creature types come from community wiki
tallies built up over many play sessions, not an official spawn table — actual numbers
per visit can vary, and pest-type spawns in particular seem to have some randomness
baked in (e.g. Aaronholt Homestead's mixed pest pool). Treat this as "go here first,"
not a guarantee.
