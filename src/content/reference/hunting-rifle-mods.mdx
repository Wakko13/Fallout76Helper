The .38 and .50 receiver families change the Hunting Rifle's ammo type entirely, which is a bigger
decision than a normal stat-tradeoff mod — .38 rounds are cheap and common, .50 rounds hit much harder
but are rarer, and the base .308 sits in between.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Suppressor's perk gate was listed inconsistently on the source page as "Gunsmith 2 / Gunsmith 3" —
worth confirming in-game which rank actually unlocks it. The Scorched Killer's Receiver's Value stat
came back unreadable ("??") on the source table; its materials and other effects are otherwise intact.
As always, exact numeric stat values and material counts shift with balance patches, so treat the above
as a strong starting point rather than a guaranteed-current recipe.
