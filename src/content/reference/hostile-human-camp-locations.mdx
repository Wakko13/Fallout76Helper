Unlike creatures, these are human NPC enemies clustered in named strongholds — useful
if you're chasing faction-specific kill challenges, human-target legendary drops, or
just want a dense fight without hunting one enemy at a time across the map.

## Blood Eagles

The Blood Eagles are Appalachia's most organized raider faction, built around
ambush checkpoints on roads plus a handful of fortified strongholds. Their camps run
alarm systems — if a member reaches the alarm, every Blood Eagle in the camp (plus
attack dogs) converges on you, so expect the fight to escalate fast rather than trickle
in one at a time.

- **Rollins Labor Camp** — the largest listed camp, around 12 Blood Eagles.
- **Summersville** — around 9.
- **Crimson Prospect** — around 8.
- **The Sludge Works** — around 7.
- **Twin Pine Cabins** — smaller but one of the more accessible/easy-to-reach spots.
- The unmarked **Blood Eagle Camp** itself, north of Vault 76 and east of Anchor Farm
  in The Forest, is their original named home base.

## Raiders (general, non-Blood Eagle)

Plain Raiders (not the Blood Eagle sub-faction specifically) are more scattered, with
raider-themed decor and occasional Raider-variant Power Armor rather than one dominant
stronghold:

- **Middle Mountain Cabins** — a raider-occupied campground with three cabins around a
  central campfire; a Raider in Power Armor has been reported spawning just outside the
  middle cabin.
- A partially-built raider shack southwest of **Wavy Willard's** in Toxic Valley, on a
  cliffside overlook.
- Watoga Underground also features raider-reprogrammed automated turrets alongside
  human Raiders, worth noting if you're clearing it for a challenge that wants both.

## Cult of the Mothman

Cultists are melee/ranged human fanatics tied to Point Pleasant and the Mothman
storyline, distinct from the Scorched — no Scorched have been observed wearing cultist
gear, and the cultists' own settlements were largely abandoned or wiped out by the
Scorched Plague per in-game lore, so don't expect the two factions mixed together at
these sites.

- **Point Pleasant** — around 26 Cultists; also where the Mothman Equinox seasonal
  event runs, so expect the count to spike further during that event.
- **Lucky Hole Mine** — around 22 Cultists, inside and outside the mine.
- **Clancy Manor** — around 13.
- **Kanawha County Cemetery** — around 11.
- **Sacramental Glade** — around 8.
- **Johnson's Acre** — around 7.
- **Blake's Offering** — around 6.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Faction headcounts at each camp are community-tallied snapshots, not official spawn
caps, and can vary between visits, especially at Blood Eagle strongholds where alarm
reinforcements can pull in Blood Eagles from adjacent areas. Point Pleasant's cultist
count in particular will spike heavily whenever the Mothman Equinox event is actively
running there.
