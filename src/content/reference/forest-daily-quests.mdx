See [how daily and repeatable quests work](../daily-and-repeatable-quests-overview/)
for the general mechanism (reset timing, drop vs. daily, how this differs from public
events). This entry is The Forest's roster specifically — for the region's public
events instead, see [The Forest public events](../forest-public-events/).

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strange Brew** | Sweetwater, at a house near **The Giant Teapot** (southwestern Forest — the same landmark that hosts the Tea Time public event) | Collect 10 honey from beehives around the site while fighting off aggressive killer bees, then bring the honey back to Sweetwater | The Special Blend recipe, honey, plus the usual chance at a legendary weapon or armor piece |
| **Ecological Balance** | A trailer on the north side of **Tygart Water Treatment** | Play the "Message for Jeff" holotape, access Amy Kerry's terminal, track down her body at Cow Spots Creamery for a password, then collect three environmental-data holotapes (air, water, soil) from randomized nearby spots before uploading everything back at the trailer terminal | Antiseptic, a random plan/armor/mod/weapon, plus a chance at legendary gear |

Both are standard **daily quests** — repeatable roughly every 24 hours, always from
the same fixed location once you've found it the first time.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

**Heart of the Enemy is not listed here.** One source (gameshedge) groups it with The
Forest because its quest-unlock note sits at Fort Defiance, but Fort Defiance itself
straddles the Savage Divide/Cranberry Bog border (see the note in
[Cranberry Bog's public events](../cranberry-bog-public-events/) about that same
ambiguity for event triggers), and the quest's actual objectives — hacking a research
terminal and harvesting Scorchbeast DNA — play out at Vault-Tec University and in the
Cranberry Bog region proper. Fextralife's dedicated Heart of the Enemy page tags its
region explicitly as Cranberry Bog, and segmentnext's region-by-region breakdown
agrees. On that basis this catalog treats it as a Cranberry Bog quest rather than a
Forest one — check that region's catalog for it instead, and don't be surprised if
another guide you read groups it differently.
