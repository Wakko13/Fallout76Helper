For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where these two sit relative
to the rest of the energy-weapon family, including ammo and acquisition, see
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/). Note that a "Plasma Sniper Rifle" isn't a separate weapon —
it's just a Plasma Rifle fitted with the Sniper Barrel mod below.

Fextralife's Plasma Rifle and Plasma Pistol pages list the same mod names, effects, and materials for
every slot — the two weapons share one mod pool, same as the Laser Rifle/Pistol pair. The tables below
are written once and apply to both.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The two sources (Plasma Rifle and Plasma Pistol Fextralife pages) agreed closely on mod names and
materials for every slot, which is a stronger cross-check than most weapons in this batch got. The
main gap is the **Stabilized Short Barrel** row — both pages gave its effect numbers but neither listed
materials, so that cell is marked "Not documented" rather than guessed. Fextralife also lists two
differently-named "Gamma Wave Emitter" capacitor rows (a burning variant and a critical variant) with
identical materials but different effects — that looks like the game genuinely offers two distinct
Gamma Wave Emitter items rather than a data-entry duplicate, but it's worth a live check if you're
specifically hunting one over the other. As always, treat exact numeric values as a strong starting
point rather than a guaranteed-current recipe.
