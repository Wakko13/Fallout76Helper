Four public events run in the Savage Divide on the general rotation — see
[event scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/) for how spawning actually works.

| Event | Trigger location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distinguished Guests** | Bolton Greens | Find and interact with 5 scattered robot waiters (one usually hides in the sauna, the rest are out on the golf fields), then gather 24 Place Settings and 6 Centerpieces and set them up while defending/repairing the waiters from attackers | Treasury Notes, scaling with how many settings/centerpieces you place |
| **Guided Meditation** | Palace of the Winding Path | Light the Brazier of Transcendence, then defend the four corner-tower "meditation hub" speakers from attacking feral ghouls (broken speakers can be repaired) | Level 1–4 "Zen Mastery" effect based on how many speakers survive, plus standard event rewards |
| **One Violent Night** | Sons of Dane compound / Buck's Den Beer House | Fill a noise meter by playing the jukebox and scattered instruments (or attacking feral ghouls with instrument weapons) to lure out a dangerous creature, then kill it | Caps, XP, Treasury Notes, Legendary Cores, and a legendary item — melee/unarmed kills reportedly pay out better across the board |
| **Uranium Fever** | Blackwater Mine (east of Whitespring Resort) | Defend uranium extractors from waves of mole miners; a disabled extractor needs 10 steel to repair | XP, caps, and uranium ore, scaled to how much uranium got extracted before time ran out; can drop Scout armor masks and jumpsuits (White Powder, Whitespring, BOS) |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Distinguished Guests' trigger point, Bolton Greens, sits right at the physical
border between The Forest and the Savage Divide (it's on the eastern edge of The
Forest map region, at the fork toward Monongah Power Plant) — Fextralife's own
event-by-region listing groups it with Savage Divide, which is what this entry
follows, but don't be surprised if you find it described as a Forest location
elsewhere. If you're chasing a broader roster of Mad Dog Malone-adjacent Toxic
Valley events, see
[Toxic Valley's public events](../toxic-valley-public-events/) instead — none of
that gang's events are in the Savage Divide.
