## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Nukes & Dragons' database groups the color options under a "Receiver (Color/Chemical)" heading
separate from its "Chemical Type (Receiver)" tank-effect heading, which reads like two different
slots sharing a receiver-adjacent name — this page treats them as the two logically distinct
groups (stat-affecting Tank vs. cosmetic-only chemical color) rather than reproducing that
ambiguous split literally. The "attack-delay-sec" field name on the Barrel mods is Nukes & Dragons'
raw stat key rather than a plain-English label; it almost certainly maps to the weapon's
projectile-arc/reload cadence rather than literal per-shot delay, but that's an inference, not a
confirmed mechanic description. The Slow-Burning Tank's damage-over-time figure (reported
informally elsewhere as roughly 80 Fire Damage/sec for 12 sec) didn't come through cleanly on any
source checked and is deliberately left out rather than stated as fact. No perk gates were found
for the Barrel or Propellant Tank slots on any source, and the plan/Gold Bullion costs for the two
gated mod groups are community-reported rather than officially published — verify in-game before
farming toward a specific Cremator build.
