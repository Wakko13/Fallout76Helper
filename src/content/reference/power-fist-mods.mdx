For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Power Fist
sits relative to the rest of the unarmed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

The Power Fist's mod slot holds two options — an armor-piercing physical pick and an energy-damage
Heating Coil, both gated behind a notably high Makeshift Warrior rank that matches its own steep
crafting-level requirement (Crafting Level 30, Makeshift Warrior rank 5, per its acquisition entry).

## General mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Puncturing | Armor piercing; superior damage; Weight +30% | Makeshift Warrior 5 | 8x Aluminum, 4x Adhesive, 5x Concrete |
| Heating Coil | Adds energy damage | Makeshift Warrior 5 | 4x Asbestos, 6x Adhesive, 5x Circuitry, 11x Copper |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Heating Coil row's weight modifier wasn't specified on Fextralife (Puncturing's +30% weight
was explicit; Heating Coil's was left blank) — worth confirming in-game if weight budgeting
matters for your build. As with the rest of this wave, Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025)
reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting melee weapons and mods entirely, so treat
Makeshift Warrior 5 as the historically-documented requirement rather than a guaranteed current
block — though the weapon's own Crafting Level 30 gate for the base item itself is a separate,
likely still-current requirement.
