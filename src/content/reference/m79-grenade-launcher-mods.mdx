Fextralife's own M79 page came back with its mod section entirely unpopulated (a bare "??"
placeholder) despite confirming the weapon has Barrel and Grip/Stock slots. The effect numbers
below come from Nukes & Dragons' percentage-based database instead; materials came from a
targeted search of individual Fandom mod-item pages rather than a single weapon-page table, so
treat them as reasonably sourced but not cross-checked against a live in-game crafting menu.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Only two mod slots turned up on any source checked (Barrel and Stock) — no Sights or Receiver slot
is documented for this weapon, which is plausible for a break-action single-shot launcher but
worth confirming in-game if you're expecting a scope option. The Long Barrel's perk gate was never
found on any source (Fextralife's page had no data at all, and the Fandom item page that supplied
its materials didn't list a perk requirement either), while the Full Stock's Gunsmith 5 gate came
through clean on the Fandom side. Both materials lists came from single-item Fandom pages found via
search rather than a fetched full weapon-mod table, so verify exact quantities in-game or against a
live wiki before farming toward either mod.
