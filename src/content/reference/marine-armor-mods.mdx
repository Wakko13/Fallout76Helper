For how the armor mod slot system works in general (material tiers, the single misc slot, underarmor
linings being separate), see [Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For
where Marine Armor sits relative to Combat/Trapper/etc. — including its defining trait of built-in
radiation resistance — see [Light and combat armor sets](../light-and-combat-armor-sets/).

**Marine Armor's material mods appear to be the same Welded/Tempered/Hardened/Buttressed family as
Trapper Armor's** — see [Trapper Armor mod options](../trapper-armor-mods/) for the fuller writeup of that
family, including the caveat about a shared Raider/Trapper/Marine grouping. Crafting-material quantities
found for Marine specifically match Trapper's almost exactly (same Adhesive/Cloth/Steel pattern scaling by
tier), which supports the shared-family claim independently of the Trapper research. Data below is for the
**chest piece**; Marine Armor also has a separate **Arctic Marine Armor** variant (same mod names, per
Nukes & Dragons) and comes in level-35 and level-45 base grades — see the Uncertain section for what
differs between them.

## Material/Resistance mods

Percentages are relative bonuses over the piece's base resistance at its current weight class, per Nukes
& Dragons; crafting materials and scrap-learn chances come from wiki-summary snippets (fallout.wiki and
fallout.fandom.com returned HTTP errors on direct fetch for Marine-specific pages, consistent with every
other set in this initiative).

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | No bonus | — | — |
| Welded Marine | Enhanced Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance. Reported known by default. | — | Reported (both level 35 and 45): 1x Adhesive, 1x Cloth, 1x Steel |
| Tempered Marine | Physical Resistance +35%, Fire/Cryo Resistance +15%, Weight +15%. Reported scrap-learned (~0.75% chance). | Not documented | Reported (both grades): 1x Adhesive, 1x Cloth, 2x Steel |
| Hardened Marine | Improved Damage Resistance with superior Energy/Radiation Resistance. Reported scrap-learned (~0.75% chance). | Not documented | Reported (both grades): 1x Adhesive, 1x Cloth, 4x Steel |
| Buttressed Marine | Exceptional Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance — the top tier. Reported scrap-learned (~0.75% chance). | Not documented | Reported (one source states level 45 only): 1x Adhesive, 1x Cloth, 5x Steel |

[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). No Marine-specific search hits
were found for this table; it's presumed shared with Trapper/Combat/Metal per the consistent naming and
effect values across every set researched in both waves of this initiative.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Fextralife's per-item Marine Armor Chest Piece page has no mod table** — it explicitly states "This
  armor has no special effects" and shows "??" placeholders for stat values, same pattern as every other
  set in this initiative. All effect numbers above come from Nukes & Dragons plus wiki-summary snippets.
- **The shared-family claim with Trapper Armor** (see [Trapper Armor mod options](../trapper-armor-mods/))
  rests on matching mod names and near-identical crafting-material quantities surfacing independently for
  both sets, not a single primary source that lists all sets together. Treat as probable, not certain —
  the same caveat the Robot Armor mods entry applied to its claimed overlap with Metal Armor.
- **Buttressed being "level 45 only"** in one source's material listing (vs. Welded/Tempered/Hardened
  listed for both level 35 and 45) is a genuinely odd asymmetry that wasn't cross-checked against a second
  source — it might mean the level-35 Marine Armor grade caps out at Hardened, or it might be an artifact
  of incomplete source data. Worth confirming in-game if the level-35 vs. 45 distinction matters for a
  build.
- **No crafting materials or perk gates were found for any Marine Armor misc mod** (Pocketed family,
  Padded/Dense, linings) — the misc-mod table above is carried over unchanged from the pattern confirmed
  for other sets, not independently verified for Marine.
- **Marine Armor's headline trait — built-in radiation resistance** — appears to live in the piece's base
  stats rather than in any mod-tier bonus documented here; nothing found suggests a Marine-exclusive
  radiation-focused material mod beyond what Welded/Tempered/Hardened/Buttressed already provide to every
  set in this shared family.
- **Arctic Marine Armor** shows the identical mod list to standard Marine Armor per Nukes & Dragons, but
  this wasn't independently confirmed beyond that one source — if Arctic Marine has any environment-themed
  exclusive mod, it wasn't surfaced.
