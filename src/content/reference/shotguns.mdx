[Legendary effects & crafting](../legendary-effects-overview/). The **Gauss Shotgun** uses energy-adjacent 2mm
electromagnetic cartridges and is covered in [Energy weapons overview](../energy-weapons/) instead of here.

## Correction: Le Fusil Terribles is not in Fallout 76

If you've seen "Le Fusil Terribles" (or "Le Fusil Terrible") referenced as a Fallout 76 unique combat shotgun,
that's a mix-up — **it's a Fallout 4-exclusive weapon**, found in Libertalia, and has never been part of Fallout
76. The closest thing Fallout 76 actually has is the unique double-barrel shotgun **Cold Shoulder** (see table
below).

## The shotguns

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Combat Shotgun** | Shotgun shells | Sold by vendor bots (e.g. Brotherhood vendor bot Phoenix at Watoga Shopping Plaza) once you're level 20-25+; low chance from Personal Matters and Daily Ops (20-49); world spawns in Savage Divide/Ash Heap | Semi-auto-capable all-rounder shotgun and the most common "starter" shotgun plan to land |
| **Double-Barrel Shotgun** | Shotgun shells | Plan is a guaranteed reward from **Flavors of Mayhem**; not sold by any vendor; low chance from several Savage Divide dailies/events (Someone To Talk To, The Messenger, Uranium Fever, Distinguished Guests, Violent Night, Guided Meditation) | Huge burst alpha-damage per trigger pull — a favorite for Bloodied/one-shot-focused builds; also the base frame for the unique Cold Shoulder |
| **Pump Action Shotgun** | Shotgun shells | Guaranteed if you pick the **Shotgunner** starting specialization at character creation; also a high-chance reward from the **Patrol Duty** event (Eastern Regional Penitentiary, Morgantown); world spawns in Forest/Toxic Valley (Charleston Herald, Clarksburg gun shop); low chance from Missing Persons and Daily Ops (5-49); not sold by any vendor | Reliable early-game workhorse shotgun with good bleed/incendiary receiver options |
| **Cold Shoulder** (unique double-barrel) | Shotgun shells | Rip Daring's signature pre-modded shotgun (Quad capacity, Paranormal +50% cryptid damage, cryo-projectile mod). Originally a Season 12 scoreboard reward; currently purchasable with **Stamps** (Expedition/Scoreboard currency) from the vendor Giuseppe Della Ripa | Comes pre-modded with capabilities otherwise unavailable on a normal double-barrel (quadruple ammo capacity, built-in cryo damage) — a strong grab-and-go pick once you have the currency, no plan-hunting required |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact vendor stock and quest/event reward tables shift with patches and aren't officially documented — treat
the routes above as "where to start looking." Cold Shoulder's acquisition path is the most patch-sensitive entry
here: it moved from a time-limited scoreboard reward to a Stamps-vendor purchase, and Stamps-vendor rotations
can change again in future updates — verify against a current source before counting on it.
