**Not to be confused with Mole Miners** — the frenzied, high-HP humanoid raiders
in mining suits covered in [Mole miner locations](../mole-miner-locations/).
Mole Rats and Rad Rats are small animal critters, not humans in suits. Mole
Miners have actually domesticated Mole Rats and fight alongside them, which is
part of why the names get mixed up — but a Mole Rat you meet on its own is
ordinary huntable wildlife.

## Mole Rats

Mutated naked mole-rats, much larger than their pre-war ancestors, that burrow
and ambush from tunnels and dark, humid spaces. Reliable spots:

- [**Welch Station**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Welch_Station) — 5-6 Mole Rats on and around the railroad, easy fast-travel
  access.
- [**Green Country Lodge**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Green_Country_Lodge) — around 3 Mole Rats near the entrance, also easy
  fast-travel access.
- [**Gauley Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Gauley_Mine) (after completing the Wastelanders quest "Strength in
  Numbers") — up to 17 Mole Rats, the single densest spawn in the game.
- [**Poseidon Power Substation PX-02**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Poseidon_Power_Substation_PX-02) — around 7 Mole Rats.
- [**Riverside Manor**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Riverside_Manor) — around 8 Mole Rats.
- [**Hillfolk Hotdogs**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Hillfolk_Hotdogs) and [**Carleton Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Carleton_Mine) — around 5 each.
- [**Transmission Station 1AT-U03**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Transmission_Station_1AT-U03) — around 4, near the front gate.
- [**Lucky Hole Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Lucky_Hole_Mine) — a further tunnel-dwelling cluster.

## Rad Rats

Rad Rats started as ordinary pre-war rats before nuclear fallout tainted them —
a different mutation path than Mole Rats, and notably tougher: more HP and more
damage per hit than a regular Mole Rat. They're more scattered and lean toward
underground bunkers and basements, especially on the **Cranberry Bog** side of
the map:

- [**Sutton**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Sutton) — a garage down the road from Sutton Station has 2 spawns that can
  roll as either Mole Rats or Rad Rats.
- Cranberry Bog bunkers and basements generally, including AMS Testing Site,
  Camp McClintock, Forward Station Delta, Rollins Work Camp, and The Thorn.
- Other reported spots: Bastion Park, Excelsior Model Home, Johnson's Acre,
  Nicholson's End, Pleasant Hills Cemetery, Sunday Brothers' Cabin, Sylvie &
  Sons Logging Camp, The Freak Show, and The Vantage.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Rad Rats don't have one standout dedicated farm the way Mole Rats do at Welch
Station or Gauley Mine — their spawns are reported as scattered single/double
encounters across many locations rather than a reliable dense pack, so treat
the Rad Rat list as "worth checking if you're passing by" rather than a
farming route. Exact spawn counts for Mole Rats are community tallies and can
shift after patches or between world instances.
