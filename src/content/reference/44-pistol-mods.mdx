For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the .44 Pistol sits
relative to the rest of the sidearm lineup, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

The .44 Pistol has a notably smaller mod pool than the 10mm Pistol or Pipe weapons — no automatic-fire
receiver branch (it stays a revolver), no dedicated Magazine or Muzzle slot documented by the source,
and only a small handful of Sight options. That's consistent with its role as a high-per-shot-damage
crit revolver rather than a sustained-fire weapon.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's .44 Pistol page did not list a Magazine or Muzzle mod section at all, and several
Receiver rows (Hair Trigger, Steadfast, Fierce) only exposed a "Value" stat rather than a clean
gameplay-facing effect description — the effect text for those three is a best-effort interpretation
and should be sanity-checked in-game or against an alternate wiki before relying on it. As with every
weapon on this site, exact numeric bonuses and material costs shift with balance patches; treat the
figures above as a strong starting point, not a guaranteed-current recipe. It's also worth confirming
whether a Magazine/speedloader or Muzzle slot has been added in a more recent update than this
source reflects, since most other revolvers in this family (Pipe Revolver, Single Action Revolver)
do have at least a barrel-mounted muzzle option.
