Radscorpions tend to spawn in tight groups of four or more rather than as
singles, and burrow up out of the ground when they aggro, so expect a sudden
multi-target fight rather than a slow trickle. The glowing variant appears mixed
into normal packs and hits harder with a radiation-tinged sting.

## Best farms

- [**Carleton Mine**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Carleton_Mine) (Toxic Valley) — up to five radscorpions reliably spawn
  inside, both before and after the "Cheating Death" Wastelanders quest.
- [**Smith Farm**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Smith_Farm) (Toxic Valley) — four radscorpions reliably spawn here, making
  it a good pair with Carleton Mine on the same loop since both are in Toxic
  Valley.

## Other reliable spots

[**World of Corn**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/World_of_Corn) (nests around the museum and corn-sculpture rows) has around
five reliable spawns. [**Hocking Hills Little League Field**](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Hocking_Hills_Little_League_Field) has three burrowed
in the diamond itself. South of the [**Meadow Breeze Storage Depot**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Meadow_Breeze_Storage_Depot), near the
crashed train, another three spawn reliably. [**Middle Mountain Cabins**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Middle_Mountain_Cabins), [**The
Freak Show**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Freak_Show), [**Graninger Farm**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Graninger_Farm), [**Top of the World**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Top_of_the_World), the garden area between
the Whitespring Resort and Whitespring Golf Club, [**Kiddie Corner Cabins**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Kiddie_Corner_Cabins), and
the [**New Appalachian Central Trainyard**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/New_Appalachian_Central_Trainyard) are all documented secondary spots.

## Giant Radscorpion and the Sinkhole Solutions event

The **Sinkhole Solutions** public event spawns dozens of radscorpions across
several waves, culminating in a **giant, glowing radscorpion** with a flaming
stinger as the final-wave highlight — guaranteed legendary radscorpions also
appear in that last wave, making the event one of the best concentrated
radscorpion farms in the game when it's active, not just a passive spawn point.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Spawn counts at the secondary locations are drawn from community farming guides
rather than official data and can shift with server population. The giant
radscorpion is specifically an event-spawn tied to Sinkhole Solutions rather than
a standalone world spawn — don't expect to find one just wandering a regular
radscorpion nest outside that event.
