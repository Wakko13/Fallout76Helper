For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Deathclaw
Gauntlet sits relative to the rest of the unarmed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

The highest-ceiling unarmed weapon in the family keeps its mod slot minimal — just one option that
adds a second claw for more damage.

## General mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extra Claw | Better damage; Weight +30% | Makeshift Warrior | 7x Aluminum, 3x Leather, 4x Bone, 4x Adhesive |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife didn't specify a Makeshift Warrior **rank** for Extra Claw, just the perk name — worth
confirming in-game if you're planning a perk budget around it. As with the rest of this wave, Patch
60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting melee weapons and
mods entirely, so treat "Makeshift Warrior" here as the historically-documented requirement rather
than a guaranteed current block.
