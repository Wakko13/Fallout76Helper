For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Salvaged Assaultron
Head compares to the rest of the energy-weapon family and where to find one, see
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/).

## No standard mod slots

Like the [Black Powder Pistol](../black-powder-pistol-mods/), the Salvaged Assaultron Head is a case
where the assumption in this wave's brief turned out to be correct: it genuinely has **no weapon mod
slots**. Fextralife's dedicated mod section for it reads "None" outright — no Receiver, Barrel, Grip,
Sight, Magazine, or Muzzle slot to attach anything to at a Weapons Workbench. As a unique held weapon
(salvaged from an Assaultron rather than assembled from a plan), what you find or loot is what you
fire, unmodified.

## The charge/reload mechanic instead

In place of mods, the weapon's identity is its own built-in damage-scaling system: **"Charged by
Reloading Repeatedly. The more Charges, the Higher the Damage,"** per Nukes & Dragons, up to a 5-charge
cap per the [Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/) overview. Repeated reloading before firing also
**irradiates the user**, per Fextralife — a real cost for stacking charges rather than a free power-up.
Nukes & Dragons lists its ammo type as **Fusion Cell** and its base stats as 93 damage, fire rate 67
(semi-auto), 30 AP cost per shot, range 0-109-218, and accuracy 100%-50% at level 25.

Two things are still worth knowing even without weapon mods:

- It can still take a **legendary effect** (the separate legendary-star system that applies to almost
  all weapons regardless of mod slots) if you find one as a legendary drop — that's independent of the
  weapon-mod system this page is about. Nukes & Dragons describes four legendary-effect slot categories
  with 40+ possible options total (e.g. Bloodied, Anti-armor, Two Shot, Explosive, Rapid, Ghost's,
  Stabilizer's), which is the legendary system, not a hidden weapon-mod tree.
- Condition/repair still applies normally; "no mods" only means the weapon-mod slots are absent, not
  that the weapon is otherwise unmodifiable in every sense.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

There's a genuine conflict between sources on ammo: this site's own
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/) overview describes the Salvaged Assaultron Head's ammo as "Built-in
charge (reload to recharge, up to 5 charges)" — implying no conventional ammo is consumed — while Nukes
& Dragons' database explicitly lists its Ammo Type as **Fusion Cell**. Both could be true at once (a
Fusion Cell-based reload cycle that also builds a separate charge stack), but that wasn't confirmed by
any source reachable this session — verify against a live wiki or in-game before assuming either
reading. The exact per-charge damage scaling curve and radiation cost per reload are also
community-tested rather than officially published; treat them as directionally right rather than exact.
