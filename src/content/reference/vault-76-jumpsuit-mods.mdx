For how underarmor lining slots work as a category, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Vault 76
Jumpsuit is acquired (given free during "Reclamation Day," Plan drops widely afterward for
spares), see [Functional attire](../functional-attire/).

The Vault 76 Jumpsuit is confirmed underarmor in Nukes & Dragons' database, and it belongs to the
**Vault-Tec group** of underarmor linings — the same lining set shared with the Vault-Tec
University Jumpsuit (a different item from the "Vault-Tec Jumpsuit" covered separately in this
wave; see the Uncertain section below for that distinction).

## Lining (single slot)

Only one lining is active at a time. Resistance values, SPECIAL bonuses, materials, and unlock
requirements below are all from the same Vault-Tec underarmor linings source and are internally
consistent with each other (increasing cost/materials at each tier, unlocks gated behind
successive Overseer's Log holotapes found across the main-quest trail).

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Lining | No resistance bonus; known by default | — | Ballistic Fiber x2, Gold x3 |
| Treated Lining | +2 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Endurance, +1 Luck | Overseer's Log — Firehouse (pickup, not a perk) | Ballistic Fiber x2, Gold x6 |
| Resistant Lining | +3 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Endurance, +2 Intelligence, +1 Luck | Overseer's Log — Camp Venture (pickup, not a perk) | Ballistic Fiber x7, Gold x9 |
| Protective Lining | +4 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Strength, +1 Endurance, +2 Intelligence, +1 Luck | Overseer's Log — Fort Defiance (pickup, not a perk) | Ballistic Fiber x9, Gold x12, Stable Cobalt Flux x2 |
| Shielded Lining | +5 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Strength, +2 Endurance, +2 Intelligence, +2 Luck | Overseer's Logs — Site Alpha, Site Bravo, and Site Charlie (all three, pickup, not a perk) | Ballistic Fiber x11, Gold x15, Stable Cobalt Flux x4 |

Unlike a Perk Card requirement, these "unlocks" are one-time holotape pickups found while following
the Overseer's main-quest trail through Appalachia — once picked up on a given character, the
recipe stays known.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **fallout.fandom.com returns HTTP 402 on direct fetch** — the table above comes from a WebSearch
  summary of its indexed content rather than a page this session loaded and read directly. Nukes &
  Dragons *was* loaded directly and confirms the same five lining names and a matching weight-cost
  progression (Treated → Shielded getting progressively heavier), which corroborates the lining
  slot's existence and tier order, though its own fetch surfaced weight percentages rather than the
  resistance/SPECIAL numbers used above.
- **"Vault-Tec group" vs. the standalone "Vault-Tec Jumpsuit"**: Nukes & Dragons' underarmor
  database lists Vault 76, Vault 94, Vault 96, and Vault Tec University jumpsuits, but has no entry
  for a plain "Vault-Tec Jumpsuit." Cross-checking Fextralife and search results indicates that
  item (covered in [Vault-Tec Jumpsuit mod options](../vault-tec-jumpsuit-mods/)) is a separate,
  cosmetic **Outfit**, not underarmor, and does not get these linings — despite the existing
  [Functional attire](../functional-attire/) overview grouping "Vault 76 jumpsuit" and "Vault-Tec
  jumpsuit" together as a pair. That overview page may be worth revisiting given this distinction.
