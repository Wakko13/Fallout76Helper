## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This page leans entirely on Nukes & Dragons for effect numbers because Fextralife's own Radium Rifle
mod tables didn't return populated rows on this pass — that's unusual for a common mid-tier rifle and
is worth re-checking against a freshly-loaded Fextralife page (their tables can be JS-rendered and may
simply not have surfaced through this fetch). Perk gates and crafting materials are unconfirmed for
every single row above; based on comparable rifles (Combat Rifle, Hunting Rifle), expect most
non-Standard receiver/barrel/stock/magazine mods to sit behind Gunsmith 2, automatic receivers to also
require Gunsmith 2, and the Prime Receiver to require either Gunsmith 2 or Gunsmith 5 depending on
weapon — but none of that is confirmed specifically for the Radium Rifle, so don't plan a build around
these numbers without checking in-game. As always, exact stat values shift with balance patches.
