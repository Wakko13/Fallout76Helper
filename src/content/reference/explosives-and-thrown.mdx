Grenades and mines are crafted at a **Tinker's Workbench**, not the Weapons Workbench used for
guns and melee weapons — see [Standard weapon and armor mods](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/)
for how the separate weapon-mod system works if you're looking for mods rather than raw crafting.
Explosives generally don't carry standard mod slots the way guns do; the "build" for most of
them is closer to a plain crafting recipe than a moddable base weapon.

## Grenades and mines

| Item | Type | How to acquire | Notable mod or niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frag Grenade | Ballistic explosive | Base recipe/plan is broadly available early; also found as loot on super mutant, Blood Eagle, and raider corpses | The reliable, cheap all-rounder — decent against everything, best against nothing in particular |
| Plasma Grenade | Energy explosive | Plan not sold by any fixed vendor — random reward from "Personal Matters," "Earth Mover," and events like Lode Baring, Breach and Clear, A Mine of Her Own, and Battle Bot | Energy damage type hits robots and power-armored players harder than Frag does, at the cost of being weaker against unarmored flesh targets |
| Pulse Grenade | Energy explosive (EMP-style) | Plan has a low chance from Cranberry Bog daily/repeatable quests and events; not sold by vendors | Devastating against robots and power armor specifically — a situational tool rather than a main-loadout grenade |
| Cryo Grenade | Cold/frost explosive | Plan not sold by vendors — low chance from Cranberry Bog dailies, a very low chance from almost any event, and found in containers anywhere (explosives crates give the best odds) | Slows and can freeze-lock enemies in place; a crowd-control pick more than a damage pick |
| Cryo Mine | Cold/frost, planted | Plan not sold by vendors — same event/quest reward pool as Cryo Grenade (Census Violence, Monster Mash, Line in the Sand, Distant Thunder, Surface to Air, AWOL Armaments), plus container drops (explosives crates best) | Planted version of the Cryo Grenade's crowd control — useful for choke points and defending a fixed position |
| Explosive Bait | Thrown, non-damaging lure | Crafting unlocks automatically during the "Flavors of Mayhem" quest — no plan to find | Not a weapon in the direct-damage sense; it's a lure/trap tool for pulling or baiting specific creatures |

## Demolition Expert (perk)

**Demolition Expert** (Intelligence tree, usable from level 10) is the perk card that scales
essentially this entire category: it boosts damage from grenades, mines, thrown explosives, and
weapons carrying the Explosive legendary effect. It currently has 3 ranks at +20%/+30%/+40%
explosive damage (a prior version had 5 ranks topping out at +60%, reduced in a later balance
pass) — see [Perk essentials](../perks-essentials/) for the general perk-acquisition picture if
you're new to how perk cards work.

## The Bottlecap Mine no longer exists

The Bottlecap Mine was originally obtainable only through an inventory-duplication-style exploit;
Bethesda patched the exploit and **removed all Bottlecap Mines from player inventories** in a
subsequent patch. It is not a currently craftable or obtainable item — don't spend time farming
for a plan that no longer drops, and treat any guide claiming a legitimate acquisition method as
outdated.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

A "Fire Nova Grenade" is a real explosive from **Fallout 4**'s Vault-Tec Workshop DLC, but
searching current Fallout 76 wikis and grenade listings turned up no matching item under that
name in 76 — it may be conflated with the Fallout 4 item, or it may exist under a different name
this research didn't surface. Don't rely on this entry for that specific item; verify directly
against a current Fextralife or Fallout Wiki grenade list before building around it. Event/quest
reward-pool percentages for the other grenades and mines above are community-datamined, not
official, and shift with loot-table rebalances.
