Cranberry Bog is Appalachia's southeastern endgame region, and its public events
lean toward higher-level defense/wave-survival fights rather than the shorter
fetch-and-clear events common in lower regions (see
[regions at a glance](../regions-and-fast-travel/)). For how public events spawn
and rotate in general, see
[event rotation and scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/) — this entry is
the roster of what exists in this region, not a live schedule.

| Event | Trigger location | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AWOL Armaments | RobCo Research Center, northeast Cranberry Bog | Sergeant Gutsy ABA-1 needs help recalling military robots that went AWOL; press the recall button in the truck beside him, then fight off three waves of military-grade robots, each led by a boss robot | Random weapon/armor/ammo loot with a shot at legendaries |
| Census Violence | Random location in Cranberry Bog | Defend a Census Taker robot (recounting "non-humans" by killing them) through a 4-minute countdown while it wanders and waves of enemies spawn; the event fails if the Census Taker is destroyed | General event loot; enemy composition varies by where it spawns |
| Distant Thunder | Fort Defiance, on the Savage Divide/Cranberry Bog border | Mark Scorched with any weapon-mounted recon scope (or the Pearly Peepers cosmetic) so Fort Defiance's artillery battery shells the marked area; you can also just fight the Scorched directly without marking anything, which still completes it | Random loot, ammo, plans, chance at legendary weapons/armor or power armor pieces |
| Line in the Sand | Fort Defiance courtyard | Activate the ASAM sensor's control computer to start pre-op checks, then defend the sonic generator and ASAM through three waves of Scorched and Scorchbeasts; failing to repair the generator in time also fails the event | Recipe: Disease Cure (Cranberry Bog), a small chance at rarer plans like T-51b Power Armor pieces |
| Surface to Air | Automated SAM sites around the Cranberry Bog's edges | Defend one or two Brotherhood-built surface-to-air missile turrets against Scorchbeasts and Scorched for 5 minutes; if a turret goes down you get 60 seconds to repair it before the event auto-fails | ~20,350 XP, random T-51b power armor pieces, Cranberry Bog plans |

## A shared theme: Scorchbeast containment

Three of these five events (Distant Thunder, Line in the Sand, Surface to Air) are
explicitly framed as anti-Scorchbeast infrastructure — Fort Defiance's artillery,
the ASAM sensor network, and the SAM turret ring are all part of the same
in-fiction effort to contain Scorchbeast activity coming out of the Cranberry Bog.
If you're specifically farming Scorchbeast-related loot or just want a
Scorched-heavy fight, any of these three deliver a similar flavor of encounter.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- Level recommendations (AWOL Armaments, Census Violence, Line in the Sand, and
  Surface to Air are all commonly listed around **level 35+**) come from community
  wikis rather than an in-game display, and Fallout 76 doesn't hard-gate event
  participation by level.
- Distant Thunder's own wiki entries are thin on exact recommended level (listed as
  "Any" on Fextralife) — it's grouped here with the other Cranberry Bog/Fort
  Defiance events because every source checked ties its trigger specifically to
  Fort Defiance's artillery battery, not to multiple regions.
- Fort Defiance sits right on the regional border with Savage Divide, so exactly
  which "region" Distant Thunder and Line in the Sand count as can vary by source;
  both are documented here under Cranberry Bog since that's where Fort Defiance
  itself is most commonly classified.
