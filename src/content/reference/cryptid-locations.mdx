Appalachia's cryptids are drawn from real West Virginia folklore, and each one has a
loosely "signature" area tied to its lore even though none of them are guaranteed
100%-of-the-time spawns.

## Mothman

Point Pleasant, in the southeast of the map, is the lore-appropriate home turf — a
basic Mothman can appear in the wooded area east of the river there, but only at
**night**; it won't spawn during the day. Encounters are otherwise random, so expect
to need a few passes. Other reported sighting spots include Wisespring Resort
Checkpoints, south of Abbie's Bunker, and the stretch between Wendigo Cave and Autumn
Acre Cabin. Note that special Mothman variants (Wise Mothman via the "Path to
Enlightenment" quest at Landview Lighthouse, Vengeful Mothman via the "Queen of the
Hunt" daily) are more reliably summonable than the wild basic version, but those are
quest-gated rather than open-world hunting spots. A basic Mothman won't attack unless
you shoot first.

## Grafton Monster

- **Charleston Trainyard** — a guaranteed spawn.
- Southeast of **Hemlock Holes** — another guaranteed spawn.
- East of the **Charleston Capitol Building**, across the river near the dam — a third
  guaranteed spot.
- Between the **Whitespring Resort** and **Whitespring Golf Club** — a chance spawn,
  one of the less reliable options.
- Shows up as an event boss too: the finale of **Grafton Day**, a Prime variant in
  **Primal Cuts** (Toxic Valley), an Alpha variant in **Project Paradise** (Arktos
  Pharma Biome Lab), and roughly a 1-in-6 chance during **Queen of the Hunt**.

## Flatwoods Monster

No fixed guaranteed spawn — it's the rarest of the four, with better odds at night.
Areas with reported higher encounter chances:

- Near **Abbie's Bunker** (the Mire).
- Across the river from **Dyer Chemical** (the Mire).
- The woods west of **Wilson Brother's Auto Repair**, roughly the middle of the map.
- Northwest of **Morgantown Airport** and north of **Gorge Junkyard**.
- **AVR Medical Center** (Toxic Valley).

The **Queen of the Hunt** daily (three Mire locations to check) can also produce a
Flatwoods Monster, and the limited-time **Invaders From Beyond** event spawns multiple
at the top of every hour when it's active.

## Snallygaster

- **Toxic Larry's Meat 'n Go** (east of Wendigo Cave, west of Berkeley Springs) — 3
  guaranteed.
- Between the **Hornwright Industrial Headquarters** and **Charleston Capitol
  Building** — 2 guaranteed.
- **Pylon V-13** — usually 2-3.
- **Federal Disposal Field HZ-21** — 1.
- **Flooded Trainyard** — reportedly up to 6.
- Also seen at Kiddie Corner Cabins, Treetops, and the crashed vertibird behind
  Hemlock Holes.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Flatwoods Monster in particular has no confirmed fixed spawn table — every source
describes it as low-probability and night-favored rather than guaranteed, so treat
those location names as "better odds," not certainties. Grafton Monster and
Snallygaster guaranteed counts are community-tallied and can shift with patches.
