For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how to acquire the Gamma
Gun itself, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

The Gamma Gun has a small, distinctive mod pool built around its "Dish" (its equivalent of a
receiver/barrel), rather than the standard receiver/barrel/grip/sight/muzzle set most ballistic
weapons use.

## Dish mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Dish | Standard | — | Not documented |
| Deep Dish | Better range and damage | Not documented | Not documented |
| Lorenzo's Artifact | Uses telekinesis to push targets away | Not documented | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This page is more incomplete than the others in this series, and that's a property of the source
data, not a research shortcut: Fextralife's Gamma Gun page and every individual mod sub-page fetched
for it (Standard Dish, Deep Dish, Standard Grip, Sharpshooter's Grip) consistently show "??" for
perk requirement, accuracy/weight change, and crafting materials — only the plain-text effect
description is filled in. A companion source, the "Plan: Gamma gun electric signal carrier antennae"
page on fallout.wiki, could not be fetched during this research (403 response), so the Antennae mod's
materials remain unconfirmed too. If exact recipes matter for planning a build, check the in-game
crafting menu directly or a wiki mirror not attempted here (e.g. the current fallout.fandom.com or
fallout.wiki page, both of which returned errors during this session) rather than relying on this
page for material costs — the mod names and effects above are solid, the "Materials" column is not.
