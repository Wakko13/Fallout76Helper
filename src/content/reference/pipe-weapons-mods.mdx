For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where these weapons sit
relative to the rest of the sidearm lineup, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

The three Pipe variants (Pipe Pistol, Pipe Revolver, Pipe Bolt-Action) share nearly the entire
non-receiver mod pool — Barrel, Grip/Stock, Magazine, Sight, and Muzzle mods use the same names and
(where documented) the same materials across all three guns, since they're built from the same junk
components at a Weapons Workbench. The **Receiver** slot is where they genuinely diverge: it's what
sets each variant's base fire mode and ammo type, so receivers are listed separately per weapon
below. A Stock mod attached to a Pipe Pistol or Pipe Bolt-Action converts it into the rifle-class
"Pipe Rifle" / "Pipe Bolt-Action Rifle" — same weapon, longer effective range.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's crafting-materials column is genuinely blank ("??") for a meaningful chunk of the rarer
receiver, barrel, magazine, and sight variants across all three weapons — this was confirmed by
fetching several of those individual mod sub-pages directly (e.g. Pipe Bolt-Action .50 Receiver,
Pipe Bolt-Action Suppressor), which show the same missing data, so it isn't a fetch error on this
end. Rows marked "Not documented on Fextralife" above are genuinely unconfirmed, not omitted for
space. The **Pipe Bolt-Action's** receiver materials in particular are almost entirely undocumented
on the source — its effect text is confirmed, but no full ingredient list was found for any of its
receivers, including the signature .50 Caliber upgrade. Exact numeric stat values (Accuracy, Range,
Fire Rate) also drift with balance patches — treat the numbers above as "roughly this," not exact
current-patch figures. The Grip/Stock table above merges Grip and Stock mods into one section since
Fextralife documents them together for these weapons; note that a Grip and a Stock are the same slot
choice on Pipe weapons (Stock swaps the weapon into its rifle form), not two independent slots.
