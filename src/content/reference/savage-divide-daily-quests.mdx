## Standalone dailies

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Someone to Talk To** | A basement terminal in a house at **Monongah** (Dr. Eddie Harrison's old lab — no living questgiver) | Take the Vox Syringer and Vox Interpreter Holotape from the lab, load the holotape into your Pip-Boy, shoot three randomly-selected animals with syringer darts, wait for the Pip-Boy's analysis timer, then return both items to the terminal | Syringer ammo, a random plan/armor/mod/weapon, plus a legendary chance |
| **Trick or Treat?** | **Jack O'Lantern**, an orange Mister Handy wearing a witch's hat, at the derelict **Pumpkin House** (northern Savage Divide, roughly between Vault 94 and Fissure Site Sigma) | Collect 10 pumpkins scattered through the hay around the house and deliver them to Jack | Pumpkin Grenades plus their crafting recipe, and Radioactive Pumpkin Seeds |

Both reset roughly every 24 hours from their fixed location.

## The Whitespring Refuge rotation

Four separate refuge dailies are handed out by NPCs at the **Whitespring Refuge**
(the refugee encampment tied to the Responders Reborn storyline and Expeditions: The
Pitt content, inside the broader Whitespring area). Unlike the two standalone dailies
above, these function as a small quest-board rotation from one hub rather than a
single fixed objective — and completing all of them in a day is what funds a
Vertibird trip for Expeditions.

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recipe for Success** | **Esme Rousseau**, Whitespring Refuge | Cook a specific stew (recipe varies — Raiders- or Settlers-flavored) using ingredients found around the resort grounds | Stimpaks, RadAway, purified water, a grenade, and soup |
| **Mutual Aid** | **Sophie Wagoner**, Whitespring Refuge | Deliver a stack of 50 of one requested crafting material — wood, steel, plastic, or cloth | Stimpaks, RadAway, purified water, and a grenade |
| **A Refugee's Guide** | **Skippy**, Whitespring Refuge (ask him for a camera to start) | Harvest a marked plant and photograph three notable NPCs (often vendor bots at specific train stations) for a wasteland survival guide | Stimpaks, RadAway, purified water, and grenades |
| **Code Blue** | **Rucker**, the Responders' leader, Whitespring Refuge | Track down a randomized terminal for medical-supply intel, retrieve the supplies it points to, then return them to the Responder Medic in the Refuge's clinic | Stimpaks, RadAway, purified water, and a Molotov cocktail |

## Cross-reference: the Syringer weapon itself

Someone to Talk To hands out **Syringer ammo**, not the base Syringer weapon plan —
don't confuse the two. See [pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/) for where
the craftable Syringer plan actually comes from (vendor bots and Mutation-event
drops); the similarly-named "Rose's Syringer" is a separate unique reward from Rose's
own questline (Signal Strength, Flavors of Mayhem, Keys to the Past), not from this
daily.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Whether all four Whitespring Refuge dailies are simultaneously available every day or
rotate through a smaller subset wasn't consistently confirmed across sources — treat
"four dailies" as the full roster you might see, not a guarantee all four show up on
any given day. Some older community write-ups list only three Refuge dailies (Recipe
for Success, Mutual Aid, Code Blue) and treat A Refugee's Guide as a related but
separately-introduced quest (tied to the Expeditions: The Pitt update specifically) —
worth keeping in mind if your Pip-Boy shows a different set than expected.
