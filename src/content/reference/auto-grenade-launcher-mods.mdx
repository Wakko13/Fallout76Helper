For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Auto Grenade
Launcher compares to its single-shot sibling the M79, see [Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/).

Both sources agree this weapon has just one mod slot — Barrel. Nukes & Dragons' database also
lists a Magazine slot, but only the Standard option appears in it with no alternate/upgrade row, so
there's no actual magazine mod to choose — the ammo-capacity gains on this weapon come from the
barrel mods below instead.

## Barrel mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Barrel | Standard | — | 1x Adhesive, 7x Screw, 11x Steel |
| Long Barrel | Range +84 (+50%), Weight +18%, Spread (hip-fire) +20%, Recoil +16%, AP Cost +20%, Sighted Transition +6% | Gunsmith 5 | 3x Adhesive, 7x Screw, 17x Steel |
| Heavy Barrel | Damage +25%, Weight +25%, Recoil +20%, AP Cost +10% | Gunsmith 5 | 1x Adhesive, 7x Screw, 11x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's scrape gave the Heavy Barrel the exact same effect text as the Long Barrel ("Range +84,
Accuracy +1") and the exact same materials as the Standard Barrel — both look like table-scraping
duplication rather than real data, so this page uses Nukes & Dragons' distinct percentage effect
(Damage +25%, Weight +25%, Recoil +20%, AP Cost +10%) for the Heavy Barrel instead, which reads as
far more plausible for a barrel named "Heavy." Its materials cell is still Fextralife's (duplicate)
figure carried over for lack of a better source — treat it as a placeholder, not a confirmed cost.
As with the rest of this series, verify exact numbers against a live wiki or in-game before farming
toward a specific barrel.
