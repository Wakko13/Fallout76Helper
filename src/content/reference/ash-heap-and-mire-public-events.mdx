Public events are the yellow-hexagon activities that pop up on the map and let any
nearby (or fast-traveling) player join, no group or signup required. This entry
covers the handful of named events tied specifically to the Ash Heap and The Mire.
For how event spawning/rotation works in general (the ~20-minute timer, mutated
event variants), see [event rotation and scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/)
rather than this page — this is a roster of what exists, not a live schedule.

| Event | Region / trigger location | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Battle Bots | Ash Heap — Mount Blair Trainyard | Fight a Hornwright security robot, then raid its bunker: loot Alpha and Beta keycards off two Mister Gutsies, use them on paired keypads within a tight window, then trigger the master shutdown | Chance at an Assaultron Head weapon, plus screws and general loot from the shutdown cabinet |
| Breach and Clear | Ash Heap — random location, usually near a Hornwright Testing Site | Clear mole miners and mole rats from a work site while "the Motherlode" (a large ore-harvesting machine) cracks open ore containers for looting | Random ore, a chance at the unique Meteoric Chinese Sword |
| Lode Baring | Ash Heap — Abandoned Mine Shaft Elaine or Abandoned Mine Site Kittery | Repair and defend three Auto-Miner robots against mole miners spawning roughly every 20 seconds, collecting Claim Tokens the Protectrons dispense every 40 seconds, then escape before the tunnel collapses | Claim Tokens (spendable on cosmetics/consumables), treasury notes, legendary cores — a minimum reward is paid even if you never enter the mine |
| Heart of the Swamp | The Mire — random location | Destroy a Strangler Heart that closes (becoming immune) and summons alternating waves of strangler mirelurks/feral ghouls whenever damaged; at 25% health it spawns a strangler mirelurk queen or strangler Grafton Monster that must also be killed | Rad-X and general aid/weapon/armor loot, with a shot at legendaries |
| Irrational Fear | The Mire — northeast of Raleigh Clay's Bunker | Protect the apiphobic NPC Beckham as he harvests honey from a series of beehives, fighting off waves of Honey Beasts and Yao Guai that spawn to defend the hives | A small chance at rare apparel (Tattered Field Jacket, Traveling Leather Coat, or Leather Coat) — shared across Mire events generally, not unique to this one |

## Notes on difficulty and level

Battle Bots and Breach and Clear are both commonly listed around a **level 25-29**
recommended range, consistent with Ash Heap being a mid-tier region. Heart of the
Swamp is listed around **level 30-34**, and Irrational Fear isn't formally
level-gated but, like most Mire events, is not considered low-level-friendly — the
Mire generally skews toward the harder end of Appalachia's regions (see
[regions at a glance](../regions-and-fast-travel/)). None of these are hard level
locks; Fallout 76's enemy scaling means a grouped low-level character can usually
survive and contribute.

Heart of the Swamp is a good example of the same "damage-phase / add-wave-phase"
structure used by several of the game's tougher public events (compare the
Strangler Heart's cycle to Eviction Notice's rad-scrubber defense, covered in
[notable bosses and encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/)) — treat it as a
sustained fight with an add-clearing rhythm, not a straight burn-it-down boss.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- Exact level recommendations above are pulled from community wikis, not an
  in-game display; Fallout 76 events don't hard-gate by level, so treat these as
  "typical difficulty" guidance rather than a requirement.
- Lode Baring's two listed spawn sites (Abandoned Mine Shaft Elaine, Abandoned Mine
  Site Kittery) and Battle Bots' single site (Mount Blair Trainyard) are the
  currently-documented trigger points; Bethesda has occasionally added or shifted
  event trigger locations in past updates, so a location could in principle change.
- Irrational Fear's exact spawn point and reward table are thinly documented
  compared to the other events here (community wiki pages for it have gaps) — the
  broad strokes (protect Beckham, Honey Beasts/Yao Guai waves) are solid, but finer
  details like exact enemy counts aren't independently confirmed here.
