Scorched are the irradiated human enemies created by the Scorched Plague — don't
confuse them with **Scorchbeasts**, the flying bat-like creatures that share the
name (those get their own entry via the Scorchbeast Queen boss fight, covered in
[Notable repeatable boss encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/)).
Regular Scorched are common across most of the map; the **glowing** variant hits
harder and radiates, and shows up mixed into the same packs at the farms below.

## Top two farms

- **Rust Kingdom** (Burning Springs, Ash Heap) — widely rated the single best
  Scorched farm in the game, with dense, close-together packs that can net 20+
  kills an hour running solo.
- **Morgantown Airport** (Forest region) — a former Responders base that the
  Scorched overran; around two dozen spawn inside the terminal building alone,
  with a smaller secondary cluster in the northwest area outside. It's also
  where the Collision Course public event takes place, which adds even more
  Scorched to clear on top of the ambient spawns.

## Other notable spots

**Camden Park**, the **Morgantown Trainyard**, and **Wavy Willard's Water Park**
all get called out as reliable secondary Scorched hotspots. **Athens** is another
dense cluster that pairs well if you're already running quests through that part
of the map. For a higher-level pack, the **Point Pleasant bridge** area (recommended
50+) is consistently Scorched-heavy.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Per-hour kill estimates ("20+ per hour") are farmer-reported, not official numbers,
and will vary with server population, your build, and whether you're fighting
solo or in a team splitting spawns. Some guides reference a "Scorched Matriarch"
loot table distinct from regular Scorched drops; that claim wasn't independently
confirmed here, so don't plan a farming route around it without checking current
patch notes first.
