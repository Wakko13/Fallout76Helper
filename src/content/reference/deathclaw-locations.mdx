This entry covers where regular wild Deathclaws spawn for general hunting and
Deathclaw Hide/meat farming. It's not the scripted Scorchbeast Queen or A
Colossal Problem fights — see
[Notable repeatable boss encounters](../notable-bosses-and-encounters/)
for those. Deathclaws are high-level, high-threat single spawns rather than
packs, so most of the value here is in guaranteed locations you can loop between.

## Guaranteed single spawns

- **Deathclaw Island** (The Forest, level ~21) — one Deathclaw, low-level enough
  to be a very early Deathclaw encounter.
- **Hopewell Cave** (Savage Divide, level ~91) — one Deathclaw.
- **Thunder Mountain Substation TM-02** (Cranberry Bog, level 41-91) — one
  Deathclaw.
- **Archery Set** (The Forest, level ~21) — one Deathclaw, near Deathclaw Island.

## Best farming spot

**Abandoned Waste Dump** (The Mire, level ~41) has **two** Deathclaws spawning
close enough together that you can find and fight both within seconds of each
other, but far enough apart to pull one at a time if you're playing it safe —
the best single stop for repeat Deathclaw kills.

## Chance spawns and named variants

**Tanagra Town** (The Mire) has roughly a 25% chance of a Deathclaw spawning.
**Dino Peaks Mini Golf** can have up to three Deathclaws reliably present. The
**Deathclaw Matriarch** — a tougher, named Deathclaw variant, distinct from the
scripted bosses covered elsewhere on this site — has a roughly 1-in-3 chance of
appearing as the final "Party Crasher" encounter after completing the
Distinguished Guests quest/event; treat her as a notable bonus spawn rather than
a location you can farm directly.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Level ranges listed above (21, 41, 91) reflect the level the encounter scales to
in different sources and may shift depending on your own character level and
whether the zone uses level-scaling. The Deathclaw Matriarch's exact spawn
trigger and odds are pulled from wiki/community write-ups rather than confirmed
patch notes, so don't rely on a single Distinguished Guests run to guarantee her
appearance.
