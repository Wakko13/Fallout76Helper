This site already catalogs Appalachia's public events (region by region, starting with
[The Forest](../forest-public-events/)) and explains their scheduling in
[how event scheduling works](../event-rotation-scheduling/). Daily and repeatable
quests are a **different content type** — actual entries in your Pip-Boy quest log,
tied to one fixed location, rather than a map-wide event notification anyone nearby
can drop into. This entry explains the mechanism; the regional catalogs (linked at
the bottom) list what's actually out there.

## Where these quests come from

There's no single delivery mechanism — Bethesda Support's own quest-types page notes
that at launch, quests were "largely received through holotapes, terminals, and notes,
although a few robot NPCs existed in the world which could also give quests," and that
pattern has held for the daily/repeatable roster specifically:

- **NPC dialogue** — a fixed human or robot NPC standing at one location: Sweetwater
  near The Giant Teapot (Strange Brew), the Huntmaster at Black Bear Lodge (Big Game
  Hunt), Jack O'Lantern at the Pumpkin House (Trick or Treat?).
- **Terminals** — reading or interacting with a terminal kicks the quest off or drives
  it forward, sometimes with no NPC at all: Ecological Balance's chain runs through
  Amy Kerry's terminal at Tygart Water Treatment, and Someone to Talk To starts at a
  basement terminal in Monongah with no living questgiver in sight.
- **Holotapes** — physical items you find and play, either to start a quest (Ecological
  Balance's "Message for Jeff" holotape) or as part of completing it (Someone to Talk
  To's Vox Interpreter Holotape, loaded into the Pip-Boy to translate animal noises).
- **Notes and posters** — a small number of quests are unlocked by reading a specific
  note or bulletin-board-style poster (train-station notice boards are the most common
  spot for these) rather than talking to anyone.

Most of these are single fixed-location quests. A few Whitespring-area dailies
(covered in [Savage Divide's catalog](../savage-divide-daily-quests/)) are handed out
by a small roster of NPCs standing in one hub instead, functioning more like a rotating
quest board.

## Daily vs. drop vs. plain repeatable

Community guides consistently split these into three buckets, and the distinction is
real, not just wiki nitpicking:

- **Daily quests** — the largest group. Once accepted and completed, the same quest
  becomes available again roughly **24 hours later**, always at the same fixed
  location. These show up under the "DAILY" heading in the Pip-Boy's quest log. Note
  that in current patches you typically have to manually check the box next to a
  listed Daily to make it active — it no longer auto-triggers just from walking into
  the area the way it originally did.
- **Drop quests** — triggered by looting a specific item (a key, a holotape, a set of
  orders) off an enemy corpse or out of a container, rather than by visiting a fixed
  spot. Bethesda Support's quest-types page and gameshedge both describe these as
  closer in spirit to a side quest than a scheduled daily: there's no 24-hour lockout,
  and finding the trigger item again can start a fresh copy right away.
- **Plain repeatables** — a smaller set with no cooldown restriction at all; you can
  chain them back-to-back as long as you're willing to redo the objective (Ash Heap's
  Earth Mover, restarting the Mount Blair excavator, is the usual example cited).

## How this differs from public events and Daily Ops

- **Public events** spawn automatically on the world map roughly every 20 minutes,
  visible to and joinable by any nearby player with no prior quest pickup required.
  See [how event scheduling works](../event-rotation-scheduling/) for that mechanism.
- **Daily Ops** are short instanced missions with their own separate availability
  schedule and a completion-time reward tier — also covered in
  [how event scheduling works](../event-rotation-scheduling/).
- **Daily/repeatable quests** (this entry's subject) are neither: they're solo-visible
  Pip-Boy quest-log entries tied to a specific NPC, terminal, or holotape at a fixed
  location, picked up deliberately rather than encountered passively.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact reset hour (one source claims a fixed 7 PM EST daily reset; others just say
"24 hours after completion") isn't consistently documented — treat it as "about a day,"
not a precise clock time, unless you're timing something exactly. A few quests sit
right on a regional border and different guides assign them to different regions —
see each regional catalog's own "Uncertain" section for the specific calls made there
(Heart of the Enemy, which spans Fort Defiance and Vault-Tec University, is the
clearest example). Whether "bulletin board" posters currently trigger any of the
quests covered in this batch specifically, versus just being one of the mechanisms
used elsewhere in the game, wasn't confirmed case-by-case.
