**This is a high-staleness topic, same caveat as** [event rotation and scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/)
**— specific challenge text and season themes rotate constantly. What follows is the underlying
mechanism, which stays stable across seasons, not a snapshot of the current season's challenge list.**

## How S.C.O.R.E. is earned

Each season runs its own set of **Daily Challenges** (6 available per day, 250 S.C.O.R.E. each — 7
for Fallout 1st subscribers) and **Weekly Challenges** that rotate on a longer cycle. Completing all
6 daily challenges unlocks a bonus **Gold Star Challenge** worth 1,000 S.C.O.R.E. on top of them.
Once you've hit a high enough rank (commonly cited around rank 100+), some challenges become
repeatable rather than one-and-done, giving a smaller but reusable S.C.O.R.E. source.

## What challenge objectives actually mean

Individual challenge text ("Kill a Cryptid," "Scrap junk to produce Antiseptic," "Drink Nuka-Cola
Quantum," "Claim a Workshop," "Visit another player's Shelter") isn't a new game system — it's
pointing at mechanics that already exist and are covered elsewhere on this site. If you're stuck on
a specific challenge, check whether it matches:

- **Kill [creature type]** — see the relevant entry under this site's `creatures` category (e.g.
  [cryptid locations](../cryptid-locations/), [insect swarm locations](../insect-swarm-locations/),
  [hostile human camp locations](../hostile-human-camp-locations/) for Blood Eagles/Cultists).
- **Scrap junk to produce [material]** — see the matching `*-farming` FAQ entry (e.g.
  [Antiseptic farming](../../faq/antiseptic-farming/), [Fiber-Optics farming](../../faq/fiber-optics-farming/)).
- **Drink/eat [consumable]** — see [Nuka-Cola mixing](../nuka-cola-mixing/),
  [farming and water basics](../farming-and-water-basics/), or
  [fermenting and alcohol](../fermenting-and-alcohol/) depending on the item.
- **Claim a Workshop** — see [workshop mechanics](../../faq/workshop-mechanics/).
- **Visit another player's Shelter** — see [CAMP Shelter basics](../../faq/camp-shelter-basics/).
- **Complete a public event / Daily Op** — see [event rotation and scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/).

If a challenge references something genuinely not covered anywhere on this site yet, that's worth
flagging so it can get added.

## Tickets and the Season Pass

As of the Season Pass system (introduced with the "America's Playground" update, replacing the
older direct Scoreboard-reward-per-rank model), ranking up from S.C.O.R.E. no longer hands you a
reward directly — instead, **each rank-up grants 25 Tickets**, a currency spent on **Season Pages**.
The season's rewards are spread across roughly 13 themed pages (10 rewards each, some marked
Fallout 1st-exclusive), plus two bonus pages unlocked after the main ones: one with higher-ticket-cost
premium items, and one with **repeatable consumable rewards** you can keep buying with excess
Tickets. Reaching rank 100 unlocks the ability to earn all base rewards; going further (historically
cited up to rank 150) lets a completionist collect literally everything a season offers.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact challenge lists, S.C.O.R.E. point values, and season length are set per-season and change
with every new season — nothing above should be read as "this season's challenges," only as how
the point-to-Ticket-to-reward pipeline works in general. The specific rank threshold where
challenges start repeating, and the exact page/reward counts, are consistently reported across
community sources but not something Bethesda publishes as a fixed spec — treat the numbers here as
"roughly this," not exact.
