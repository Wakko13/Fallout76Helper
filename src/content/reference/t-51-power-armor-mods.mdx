For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how T-51 stacks up against
the other five models and where to find one, see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/). For the
fullest treatment of the shared torso/leg/arm/helmet functional-mod pool that T-51 draws from,
including confirmed per-mod material costs and the perk-requirement caveats, see
[T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) — every mod name below matched T-45's list
exactly across the sources checked for this file.

As with every power armor set, there is **no Sturdy/Hardened-style resistance-tier ladder** — the
"material tier" concept from regular armor mod files doesn't apply here; T-51's paint options
(below) are the closest equivalent, and most are cosmetic-only or grant SPECIAL points rather than
resistance.

## Torso mods (incl. Jet Pack)

Confirmed present via both Fextralife's T-51 page and Nukes & Dragons' T-51b torso listing — same
ten mods as T-45, same effects. Materials were not independently re-checked per mod for T-51; T-45's
confirmed costs (Motion-Assist Servos, Kinetic Dynamo, Reactive Plates, Jet Pack) are a reasonable
guide to rough material categories but T-51 sits one tier above T-45 so exact quantities likely
scale up, similar to how T-60's confirmed Jet Pack cost (6/9/8/10) scales above T-45's (4/6/5/7).

## Paint / material tiers

T-51's paint list, as returned by Fextralife's T-51 page, is notably shorter than T-45's or T-60's —
only three named options turned up, versus roughly a dozen for the other two sets. This may reflect
an incomplete fetch rather than T-51 genuinely having fewer paints; see Uncertain.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vim! Paint | Cosmetic (Vim!-branded) | Not documented | Reported: 2x Oil, 2x Plastic, 2x Steel |
| Military Paint | Strength +1 per piece, full-set bonus (per T-45/T-60 pattern) | Not documented | Reported: 2x Oil, 2x Plastic, 2x Steel |
| Winterized Coating | Increases Energy Resistance | Not documented | Reported: 2x Oil, 2x Plastic, 2x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **The three paint-mod material costs above (all identically "2x Oil, 2x Plastic, 2x Steel") are
  suspicious** — that's an unusually flat, round number for three mods with different effects, and
  it reads like an AI-summarized search result rather than a verified wiki table. Confirm at a Power
  Armor Station before relying on it; it's flagged rather than dropped because it's at least a
  plausible ballpark (paint mods are generally cheap and low-tier compared to functional mods).
- **T-51's paint list is much shorter than T-45's/T-60's in what was fetched** (3 named paints vs.
  roughly a dozen) — likely an artifact of what the fetch tool surfaced rather than T-51 actually
  having fewer cosmetic options; T-51 almost certainly also supports the Hot Rod/Minutemen/Vault-Tec/
  Titanium/Lead/Prism paint families seen on T-45 and Ultracite, this just wasn't confirmed directly.
- **No per-mod material costs were independently confirmed for T-51's torso, arm, or most leg mods**
  — T-51's own Fextralife per-mod pages (e.g. "T-51 Jet Pack") 404'd on the URL patterns that worked
  for T-45/T-60/Excavator/Ultracite, so this file leans on the confirmed T-45 numbers as a same-
  family reference rather than T-51-specific data. Flagged rather than guessed at.
- **Perk requirements are entirely undocumented for T-51** specifically, same gap as T-45 — see that
  file's Uncertain section for the aggregated (unverified) Science!/Armorer rank claims that may
  apply here too by extension.
- **Base T-51 torso stats** (context, not a mod): 482 Damage Resistance / 460 Energy Resistance / 380
  Radiation Resistance at level 50, plus a listed +180 Carry Capacity figure that reads like it may
  already include a Calibrated Shocks leg mod rather than being a base-piece stat — per Nukes &
  Dragons; worth sanity-checking in-game rather than taking at face value.
