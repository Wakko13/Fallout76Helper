For how mod slots work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Diver suit's
effects compare to the Cave diving suit (same category, lower Radiation Resistance), see
[Functional attire](../functional-attire/).

## No mod slots

The Diver suit belongs to the same **body suit** grouping as the Hazmat Suit, Chinese stealth
armor, and Cave diving suit — a single item occupying all armor-related slots, described alongside
those other suits as unable to take legendary effects. No mod table for it turned up on any source
checked this session, consistent with that classification. Its fixed effects (underwater
breathing, water radiation/disease immunity, airborne hazard protection, 250 Radiation Resistance)
are the entire package — there's no lining or material tier here the way there is for the Vault
jumpsuits or Marine Wetsuit.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

One search result claimed the Diver suit has "5 modification slots," but this reads as a garbled
reference to the **5 armor-coverage slots** (head/torso/arms/legs) that a single body suit fills
simultaneously — the same "takes all armor, underarmor, and overall slots" phrasing search results
used for the Hazmat Suit and Cave diving suit — rather than 5 actual craftable mod slots. No wiki
mod table backs that claim up, it isn't consistent with how every other body suit in this wave is
documented, and it wasn't treated as reliable for this page. If you want to rule this out
definitively, checking the Diver suit at an Armor Workbench in-game is the fastest way to settle
it. The suit's exact Radiation Resistance figure (250, per the existing
[Functional attire](../functional-attire/) entry) is also wiki-sourced and worth a sanity check.
