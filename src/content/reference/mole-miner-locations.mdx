Mole miners are former coal-mining robots'-turned-raiders (irradiated humans in
mining gear, not robots) that overwhelmingly spawn underground or around mine
entrances in the **Ash Heap** and southern **Savage Divide** — you won't find
them scattered generally across the map the way you will feral ghouls or
Scorched.

## Best single-spot farms

- **Welch** (Ash Heap) — regularly cited as the single best mole miner location
  in the game, with all ten of the game's mole miner variant types reportedly
  able to spawn in this one area, including 1-3 **Treasure Hunter** mole miners
  (the variant most worth farming for their loot).
- **Kerwood Mine** (Ash Heap) — a dark, mole-miner-infested shaft with roughly 17
  mole miners spawning inside and immediately outside the entrance.

## Other named spots

**Big Bend Tunnel** (also a super mutant site), **Blackwater Mine**, **Brim
Quarry**, **Hornwright Industrial Headquarters** and the nearby **Hornwright
Testing Sites**, **Monongah** and **Monongah Power Plant**, **Mount Blair** and
**Mount Blair Trainyard**, **Pleasant Valley Ski Resort**, **South Cutthroat
Camp**, **The Burning Mine**, and **The Rusty Pick** all have documented mole
miner spawns and are worth working into a farming loop around the Ash Heap.

## Notable variants

Beyond the base mole miner, look out for **Foreman**, **Supervisor**, **Sapper**,
**Dredger**, and **Skullcrusher** mole miners — tougher named-role variants that
mix into the same spawn pools — plus the **Treasure Hunter** variant mentioned
above, which is the one most worth prioritizing if you're farming for loot rather
than pure XP.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

None of the sources here give a precise total spawn count for the secondary
locations (only Welch and Kerwood Mine have solid numbers attached), so treat the
"other named spots" list as confirmed spawn points rather than ranked by density.
Mole miner variant names and exact roles have shifted slightly across patches
historically — double check current tooltips in-game if you're hunting a specific
variant for a challenge.
