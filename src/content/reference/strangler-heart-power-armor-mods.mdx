For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how Strangler Heart stacks
up against the other five endgame models and how to buy its plans (Vault 94 raid drop, or Regs for
Gold Bullion), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).

Strangler Heart is built on the same chassis as Ultracite power armor ("a set of Ultracite power
armor that has been completely overrun by strangler vines"), and shares Ultracite's mods. It has
the same four-slot functional-mod pool (torso/legs/arms/helmet) documented in
[T-45's mod file](../t-45-power-armor-mods/), **plus one Ultracite-line-exclusive torso mod
(Welded Rebar)** not available on T-45/X-01/Union/T-65/Hellcat/Vulcan, and an inherent acid-damage
set bonus.

## Set bonus

Wearing all six Strangler Heart pieces grants the **Strangler heart set bonus**: adds acid damage
to all armed (melee weapon) attacks (10 acid damage over 3 seconds, does not stack from repeated
hits), and makes nearby enemies take ambient acid damage (4 per second) just from being close to
the wearer. This is an inherent trait of the pieces themselves, not a craftable mod.

source checked.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Welded Rebar is shared with Ultracite power armor, not truly "Strangler Heart-exclusive"** —
  the plan is labeled "Plan: Ultracite welded rebar" and a source explicitly states "Strangler
  Heart and Ultracite share the same mods." Framed here as a torso-mod option available to
  Strangler Heart, but it's really an Ultracite-lineage mod that both Ultracite and its
  Strangler-Heart variant can use — not confirmed exclusive to just these two among the six sets in
  this wave.
- **Whether Welded Rebar and Reactive Plates can both be equipped at once, or are mutually
  exclusive torso-slot alternatives (like the rest of the torso pool), was not directly confirmed**
  — treated here as an alternate torso-slot pick consistent with "only one mod per slot" from the
  T-45 file's established mechanics, but worth an in-game double-check.
- **Strangler Heart's arm-mod page (Nukes & Dragons) only listed 3 of the expected 4 arm mods**
  (Hydraulic Bracers, Rusty Knuckles, Optimized Bracers — Tesla Bracers was missing from that
  fetch). Tesla Bracers is included above on the assumption it's simply present everywhere else in
  the shared pool and the omission was a scrape/rendering gap, not a real absence — not directly
  confirmed.
- **Rad Scrubbers' absence from Strangler Heart's helmet pool is inferred from omission**, not a
  direct "this mod doesn't exist" statement — a broader search confirmed Rad Scrubbers is
  compatible with Excavator, Raider, T-45, T-51b, T-60, Ultracite, and X-01. Strangler Heart's
  relationship to "Ultracite" here is ambiguous — if Strangler Heart truly shares Ultracite's full
  mod pool (as stated for Welded Rebar), it may also have Rad Scrubbers despite not being separately
  listed; unresolved.
- **Strangler Heart torso base stats** (for context, not a mod): 418 Damage Resistance / 418
  Energy Resistance / 767 Radiation Resistance at level 50 per Nukes & Dragons — the highest
  radiation figure of any set in this wave, consistent with Strangler Heart's reputation as the
  best radiation-resist power armor in the game.
- **Materials for every base-pool mod on this page were not found on any reachable source** — only
  Welded Rebar's materials were confirmed, via a dedicated Fallout Fandom search rather than a
  Fextralife/Nukes & Dragons fetch.
