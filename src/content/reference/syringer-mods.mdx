For general modding mechanics (Weapons Workbench, Gunsmith perk, mod plans), see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Syringer sits among
the other sidearms, see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/).

The Syringer doesn't use the usual receiver/barrel/grip/magazine/sights loadout that most guns do. Instead,
its main "mod" system is the **syringe barrel** — swapping which dart type the gun fires — plus a single sight
upgrade. Most dart types are actually learned by scrapping found syringer ammo rather than bought as full mod
plans (see [Pistols and sidearms](../pistols-and-sidearms/) for how the special Karma dart is unlocked via the
Flavors of Mayhem quest), but each barrel is still built at a Weapons Workbench with the materials below once
known.

## Syringe barrel (dart type)

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bloatfly Larva Syringe Barrel | Chance on death for the target to spawn a bloatfly | Not specified on source | 2x Adhesive, 1x Black Titanium, 10x Bloatfly Gland, 3x Screw, 7x Steel |
| Bleedout Syringe Barrel | Does 30 points of damage over 10 seconds to target | Not specified on source | 2x Adhesive, 1x Black Titanium, 3x Screw, 7x Steel |
| Endangerol Syringe Barrel | Reduces target's damage resistance by 25% for 2 minutes | Not specified on source | 2x Adhesive, 1x Black Titanium, 3x Screw, 7x Steel |

## Sights

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glow Sights | Improved focus and better sighted accuracy | Not specified on source | 1x Adhesive, 1x Nuclear Material, 3x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The Fextralife mod table for the Syringer only lists three syringe-barrel darts (Bloatfly Larva, Bleedout,
Endangerol) plus Glow Sights — it does not list perk requirements for any of them, and it does not cover the
other dart types referenced elsewhere on this site (Health, Cloaker, and the quest-locked Karma dart), which
are apparently obtained/learned outside the standard mod-plan system rather than crafted as a barrel swap with
listed materials. Treat this page as covering only the mods Fextralife documents with real material costs —
if you're chasing a specific dart type not listed here, check a current wiki page directly. As with all mod
data, exact material costs can shift with balance patches.
