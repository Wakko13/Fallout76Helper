For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For the Tesla Rifle's
chain-lightning damage mechanic, its fusion-cell ammo, and how it compares to the rest of the
energy-weapon family, see [Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/).

**A source note up front:** Fextralife files the weapon's main firing-mode mods (semi-auto/automatic/
charging-shotgun) under a slot it calls **Receiver**; Nukes & Dragons documents largely the same
options — plus two more (Lobber Barrel, plain Charging Barrel) — under a slot it calls **Barrel**.
They're the same physical slot on the weapon under two different source names. The table below keeps
Fextralife's "Receiver/Barrel" label and merges both option lists; rows with materials came from
Fextralife, the two Nukes & Dragons-only additions are marked "Not documented."

## Receiver/Barrel mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semi-auto Barrel | Standard | — | 1x Adhesive, 1x Circuitry, 1x Crystal, 2x Nuclear Material, 4x Plastic, 2x Screw |
| Automatic Receiver | Improved rate of fire; inferior range, damage. Damage -15, Fire Rate +71, Range -24, Weight -0.8 | Not documented | 1x Adhesive, 1x Circuitry, 2x Crystal, 5x Fiber Optics, 2x Gear, 5x Plastic, 3x Screw |
| Charging Shotgun Barrel | Hold fire to charge, release shotgun blast. Damage +30, Fire Rate -2, Range -24, Weight -2.4 | Not documented | 1x Adhesive, 2x Circuitry, 2x Crystal, 8x Fiber Optics, 7x Plastic, 4x Screw, 1x Silver |
| Lobber Barrel | Speed -50%, Weight +35%, Spread (hip-fire) +200%, Recoil +150% | Not documented | Not documented |
| Charging Barrel | Hold fire to charge | Not documented | Not documented |
| Automatic Barrel | Spread (hip-fire) +35%, Recoil +35%, Range -17%, Ammo Capacity +50%, Damage -30%, Weight +8%, Reload Speed -10%, Energy/Cryo/Fire/Poison/Radiation Damage -30% each, Critical Damage Multiplier -20% | Not documented | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's page noted "two additional incomplete mod entries with no detailed information provided"
beyond what's listed above, so the Receiver/Barrel lineup here may not be fully exhaustive — the
Lobber Barrel and plain Charging Barrel from Nukes & Dragons could be part of those missing entries,
or could be separate options Fextralife didn't surface at all. No perk requirements were confirmed for
any individual mod on either source; expect the weapon's own Science! gate (noted on the
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/) overview for modding the base weapon) to extend to at least some
of these, but verify per-mod gates in-game. Materials for the three Nukes & Dragons-only additions
weren't found anywhere reachable this session.
