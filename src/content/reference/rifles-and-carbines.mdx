guns rather than here.

## The rifles

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Combat Rifle** | 5.56mm | Plan is a guaranteed reward from **The Motherlode** quest (Savage Divide); not sold by any vendor; low chance from Daily Ops (level 20-49); scrapping a found one can also teach it | Versatile mid-tier rifle whose receiver mod lets you swap between semi-auto and full-auto — a flexible bridge between Rifleman and Commando builds |
| **Hunting Rifle** | .308 round | Plan is a guaranteed reward from **Hunter for Hire**; also widely sold at vendors; very low chance from Forest daily/repeatable quests | Classic hard-hitting non-automatic rifle, cheap ammo, a strong low-investment sniper/Rifleman base |
| **Handmade Rifle** | 5.56mm | Plan sold by several vendor bots (Free States vendor at Whitespring Resort, Grahm the traveling vendor, Whitespring mall vendor, Wallace at Berkeley Springs Station, Watoga vendor bot) at level 15+; also low chance Daily Ops (15-49), 10% from Patrol Duty, Toxic Valley world spawns | Raider-styled automatic rifle and one of the most popular Commando-build mainstays — strong sustained DPS, ammo-hungry |
| **Assault Rifle** | 5.56mm | Not sold by any vendor — random reward from various Mire/Cranberry Bog quests and events (One of Us, Heart of the Swamp, Always Vigilant, Irrational Fear, It's a Trap, Queen of the Hunt, Idle Explosives, Waste Not, Play Time); scrapping a found copy can also teach it | Military-pattern automatic rifle, similar niche to the Handmade Rifle with a different mod pool/receiver options |
| **Lever Action Rifle** | .45-70 round | Plan sold at Grafton, Welch, and Lewisburg Station vendor bots at level 25+; farmable off enemies/containers in Savage Divide, notably the Deathclaw cave at Abandoned Waste Dump; low chance from Personal Matters and Daily Ops (25-49) | Non-automatic western-style rifle with big per-shot damage — a VATS-crit-friendly Rifleman pick |
| **Radium Rifle** | Radium round | Random reward from several Ash Heap/Mire/Cranberry Bog quests and events (Census Violence, Monster Mash, Line in the Sand, Distant Thunder, Surface to Air, AWOL Armaments, Novice of Mysteries, and various Wastelanders quests); vendor purchase at level 40+; low chance Daily Ops (40-49) | Fires irradiated rounds that deal bonus radiation damage on top of ballistic damage — especially effective against human enemies (very rad-susceptible) and other players, less so against the many creatures with radiation resistance |
| **Black Powder Rifle** | Black powder | Plan only sold by Flintlock at The Whitespring Resort, level 15+; the weapon itself can be found as display-case loot at Philippi Battlefield Cemetery's museum and a cabin at Prickett's Fort | Slow single-shot, big per-hit damage, tied to the Free States questline; a flavor/collector pick rather than a meta one |
| **Crossbow** | Bolts (craftable from cheap materials) | Plan guaranteed to spawn on a table at the Pioneer Scout camp archery range in Toxic Valley; also sold by vendors at level 15+, low chance Daily Ops (15-49), 10% from Patrol Duty | Not a firearm — silent, no gunpowder ammo, and bolts are cheap to craft yourself; a natural pick for stealth/sneak-attack builds |
| **Railway Rifle** | Railway spikes | Plan sold by vendor bots (Berkeley Springs Station, Harper's Ferry, Watoga Shopping Plaza, Whitespring Resort) at level 30+; low chance Daily Ops (30-49); world spawns and creature drops across Savage Divide, Ash Heap, The Mire, and Cranberry Bog | A full two-handed rifle despite its spike-firing gimmick — spikes can pin low-health enemies in place, giving it a strong crowd-control/limb-damage niche distinct from raw DPS rifles |

## Unique and specialty rifles

Two more long guns exist as their own distinct weapons rather than variants of the ones above:

| Weapon | Ammo | How to acquire | Role / niche |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Dragon** | Black powder | An old-style muzzle-loading rifle that fires all four barrels at once per shot before a full reload; reported as a rare spawn from Free States vendors and treasure-map-marked locations | One of the hardest-hitting single shots in the game, offset by the slow reload — a burst-alpha-damage weapon rather than a sustained one |
| **Marksman's Hardened Sniper Rifle** | 5.56mm | A unique Combat Rifle variant; specific current in-game acquisition wasn't pinned down with confidence during this research — verify against a live wiki before planning a run around it | Sniper-focused unique version of the Combat Rifle line |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact vendor stock, quest/event reward tables, and drop-rate percentages shift with balance patches and aren't
officially documented — treat the acquisition routes above as "where to start looking" rather than guarantees,
and check a current wiki page if a specific plan is proving hard to find. The Radium Rifle's radiation-damage
interaction with resistant creatures versus human/PvP targets is consistently reported in community sources but
worth re-verifying if you're building specifically around it. Marksman's Hardened Sniper Rifle's acquisition
method is the weakest-sourced entry on this page — the weapon is real, but this research couldn't confidently
pin down how you actually get one right now.
