For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how T-45 stacks up against
the other five models and where to find one, see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).

Power armor mods work differently from regular armor mods covered elsewhere in this initiative:
there is **no Sturdy/Hardened-style resistance-tier ladder** — a power armor piece's damage/energy/
radiation resistance comes from the piece itself (and its crafted level, if the model has one) plus
a full plate of standalone **functional mods** that don't exist on cloth/leather/combat armor at all.
Each of the four moddable slots (torso, arms, legs, helmet) has its own distinct mod pool, and only
one mod per slot can be active at a time. **The Jet Pack is a torso mod, not a leg mod** — it's easy
to assume otherwise since it affects movement, but every source checked places it in the torso mod
list alongside Motion-Assist Servos and Emergency Protocols.

All mods below require a **Power Armor Station** (not a regular armor workbench) and the piece
attached to a frame while crafting. Every per-mod Fextralife page checked gave only the generic
"Special Perks like Armorer are required to improve these Power Armor" boilerplate rather than a
specific rank — see Uncertain section for what a broader search suggested instead.

## Paint / material tiers

grant a flat SPECIAL bonus **when all six pieces share the same paint**, and a separate handful of
"protective coating" paints add real resistance. Materials weren't found for any of these.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Perk requirements are the single biggest gap in this file.** Every individual Fextralife mod
  page returns the same generic "Special Perks like Armorer are required to improve these Power
  Armor" boilerplate rather than a specific rank. A broader web search surfaced more specific (but
  unverified against a primary table) claims: Motion-Assist Servos/Tesla Coils need **Science! rank
  3**; Kinetic Dynamo/Medic Pump/Emergency Protocols need **Science! rank 4**; Reactive Plates needs
  **Armorer rank 4**; Jet Pack needs **Science! rank 4 or Armorer rank 4**. Treat these as leads to
  confirm in-game, not settled facts — they weren't cross-checked against a second independent
  source.
- **Calibrated Shocks materials directly conflict between two sources**: the per-mod Fextralife page
  fetched for this file gives 2x Adhesive/5x Aluminum/4x Fiberglass (no flux), while a separate
  aggregated search result claimed 25x Aluminum/25x Fiberglass/25x Adhesive/5x Pure Violet Flux for
  T-45 specifically. These numbers are far enough apart that one is likely wrong (possibly the
  aggregated source conflated T-45 with a higher-tier set, or vice versa) — verify at a Power Armor
  Station before budgeting materials.
- **Base T-45 torso stats** (for context, not a mod): 482 Damage Resistance / 362 Energy Resistance
  / 313 Radiation Resistance at level 50, per Nukes & Dragons.
- **Materials for most torso/leg/arm/helmet mods beyond the ones explicitly confirmed above were not
  found on any reachable source** — Fextralife's per-mod pages exist for some but not all entries
  (several 404'd on direct URL guesses), and fallout.wiki/fallout.fandom.com pages that likely have
  fuller tables returned 403 on every fetch attempt this session.
- **No damage/energy/radiation-resistance "material tier" mod exists for power armor** — this was
  confirmed by an explicit web-search summary stating power armor pieces already carry high base
  resistance and don't get a Buttressed/Hardened-style ladder. Worth stating clearly since it's the
  single biggest structural difference from every other mods file in this initiative.
