## No mod slots

The Cryo Grenade genuinely has **no weapon mods** — Fextralife lists it under "Cryogenic Grenade" and
leaves the mod section unfilled/not applicable, and nukesdragons' interactive weapon database shows no
mod configuration for it either. It's crafted at a **Tinker's Workbench**, requiring the Demolition
Expert 3 and Science 1 perks, from the Plan: Cryo Grenade — there's no separate Weapons-Workbench mod
tree layered on top. What you build is a fixed 100 ballistic damage explosive with a slow/freeze
crowd-control effect and no attachment slots.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- **Demolition Expert** still scales its damage as a perk-card multiplier — see
  [Grenades, mines, and thrown explosives](../explosives-and-thrown/) for the current rank breakdown.
  That's independent of the (absent) weapon-mod system.
- Legendary Cryo Grenades exist as a separate legendary-star system, unrelated to weapon mods.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's Cryogenic Grenade page has several other placeholder fields (exact crafting materials
beyond the perk gate, drop locations) beyond mods, so this is a thin-documentation source rather than
one that explicitly rules mods out with a citation. The consistent absence across both Fextralife and
nukesdragons, and the same pattern holding for every other grenade/mine checked in this wave, makes
"no mods" the safe read — but a quick in-game Weapons Workbench check would remove any remaining doubt.
