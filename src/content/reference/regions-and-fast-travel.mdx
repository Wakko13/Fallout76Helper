## The regions

Appalachia is divided into distinct climate/level zones. Newer content (Skyline
Valley, Burning Springs) has added areas since launch, but the six original regions
plus Whitespring remain the core map most guides and quest lines orient around.

| Region | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| The Forest | Starting region — Vault 76's exit point, Flatwoods, low-level questing and tutorial content |
| Toxic Valley | North of the Forest; irradiated terrain, Blackwater Mine, Watoga suburb ruins, mid-low level enemies |
| Savage Divide | The mountainous spine running the length of the map, dividing the low-level north from the harder south/east; home to the Wayward, Thunder Mountain, and much of the Scorched/Scorchbeast activity |
| Ash Heap | Former coal-mining and industrial territory around Mount Blair, permanently soot-covered; Blackwater Mine's neighbor region, Grafton, raider and Ash Heap-specific enemy types |
| The Mire | Swampy eastern region full of strangler vines, bogs, and Mirelurks; generally considered one of the more hazardous mid-to-late regions |
| Cranberry Bog | Southeastern endgame territory centered on the rebuilt Watoga; high-level enemies, dense with harder public events |
| Whitespring / Southern Appalachia | The Whitespring Resort, golf club, and bunker — a relatively intact, heavily guarded pre-war luxury resort area that doubles as a hub for vendors, the Overseer's questline, and (depending on faction state) Enclave content |

Each region roughly scales in recommended level from the Forest (lowest) outward
toward Cranberry Bog and the Mire (highest), though scaling content means low-level
characters can survive most areas in a group.

## Fast travel pricing, briefly

Fast travel almost always costs caps, scaling with distance (and, by most player
reports, character level) — see the dedicated FAQ entry for the full cost breakdown,
the complete list of always-free destinations, and tips for minimizing spend.
