## No mod slots

Explosive Bait genuinely has **no weapon mods** — Fextralife's entry lists the mod section as
unfilled/not applicable, and it isn't a moddable-weapon-database entry on nukesdragons either. It's
crafted at a **Tinker's Workbench**, with the recipe unlocked automatically during the "Flavors of
Mayhem" quest rather than found as a separate plan — there's no Weapons-Workbench mod tree involved
at all. What you build is a fixed 200 ballistic damage lure/mine hybrid with no attachment slots; its
niche is baiting and trapping specific creatures (notably usable against Fasnacht event robots) rather
than raw direct damage, and unlike other mines you can have more than two placed at once.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- **Demolition Expert** still scales explosive damage generally as a perk-card multiplier, though
  Explosive Bait's role as a lure/trap tool means damage scaling matters less here than for the other
  items in this family — see [Grenades, mines, and thrown explosives](../explosives-and-thrown/).
- Because it comes from a quest-unlocked recipe rather than a droppable/craftable legendary plan,
  legendary variants aren't a relevant consideration the way they are for the other grenades and
  mines in this wave.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's Explosive Bait page has several other placeholder fields (perk-to-improve, exact
crafting-material list beyond "quest gives the recipe") beyond mods, so this is a thin-documentation
source rather than one that explicitly rules mods out with a citation. The consistent absence lines up
with every other grenade/mine checked in this wave, and with its identity as a lure/trap item rather
than a combat-focused platform, so "no mods" is the safe read — but a quick in-game Weapons Workbench
check would remove any remaining doubt.
