For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For the Gauss Shotgun's
charge-and-release mechanic, ammo, and how it compares to the rest of the energy-weapon family, see
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/).

**A naming correction worth leading with:** unlike the [Gauss Rifle](../gauss-rifle-mods/), whose
charge-boosting slot is called **Capacitor**, the Gauss Shotgun's equivalent slot is called
**Receiver** — confirmed by the actual plan name, "Plan: Prime Gauss shotgun receiver" (bought from
Samuel at Foundation for 200 Gold Bullion at max Settlers "Ally" reputation), not "Prime Gauss shotgun
capacitor." Don't assume the two Gauss long guns share a slot name; they don't.

Fextralife's dedicated Gauss Shotgun page returned a 404 on repeated attempts this session, so the
table below comes from Nukes & Dragons' game-data-driven database, which — as with the Gauss Rifle
page in this series — uses **percentage-based** effect values and does not publish crafting materials
or perk gates for any row. Individual mod plans (e.g. Forceful Stock) are purchased piecemeal from
Samuel at Foundation for Gold Bullion, per the mod plan pages checked, rather than all unlocking at
once with the base weapon plan.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's own Gauss Shotgun page wasn't reachable this session (404 on the direct URL and on a
re-encoded variant), so this table leans entirely on Nukes & Dragons, which doesn't publish crafting
materials or most perk requirements — those cells are honestly marked "Not documented" rather than
guessed. The Prime Receiver's Gold Bullion price and vendor (Samuel, 200 Gold Bullion) is corroborated
by a dedicated Fandom plan page, and the Forceful Stock's vendor is corroborated similarly, but the
remaining individual mods' Gold Bullion costs and any perk gates (Gunsmith rank, Science!) weren't
confirmed anywhere reachable this session — verify against a live wiki before farming Gold Bullion for
a specific mod.
