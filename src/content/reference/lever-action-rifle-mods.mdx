## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife did not list a Magazine category for the Lever Action Rifle at all — this matches its
in-game design, since it's a tube-fed lever gun rather than a detachable-magazine weapon, so this is
likely a genuine absence rather than a data gap. The two Reflex Sight rows came back with no materials
listed (blank cell on the source table) despite having effect and accuracy values, and Glow Sights'
perk requirement wasn't documented either. No Muzzle Brake option was listed for this weapon (unlike
the Combat Rifle and Assault Rifle), which may be a real gap in the Lever Action's mod pool rather than
a missing row. As always, exact numeric stat values and material counts shift with balance patches, so
treat the above as a strong starting point rather than a guaranteed-current recipe.
