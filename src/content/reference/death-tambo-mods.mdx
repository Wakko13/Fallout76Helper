For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For the Death Tambo's
acquisition details among the unique/named melee weapons, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

## No mod slots

Fextralife's mod section for the Death Tambo reads simply **"None"** — confirmed on two separate
fetches of the page. There's no Blade, Head, or Grip slot to attach anything to at a Weapons
Workbench for this weapon; what you loot or get from Rose's questline is what you swing. That fits
its flavor as a novelty "musical weapon" alongside the game's other Sons of Dane-adjacent instrument
weapons, rather than a min-max combat pick — see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/) for its
acquisition path.

It can still take a **legendary effect** (the separate legendary-star system that applies to almost
all weapons regardless of mod slots) if you find or craft a legendary copy — that's independent of
the weapon-mod system this page is about.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

A secondary source (nukesdragons.com) briefly surfaced two named mods, "Sharp" and "Barbed," for
the Death Tambo when queried — but Fextralife's own page states "None" for mods in plain text on
two separate checks, and Sharp/Barbed are common mod names shared by several other blunt one-handed
weapons in this wave (Baseball Bat, Board, Knuckles), which makes cross-contaminated/mis-scraped
data from a nearby weapon's table a more likely explanation than a genuinely undocumented mod slot.
Treat "no mods" as the primary reading, but it's worth a quick in-game check if you specifically
care about maximizing this weapon.
