Both of these are swamp/river ambush predators concentrated almost entirely in and
around the Mire's wetlands — if you're hunting either one, that region is where to
start.

## Anglers

- **Gnarled Shallows** — a swampy area east of Abbie's Bunker at the northern tip of
  the Mire (head northeast of the bunker and follow the river north to a large pond).
  3 Anglers guaranteed here.
- Just north of **Gnarled Shallows** — a second guaranteed pocket of 3 more.
- **Thunder Mountain Substation TM-01** — another guaranteed spawn of 3.
- The **Moonshine Jamboree** public event at Sunday Brothers' Cabin (just south of
  Substation TM-01) also spawns Anglers as part of its defense waves, alongside
  Gulpers and Feral Ghouls.
- Chance spawns at **Braxson's Medical Supplies** parking lot and **Pylon V-13**.

## Gulpers

- **Gulper Lagoon** — 4 Gulpers guaranteed, the single best dedicated stop.
- Northwest of **Dyer Chemical** — 2 more guaranteed.
- Chance-based spawns: roughly 33% at **Treetops**, 25% at **Pylon V-13**, 20% at
  **Dolly Sods Campground**, and 10% at **Beckwith Farm**.
- Found generally throughout the **Mire** and **Cranberry Bog**, and occasionally show
  up as random encounters fighting Mirelurks, Scorched, or Super Mutants.
- Other reported spots include Big Maw, Dabney Homestead, and Treehouse Village.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The specific guaranteed headcounts (4 at Gulper Lagoon, 3x3 for Anglers) and the
percentage chance spawns are pulled from community wiki tallies rather than official
data, and both creature types occasionally show up as unscripted random encounters
outside these named spots. Treat the percentages as rough odds, not exact numbers.
