**This is a starting map, not a full boss catalog.** Fallout 76 has grown a lot of
repeatable boss content across events, Daily Ops, expeditions, and raids since
launch — this entry covers the handful that come up constantly in general play.
Expanding it to a fuller catalog (Daily Ops bosses, raid-specific encounters, etc.)
is a future addition.

## Scorchbeast Queen (Earle Williams' original event, "Queen")

Appalachia's signature server-wide endgame boss — a 30-minute timed fight recommended
for 8+ players. She's shrouded in a radiation-damage cloak throughout, alternates
between a long-range sonic attack and a poison-fog strafing run while airborne, and
must be forced to land by crippling a wing before melee/close-range damage becomes
practical.

**Strategy tip:** prioritize headshots (150% damage multiplier) and use VATS —
cracking a wing to ground her is usually the actual turning point of the fight, not
raw sustained damage.

## A Colossal Problem (Earle Williams as the Wendigo Colossus)

A public event where a transformed Earle Williams fights as a wendigo colossus while
continuously summoning wave after wave of regular Wendigos.

**Strategy tip:** he applies a stacking debuff that weakens him if 4+ different
players land hits within its ~2-minute duration window — spread damage across the
group rather than having one player tunnel him, and watch for falling embers and
leaking acid barrels as environmental hazards on top of the adds.

## Eviction Notice

A level-50-recommended public event (can roll as a mutated event) where you destroy
six meat bags and then defend a rad scrubber against escalating waves of super
mutants, including Suiciders and a Firestarter-type heavy.

**Strategy tip:** treat it as a defense event, not a clear-and-move fight — stay near
the rad scrubber and prioritize Suiciders/Firestarters on sight, since letting them
reach the scrubber unchecked is what usually causes a failed run.

## Expedition bosses (The Pitt / Atlantic City)

Expeditions are repeatable 10-20 minute instanced missions outside Appalachia. Their
notable fixed boss encounters include:

- **The Pitt** — a Cathedral fanatic boss fight where the boss periodically frees
  half-health fanatic adds that must be cleared before you can keep damaging him.
- **Atlantic City** — the **Batsuuri Twins** encounter, a two-target fight where one
  twin always fights melee and the other always fights ranged — separating them (or
  focusing one down fast) beats trying to split damage evenly.

**Strategy tip common to both:** expeditions are built around short, repeatable runs
with optional bonus objectives — check the mission's optional objectives before
engaging a boss, since some change the fight's approach (e.g. rescuing an NPC via a
Charisma check instead of a straight fight in Atlantic City's Casino Quarter).

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Boss mechanics (debuff timers, damage thresholds, add-wave patterns) are pulled from
community write-ups and get tuned in balance passes — treat the strategy notes above
as directional, not exact numbers. This list deliberately excludes Daily Ops bosses,
raid encounters, and several other repeatable fights (e.g. other seasonal/legendary
event bosses) that deserve their own future entries.
