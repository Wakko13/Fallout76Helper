For general power armor mechanics — chassis vs. pieces, fusion cores, entering/exiting, repair
stations — see [power armor basics](../../faq/power-armor-basics/). For how Raider power armor
stacks up against the other five models and how to get it (a full ready-to-wear set handed to you
by "Key to the Past," no plan needed), see [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/). For the full
functional-mod pool Raider mostly shares and the perk-requirement caveats common to every power
armor set, see [T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/).

As with every power armor set, there is **no Sturdy/Hardened-style resistance-tier ladder** for
Raider power armor.

## Torso mods (incl. Jet Pack)

Same family as Excavator/Ultracite: **Welded Rebar in place of Reactive Plates**. Notably, unlike
every other set in this initiative, **Stealth Boy also did not appear** in the fetched Raider torso
mod list (same caveat as Excavator — may be an incomplete source rather than a true exclusion).

## Arm mods

Fextralife's Raider power armor page listed only three of the four arm mods seen on every other
set — **Optimized Bracers, Rusty Knuckles, and Hydraulic Bracers**, with **Tesla Bracers missing**
from the fetched list. Not confirmed whether this is a genuine exclusion or an incomplete source.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optimized Bracers | Reduces AP cost for Power Attacks | Not documented | Not documented for Raider power armor specifically |
| Hydraulic Bracers | Increased unarmed damage | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Rusty Knuckles | Unarmed attacks cause bleeding damage | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Tesla Bracers (unconfirmed for this set) | Adds Energy damage to unarmed attacks | Not documented | Not documented — not confirmed present on Raider power armor at all |

## Helmet mods

Same named pool as T-45 (Sensor Array, Internal Database, Targeting HUD, Recon Sensors, VATS Matrix
Overlay, Rad Scrubbers, Headlamp variants) — confirmed present on Raider power armor's Fextralife
page under a "Raider [Mod Name]" naming convention (e.g. "Raider Sensor array"), no materials or
perk data found for this set specifically.

## Paint / material tiers

**Raider power armor has no unique paint scheme of its own** — a search-result summary of its
Fallout Wiki entry states directly that "the raider power armor has no unique paints, with the
exception of the unobtainable variant worn by Blood Eagles [NPCs]," and that it instead takes the
general Atomic-Shop-purchased or plan-crafted power armor paints (Hot Rod, Military, Minutemen,
Vault-Tec, and similar — see [T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) for that
shared list) like most other sets. No Raider-exclusive paint table is included here since none was
found to exist.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **Jet Pack and Calibrated Shocks materials for this set come entirely from aggregated search
  summaries, not direct per-mod page fetches** — Raider's own Fextralife "Raider Jet Pack" page
  404'd on every URL pattern tried. Treat the 25-each Jet Pack figure and the 17-each Calibrated
  Shocks figure (borrowed from Excavator's reported number, not confirmed independently for Raider)
  as leads, not confirmed costs.
- **Whether Tesla Bracers exists on Raider power armor at all is unresolved** — it's absent from the
  fetched arm-mod list where every other set in this initiative has all four arm mods. Could be a
  genuine exclusion (Raider power armor's "junk aesthetic" theme might plausibly skip an energy-
  themed bracer mod) or just a fetch gap.
- **Stealth Boy's absence** from the torso list has the same ambiguity noted for Excavator — not
  confirmed as a real exclusion.
- **Welded Rebar's exclusivity claim is directly contradicted by this same initiative's Excavator and
  Ultracite files**, which also list Welded Rebar in their torso mod pools per Nukes & Dragons — see
  [Excavator Power Armor mod options](../excavator-power-armor-mods/) Uncertain section for the full
  discussion. Raider is the set the "Armorer rank 1, Raider-exclusive" claim was originally attached
  to in search results, so it's presented here at face value but flagged as inconsistent with what
  the other files found.
- **Perk requirements are otherwise entirely undocumented for Raider power armor** — see
  [T-45 Power Armor mod options](../t-45-power-armor-mods/) Uncertain section for the aggregated
  (unverified) Science!/Armorer rank claims for the shared mod family.
- **Base Raider power armor torso stats** (context, not a mod): 504 Damage Resistance / 255 Energy
  Resistance / 362 Radiation Resistance at level 50, per Nukes & Dragons — notably the lowest Energy
  Resistance of the six sets covered in this initiative's torso stats, consistent with its "mid-tier"
  placement in [Power armor sets](../power-armor-sets/).
