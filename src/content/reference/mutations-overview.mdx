## How the system works

Sustained radiation exposure (standing near waste pools, nuclear barrels, or other
rad sources) has a chance to grant a **random mutation** once it's chipped away
enough health — you don't pick which one. There's roughly a 30-minute cooldown
between mutation rolls, though logging out and back in appears to reset it. Every
mutation pairs a **positive effect with a negative one** — nothing here is free.

- **RadAway (and RadAway: Diluted, and decontamination showers)** clear radiation
  and have a chance to strip a mutation off in the process — but the removal target
  is random, not a mutation you select. If you have several mutations and want to
  drop just one, the practical method is: unequip Starched Genes, dose with RadAway
  repeatedly, and check your Pip-Boy after each dose until the unwanted one is gone
  (then re-equip Starched Genes to lock in whatever's left).
- **Starched Genes** (an Endurance perk card) does two things at once: it stops you
  from picking up *new* mutations from radiation, and it prevents RadAway from
  stripping the mutations you already have. Most players farm the mutations they
  want first, *then* equip Starched Genes to freeze that set permanently.
- **Mutation serums**, crafted at a chemistry station once you've had the mutation
  before, let you re-apply a specific mutation without relying on random radiation
  exposure — useful for restoring one you accidentally scrubbed.
- **Class Freak** (Intelligence) reduces the severity of every mutation's negative
  side by 25/50/75% per rank, without touching the positive side — close to a
  must-have if you're running mutations seriously.
- **Strange in Numbers** (Charisma) grants the positive effects of your mutations at
  full strength even to un-mutated teammates, and boosts your own positive effects
  when a teammate is also mutated — a strong group-play card.

## Commonly-sought positive mutations

| Mutation | Positive effect | Paired downside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marsupial | Increased jump height, +20 carry weight | -4 Intelligence |
| Speed Demon | Faster movement and reload speed | Hunger/thirst drain roughly 50% faster |
| Bird Bones | +4 Agility, greatly reduced fall damage | -4 Strength, increased limb damage |
| Eagle Eyes | Large crit damage boost, +4 Perception | -4 Strength |
| Adrenal Reaction | Stacking damage bonus per recent kill | Reduced maximum HP |
| Herbivore | Doubled benefit from plant-based food, no food-related disease | Meat/food from creatures becomes harmful instead |
| Healing Factor | Greatly increased passive health regeneration | Chems (Stimpaks included) are notably less effective |
| Herd Mentality | Bonus to all SPECIAL stats while grouped with others | Penalty to all SPECIAL stats while playing solo |

Herbivore and its opposite, Carnivore, are mutually exclusive — you can only benefit
from one food-effect mutation at a time.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact numeric values above (percentages, SPECIAL point deltas, the 30-minute
cooldown) come from community testing rather than a single current patch note, and
mutation balance has been adjusted before — cross-check FalloutBuilds' mutation
guide if you're planning a build tightly around specific numbers. This list also
isn't exhaustive; it covers the mutations that come up most often in build
discussions, not the full set available in-game.
