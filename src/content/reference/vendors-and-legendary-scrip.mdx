## Purveyor Murmrgh and legendary scrip

The Purveyor (found at The Rusty Pick, Berkeley Springs Station in the Ash Heap) is
the endgame gear-reroll loop:

1. **Earn scrip** by breaking down legendary weapons/armor you don't want at the
   Exchange Machines located at train stations (also obtainable in smaller amounts
   from certain daily/ally quests). Higher star-rating (more legendary effects) items
   yield more scrip when scrapped.
2. **Spend scrip with the Purveyor.** She sells legendary items with randomly rolled
   effects — you choose the category (armor, melee weapon, or ranged weapon) and how
   many legendary effect slots you want (1, 2, or 3 stars), but not the specific
   effects themselves; those roll at time of purchase.
3. This makes scrip farming the main way to "reroll" for a specific weapon/effect
   combination you're chasing, since you can't target an exact result — only buy
   more chances.

## Minerva's rotating stock

Minerva is a traveling Gold Bullion vendor connected to the Blue Ridge Caravan
Company. She:

- Rotates between **four possible locations** — Foundation, Crater, Fort Atlas, and
  the Whitespring Resort — on a weekly basis.
- Sells a **rotating selection of rare plans** (pulled from the same pool other Gold
  Bullion vendors and Daily Ops offer), split across "Emporium" visits (a wider
  rotating list) and less frequent "Big Sale" visits (a single specific list).
- Prices her stock **25% cheaper** than buying the same plans from their normal Gold
  Bullion vendor sources — worth waiting for if you're patient about a specific plan.

Check FalloutBuilds' Minerva page for her current location and active inventory list,
since both change on a schedule rather than being static.

## Personal vendors and CAMP shops

Any player can set up a vendor at their CAMP to sell items to other players for caps:

- Listed prices can range from 0 up to **40,000 caps per item** — that's a hard cap
  on individual listing price, not just a suggestion.
- Sales route the caps to you automatically, minus a **10% cut**.
- Player vendor inventories are shared/global in the sense that any player can browse
  and buy from your shop while it's placed and you're not blocking access, not just
  players physically nearby.

## NPC vendor caps limits

Regular NPC vendors (shopkeepers scattered around Appalachia, not player CAMPs) don't
have infinite money:

- All NPC vendors share a **combined cap pool of 1,400 caps** (doubled to 2,800
  during a Caps-a-Plenty event weekend) for buying items from you.
- That pool **resets daily around 17:00 UTC**, so if you're dumping a large haul of
  loot for caps, spreading sales across resets (or across multiple vendors before the
  pool's exhausted) gets you more than trying to sell everything to one vendor in one
  sitting.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The specific cap pool number (1,400 / 2,800) and the 40,000-cap player vendor
ceiling are pulled from community-maintained wiki pages rather than a single current
patch note — Bethesda has adjusted economy numbers before, so treat these as "the
right order of magnitude" and verify if a large transaction depends on precision.
Minerva's location/inventory rotation specifically should always be checked live
(FalloutBuilds' page) rather than assumed from this entry.
