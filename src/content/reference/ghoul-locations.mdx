Feral ghouls are the most common enemy type in Appalachia, and they come in a
straightforward regular flavor plus an irradiated **Glowing One** variant (also
just called a "glowing ghoul") that hits harder, glows visibly, and heals nearby
ghouls with a radiation pulse — kill glowing ones first in a mixed pack. This
entry is about the wild spawns you farm for XP, junk, and Nuclear Waste drops,
not a scripted boss fight.

## The three reliable farms

- [**Watoga Emergency Services**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Watoga_Emergency_Services) (Cranberry Bog) — take the elevator up, then head
  right to the stairs leading to the top floor; eight or more ghouls spawn here
  depending on luck, frequently including Glowing Ones. This is the go-to spot
  for grinding ghoul kills fast.
- [**The Burrows**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Burrows) (Harper's Ferry, in the swamp/Mire region) — a dense tunnel
  network genuinely "jam-packed" with ferals across the whole level range, with
  the occasional wendigo mixed in deeper inside.
- [**Whitespring Golf Club**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Whitespring_Golf_Club) (Forest region, just east of the Whitespring Resort,
  near the tennis courts down the road) — ghouls wander the exterior grounds and
  the clubhouse interior, with Glowing Ones showing up regularly.

## Secondary spots

[**West Tek Research Center**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/West_Tek_Research_Center_(Fallout_76)) (Savage Divide) reliably has a mixed pack including
Glowing Ones alongside its usual super mutant population. [**Morgantown**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Morgantown)
(especially Vault-Tec University) and [**Charleston**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Charleston) (fire department, landfill,
Capitol building area) both have looser, less consistent ghoul counts, but are
worth a pass if you're already farming those zones for other loot. [**Monongah**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Monongah)
town and its mine shafts are another lower-reliability option.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact spawn counts at any given location fluctuate with server population and
patch-to-patch tuning — treat the numbers above ("eight or more," etc.) as
approximate, not guaranteed. Glowing One spawn ratio within a given ghoul pack
isn't a fixed percentage as far as public data shows, so don't expect a
consistent yield if you're specifically farming Glowing Ones for their higher-value
drops.
