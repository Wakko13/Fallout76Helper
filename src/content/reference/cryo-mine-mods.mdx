## No mod slots

The Cryo Mine genuinely has **no weapon mods** — Fextralife's entry lists the mod section as
unfilled/not applicable, and nukesdragons' interactive weapon database shows no mod configuration for
it either. It's crafted at a **Tinker's Workbench**, requiring the Demolition Expert 3 and Science 1
perks, from its plan — there's no separate Weapons-Workbench mod tree layered on top. What you build
is a fixed 175 ballistic damage planted mine with a slow/crowd-control effect and no attachment slots,
the planted counterpart to the Cryo Grenade's thrown version.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- **Demolition Expert** still scales its damage as a perk-card multiplier — see
  [Grenades, mines, and thrown explosives](../explosives-and-thrown/) for the current rank breakdown.
  That's independent of the (absent) weapon-mod system.
- Legendary Cryo Mines exist as a separate legendary-star system, unrelated to weapon mods.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's Cryo Mine page has several other placeholder fields (exact crafting materials, drop
locations) beyond mods, so this is a thin-documentation source rather than one that explicitly rules
mods out with a citation. The consistent absence across both Fextralife and nukesdragons, and the same
pattern holding for every other grenade/mine checked in this wave, makes "no mods" the safe read — but
a quick in-game Weapons Workbench check would remove any remaining doubt.
