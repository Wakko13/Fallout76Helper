For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where the Pole Hook sits
relative to the rest of the one-handed lineup, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

The Pole Hook's whole appeal is reach, not modding depth — it has exactly one Head mod, and
Fextralife's own page lists nothing else in the mod section.

## Head mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Puncturing | Armor piercing; superior damage | Makeshift Warrior 3 | 8x Aluminum, 4x Adhesive, 1x Oil, 3x Steel |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's own Pole Hook page came back nearly blank for this mod — effect, perk, and materials
were all placeholder ("??") in its raw table, listing only the mod's name. The effect and materials
above are reconstructed from the separate "Plan: Puncturing pole hook" wiki entry via search
(fallout.fandom.com/fallout.wiki returned 402/403 on direct fetch this session, a known
limitation), so treat this as reasonably solid but not double-sourced against Fextralife directly.
Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on crafting melee
weapons and mods entirely, so treat the Makeshift Warrior 3 requirement above as historical rather
than a guaranteed current block. If the Pole Hook has picked up additional mods since this page was
last touched, this entry doesn't reflect them.
