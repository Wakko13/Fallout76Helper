For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For where these two sit relative
to the rest of the energy-weapon family, including ammo and acquisition, see
[Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/).

The Laser Rifle and Laser Pistol use the **same mod names and materials** across every slot on
Fextralife — a Laser Rifle's "Calibrated Capacitor" and a Laser Pistol's "Calibrated Capacitor" are the
same item with the same effect and cost. The only structural difference is that the Rifle has a
**Stock** slot (the Pistol doesn't — a pistol fitted with certain stock-family mods becomes, in effect,
the rifle). Both share Capacitor, Barrel, Sights, and Lens slots identically. The tables below are
written once and apply to both weapons except where noted.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's tables for both weapons are unusually deep — 15 capacitor names, 18+ barrel names, 9
stock names — but roughly half of each list came back as "data incomplete" with no effect numbers,
perk gate, or materials at all (Photon Exciter, Beta Wave Tuner, Boosted/Maximized/Overcharged
Capacitor variants, most of the night-vision and recon scopes, and several stock/grip names). Those
rows are included above with their effect *text* where Fextralife had it, but materials and perk gates
are marked "Not documented" rather than guessed. Only two Barrel-family mods (Aligned Short, Aligned
Sniper) and no Capacitor, Stock, Sight, or Lens mods showed an explicit perk gate on this source, which
is a thinner Science!/Gunsmith gating pattern than most ballistic weapons — worth confirming in-game if
you're chasing a specific late-tier variant. As always, treat exact numbers as a strong starting point
rather than a guaranteed-current recipe.
