See [how daily and repeatable quests work](../daily-and-repeatable-quests-overview/)
for the general mechanism. For the region's public events instead, see
[Toxic Valley public events](../toxic-valley-public-events/).

| Quest | Giver / location | What it involves | Notable reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buried with Honor** | The Curator, **Prickett's Fort** | Retrieve a Civil War Union soldier's remains from a table in the barracks, then carry them to the open grave at Philippi Battlefield Cemetery and bury them | Black powder, plus a chance at legendary weapon/armor |
| **Big Game Hunt** (also called "Big Game Hunter" on some wikis) | The Huntmaster, basement of **Black Bear Lodge** | Track down and kill three randomly-chosen marked creatures — the pool includes honey beasts, mirelurks, radscorpions, radstags, radtoads, snallygasters, stingwings, super mutants, bloodbugs, and yao guai | The Hunter perk, a random plan/armor/mod/weapon, plus a legendary chance |
| **Target Rich Environment** | The range attendant, **Clarksburg Shooting Club** | Retrieve a box of paper shooting targets from the flooded lower level of Wavy Willard's Water Park (fighting through feral ghouls and radroaches), then carry it back and mount the targets on the range's holders | Random ammo/weapons/aid, plus a legendary chance |
| **Pass the Buck** | A radio transmission from Grafton's mayor, triggered at **Grafton Dam** | Retrieve three toxic barrels from Grafton Lake and dispose of them — drop them on the spot with 5+ Agility, or haul each to a dumpster near Hemlock Holes or Prickett's Fort otherwise | Nuclear material, plus a legendary chance |
| **Thrill of the Grill** | The robot cook, **Hemlock Holes Country Club** | Gather "fresh" meat and vegetables from marked spots around the area for the robot's planned cookout | A cooking recipe, plus a legendary chance |

All five are standard daily quests, repeatable roughly every 24 hours from the same
fixed location once discovered.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

**Pass the Buck** has a documented history of being pulled offline: it was disabled
in 2021, fixed and re-enabled around October 2022, then disabled again in November
2022 after a bug caused mirelurks to spawn at each barrel in huge numbers (players
reported well over 100 in a single run). Patch notes since then indicate it was fixed
again, but no source checked gives a confirmed "currently stable" date — if it's
missing from your Pip-Boy's Daily list or behaves oddly, that history is worth
knowing about before assuming you're doing something wrong. "Big Game Hunt" vs. "Big
Game Hunter" is the same quest under two names across different wikis; both refer to
the Black Bear Lodge hunt.
