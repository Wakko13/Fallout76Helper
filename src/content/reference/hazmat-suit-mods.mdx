## No mod slots

The Hazmat Suit is a **body suit** — a single equipped item that occupies the armor, underarmor,
and outfit slots all at once, rather than a chest/arms/legs set built from separate moddable
pieces. Fextralife's own item page carries no mod table for it at all, and it doesn't appear
anywhere in Nukes & Dragons' underarmor listing (which is where items like the Vault 76 Jumpsuit
and Marine Wetsuit — the other apparel in this wave that genuinely do have lining mods — are
listed). Body suits in Fallout 76 are consistently described across multiple sources as unable to
take legendary effects, which lines up with them having no standard mod slots either: what you
loot or craft (1,000 Radiation Resistance, 98% radiation damage reduction) is what you wear.

Two things are still worth knowing even without mods:

- Its fixed 1,000 Radiation Resistance / 98% radiation damage reduction is already the headline
  effect — there's no lining or material upgrade path to improve on it.
- No other armor or clothing can be worn over the suit, which is consistent with it filling every
  armor-related slot at once rather than being a single moddable piece within a larger loadout.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This entry leans on the absence of a mod table (Fextralife) and the absence of the item from the
underarmor database (Nukes & Dragons) rather than an explicit "None" mod-section like the Black
Powder Pistol or Death Tambo weapon pages had. That's slightly weaker evidence than a page that
explicitly states "no mods," so if a future patch turns the Hazmat Suit into a moddable piece
(unlikely given its body-suit classification, but not impossible), this page would need a revisit.
