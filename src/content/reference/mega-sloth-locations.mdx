Mega Sloths are the odd one out on this page: most regular wildlife (Radstags,
Mole Rats, Brahmin) go down easily, but a Mega Sloth is a genuinely tough
fight if you provoke one — high HP and hard-hitting melee, closer to a
mini-boss than a critter. The saving grace is that they spawn **passive** and
won't attack unless you shoot first, so they don't show up as a threat on your
HUD and are easy to miss (or easy to avoid if you're not geared for one).
Confirmed drops include Megasloth Meat and Caps every time, with a chance at
Megasloth Claw, Megasloth Mushroom, Megasloth Pelt, and rarer grenade plans.

## Fixed spawns

- [**Mosstown**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Mosstown) — near a rusty blue car; one of the two most reliable spots.
- [**Tanagra Town**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Tanagra_Town) — at the base of the giant tree structure.

## Chance spawns

- [**Southern Belle Motel**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Southern_Belle_Motel) — south of the motel, often seen in the trees above
  the pond; two possible spawn points in the area.
- [**Sunday Brothers' Cabin**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Sunday_Brothers'_Cabin).
- [**Treetops**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Treetops) (southern Mire).
- [**KMAX Transmission**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/KMAX_Transmission) — east of Treetops; two possible spawn points.
- [**Dark Hollow Manor**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Dark_Hollow_Manor) — southeast edge of the perimeter, near The Trading
  Post.
- South of **Fissure Site Omicron**.

## Other ways to encounter one

- **Scorched Earth** and **Fasnacht Day** (as a boss-tier spawn) public
  events.
- The **Queen of the Hunt** daily has roughly a 1-in-6 chance of a Mega Sloth
  at its investigation sites.
- Random world encounters can pit a Mega Sloth against Super Mutants or
  Wolves.
- Any region can spawn one after a nuclear launch (nuked-zone enemy pool).
- Vendor NPCs Grahm, Marie (Whitespring Resort), and Maul (Camp Ally) each
  have roughly a 25% chance of offering a Mega Sloth-related purchase/event.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

"Boss-tier" here is a relative description from community guides, not an
official difficulty label — a Mega Sloth is still wildlife-tier compared to a
scripted boss like the Scorchbeast Queen, just noticeably tankier than a
Radstag or Brahmin. Chance-spawn locations and vendor odds (the 25% figures)
are drawn from community tallies rather than confirmed drop tables.
