For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Fat Man fits
into the rest of the heavy-weapon family and its own ammo-scarcity problem, see
[Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/).

The Fat Man has just one mod slot — the launcher itself — with two non-standard options.

## Launcher mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Launcher | Standard | — | — |
| MIRV Launcher | Fires multiple mini nukes at once instead of one, spread across a wider area, at reduced per-nuke damage; Weight +50%, AP Cost +50%, Sighted Transition +25% | Prior to Update 60 this required Gunsmith ranks; current gating is unconfirmed | 5x Adhesive, 7x Aluminum, 3x Circuitry, 5x Gear, 6x Nuclear Material, 6x Screw, 4x Spring |
| Bowling Ball Launcher | Launches bowling balls instead of mini nukes | Unused/leftover content — not obtainable through any current plan, quest, or vendor | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The **Bowling Ball Launcher is unused, unobtainable content** left over in the game files — it has
no plan, no vendor source, and no known legitimate way to acquire it; if you ever see it, it's
worth flagging as a rarity rather than something to farm toward, and by most accounts the bowling
balls it fires deal essentially no damage (1 damage, or missed collision) due to being unfinished.
The **MIRV Launcher's** perk gate is murky: community sources note that Gunsmith ranks used to gate
this mod prior to Update 60, but whether it's now unlocked by default, tied to a different perk, or
still Gunsmith-gated wasn't confirmed by any source checked for this page — verify in-game before
assuming either way. This page deliberately does not list the Fat Man's legendary-effect roster
(Stalker's, Bloodied, Two Shot, etc.) — those are the universal cross-weapon legendary system, not
Fat-Man-specific weapon mods, and belong with this site's legendary-effects reference material
instead.
