**A source note up front:** Fextralife's Gauss Rifle page confirms the five mod categories exist
(Barrel, Stock, Capacitor, Sights, Muzzle) but its table came back with essentially no populated rows —
repeated fetch attempts returned category names only, consistent with the page's own "??" markers for
several fields. The table below instead comes from Nukes & Dragons' game-data-driven database, which
uses **percentage-based** effect values (unlike the flat +N numbers on most other pages in this
series) and does not list crafting materials or perk gates for any row.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

This page corrects a detail implied by this site's [Energy weapons](../energy-weapons/) overview: that
page describes a Gauss-specific "Calibrated Capacitor" for crit damage, but the sources checked for
this page (Nukes & Dragons' full mod list, plus targeted searches) show **no Calibrated Capacitor in
the Gauss Rifle's lineup at all** — Calibrated Capacitor appears to be a Laser/Plasma-family mod name
only (see [Laser Rifle and Laser Pistol mod options](../laser-rifle-mods/) and
[Plasma Rifle and Plasma Pistol mod options](../plasma-rifle-mods/)). The Gauss Rifle's actual
capacitor lineup — Standard, Quick, Large, Swift, Perforating, Piercing, Stinging, Prime — reuses the
same naming pattern as a ballistic weapon's Magazine slot rather than following the Laser/Plasma
capacitor names. The Prime Capacitor's numbers (+25% damage, +60% more against Scorched/Scorchbeasts,
requires Ultracite ammo) came from a Fandom wiki page rather than Fextralife or Nukes & Dragons (whose
own Prime Capacitor entry only listed the flat "+65% to Scorched" figure) — treat the fuller
description as the more precise of the two if they seem to disagree. No crafting materials were found
for any row on any source checked (Fextralife's table was effectively empty, and Nukes & Dragons
doesn't publish materials at all), and perk gates are undocumented except for the Prime Capacitor's
plan-drop requirement. If you're planning a specific charge-build around this weapon, verify exact
numbers and any Science!/Gunsmith gate against a live wiki or in-game before farming materials.
