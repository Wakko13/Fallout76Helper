The Automatic-family receivers (Automatic, Tweaked Automatic, Powerful Automatic) switch the Combat
Shotgun to full-auto fire, which is what separates spray builds from the single-shot-focused
receivers above them.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife lists the Aligned Short/Long Barrel mods with no perk gate, which is unusual — on several
other weapons in this series (e.g. the Combat Rifle) the "Aligned" tier is Gunsmith-locked. That could
be a genuine per-weapon difference or a documentation gap on the source page; worth a live check if
you're specifically counting on early access to those barrels. As always, exact numeric stat values
and material counts shift with balance patches, so treat the above as a strong starting point rather
than a guaranteed-current recipe.
