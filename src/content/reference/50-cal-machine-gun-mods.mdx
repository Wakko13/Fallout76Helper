For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the .50 Cal Machine
Gun's ammo economy compares to the 5mm miniguns, see [Heavy weapons](../heavy-weapons/).

Fextralife's page only populated a Barrel table (with materials); Nukes & Dragons separately
confirms a Receiver slot with a Prime option but — as usual for that source — no materials or
perk gate.

## Barrel mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Barrel | Standard | — | 1x Gear, 2x Oil, 1x Screw, 4x Spring, 6x Steel |
| Heavy Barrel | Damage +4, Weight +2.7 | Gunsmith 4 | 2x Gear, 3x Oil, 1x Screw, 4x Spring, 10x Steel |

## Receiver mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Receiver | Standard | — | Not documented |
| Prime Receiver | Damage to Scorched +65% | Learned via **Plan: Prime .50 cal machine gun receiver** (rare drop) | Not documented |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Fextralife's Heavy Barrel effect (flat "+4 Damage", Weight +2.7) and Nukes & Dragons' Heavy Barrel
effect (Weight +25%, Recoil +20%, AP Cost +10%, Damage +25%) don't agree on either the numbers or
which stats change — this looks like the same recurring pattern from earlier waves where
Fextralife's flat display values and Nukes & Dragons' percentage-based database diverge for the
same mod rather than one source being simply wrong. This page uses Fextralife's version because it
came with a confirmed perk gate and materials list attached to it; treat the Nukes & Dragons
percentages as an alternate reading worth checking in-game. No materials or perk gate were found
for either Receiver option on any source — the Prime Receiver's plan-drop gate is inferred from the
existence of "Plan: Prime .50 cal machine gun receiver" as a named item rather than confirmed
crafting-menu text.
