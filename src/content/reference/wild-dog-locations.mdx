"Canines" is the game's own catch-all term for these two — Mongrels are the scrawnier,
mangier variant and Wolves are the larger pack predator, but both act as the go-to
targets for dog-type kill challenges and Tadpole Hunter-style objectives.

## Mongrels

- [**Aaronholt Homestead**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Aaronholt_Homestead) (The Forest) — a reliable mixed spawn of roughly 5 Mongrels
  and 5 (feral) Dogs, making it one of the best single stops for canine challenges.
- [**Big Bend Tunnel**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Big_Bend_Tunnel) — a dense indoor spawn: around 11 Mongrels plus 4 Alpha Mongrels,
  good for farming in bulk without much travel between kills.
- The road between [**Overseer's Camp**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Overseer's_Camp) and [**Green Country Lodge**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Green_Country_Lodge) — a small pack of three
  spawns reliably along this stretch.
- The hill north of [**Becker Farm**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Becker_Farm), next to the windmill — a group always appears here.
- The hills north of the [**Whitespring Golf Club**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/The_Whitespring_Golf_Club) — another guaranteed group spawn.
- [**Philippi Battlefield Cemetery**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Philippi_Battlefield_Cemetery) — a further group of Mongrels worth checking if
  you're already in the area.

## Wolves

- [**Autumn Acre Cabin**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Autumn_Acre_Cabin) — the single most consistent Wolf location; a pack of about 6
  always appears here, making it the best dedicated farming stop.
- [**Investigator's Cabin**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Investigator's_Cabin) — 3 Wolves reliably spawn.
- Northwest of [**Widow's Perch**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Widow's_Perch) — another 3 reliable spawns.
- An unmarked Brotherhood of Steel cemetery near [**Huntersville's**](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Huntersville) southeast entrance —
  2 Wolves.
- Public events also guarantee Wolves if one happens to be up: **Free Range** spawns
  roughly seven near the start, and **Leader of the Pack** is a solid bet specifically
  for the Tadpole Hunter challenge.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Pack sizes and exact spawn counts (e.g. "11 Mongrels + 4 Alphas" at Big Bend Tunnel)
come from community wiki tallies rather than an official spawn table, and can shift
between visits or after content patches. Treat named locations as reliably-canine
spots rather than guaranteed fixed headcounts.
