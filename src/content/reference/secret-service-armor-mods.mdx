For how the armor mod slot system works in general (material tiers, the single misc slot, underarmor
linings being separate), see [Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For
Secret Service Armor's place as the Gold Bullion endgame set and how to unlock/buy it, see
[Light and combat armor sets](../light-and-combat-armor-sets/).

**Secret Service Armor's material mods use the same Welded/Tempered/Hardened/Buttressed names as Trapper
and Marine Armor** (see [Trapper Armor mod options](../trapper-armor-mods/)) — this was directly checked
per the task's instruction not to assume, and it holds up: Nukes & Dragons lists identical mod names for
the Secret Service chest piece, and multiple Fallout Wiki plan pages ("Plan: Secret Service welded armor
torso," "...buttressed armor torso," "...hardened armor torso") confirm the same four-tier structure.
**The acquisition method is different, though**: every Secret Service material-mod plan is bought from
Gold Bullion vendors (Regs in Vault 79, and Minerva when she rotates it into stock) rather than
scrap-learned like Trapper/Marine's lower tiers — see the Uncertain section for confirmed prices. Data
below is for the **chest piece**; limb pieces use the same mod names at smaller base resistance values.

## Weight class

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light (standard) | Baseline weight and resistance | — | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Medium (Sturdy) | Weight +40%, all resistances +30% | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Heavy | Weight +65%, all resistances +65% | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |

## Material/Resistance mods

Percentages/descriptions per Nukes & Dragons; Gold Bullion prices confirmed via a build-guide source
cross-referencing Regs' and Minerva's rotating stock. No crafting-material (Steel/Adhesive/etc.) list was
found for these tiers specifically — unlike Trapper/Marine, Secret Service mods are plan purchases rather
than a scrap-and-build progression, so a component-material cost may not apply the same way, or simply
wasn't documented anywhere reachable.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials / cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | No bonus | — | — |
| Welded Secret Service | Enhanced Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance | — | Plan: 100 Gold Bullion (Regs, Vault 79) |
| Tempered Secret Service | Improved damage, radiation, and energy resistance | Not documented | Plan cost not confirmed (falls between Welded's 100 and Hardened's 350 Gold Bullion) |
| Hardened Secret Service | Improved Damage Resistance with superior Radiation/Energy Resistance | Not documented | Plan: 350 Gold Bullion (Regs, Vault 79) |
| Buttressed Secret Service | Weight +30%, Physical Resistance +65% — the top tier | Not documented | Plan: 500 Gold Bullion (Regs, Vault 79) / 400 Gold Bullion (Minerva, when in rotation) |

| Pocketed | Carry Weight +10 | Not documented | Not documented on reachable sources |
| Deep Pocketed | Carry Weight +20 | **Armorer rank 1 (confirmed)** | **Confirmed: 8x Aluminum, 5x Cloth, 7x Gold, 6x Screw.** Plan: 500 Gold Bullion (Regs, Vault 79) |

## Chest-exclusive: Jet Pack

Secret Service is one of only two non-power-armor sets (alongside Brotherhood Recon Armor) whose **chest
piece** can take a Jet Pack mod, letting it substitute for a jetpack chassis normally exclusive to power
armor. This occupies its own slot, separate from the misc mod slot above.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jet Pack | Enables jet-assisted boost while jumping; increases Weight | Not documented | Reported: 17x Adhesive, 17x Aluminum, 14x Asbestos, 9x Nuclear Material, 5x Stable Cobalt Flux, 5x Stable Violet Flux |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **No dedicated Fextralife per-item page for the Secret Service chest piece could be located.** Every URL
  pattern that worked for other sets (`Secret+Service+Armor+Chest+Piece`, `Secret+Service+Chest+Piece`)
  404'd, and a site-scoped search turned up only faction-lore pages, not an armor stat page — this looks
  like Secret Service simply isn't covered in Fextralife's per-item format, not a fetch block. All
  effect/stat data here leans more heavily on Nukes & Dragons and build-guide sites than the pattern used
  for the other five sets in this initiative.
- **The shared-family claim with Trapper/Marine** is better-supported here than the analogous Robot↔Metal
  claim from part 1 — it's backed by both a live database (Nukes & Dragons) and named Fallout Wiki plan
  pages for all four tiers — but no single source explicitly states "Secret Service reuses the Trapper/
  Marine family" as a design fact; it's inferred from matching names and descriptions.
- **Tempered's exact Gold Bullion price is a gap**, not a guess: Welded (100) and Hardened (350) and
  Buttressed (500/400) prices are confirmed from a source that didn't list Tempered at all. Don't assume
  it's the midpoint — confirm at Regs' inventory in-game.
- **Crafting-component materials (as opposed to Gold Bullion plan cost) for the four material-tier mods
  themselves were not found anywhere reachable.** Only the Deep Pocketed misc mod and the Jet Pack mod had
  component lists (Aluminum/Cloth/Gold/Screw and Adhesive/Aluminum/Asbestos/Nuclear Material/Stable Flux
  respectively) turn up in search results — it's plausible the material tiers cost Gold Bullion for the
  *plan* only and then a separate, undocumented component list to actually craft each piece, similar to
  Deep Pocketed's pattern above, but this wasn't confirmed.
- **Jet Pack's perk requirement is unconfirmed** — nothing found stated whether it needs Armorer at a
  specific rank, a Power Armor-adjacent perk, or nothing at all.
- **Base chest-piece crafting cost** (found but deliberately excluded from the tables above since it's the
  armor piece itself, not a mod: 1x Adhesive, 4x Leather, 13x Plastic, 5x Rubber, 17x Steel, 1x Legendary
  Module) gives a sense of how much heavier Secret Service's baseline material cost is than the early sets
  — worth keeping in mind when reading "not documented" against the mod rows above; the underlying
  material *pool* for this set skews toward Aluminum/Gold/Legendary Module rather than Leather/Cloth/Steel.
