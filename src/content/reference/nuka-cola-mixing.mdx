If you're coming from Fallout 4's Nuka-World DLC and looking for the Nuka-Mixer Station where you collect
"Formula" recipe books and freely blend flavors like Nuka-Cola Quartz or Nuka-Cola Victory — that system doesn't
exist in Fallout 76. There is no dedicated mixing station and no "Formula" item type here. This entry covers
what Fallout 76 actually has instead. Note the spelling: it's almost always written **Nuka-Cola** (hyphenated)
in-game, though you'll see "Nuka Cola" without the hyphen used interchangeably in guides and chat.

## What the "Nuka-Cola machine" actually is in your CAMP

`Plan: Nuka-Cola machine` builds a **decorative vending-machine prop** for your CAMP or a claimed Workshop — it
doesn't mix or dispense drinks on its own. Two related but distinct CAMP objects that do dispense something are
easy to confuse with it:

- **Nuka-Cola mystery machine** — unlocked via Season rank or its own plan. Insert 15 caps and it dispenses one
  random Nuka-Cola flavor bottle, on a 5-minute cooldown before it can be used again. This is the closest thing
  Fallout 76 has to "pick a flavor," except the pick is random, not chosen.
- **Nuka-Cola Quantum Collectron station** — a Collectron unit that passively gathers a Nuka-Cola Quantum (with
  a chance at Nuka-Cola Wild) roughly every 7 minutes without you spending caps.

Neither of these "crafts" a flavor from ingredients — they're loot-random dispensers, not recipes.

## How you actually get each flavor

There's no single unified crafting path. Depending on the flavor, you get it one of these ways:

- **Found as world loot** — most flavors, including plain Nuka-Cola, Nuka-Cola Quantum, Nuka-Cherry, Nuka-Grape,
  Nuka-Cola Orange, Nuka-Cola Cranberry, and Nuka-Cola Wild, are just found in containers and vendor stock
  around Appalachia (Kanawha Nuka-Cola Plant, Nuka-World on Tour park, Eta Psi House, and similar locations).
  There's no learnable recipe for these — you loot or buy them.
- **Learned as a one-off cooking/brewing Recipe** — a handful of flavors are craftable once you've learned the
  specific `Recipe:` item for them, same mechanic as a food Recipe (see
  [cooking basics](../cooking-basics/) for how learned Recipes work in general). Notably **Nuka-Cola Dark** is
  crafted at a Brewing Station once you've learned its Recipe (a random reward from the "Tipsy Taste-Test"
  daily quest at The Nukashine, or purchasable from vendor Leo Petrov) — it's alcoholic and treated as a light
  alcohol, not a soft drink.
- **Granted directly by a quest, one time** — the "The New Arrivals" main quest has you name a brand-new
  Nuka-Cola flavor at a manufacturing terminal, choosing between **Nuka-Cola Scorched**, **Nuka-Cola
  Vaccinated**, or **Nuka-Cola My Blood's In It**. Whichever name you pick is cosmetic flavor text only — the
  item you get functions the same regardless of which of the three you choose, and the choice can't be changed
  afterward.
- **Random event/challenge reward** — **Nuka-Cola Twist** is handed out by Nuka-World on Tour park attractions
  (Most Wanted, Seismic Activity, Spin the Wheel, Tunnel of Love) rather than crafted or looted from containers.

## Flavor effects

| Flavor | Effect | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Nuka-Cola | Restores ~40 HP, 10 AP, quenches thirst; +5 Rads | World loot / vendors |
| Nuka-Cola Quantum | Restores ~200 HP, 100 AP, quenches thirst; +10 Rads | World loot / vendors / Quantum Collectron |
| Nuka-Cherry | Larger HP/AP restore than base Nuka-Cola; small Rad gain | World loot / vendors |
| Nuka-Grape | Large HP/AP restore plus a strong Rad **reduction** — the closest thing to an anti-rad soda | World loot / vendors |
| Nuka-Cola Orange | HP/AP restore plus +25 Radiation Resistance for several minutes | World loot (Eta Psi House and similar) |
| Nuka-Cola Cranberry | Modest HP restore plus a small bonus-XP buff for ~10 min | World loot (seasonal/Cranberry Bog-flavored) |
| Nuka-Cola Wild | HP/AP restore with a slightly higher Rad cost than base Cola | World loot / Quantum Collectron (rare) |
| Nuka-Cola Twist | Heals HP/AP and grants **one random positive buff** for ~10 min; small Rad gain | Nuka-World on Tour event rewards |
| Nuka-Cola Dark | Alcoholic; +1 Strength, +1 Endurance, -1 Perception | Learned Recipe, crafted at a Brewing Station |
| Nuka-Cola Scorched / Vaccinated / My Blood's In It | Cosmetic naming only — same underlying item regardless of choice | One-time quest reward ("The New Arrivals") |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact HP/AP/Rad numbers for the loot-found flavors (Cherry, Grape, Orange, Cranberry, Wild, Twist) come from
community item databases rather than a single current patch note, and Bethesda has adjusted consumable numbers
before — treat the table as directionally right rather than exact. Search results disagreed on whether
Nuka-Cola Quantum currently restores 200 HP/100 AP or an older 400 HP/100 AP figure; 200/100 with 10 Rads
appears to be the more consistently cited current value and is what's listed above. Nuka-Cola Quartz and
Nuka-Cola Victory are real Nuka-Cola flavors, but they belong to Fallout 4's Nuka-World DLC — they were not
found to exist as Fallout 76 items during this research, despite occasionally being asked about in a Fallout 76
context.
