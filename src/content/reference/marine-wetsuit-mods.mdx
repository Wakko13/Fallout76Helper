For how underarmor lining slots work as a category, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For how the Marine
Wetsuit compares to the Vault jumpsuits and where its plan drops (the Mire/Cranberry Bog, or a rare
Mirelurk Queen drop), see [Functional attire](../functional-attire/).

Unlike most of this wave, the Marine Wetsuit is real underarmor confirmed in Nukes & Dragons'
underarmor database, and it carries its own lining tier — the **Marine underarmor linings** group,
separate from the Vault-Tec group used by the Vault 76 Jumpsuit.

## Lining (single slot)

Only one lining is active at a time. Percentages/values below are cross-checked between Nukes &
Dragons (which confirms the lining names and a weight-only view) and a fallout.wiki/fandom summary
surfaced via search (which supplies the resistance and SPECIAL numbers) — see the Uncertain section
for how firm that split-source picture is.

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Lining | No resistance bonus (baseline); known by default | — | — |
| Treated Lining | +4 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Strength, +1 Endurance | Not documented | Ballistic Fiber x4, Circuitry x6 |
| Resistant Lining | +6 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Strength, +1 Perception, +1 Endurance | Not documented | Ballistic Fiber x7, Circuitry x9 |
| Protective Lining | +8 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +1 Strength, +1 Perception, +2 Endurance | Not documented | Ballistic Fiber x9, Circuitry x12, Stable Fluorescent Flux x2 |
| Shielded Lining | +9 Damage/Energy/Radiation Resistance, +2 Strength, +1 Perception, +2 Endurance | Not documented | Ballistic Fiber x11, Circuitry x15, Stable Fluorescent Flux x4 |

The Plan for Treated Lining Marine Underarmor is reported as sold by Brotherhood of Steel vendors;
higher tiers presumably follow a similar vendor/world-drop pattern but weren't independently
confirmed this session.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **fallout.fandom.com and fallout.wiki both return HTTP 402/403 on direct fetch** — the resistance
  values, SPECIAL bonuses, and materials table above come from a WebSearch summary of those pages'
  indexed content, not a page this session loaded and read directly. Nukes & Dragons *was* loaded
  directly and independently confirms the five lining names exist for this item, which corroborates
  the lining slot's existence, but its own numbers on that fetch were weight-percentage figures
  (e.g. Treated ≈ Weight +20%) rather than the resistance numbers used above — the two sources were
  not cross-checked against each other line-by-line for the same stat.
- **Perk requirements are undocumented** on every source checked — unlike the Vault-Tec linings,
  no Overseer's-Log-style unlock chain was found for the Marine lining tiers beyond "Treated sold
  by Brotherhood vendors," so how Resistant/Protective/Shielded are unlocked is unconfirmed.
