## No mod slots

Like the Hazmat Suit, the Chinese Stealth Armor is a **body suit**: a single item that covers
every armor slot at once, rather than the usual five-piece set (chest, two arms, two legs) that
gets its own mods per piece at the Armor Workbench. Multiple sources describing stealth-build
options are consistent on two points that both point the same direction:

- It **cannot take a legendary effect** — a limitation specifically called out as applying to body
  suits in general, the same category the Hazmat Suit, Cave diving suit, and Diver suit fall into.
- Being "only one piece" is explicitly framed (in build-guide discussion) as the tradeoff against
  standard armor's five separately-moddable pieces — i.e., there's nothing to slot mods into here.

No mod table for it turned up on Fextralife, Nukes & Dragons, or in general search — consistent
with the body-suit pattern rather than a documentation gap.

What you get instead is the fixed built-in package: the same 1,000 Radiation Resistance / 98%
radiation damage reduction as the Hazmat Suit, plus the crouch-triggered stealth field and 90%
fall-damage reduction — none of it adjustable through mods, all of it baked into the item itself.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

No source found this session states "no mods" for this item in as many explicit words the way the
Black Powder Pistol or Death Tambo weapon pages do — this conclusion is inferred from (a) its
body-suit classification, (b) the absence of any mod table across the sources checked, and (c) the
same no-legendary-effects limitation independently confirmed for the other body suits in this
wave. That's a consistent pattern across independent sources, but if you specifically care about
maximizing this armor, a quick in-game check at the Armor Workbench is worth it before assuming
it's permanently mod-less.
