## No mod slots

Cultist's garments are categorized as an **Outfit** — the same cosmetic-disguise category as
things like the Cultist adept robe it's visually identical to — not a piece of standard armor with
Armor Workbench mod slots. Outfits in Fallout 76 don't carry the material/misc mod slots that
armor pieces do, and no mod table for this item exists on any source checked.

Even setting the category aside, there's a second, more practical reason mods don't matter here:
it's a **single, unique copy** found in a shack outside Old Crimora Mines during "Oldest Trick in
the Book," and it's **automatically removed from your inventory** once Alex Guerra dies as part of
that quest. There's no window in which you'd own a permanent copy to bring to a workbench even if
it did have mod slots.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The "Outfit, not armor" classification comes from search-engine summaries of fallout.fandom.com
pages that return HTTP 402 on direct fetch, not a page this session loaded directly — treat it as
likely correct (it's consistent with how the item is used purely as a faction-disguise check) but
worth confirming in-game if you specifically want certainty. This is also genuinely new,
Skyline-Valley-era content, so it's more likely than older items to shift with a hotfix.
