For how mod slots and Weapons Workbenches work in general, see
[Weapon and armor mod basics](../../faq/weapon-and-armor-mod-basics/). For the Shishkebab's
acquisition details among the unique/named melee weapons, see [Melee weapons](../melee-weapons/).

The Shishkebab is a Machete blade rigged to a fuel line for a built-in fire-damage-over-time bonus
on every hit — but that fuel line is part of the base weapon's design, not a separate moddable fuel
tank slot. Flamer fuel is what the weapon consumes/needs to function as intended, similar to ammo,
rather than something you swap mods on. The only actual mod slot is a single Blade option.

## Blade mods

| Mod | Effect | Perk required | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extra Flame Jets | Increased energy/fire damage (+10 damage per one source) | Makeshift Warrior | 5x Asbestos, 10x Steel, 6x Copper, 3x Adhesive |

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Sources disagreed on the exact material counts for Extra Flame Jets: Fextralife listed 5x Asbestos,
7x Steel, 10x Copper, 6x Adhesive, while a search-indexed fandom source listed 5x Asbestos, 10x
Steel, 6x Copper, 3x Adhesive for the same mod — same four materials, different quantities on
three of them, which looks like the kind of copy/transcription drift flagged as a known risk for
this wave rather than two genuinely different recipes. The table above uses the fandom-sourced
numbers as the primary reading since Fextralife's Shishkebab page otherwise looked thin, but verify
in-game before treating either as gospel. No separate fuel-tank mod or gas-tank upgrade was found
on either source — if one exists, it isn't documented anywhere checked for this page. As with the
rest of this wave, Patch 60 ("Gone Fission," June 2025) reportedly removed the hard perk-gate on
crafting melee weapons and mods entirely, so treat "Makeshift Warrior" here as historical rather
than a guaranteed current block.
