Most public events are anchored to one region, but a handful spawn at any of
several fixed locations spread across Appalachia, plus a separate cluster tied
specifically to the Enclave faction. Both groups are still yellow-hexagon
map-triggered events like any other — see
[event rotation and scheduling](../event-rotation-scheduling/) for how spawn
timing works in general.

## Multi-region public events

| Event | Where it can trigger | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Always Vigilant | Any Relay Tower — documented across The Forest, Ash Heap, and Savage Divide | Repair and defend an Eyebot named Rover for up to 30 minutes while he restores a communications uplink; attacking enemy type (robots, Super Mutants, Scorched) depends on which region's tower you're at | Springs and Acid guaranteed, plus general loot and a legendary chance |
| It's a Trap | The Mire, Ash Heap, or Cranberry Bog, random location (never at Holdingford Homestead, Huntsman's Glade, Hemlock Holes, Old Pete's End, or Dusty Heap) | Activate a lure that grounds a nearby Scorchbeast, then kill it before the lure's battery runs out and it takes flight again | Scorchbeast parts, plus general loot and a legendary chance |
| Powering Up Power Stations | Poseidon Energy Plant WV-06, Monongah Power Plant, or Thunder Mountain Power Plant | Repair three leaking pipe/valve stations, override two reactor lockdowns via a terminal password puzzle, then restart the whole plant | Power Box Power and Power Generator plan, guaranteed Nuclear Material and Copper, Fusion Cell ammo |
| Defend Workshop / Retake Workshop | Any player- or faction-owned workshop, map-wide | Triggers periodically after claiming a workshop: fight off up to several waves of hostiles to keep it. Ignore the Defend event too long and it's replaced by a single-wave Retake event, during which the owner can't enter build mode | Workshop resource production continues uninterrupted; no unique loot table beyond standard event drops |
| The Messenger (currently disabled) | Formerly any random location | Escort a damaged robot NPC ("Mr. Messenger") along its route after recovering its Signal Resistor, protecting it until it reaches its destination | N/A — see status note below |

## Enclave-faction events

These three are technically "Enclave Activities" rather than standard public
events in the game's own categorization, tied to MODUS and the Kovac-Muldoon
resource network rather than a region. As of **The Backwoods** update (March
2026), all three were opened up to every player — previously they required
having progressed the Enclave storyline far enough to unlock them.

| Event | Where it can trigger | What it involves | Notable reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Real Blast | Random Enclave-relevant locations (e.g. Seneca Rocks Visitor Center, Harper's Ferry) | Activate a set of uplink array components across the site while fighting off enemies; several components break partway through and must be repaired before the operation completes | Enclave Underarmor, general loot, 2 Promotional Commendations toward the Officer on Deck main-quest step |
| Bots on Parade | Bot-Stop stations at various locations (e.g. Camden Park, Berkeley Springs) | Connect MODUS to a Bot-Stop station, then defend the bots inside from waves of Scorched/mole miners/feral ghouls/super mutants while they're reprogrammed and unable to fight back | General loot, 1 Promotional Commendation |
| Dropped Connections | Sites with MODUS triangulation dishes, various locations | Activate three triangulation points so MODUS can call in a Kovac-Muldoon resource drop, then clear the enemies the drop attracts before the container unlocks | The only event that can drop Enclave Plasma Guns (EPGs); 1 Promotional Commendation |

Promotional Commendations earned from these three feed into unlocking (or were
historically required for) the **Officer on Deck** main-quest step, which itself
requires 10 commendations from a mix of Enclave activities and starred
epic/Scorchbeast kills — see the Fallout Wiki source above if you're chasing that
questline specifically rather than just farming loot.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

- **The Messenger has been disabled since mid-2021** (a Bethesda community
  statement confirmed it was turned off for pathing/stuck-NPC issues) and has not
  been re-enabled as of this writing — it's listed here because it's a real named
  event players will still find referenced in older guides and in-game
  terminal/loading-screen text, not because you can currently trigger it. Treat any
  guide describing it as currently live as outdated.
- **Distant Thunder** is deliberately *not* included here even though it's
  sometimes grouped with "region-spanning" events in community lists — every
  source checked ties its trigger specifically to Fort Defiance's artillery
  battery, so it's covered instead in
  [Cranberry Bog public events](../cranberry-bog-public-events/).
- Whether Defend Workshop / Retake Workshop should really count as "public events"
  in the same sense as the others is a judgment call — they're gated behind
  someone owning the workshop first, unlike a pure walk-up event, but once
  triggered they do show a map marker any player can join, matching this site's
  definition of a public event.
- The Backwoods update's "opened to all players" change for Enclave activities is
  recent as of this writing (March 2026) — if you're reading this well after that,
  double check it hasn't been revised again, and that commendation values per event
  haven't been rebalanced.
