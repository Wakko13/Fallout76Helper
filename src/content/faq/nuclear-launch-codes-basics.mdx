## Prerequisites

Launch codes (aka nuke codes, launch codes) are gated behind the Enclave main questline. You need to reach **General** rank with the Enclave (via the "Officer on Deck" quest) and complete **"I Am Become Death"**, which has MODUS walk you through the nuke system for the first time. Until then, silo terminals won't let you do anything.

## The three pieces you need

1. **A Nuclear Keycard** — obtained by picking a Nuclear Keycard Escort mission from the Surveillance System Terminal in the Enclave's Whitespring bunker, then shooting down the marked Cargobot, which drops one keycard.
2. **8 code fragments for one specific silo** — Site Alpha, Bravo, or Charlie each have their own set of 8. Fragments are carried by scorched or ghoulified former military officers out in the world (look for the radio pack on their back, which flashes red and emits a periodic high-pitched tone). The Whitespring bunker's command-wing surveillance terminals will point you to current fragment locations for whichever silo you pick. Collected fragments sit in your Pip-Boy under Items > Aid.
3. **The weekly keyword** — a cipher keyword that's revealed letter-by-letter over the course of the week on a screen in the Whitespring bunker command center. You need the complete word to decrypt your 8 fragments into the actual 8-digit launch code.

Everything resets weekly (Tuesdays ~12am UTC): fragments disappear from inventory, the keyword changes, and you start over. Community sites like FalloutBuilds and F76.WORLD publish the current week's decrypted codes if you'd rather skip the fragment grind.

## Launching

At the Launch Control Center of your chosen silo, you'll first have to fight through the site's defenses (level-50+ Sentry Bots, Aggressotrons, and other Enclave automation). Inside, enter the Nuclear Keycard into one terminal and the decrypted launch code into the other, then use the central console to pick a target location on the map and confirm. The missile takes a few minutes to arrive, and there's a **server-wide announcement** when it does — everyone on your world gets a heads-up that a strike is inbound and where.

## Why bother

A detonation flips the target area into a temporary "Blast Zone" state: enemy levels spike well above normal, and most creatures in the zone (including any Scorchbeasts) get tougher, glowing variants. In exchange:

- **Loot and legendary drop quality goes up** across the zone for as long as it stays irradiated.
- **Mutated flora** appears — bright, mutated plants that yield Flux (Raw Cobalt, Crimson, Yellowcake, Fluorescent, or Violet depending on location), the key crafting material for high-end power armor and weapon mods. Defeated enemies also drop Hardened Mass, Ionized Meat, and sometimes Irradiated Blood, all used in stabilizing Flux.
- **Nuking the Cranberry Bog Prime Fissure Site specifically** triggers the **Scorched Earth** public event and, eventually, a Scorchbeast Queen fight — one of the game's best sources of Treasury Notes, plans, and 3-star legendaries.

## The risk

This isn't a free loot pinata. The zone is genuinely dangerous: high radiation, elevated enemy levels, and dense packs of Scorched (including glowing variants) that can overwhelm an underprepared or solo player. Go in with rad protection, a group if possible, and don't expect to just wander in at low level — the whole point of the level-50+ silo defenses and post-blast enemies is that this is endgame content.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact keyword-reveal schedule and specific fragment spawn locations are patch- and week-dependent — cross-check against FalloutBuilds' or F76.WORLD's current-week pages before planning a run, since anything more specific here would be stale within days.
