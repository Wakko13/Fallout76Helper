Leather is mostly an animal-hunting resource rather than a pure loot-and-scrap one —
the best runs involve killing specific creatures, not just looting buildings.

## Hunting for hides

**Brahmin** are the single best source: each hide scraps for **8 Leather**, and
they're common and barely dangerous. **Flatwoods** has a reliable cluster of them.
Other usable hides — Radstag, Yao Guai (4 Leather each), Mole Rat (1 Leather),
Deathclaw (5 Leather, plus 2 more from a Deathclaw Hand) — turn up on corpses and
tanning racks around **Treetops** and **Prickett's Fort**, which both have loose
hides and hide bundles scattered throughout.

## Scrapping consumer items

**Baseball gloves** (3 Leather each, including undamaged and Youth League variants),
**teddy bears** (1 Leather), and **Mr. Fuzzy** souvenirs all scrap for Leather.
**Camden Park** has a good concentration of both teddy bears and Mr. Fuzzy dolls
lying around if you'd rather loot toys than hunt.

## No mining or workshop route

Leather has no ore vein to mine and no confirmed workshop extractor, so hunting and
scrapping loops are the only farming methods.

## Note

Like other junk and creature-loot resources, spawns and hide drop rates can shift
with world content updates — check the sources above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
