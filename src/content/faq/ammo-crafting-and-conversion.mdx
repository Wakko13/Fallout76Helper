## Crafting ammo at the Tinker's Workbench

Ammunition is crafted at a **Tinker's Workbench**, not a regular weapons workbench.

- **Unlocking it**: the plan is a quest reward from **"Second Helpings"**, one of the
  early Flatwoods-area main quests — nearly every character has it available very
  early on.
- **Building it**: once learned, place it at your CAMP for roughly 8 Aluminum, 5 Gear,
  7 Oil, 4 Screw, and 4 Spring (costs can shift slightly with balance patches — check
  the in-game menu for the current recipe).
- **What it makes**: blueprints for every ammo type in the game, ballistic and energy,
  plus grenades, mines, and bulk crafting components. Most conventional ammo just needs
  **lead and gunpowder** (gunpowder itself comes from crafting/scrapping at a
  cooking station or from certain junk).
- **Perks that boost yield**: **Ammosmith** (Agility) increases the amount of ammo you
  get per crafting action, up to a large percentage bonus at max rank; stack it with
  **Super Duper** (Luck) for a chance at double output on top of that.

## The Ammo Converter (cross-caliber exchange)

This is a separate machine from the Tinker's Workbench and handles turning ammo you
don't use into ammo you do:

- **What it does**: you feed it unwanted ammunition through its terminal and it converts
  that ammo into **AmmoPoints**, a universal currency (capped at 100,000 points per
  machine — hit the cap and you have to spend some down before it'll take more). You
  then spend AmmoPoints back on any ammo type you actually want, within one of four
  categories: Ballistic, Energy, Explosive, or Other. This is effectively the "loose
  rounds" exchange — sell off a caliber you're drowning in, buy back the caliber your
  current build actually uses.
- **Unlocking it**: the Ammo Converter plan is **not** a normal crafting unlock — it's
  gated behind the Raiders faction. It becomes purchasable from **Mortimer at Crater
  Core** once you hit **Friendly reputation with the Raiders**, priced in **Gold
  Bullion** (Raider reputation currency), or alternatively from Minerva when she's
  selling it, or directly for Atoms in the Atomic Shop.
- **Placing and using it**: once learned, craft it at your CAMP (Copper, Circuitry,
  Glass, Gear, Steel) and interact with its terminal (the converter housing itself is
  just cosmetic/animation — the terminal is where the actual buy/sell menu lives).

## Practical notes

- If you're not into Raiders reputation grinding yet, you can still buy the plan
  outright with Atoms from the Atomic Shop — useful if you just want the machine and
  don't care about the Gold Bullion price.
- Because AmmoPoints are shared across all four categories at the terminal, it's an
  efficient way to dump ammo from a weapon type you've stopped using (e.g. switching
  builds) without it just sitting dead weight in your stash.
