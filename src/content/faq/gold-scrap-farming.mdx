Don't confuse this with **Gold Bullion** (a currency spent at Minerva and other
bullion vendors) — Gold Scrap is a separate junk-derived crafting component, used in
energy weapon mods, some CAMP decorations, and advanced electronics/power armor mods.

## Scrapping junk

Gold Scrap comes from scrapping gold-colored junk: lighters, watches, and cutlery
(forks, silverware). **Appalachian Antiques** commonly has multiple watches and
lighters, and the **Whitespring Resort** interior has Gold Plated Glasses and forks.
Helena, a robot vendor at the Whitespring Mall, also sells gold-junk items outright
(Gold Plated Flip Lighters, Gold Pocket Watches) if you'd rather buy than hunt.

Scrappable gold junk is fairly limited compared to other materials, so treat this as
a supplement to the ore method below rather than your main source.

## Mining gold ore

Process **gold ore** at a chemistry station: 2 ore + 4 acid → 2 Gold Scrap. Get ore
by:

- **Mining veins directly** — the Burning Mine in the Ash Heap has a dense cluster of
  gold veins; Excavator power armor increases yield per swing.
- **Workshop extractors** — several workshops have gold deposits for a passive
  extractor, including Spruce Knob, Abandoned Bog Town, Beckley Mine Exhibit, Billings
  Homestead, Dolly Sods Campground, and Hemlock Holes Maintenance.

## Note

Like other junk/ore resources, respawns and workshop deposit availability shift with
world content changes — check FalloutBuilds' resource map above for the current
picture if a listed spot feels dead.
