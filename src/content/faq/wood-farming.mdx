Wood is one of the simplest materials to farm — no ore, no smelting, just piles to
grab and junk to scrap.

## Collecting Wood Piles

Wood Piles are a harvestable world object, not junk you scrap. Best spots:

- **Prickett's Fort** — 48 Wood Piles, concentrated in the fort's center and around
  the fireplace stacks.
- **Helvetia** — 40 Wood Piles, including several behind restaurants.
- **Sylvie & Sons Logging Camp** — 36 Wood Piles.
- **Gilman Lumber Mill** — 26 Wood Piles plus 13 Logs.
- **Camp Liberty** — 23 Wood Piles in sheds.

## Scrapping

Shovels and hoes give 3 Wood each; brooms, board games, flags, boxes, mops,
chessboards, cutting boards, picture frames, wooden spoons, yardsticks, cigar boxes,
bowling pins, and toy cars give 2 each; hammers, paintbrushes, wooden blocks,
pencils, clipboards, screwdrivers, hack saws, fishing rods, and makeshift batteries
give 1 each. These are common enough that they add up passively during any junk run.

## Workshop extractors

Claim a workshop with a wood extractor for a passive supply with no processing step.
Locations include **Gorge Junkyard**, **Lakeside Cabins**, **Thunder Mountain Power
Plant Yard**, and **Dabney Homestead**.

## Note

Like other resources, Wood Pile counts and workshop extractor availability shift with
world content changes — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
