Nuclear Material — labeled **Nuclear Waste** in your inventory — is one of the
scarcer end-game materials, needed for high-tier power armor and heavy weapon mods.

## Scrapping

Items like **Biometric Scanners, Blast Radius Board Games, High-Powered Magnets,
Ignition Cores, Mini Nuke Beryllium Caps, and alarm clocks** all yield Nuclear Waste
when scrapped. A dense loot route hits **Big Al's Tattoo Parlor** (3), **Kanawha
Nuka-Cola Plant** (9), and volatile materials boxes at **The Rusty Pick**, **Hornwright
Air Purifier Site #01**, and **Camp Venture** (3 each).

## Farming glowing enemies

Glowing/irradiated enemies drop Nuclear Waste directly. Two repeatable events are the
best source:

- **Radiation Rumble** at Emmett Mountain Disposal Site — waves of irradiated feral
  ghouls.
- **Uranium Fever** at Blackwater Mine — Mole Miners drop nuclear waste.

Nuking Whitespring Resort to spawn a wave of glowing enemies is another option if
you're already running nuke events.

## Mining veins

**Federal Disposal Field HZ-21** in Savage Divide has 3 uranium deposits and is the
best mining/extractor location, yielding roughly 30 Uranium Ore every 30 minutes.
Smelt it at a chemistry station: **5 Uranium Ore + 5 Acid → 2 Nuclear Waste**.

## Note

Like other rare materials, event spawns and workshop deposit availability shift with
world content changes — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
