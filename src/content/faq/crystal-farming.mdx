Crystal has both a scrapping route and a rare passive-deposit route, which makes it a
little more forgiving than most junk-only materials.

## Scrapping

Junk that yields Crystal: **Crystal Liquor Decanter** (3), **Floater Freezer Pus
Sac and High Int Rock and High-Powered Microscope** (2 each), and **Broken Camera,
Broken ProSnap Camera, Crystal Shards, Magnifying Glass, and Microscope** (1 each).
Tag Crystal in your Pip-Boy junk list to make spotting it easier while looting.

Dense spawn locations:

- **Clancy Manor** (Toxic Valley, next to Hemlock Holes) — the best single stop, with
  25+ Crystal Liquor Decanters plus magnifying glasses and cameras spread across
  multiple rooms; roughly 80 Crystal on a full clear.
- **Black Bear Lodge** (Toxic Valley) — four decanters in the kitchen, dresser, bar
  shelf, and wine rack; farmable in a couple of minutes.
- **Appalachian Antiques** (Cranberry Bog) — four decanters on shelves, under tables,
  and beneath the stairs.

## Deposit and workshop extractors

Crystal is one of the few junk-tier materials with an actual resource deposit: there's
a **CAMP-buildable Crystal deposit east of Tyler County Fairgrounds** for a passive
extractor. Workshops with Crystal production include **Beckley Coal Mine Exhibit,
Berkeley Springs West, Billings Homestead, Hemlock Holes Maintenance, Lakeside
Cabins, and Thunder Mountain Power Plant Yard**.

## Note

Like other junk/deposit resources, respawns and workshop availability shift with
world content changes — check the sources above for the current picture if a listed
spot feels dead.
