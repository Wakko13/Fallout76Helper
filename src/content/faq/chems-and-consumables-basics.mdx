## Chems and addiction (aka the "drugs" system)

Chems (Buffout, Psycho, Mentats, Med-X, Rad-X, Stealth Boys, and 30+ others) give strong temporary buffs —
SPECIAL stat boosts, damage/AP bonuses, radiation resistance, invisibility, and so on — which makes them
tempting to chain-use before fights or events.

- **Addiction risk is per-use and cumulative.** Consuming the same chem repeatedly in a short window carries an
  increasing chance of triggering addiction to it. There's no visible "addiction meter" shown up front — it's
  effectively a rolled chance each time you take more of something you've already been using heavily.
- **Alcohol works the same way** as its own addiction category, separate from chems.
- **Effects of being addicted:** stacking negative penalties to relevant SPECIAL stats and other stats while
  addicted, plus withdrawal-style symptoms (e.g. faster thirst drain) if you stop taking the substance you're
  addicted to. Multiple simultaneous addictions (different chems + alcohol) can stack their penalties.
- **Curing addiction:**
  - **Addictol** — a dedicated anti-addiction chem; found as loot and awarded by some events (e.g. Guided
    Meditation).
  - **Tasty Radscorpion Egg Omelette** — a cookable recipe (learned when you first pick up Radscorpion Eggs)
    that cures all current addictions at once, no Addictol needed.
  - **Chem Resistant** and **Professional Drinker** perks reduce/prevent new addictions from chems and alcohol
    respectively going forward — they don't retroactively guarantee curing an addiction you already have,
    though some players report they help clear it; treat that as unconfirmed.

## Hunger and thirst (aka Food and Water meters)

These are separate meters visible in your Pip-Boy status, filled by eating food and drinking water:

- **There's no penalty for letting them run empty** — since the Steel Dawn update, running out of food/water
  no longer inflicts stat debuffs. It's purely upside for maintaining them now, not survival pressure.
- Keeping either meter **above roughly 75%** triggers a bonus buff: a full Food meter grants bonuses to max HP,
  Strength, and disease resistance; a full Water meter grants bonuses to AP regeneration, Endurance, and disease
  resistance.
- Because both buffs include a disease-resistance component, staying well-fed/well-hydrated is the main reason
  to bother eating and drinking at all past the early game, on top of any food-specific perks you're running.

## Disease

Disease is a separate risk system from hunger/thirst, unlocked once you hit **level 15**:

- **How you catch one:** eating raw/spoiled/irradiated food, drinking dirty water, fighting enemies tagged
  "Diseased" (which can pass a specific matching disease), wading through contaminated water, or breathing
  toxic gas.
- There are **22 known diseases**, each with its own debuff (stat penalties, damage penalties, faster
  hunger/thirst drain, etc.), generally lasting somewhere in the 15-60 minute range before wearing off on their
  own.
- Catching a disease has a **cooldown** before you can catch another (or the same one again), so you're not
  meant to stack many at once from repeated exposure.
- **Curing early:** a Disease Cure or Antibiotics item, the constructible **Sympto-Matic** station, hiring
  Doctor Solomon Hardy as a CAMP companion, or drinking from the sulfur water fountain near the Whitespring
  Golf Club gazebo.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

The exact addiction-chance curve (how many uses before it becomes likely) isn't published by Bethesda and is
mostly community-inferred from play experience — treat "repeated use in a short window raises risk" as directionally
right rather than a precise formula. Whether the immunity perks (Chem Resistant/Professional Drinker) actually
clear an existing addiction rather than just preventing new ones is explicitly unconfirmed in community sources
too.
