Concrete only comes from a handful of items, which makes farming it a matter of
knowing exactly where the dense spawns are.

## Scrapping

**Bags of Cement** are the best source at 7 Concrete each. **Blue, Red, and Broken
Garden Gnomes** each give 3 Concrete. Good stops:

- **Kanawha County Cemetery** — 5 bags of cement in a shed.
- **Lewis & Sons Farming Supply** — 4 bags in a broken building.
- **New River Gorge Bridge (East)** — 4 bags behind a crashed truck.
- **Huntersville** — 7 garden gnomes across a shed and garden.
- **Monorail Elevator** — 2 piles of cement bags at the top.
- **Abandoned Bog Town** — multiple cement bags in a broken house.
- **Harpers Ferry** — 3 garden gnomes and 8 cement bags near the station.

## Workshop extractors (best passive source)

**Abandoned Bog Town** in Cranberry Bog doubles as the best workshop option — up to
3 Concrete Extractors running at once produce roughly 120 Concrete every 72 minutes.

## Note

There's no ore vein or smelting step for Concrete — it's junk-scrap only. Like other
junk resources, spawns and workshop deposit availability shift with world content
changes — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot
feels dead.
