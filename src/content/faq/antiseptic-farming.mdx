Antiseptic is purely a scrapping resource — there's no ore vein or workshop extractor
for it, so it comes down to hitting the right item clusters.

## Scrapping

Items that scrap into Antiseptic: **Abraxo Cleaner** (standard, Industrial Grade, and
Undamaged variants), **Turpentine**, **Industrial Solvent**, **Toothpaste**, **Blood
Sac** and **Empty Blood Sac**, **Royal Jelly**, **Tick Blood Sac**, and **Waste
Antiseptic**.

Dense spawn locations:

- **Overseer's Home** (basement) — 4x Abraxo Cleaner Industrial Grade, 6x Undamaged
  Abraxo Cleaner, 3x Industrial Solvent, 2x Abraxo Cleaner, 1x Turpentine, all on one
  shelf.
- **The Whitespring Resort** — similar quantities to Overseer's Home, split between
  the "Employees Only" building and near a blue car by the northwestern cottages.
- **Garrahan Estate** (top floor kitchen) — 3x Abraxo Cleaner, 1x Abraxo Cleaner
  Industrial Grade, 1x Turpentine.
- **Abbie's Bunker** — one each of Abraxo Cleaner Industrial Grade, Industrial
  Solvent, Abraxo Cleaner, Turpentine, and Toothpaste.

## Killing ticks

Ticks drop **Tick Blood Sac**, which also scraps into Antiseptic. Good tick-heavy
areas include west of Big B's Rest Stop, Moonshiner's Shack, Lake Eloise, and Spruce
Knob Campground — useful as a side source while you're farming XP or other loot there.

## Note

Item respawns and exact spawn counts can shift with world content — check
FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
