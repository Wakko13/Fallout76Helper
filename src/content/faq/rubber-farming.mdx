Rubber (labeled **Raw Rubber** in your inventory) has no vein or workshop extractor —
it's a pure scrapping material, so route planning matters more than for most.

## Scrapping

Basketballs, kickballs, life preservers, fire extinguishers, coolant, bonesaws,
plungers, mops, toy aliens, TV dinner trays, baby bottles, and mine suit
breathers/filters all yield Raw Rubber. Dense spots:

- **Grafton High School** — 16 basketballs and 12 kickballs in the gym.
- **Watoga High School** cafeteria — 23 TV dinner trays.
- **Wavy Willard's Water Park** — 8 life preservers, 7 basketballs, and more.
- **Ohio River Adventures** — 17 life preservers plus extinguishers and coolant.
- **WV Lumber Co** — 6 coolants and 5 extinguishers.

## Enemy drops

Super Mutants frequently carry pipe guns, laser guns, and assault rifles that scrap
for Raw Rubber alongside their other components — worth looting instead of selling
if you're already fighting them.

## No mining or workshop route

Unlike most crafting materials, Rubber has no ore vein to mine and no known workshop
extractor, so scrapping loops are the only farming method.

## Note

Like other junk resources, item spawns respawn once you've collected roughly 250 more
items since your last visit, and dense locations can shift with world content updates
— check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels
dead.
