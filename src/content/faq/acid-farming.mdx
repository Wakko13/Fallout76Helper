Acid is used constantly for smelting other ores, so it's worth running a dedicated
loop rather than relying on scrap alone.

## Scrapping

**Abraxo Cleaner, Coolant, HalluciGen Gas Canisters, and Makeshift Batteries** are
reliable scrap sources, along with creature parts like bloatfly glands and stingwing
barbs picked up from looting enemies.

## Creature farming (no smelting needed)

Acid drops directly from **Cave Crickets, Snallygasters, Grafton Monsters, and Yao
Guai**. A tight loop through six spots — including **Flooded Trainyard, Tanagra
Town, and Carleton Mine** — can net around 200 Acid in 10 minutes.

## Workshop extractors (best passive source)

**Hemlock Holes Maintenance** in Toxic Valley supports up to 3 Acid Extractors at
once, producing roughly 30 Waste Acid every 30 minutes.

## Note

Like other junk/creature resources, respawns and workshop deposit availability shift
with world content changes — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture
if a listed spot feels dead.
