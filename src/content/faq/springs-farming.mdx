Best manual source: **alarm clocks** and especially **battered clipboards**, both
common at Mountainside Bed & Breakfast. No great passive/extractor source exists for
springs specifically — this one's mostly junk-scrapping.

Like most junk-based resources, these spots respawn once you've collected **250 more
items of any kind** since you last looted that spot — loop a few nearby locations
rather than camping one.
