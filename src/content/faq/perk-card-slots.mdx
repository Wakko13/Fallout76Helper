This is a common misconception — there isn't a hidden "slot count" that unlocks at
certain levels per stat. It works differently:

- Each SPECIAL stat runs 0–15. Whatever number you've put into a stat **is** your
  point budget for equipping perk cards tied to that stat.
- Every perk card has a point cost that equals its current rank (a rank-1 card costs 1
  point, rank 2 costs 2, and so on). You can equip any combination of cards in a stat
  as long as their total cost doesn't exceed your points in that stat.
- Ranking a card up (by combining two duplicate cards) makes it stronger but also
  raises its point cost — so a maxed-out rank-3 Strength card might eat all 3 of your
  early Strength points on its own.
- So "more perk card slots" in practice just means: put more SPECIAL points into a
  stat, and you unlock room for more/cheaper-combined cards in it. Max is 15 points
  per stat, 56 total across all seven by level 50.

Separately, individual perk cards also have their own **character-level requirement**
to become available at all (shown as "Req: Lvl X & [Stat] Y" on the card) — that's
independent of the point-budget mechanic above and just gates which cards exist in
your pool yet.

**Don't confuse this with Legendary Perks** — a completely separate system of
general-purpose perks (not SPECIAL-specific) that unlock account-wide slots at fixed
character-level milestones: level 50 (1st slot), then 75, 100, 150, 200, and 300 (up
to 6 slots total). Those don't consume SPECIAL points at all.

One more wrinkle: at 3+ Charisma, you can share your equipped perk cards with
teammates, provided your Charisma is at least 3x the shared card's point cost.
