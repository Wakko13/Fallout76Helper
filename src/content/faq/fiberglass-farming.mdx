Fiberglass comes entirely from scrapping junk — there's no vein to mine and no
workshop deposit for it.

## Scrapping

Items that yield 2 Fiberglass: **Abraxo Cleaner Industrial Grade, Blood Sac, Cigar
Box, Composite Ski, and Industrial Cleaner**. Items that yield 1: Aluminum Canister,
Fiberglass Spool, Jangles the Moon Monkey, Rat Poison, Telephone, and Undamaged
Abraxo Cleaner.

Dense spawn locations:

- **The Whitespring Resort** — 19 Telephones in the mall, plus 10 Abraxo Cleaners
  spread across specific rooms.
- **Pleasant Valley Cabins & Ski Resort** — 17 Composite Skis.
- **Top of the World** — 11 Composite Skis.
- **Blackwater Mine** — 13 Aluminum Canisters.
- **National Isolated Radio Array** — 11 Telephones.
- **Overseer's Home** — 10 Abraxo Cleaners in the basement.
- **Valley Galeria** — 8 Telephones.
- **Sugar Grove** — 6 Telephones.
- **Kanawha County Cemetery** — 5 Rat Poisons.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts can shift with world content —
check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
