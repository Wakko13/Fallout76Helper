Fertilizer has both a scrapping route and a passive workshop route through **Raw
Fertilizer**, its extractor-produced counterpart.

## Scrapping

Items that scrap into Fertilizer: **Bag of Dogwood Fertilizer**, **Bag of
Fertilizer**, **Bulk Fertilizer**, **Radioactive Pumpkin Seeds**, and **Straw
Pillow**.

Dense spawn locations:

| Location | Yield |
|---|---|
| Farm southeast of NAR Repair Yard | 13 bags + 2 raw fertilizer |
| Wixon Homestead (shed) | 13 bags |
| Lewisburg (rooftops) | 10 bags |
| Tanagra Town (barn) | 5 bags |
| Vault-Tec Agricultural Research Center (greenhouses) | 5 bags |
| Horizon's Rest | 4 bags |
| Fort Defiance (graveyard) | 4 bags |
| Lewis & Sons Farming Supply (greenhouse) | 4 bags |
| Anchor Farm (garden) | 3 bags |
| Kanawha County Cemetery (shed) | 3 bags |

Like most junk spawns, these reset once you've picked up roughly 250 more items of
any kind elsewhere.

## Workshop extractors (Raw Fertilizer)

Claim a workshop with phosphate deposits and drop a Fertilizer extractor — each
deposit produces roughly **15 Raw Fertilizer every 27 minutes**. **Sunshine Meadows
Industrial Farm** is the standout, with 3 deposits for about 45 Raw Fertilizer per
27-minute cycle. Note that Raw Fertilizer needs to be bulked at a tinker's workbench
before it can be sold to vendors.

## Note

Farm layouts and deposit availability shift with world content — check FalloutBuilds'
guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
