Not to be confused with "gear" in the general sense (weapons and armor) — this is the
Gear crafting component, used heavily in power armor crafting and repairs, plus
turrets and hardened receiver gun mods. It comes entirely from scrapping mechanical
junk and from one notable enemy drop; there's no vein to mine and no workshop
deposit for it.

## Scrapping

Items that scrap into Gears: **Typewriters, Desk Fans, Cameras, Silver Pocket
Watches, and Bone Cutters**.

Dense spawn locations:

- **RobCo Research Center** — typewriters, desk fans, cameras, and bone cutters
  scattered across every floor.
- **Charleston Herald** (south of Vault 76, north of Charleston, east of Camp Adams)
  — typewriters and desk fans on every level, reached via ramps and stairs.
- **National Radio Astronomy Research Center** — another solid cluster of typewriters
  and mechanical junk.

## Enemy drops

The **Deathclaw Matriarch** at **Thunder Mountain Substation TM-02** (southeastern
map, directly north of Watoga) drops around 7 Loose Gears on defeat — no scrapping
needed. The area is fenced in and refreshes on a world/map change, so it's repeatable
across server hops.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts can shift with world content —
check the sources above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
