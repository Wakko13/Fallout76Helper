There's no dedicated "respec" item or menu that lets you freely redistribute all your
SPECIAL points at once. What you actually get is a slow, level-gated reallocation:

- From level 2 up to level 50, every level-up gives you a new SPECIAL point to spend
  plus a perk card choice. By level 50 you've accumulated the full pool of 56 SPECIAL
  points (across all seven stats) and stop earning new ones.
- **From level 51 onward**, each level-up gives you a choice: pick a new perk card, or
  move **one existing SPECIAL point** from one stat to another. You keep leveling
  indefinitely past 50, so over enough level-ups you can meaningfully reshape a build —
  it's just one point at a time, not a bulk reset.
- If a stat you're pulling a point out of has perk cards equipped that cost points in
  that stat, you'll need enough headroom (or to unequip a card first) — the point-cost
  budget for equipped cards is checked against your current stat value.

There's no way to speed this up with a consumable or workbench item as of this
writing. Practically: if you picked a bad build early, either grind level-ups
past 50 and reallocate gradually, or start a second character — Fallout 76 lets you
run multiple characters per account/world.

Don't confuse this with **Starched Genes** (an Endurance perk that locks your current
mutations so Rads/RadAway stop changing them) — that's about mutation stability, not
SPECIAL point respeccing, but it comes up in the same searches.
