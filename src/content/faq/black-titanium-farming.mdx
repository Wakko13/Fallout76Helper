Black Titanium is a Fallout 76-specific crafting material (not to be confused with
real-world titanium) used heavily for Excavator power armor. It has three practical
farming routes.

## Killing Mole Miners and Deathclaws

**Mole Miners** drop **Miner Suit Scraps**, which scrap into Black Titanium Scrap
directly — no smelting needed. Reliable Mole Miner spots include Blackwater Mine
(around 6 spawn there), South Cutthroat Camp, Monongah Power Plant, Uncanny Caverns,
Top of the World, and Big Bend Tunnel West. Events like **Lode Baring**, **Tunnel of
Love**, **Uranium Fever**, and **Breach and Clear** spawn large numbers of them at once.

**Deathclaws** drop Black Titanium Scrap directly as loot (no scrapping step at all) —
Deathclaw Island, Abandoned Waste Dump, and Thunder Mountain Substation are consistent
spawn spots.

## Mining veins

Black Titanium Ore veins cluster in the **Ash Heap**:

- **Welch Station** — around 76 ore.
- **AMS Testing Site** (east of Welch) — around 76 ore.
- **Belching Betty** (north of AMS Testing Site) — around 96 ore.
- **Blackwater Mine** (Savage Divide) — has both ore deposits and Mole Miners.

Ore needs smelting at a chemistry station: **3 Black Titanium Ore + 4 Acid → 2 Black
Titanium Scrap**. Equip the Super Duper perk card before smelting to stretch your ore
further.

## Workshop extractors

**Gorge Junkyard** is the only workshop with a Black Titanium deposit, producing
roughly 10 Black Titanium Ore every 30 minutes per extractor. You can also drop a
C.A.M.P. extractor on non-workshop Black Titanium deposits found around Appalachia.

## Note

Like other ore resources, vein and deposit availability can shift with world content —
check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
