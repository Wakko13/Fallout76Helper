## It's consent-based, not automatic

Fallout 76 PvP is not "anyone can kill you at any time" the way a hardcore PvP server would work. When one player attacks another, the attacker only deals heavily reduced **"chip damage"** at first — not enough to meaningfully hurt the target. Full damage only turns on once the **target fights back** (deals any damage to the attacker). In other words, you have to consent into a fight by returning fire; if you never shoot back, the other player basically can't kill you through normal means.

New characters also get an automatic **Pacifist buff** until level 5 that blocks player-vs-player damage entirely in both directions, so brand-new players can't be ganked while they're still learning the game.

## Pacifist mode (aka PvP opt-out)

Once you hit level 5, you can toggle **Pacifist Mode** on manually from the game/settings menu at any time. With it on:

- You can't deal damage to other players, and they largely can't effectively hurt you either — your damage resistance while flagged pacifist is massively increased, so even someone attacking you struggles to bring you down before you can heal, run, or fast travel out.
- It does **not** guarantee 100% immunity — other players can still technically attack you — but between the resistance boost and the chip-damage rule, it's close to opt-out in practice.

## The exception: workshops

Claiming or contesting a **workshop** forces full PvP regardless of your pacifist setting. If you own a workshop and someone else attempts to claim it, or you attempt to claim one someone else owns, everyone in that workshop's zone becomes a fair, full-damage target for the duration of the contest — pacifist mode will not protect you there. This is the one deliberate "forced PvP" pocket in an otherwise opt-in game, and it's caught more than a few players off guard (complaints about getting one-shot moments after a takeover notification are common).

## Repeat attackers: the Wanted/murderer system

Killing a player who never fought back (i.e., who was still on chip damage, or flagged pacifist) marks you **Wanted**:

- You're flagged as a **murderer**, shown to every player on the server as a red star/marker on the map.
- You **lose the ability to see other players' or CAMPs' locations** on your own map (except teammates), making you comparatively blind while everyone can see you.
- A **bounty** is placed on you, paid out of your own caps if anyone hunts you down and kills you — the bounty amount grows if you keep committing "crimes" (killing non-combatants, stealing from other players' CAMPs/workshops/extractors, or destroying their structures) while already wanted.
- Killing a pacifist-flagged or non-fighting-back player nets the killer **no caps or XP reward** — there's no mechanical upside to griefing, only the bounty on your head as a downside.
- The only confirmed way to clear Wanted status is for someone to actually claim the bounty by killing you; switching servers doesn't remove it.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Exact numbers for bounty scaling and pacifist-mode damage resistance aren't published by Bethesda in hard figures and are pieced together from community testing, so treat any specific percentage you see elsewhere as approximate and potentially patch-dependent.
