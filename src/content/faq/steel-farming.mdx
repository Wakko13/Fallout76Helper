Steel is one of the easiest materials to stockpile because ordinary weapons scrap for
huge amounts of it.

## Scrapping

Household junk like Ball-Peen Hammers, Adjustable Wrenches, and Bandage Scissors all
yield some Steel, but **weapons are the efficient route** — an Assault Rifle alone
scraps for 20 Steel Scraps. Loot and scrap enemy weapons whenever you're clearing an
area rather than selling them.

## Mining veins

There's no dedicated Steel ore vein — Steel comes from smelting **Iron Ore** at a
chemistry station (2 Iron Ore + 1 Acid → 5 Steel Scrap). Mine iron at spots like
**Hornwright Testing Site #03** in the Ash Heap.

## Workshop extractors (best passive source)

**Grafton Steel** in Toxic Valley is the standout workshop — it has 4 iron deposits,
and a full set of extractors produces roughly 80 Steel Scrap every 36 minutes.

## High-yield endgame route

For characters with the **Scrapper** perk card, running **West Tek Research Center**
and scrapping the enemy weapons dropped there can net 500+ Steel Scrap in a single
loop, alongside XP and legendary drops.

## Note

Like other junk/ore resources, respawns and workshop deposit availability shift with
world content changes — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
