## Intelligence is the core multiplier

Your Intelligence stat sets your baseline XP gain before any other bonus is applied —
each point of Intelligence is worth **a bit over 2% more XP on everything you earn**.
Stack it from as many sources as you can when you're specifically trying to level fast:

- Full 15 SPECIAL points in Intelligence (via a temporary respec or a dedicated build).
- The Legendary Intelligence perk slot (+5, once unlocked at level 50).
- Intelligence-boosting mutations (Egg Head, Herd Mentality) — pair Egg Head with
  Class Freak or a Solar-powered/similar mitigation if you don't want the Perception/
  Charisma penalty it carries.
- Armor with +1 Intelligence per piece (up to +5 for a full matching set).
- Consumables: Berry Mentats and similar Intelligence chems, stacked right before a
  farming session.

## Stack XP-boosting consumables and buffs

- **Lunchboxes**: up to 4 can be active for a stacking XP bonus (up to +100% between
  them), plus their other perks (extra chance at crafting yields, etc.).
- **Well Rested** (+5%, from sleeping in a bed) and **Cranberry Relish** (+25%, food
  buff) are cheap, easy stacks — always have both up before a grind session.
- **Bobblehead: Leader** gives a flat XP bonus while active.
- **Double XP events/weekends**: Bethesda runs these regularly (often tied to
  in-game events or the Atomic Shop calendar) and they stack multiplicatively with
  everything above — realistic combined multipliers can reach 300-400%+ XP during one
  of these weekends, so save big farming pushes for them when you can.

## Team up (Casual team specifically)

Join or start a **public Casual team** — its team goal grants a shared Intelligence
bonus (scaling up with more bonded teammates) on top of everyone's individual builds,
at no cost to you (no quest-sharing downsides the way main-quest progress has). This
is close to a free, no-effort XP stack for anyone in the team, not just the leader.

## Where to actually spend the buffs

Buffs are time-limited, so burn them on the highest XP-per-minute content you can
survive, not random overworld looting:

- **West Tek Research Center** is widely cited as the best repeatable farm — level
  100 mutants and mutant hounds (the effective enemy level cap) mean maximum XP per
  kill. Clear it, take the elevator, wait roughly a minute for respawns, repeat.
- **High-XP public events**: Radiation Rumble, Scorched Earth, A Colossal Problem,
  Line in the Sand, Test Your Metal, and Neurological Warfare are commonly recommended
  for strong XP-per-minute alongside good loot.
- **Daily Ops and Expeditions** (e.g. the Atlantic City expedition "The Most
  Sensational Game") give large lump-sum XP on completion — a single well-buffed
  Expedition run can net tens of thousands of XP, making them a strong alternative to
  straight grinding when you want variety.
- **Quests** (main story and faction) are one-time but give large flat XP chunks —
  worth clearing out backlog quests specifically during a Double XP weekend rather
  than burning them for free beforehand.

## Practical order of operations

1. Queue Double XP weekend/event if one's active or upcoming.
2. Pop Lunchboxes (up to 4), Cranberry Relish, Well Rested, and any Intelligence
   chems right before you start.
3. Join a public Casual team for the shared Intelligence bonus.
4. Spend the buffered window on West Tek, a strong public event, or an Expedition —
   whichever you can clear fastest without dying and losing buff uptime.
