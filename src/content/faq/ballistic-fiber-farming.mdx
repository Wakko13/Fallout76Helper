The rarest of the classic bottleneck materials. Two enemy drops are your best bet:
**Mothman** (Torn Mothman Wing) and **Sheepsquatch** (Mysterious Fur) each convert to
ballistic fiber. For junk, scrap **Military Ammo Bags** and **Military Grade Duct
Tape** — Fort Defiance and Camp McClintock have good concentrations of both.

Like most junk-based resources, those spots respawn once you've collected **250 more
items of any kind** since you last looted them — loop a few nearby locations rather
than camping one.
