The renewable, no-combat method: grow **corn, mutfruit, and tato** at your CAMP (plant
one of each + fertilizer), build a water purifier for **purified water**, then combine
2 corn + 2 mutfruit + 2 tato + 1 purified water at a cooking station into **Vegetable
Starch**. Scrapping Vegetable Starch at a workbench yields adhesive (Green Thumb and
Super Duper perks increase the yield).

If you'd rather not set up the farm, Duct Tape and Wonderglue scrap for adhesive too,
and vendors (Whitespring Mall, Train Station vendors) often sell bulk adhesive
directly for caps — a fast way to catch up if you're short right before a big crafting
session.
