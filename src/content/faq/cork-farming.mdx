Cork is entirely a scrapping resource — there's no vein to mine and no workshop
deposit for it, so it comes down to hitting golf courses and a handful of other
clutter-heavy spots.

## Scrapping

Items that scrap into Cork: **Antique Globe**, **Baseball**, **Bulk Cork**, **Clean
Globe**, **Collectible Baseball**, **Cork Scrap**, **Crystal Liquor Decanter**,
**Globe**, and **Golf Ball**.

Dense spawn locations:

- **The Whitespring Service Entrance** — around 44 golf balls lying around the
  putting green just west of the fast travel point.
- **The Whitespring Golf Club** — several dozen more golf balls spread across the
  driving range (~33), putting greens (~23), and the main building.
- **Clancy Manor** — around 19 Crystal Liquor Decanters plus an Antique Globe.
- **Ohio River Adventures** — around 18 golf balls along the riverbank.
- **Hemlock Holes** and **Morgantown High School** — around 16 golf balls each.
- **Bolton Greens** — around 17 golf balls plus an Antique Globe and a Baseball.
- **Tyler County Fairgrounds** — around 15 golf balls plus 3 baseballs.
- **Wavy Willard's Water Park** — around 8 golf balls plus 4 baseballs.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts can shift with world content —
check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
