Plastic comes entirely from scrapping — over 60 different items break down into it,
mostly cafeteria and household junk.

## Scrapping

**Championship Bowling Ball** and regular **Bowling Ball** are the best single items
at 7 Plastic each; **Life Preserver**, **Bread Box**, and **Suprathaw Antifreeze**
each give 3, and most other items (cigarette packs, hairbrushes, bowls, toys, office
supplies) give 1-2. Dense spots:

- **Watoga High School** cafeteria — well over 100 Plastic worth of trays, plates,
  forks, spoons, and knives.
- **Morgantown High School** gymnasium — 70+ Plastic worth of plastic pumpkins,
  plates, golf balls, and bowling pins.
- **The Wayward** (south of Vault 76) — trays, tubs, and plastic boxes throughout, and
  an easy overall junk stop since it also yields other materials.
- **Pleasant Valley Ski Resort** — plastic alongside fiberglass and wood.

## No mining or workshop route

Plastic has no ore vein to mine and no confirmed workshop extractor, so scrapping
loops through the locations above are the only farming method.

## Note

Like other junk resources, item counts and dense spots can shift with world content
updates — check the sources above for the current picture if a listed spot feels
dead.
