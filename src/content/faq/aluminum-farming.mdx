Scrap **ski poles** in bulk — Pleasant Valley Ski Resort alone has dozens. Aluminum
also drops from scrapping robots (Protectrons, Assaultrons, Eyebots, Mr. Gutsy/Mr.
Handy variants). For a passive source, claim a workshop with aluminum deposits (e.g.
Red Rocket Mega Stop in Savage Divide has 3) and drop an extractor — roughly 15 ore
per deposit every 45 minutes, smelted at a chemistry station (3 ore + 2 acid → 2
aluminum scrap).

Like most junk-based resources, ski poles and other scrap spawns respawn at their
pickup location once you've collected **250 more items of any kind** since you last
looted that spot — loop a few nearby locations rather than camping one.

Farming routes get outdated as world content shifts. If a listed location feels dead,
check FalloutBuilds' resource map above for the current version before assuming the
method itself stopped working.
