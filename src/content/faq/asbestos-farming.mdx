Asbestos is mostly a scrapping resource, though a small number of workshops let you
work junk piles for a passive trickle.

## Scrapping

Items that scrap into Asbestos: **Chalk**, **Teapot**, **Coffee Pot** (including
Untarnished and Luxobrew variants), **Pack of Cigarettes**, **Cigarette Carton**,
**Preserved Cigarette Pack**, **Undamaged Cigarettes**, **Rat Poison**, **Biometric
Scanner**, **Extinguisher**, **Catch The Commie Board Game**, and **Oven Mitt**
(including the Unscorched variant).

Dense spawn locations:

- **Palace of the Winding Path** — around 14 Teapots, 4 Coffee Pots, and 3 Oven
  Mitts in the kitchen area.
- **Sugar Grove** — around 9 Cigarette Cartons and 12 Packs of Cigarettes.
- **Wavy Willard's Water Park** — around 9 Packs of Cigarettes in front of two
  cigarette machines.
- **Kanawha County Cemetery** (church) — around 5 Rat Poison.
- **Big Al's Tattoo Parlor** — around 5 Packs of Cigarettes.
- **Morgantown Airport Terminal** — around 5 Coffee Pots.
- **Helvetia** (kitchen areas) — around 4 Oven Mitts.
- **Sunday Brothers' Cabin** — around 4 Packs of Cigarettes.
- **WV Lumber Co.** — around 4 Cigarette Cartons.

Like most junk spawns, these reset once you've picked up roughly 250 more items of
any kind elsewhere.

## Workshop junk piles (no true ore vein)

Asbestos has no mineable ore vein, but some workshops have **junk piles** that yield
Raw Asbestos and can be worked with a Junk Extractor for a boosted passive rate —
**Charleston Landfill** has three such piles. Killing **Anglers** is also a reliable
source of Raw Asbestos as creature loot.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts and workshop availability shift
with world content — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
