The crafting component is simply called **Silver**, though you'll also run into
**Silver Scrap** as an intermediate junk item that smelting produces — both end up
adding to the same Silver total once processed.

## Scrapping

Silverware (forks, knives, spoons), silver bowls, lockets, plates, and pocket
watches, plus Fancy Hairbrushes, Assaultron Circuit Boards, and Enhanced Targeting
Cards, all scrap for Silver. **The Whitespring Golf Club** is a strong hunting ground
for silver junk — it also has one of the densest Feral Ghoul populations in the game,
and ghouls have a good chance to spawn carrying silver-scrappable junk.

## Mining veins

Silver veins give **Silver Ore**, which needs smelting at a chemistry station (3
Silver Ore + 4 Acid → 2 Silver Scrap) before it's usable. **Wendigo Cave** in the
Savage Divide has a dense cluster of nine veins in a roughly two-minute loop, good
for 40-80 ore per run in Excavator power armor and easy to repeat by server hopping.
A vein cluster also sits up the road northeast of **Watoga**, near the Pylon V-13
spawn point.

## Workshop extractors

Claim a workshop with silver deposits and drop an extractor. **Tyler County Dirt
Track** has three deposits; **Abandoned Bog Town**, **Converted Munitions Factory**,
**Lakeside Cabins**, and **Wade Airport** each have at least one.

## Note

Like other junk/ore resources, respawns and workshop deposit availability shift with
world content changes — check the sources above for the current picture if a listed
spot feels dead.
