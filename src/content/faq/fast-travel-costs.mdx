## How the cost is calculated

Bethesda has never published the exact formula, but it's confirmed to be **distance-based**:
the farther the destination is from your current position on the map, the more Caps it
costs. A fast travel to a spot a short walk away can cost as little as 1-4 Caps; jumping
roughly a quarter of the map away lands around 20-30 Caps; a genuine cross-map trip
(southwest corner to northeast corner) tends to land in the **60-70 Caps** range based on
widespread player reports. There isn't a hard, documented maximum — treat "~70 Caps" as a
practical ceiling for a single jump, not a guaranteed cap.

Two things reduce the bill:
- **Travel Agent** perk card (Luck) cuts fast travel costs by 30%.
- Low-level characters (roughly under level 25) pay reduced rates compared to higher-level
  characters making the same trip.

Team status and encumbrance do not change the cost of traveling to a paid destination —
what changes the price is purely where you're going.

## Always-free destinations

These cost 0 Caps no matter how far away they are:

- **Your own CAMP** (whichever one is currently active).
- **A teammate's CAMP** — free to any fast travel destination owned or occupied by someone
  on your team, including their placed CAMP.
- **Any teammate directly** (fast traveling to a party member's location, not just their camp).
- **Vault 76** — always free, from anywhere.
- **Public events** you've joined.
- **Named faction hub locations**: Fort Atlas, Foundation, The Crater, The Whitespring
  Resort, The Rusty Pick, and The Rose Room are all permanently free fast-travel points
  once discovered.
- **Workshops you currently own** — free once claimed, for as long as you hold them.
- **Train stations**, specifically station-to-station via the rail network (not a
  general free-travel toggle — only between two connected stops).
- **Fallout 1st Survival Tent** — free to fast travel to for everyone in the world, not
  just the Fallout 1st subscriber who placed it.

## Practical tips to minimize spend

- **Chain trips through free hubs.** Fast travel free to your CAMP or a faction hub near
  where you actually need to go, then walk or use a much shorter (cheaper) paid hop from
  there instead of one long jump.
- **Place your CAMP centrally**, or keep a second CAMP slot near a region you farm often —
  since CAMP travel is always free, a well-placed camp turns a 40+ Cap trip into 0.
- **Travel to teammates instead of coordinates.** If a friend is already near your target
  area, fast traveling to them (or their CAMP) is free even though traveling to that same
  map location solo would cost Caps.
- **Grab Travel Agent** if you fast travel constantly and don't already run a full Luck
  build — the 30% cut adds up over a full session.
- Don't stress about it too hard: Caps income from quests, events, and vendor sales
  generally outpaces fast-travel spending unless you're teleporting across the whole map
  many times per session.
