## What a Shelter actually is

A Shelter is a separate, **instanced underground space** tied to your CAMP — not a room you build
above ground, but its own interior (Vault-style: Utility Room, Lobby, Atrium types) that you enter
through a hatch. It has its own build budget entirely separate from your main CAMP's — see
[CAMP budget and blueprints](../../reference/camp-budget-and-blueprints/) for how that budget works
and how many shelter types you can own at once.

## Unlocking your first Shelter

Pick up **"Home Expansion"**, which sends you to the **Shelter Claim Center** next to the sealed
entrance of Vault 51. Talk to the Mr. Handy inside (Mr. Clark), register on his terminal, then talk
to him again — completing the quest lets you build your first Shelter entrance at your CAMP.

## Visiting another player's Shelter

Not every CAMP has one. Look at the map: a CAMP with a Shelter shows a **small icon beneath the
normal CAMP marker**. Fast travel or walk to that CAMP, find the hatch, and go down it — you don't
need permission, though the shelter's owner gets a notification that someone visited. This is also
how you complete the "Visit another player's Shelter" challenge that shows up periodically in the
Season/S.C.O.R.E. challenge list — see
[S.C.O.R.E. and the Season Pass](../../reference/score-and-season-pass/) for how that challenge
system works in general.

## Uncertain / worth double-checking

Whether visiting requires the CAMP owner to be online, and whether there's any restriction on
visiting during active building/editing, wasn't confirmed during this research — if a shelter visit
isn't registering, trying a different active CAMP is the simplest troubleshooting step.
