General rule before the specifics: most junk-based resources respawn at their pickup
location once you've collected **250 more items of any kind** since you last looted
that spot — so a farming "loop" of several nearby locations beats camping one spot.

## Adhesive

The renewable, no-combat method: grow **corn, mutfruit, and tato** at your CAMP (plant
one of each + fertilizer), build a water purifier for **purified water**, then combine
2 corn + 2 mutfruit + 2 tato + 1 purified water at a cooking station into **Vegetable
Starch**. Scrapping Vegetable Starch at a workbench yields adhesive (Green Thumb and
Super Duper perks increase the yield). If you'd rather not set up the farm, Duct Tape
and Wonderglue scrap for adhesive too, and vendors (Whitespring Mall, Train Station
vendors) often sell bulk adhesive directly for caps.

## Aluminum

Scrap **ski poles** in bulk — Pleasant Valley Ski Resort alone has dozens. Aluminum
also drops from scrapping robots (Protectrons, Assaultrons, Eyebots, Mr. Gutsy/Mr.
Handy variants). For a passive source, claim a workshop with aluminum deposits (e.g.
Red Rocket Mega Stop in Savage Divide has 3) and drop an extractor — roughly 15 ore
per deposit every 45 minutes, smelted at a chemistry station (3 ore + 2 acid → 2
aluminum scrap).

## Springs

Best manual source: **alarm clocks** and especially **battered clipboards**, both
common at Mountainside Bed & Breakfast. No great passive/extractor source exists for
springs specifically — this one's mostly junk-scrapping.

## Screws

Scrap **desk fans, typewriters, and antique globes** — Sugar Grove and the Whitespring
Golf Club/Bunker area have dense clusters. If you're farming with a group, "A Colossal
Problem" public event spawns multiple Wendigos that drop Loose Screws directly (up to
~200 screws split across a full 8-player group per run).

## Ballistic Fiber

The rarest of this list. Two enemy drops are your best bet: **Mothman** (Torn Mothman
Wing) and **Sheepsquatch** (Mysterious Fur) each convert to ballistic fiber. For
junk, scrap **Military Ammo Bags** and **Military Grade Duct Tape** — Fort Defiance
and Camp McClintock have good concentrations of both.

## Lead

Scrap **weights/barbells** and ammo casings at gyms and firing ranges, or mine lead
veins directly (Lucky Hole Mine in Savage Divide has ~40 veins — wear Excavator power
armor to maximize the yield) and smelt at a chemistry station (2 ore + 1 acid → 5 lead
scrap). Six workshops also have lead deposits for a passive extractor (~15 scrap per
27 minutes), including Converted Munitions Factory and Grafton Steel Yard.

## Note

Farming routes and specific respawning junk-item spreadsheets get outdated as world
content shifts (seasonal map changes, new points of interest). If a listed location
feels dead, check FalloutBuilds' resource map pages above for the current version
before assuming the method itself stopped working.
