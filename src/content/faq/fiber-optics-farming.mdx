You'll see this material written both as **Fiber Optics** and **Fiber-Optics** (with
a hyphen) depending on the source — same item either way.

## Enemy drops (fastest method)

**Fog Crawlers** drop Fiber Optics Bundles directly, worth 1 Fiber Optics each with
no scrapping needed. **Dabney Homestead** — far east of the map, close to Blake's
Offering and the Valley Galeria — spawns three high-level Fog Crawlers and is
considered the single best farming loop for this material.

## Scrapping

Items that scrap into Fiber Optics: **Flight Data Recorder** (2), and **Biometric
Scanner, Fiber Optics Bundle, High-Powered Microscope, and Microscope** (1 each).
Dense spots for microscopes:

- **AVR Medical Center** (south of the map) — roughly 7 microscopes on desks
  throughout the building.
- **West Tek Research Center** — roughly 5 microscopes, though you'll need to clear
  FEV-altered Super Mutants first.
- **Morgantown Airport** — 3 microscopes in the medical hangar, plus a biometric
  scanner between the Freak Show and the monorail elevator.
- **National Isolated Radio Array** — another solid cluster of microscopes.

## Junk piles

Junk piles that spawn Fiber Optics Bundles exist at various workshops; dropping a
**Junk Extractor** on top of one of these piles increases the passive yield, though
there's no true ore vein or mineral deposit for this material.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, spawn rates and enemy density shift with world
content changes — check the sources above for the current picture if a listed spot
feels dead.
