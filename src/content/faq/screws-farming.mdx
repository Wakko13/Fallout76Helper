Scrap **desk fans, typewriters, and antique globes** — Sugar Grove and the Whitespring
Golf Club/Bunker area have dense clusters. If you're farming with a group, "A Colossal
Problem" public event spawns multiple Wendigos that drop Loose Screws directly (up to
~200 screws split across a full 8-player group per run) — see the
`notable-bosses-and-encounters` reference entry for that fight.

Like most junk-based resources, desk fans/typewriters/globes respawn once you've
collected **250 more items of any kind** since you last looted that spot — loop a few
nearby locations rather than camping one.
