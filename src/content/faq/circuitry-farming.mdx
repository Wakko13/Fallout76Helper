Circuitry comes entirely from scrapping electronics and from robot enemy drops —
there's no vein to mine and no workshop deposit for it.

## Scrapping

Thirteen junk types scrap into Circuitry. The best yields are **Sensor Module** (5),
**Scrap Assaultron Head** (3), and **Detonator, Enhanced Targeting Card,
Fusion Pulse Charge, Military-Grade Circuit Board, Radio Jammer, and Security Bot
Component** (2 each) — plus Assaultron Circuit Board, Circuits, Flight Data Recorder,
Hot Plate, and Telephone at 1 each.

Dense spawn locations:

- **The Whitespring Resort** — 19 Telephones plus a Sensor Module.
- **National Isolated Radio Array** — 11 Telephones, 3 Military-Grade Circuit Boards,
  and a Hot Plate.
- **Valley Galeria** — 8 Telephones.
- **Sugar Grove** — 6 Telephones.
- A shack southeast of The Crater — 2 Military-Grade Circuit Boards, an Enhanced
  Targeting Card, and a Flight Data Recorder.

## Enemy drops

Robot-type enemies — **Sentry Bots, Turrets, Protectrons, and Assaultrons** — drop
circuitry-producing components, as do Aliens during the **Invaders from Beyond**
event.
