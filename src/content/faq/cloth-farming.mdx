Cloth comes entirely from scrapping paper and fabric junk — there's no vein to mine
and no workshop deposit for it.

## Scrapping

Most paper and soft-goods junk yields 1 Cloth each: Burnt Books, Cigarette Cartons
and Packs, Comics, Duct Tape, Folders, Magazines, Newspapers, Napkins, Pillows,
Pre-War Money, and Toilet Paper. Mr. Fuzzy items yield 2-3 depending on the variant.
If you want a compact stack, craft **Bulk Cloth** at a Tinkerer's Workbench from 30
raw Cloth plus 2 Plastic — it yields 30 Cloth back when scrapped, useful for quickly
clearing timed challenges.

Dense spawn locations:

- **Summersville** — fast-travel in and head to the big white house on the north
  side; it holds well over 250 books, enough to fill your carry weight on its own.
- **Charleston Capitol Building**
- **Fort Defiance**
- **Grafton**
- **Lewisburg**
- **West Tek Research Center**
