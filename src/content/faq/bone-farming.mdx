Bone comes entirely from scrapping skeletal junk — there's no vein to mine and no
workshop deposit for it, so it's about finding rooms full of the right clutter.

## Scrapping

The highest-yield junk items are **Bulk Bone Shards** (20 Bone — craft these at a
Tinkerer's Workbench from 20 raw Bone if you want a compact stack), plus **Brahmin
Skull, Jawless Brahmin Skull, Rib Cage, Sheepsquatch Skull, and Spine** (5 each).
Mid-tier items like Beaver Teeth, Broken Femur, Deathclaw Hand, Femur, Human Jaw, and
various antlers and limb bones yield 2-3 apiece.

Dense spawn locations:

- **Braxton's Quality Medical Supplies** (The Mire) — the standout spot, with a vast
  number of human skeletons scattered through the building and a workbench on-site so
  you can scrap immediately without hauling weight back.
- **The Crater** — a large bone pile sitting on a pool table.
- **Whitespring Service Entrance** — dumpsters nearby contain bones.
- **Wendigo Cave** — bone wind chimes are scattered through the cave.
- **Uranium Fever** (public event) — Mole Miners drop gauntlets that yield Mole Rat
  Teeth.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts can shift with world content —
check the sources above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
