Scrap **weights/barbells** and ammo casings at gyms and firing ranges, or mine lead
veins directly (Lucky Hole Mine in Savage Divide has ~40 veins — wear Excavator power
armor to maximize the yield) and smelt at a chemistry station (2 ore + 1 acid → 5 lead
scrap). Six workshops also have lead deposits for a passive extractor (~15 scrap per
27 minutes), including Converted Munitions Factory and Grafton Steel Yard.

Like most junk-based resources, gym/firing-range spawns respawn once you've collected
**250 more items of any kind** since you last looted that spot — loop a few nearby
locations rather than camping one.
