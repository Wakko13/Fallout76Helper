Copper comes from three different methods, and unlike most materials, one of them
skips the smelting step entirely.

## Scrapping (no smelting needed)

**High-Powered Magnets, Pre-War Lamps, and Trumpets** each yield 3 Copper directly
when scrapped — no chemistry station step required. **Landview Lighthouse** is a
reliable spot for lighthouse-themed souvenirs that scrap for copper too.

## Mining veins

Copper veins give **Copper Ore**, which needs smelting at a chemistry station before
it's usable. Good vein locations:

- **Whitespring Station** (east of the Whitespring Resort, near the middle of the
  map) — three veins in the rock wall near the red water tower.
- **Bolton Greens** (The Forest) and **Abandoned Bog Town** (top floor).
- A longer farming loop through **Ash Heap**'s veins takes roughly 30 minutes for a
  full circuit.

Excavator power armor's carry-weight bonus is worth wearing on a mining run purely so
you don't cap out on inventory before you're done.

## Workshop extractors (best passive source)

Claim a workshop with copper deposits and drop an extractor. **Dabney Homestead** is
the standout — it supports up to 3 extractors simultaneously, each producing roughly
20 Copper Ore per hour.

## Note

Like other junk/ore resources, respawns and workshop deposit availability shift with
world content changes — check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
