The junk item you pick up is **Waste Oil**; it converts into the **Oil** crafting
component when scrapped or auto-consumed at a workbench — worth knowing if you're
hunting for "oil" and only waste oil shows up in your inventory.

## Scrapping

**Industrial Size Shortening** (5 Oil) and **Oil Can** (4 Oil) are the best single
items; **Cooking Oil**, **Cutting Fluid**, and **Fumigus Blowtorch** each give 3 Oil,
and various paint cans, fuel canisters, and lighters give 1-2 Oil apiece. A common
loop is crafting and immediately scrapping **Cutting Fluid** (2 Acid, 8 Bone, 2
Purified Water, 3 Steel), which nets 3 Oil and 1 Steel back per craft. Good junk
locations: **Blackwater Mine**, **Silva Homestead**, **Gilman Lumber Mill**,
**Clarksburg Hardware Store**, and **Abbie's Bunker**.

## Enemy drops (fastest single method)

The two **Grafton Monsters** — one near Hornwright Industrial Headquarters, one near
the Charleston Capitol Building — each drop 3-8 Waste Oil plus a Sticky Tar on a kill,
and both have consistent spawn points, making them the quickest source if you're
geared to fight them.

## Workshop extractors (oil seeps)

Claim a workshop with an oil seep and drop an extractor; it produces one Waste Oil
every three real-time minutes up to a cap of five, so emptying it roughly every 15
minutes keeps it efficient. Workshops with oil seeps include **Wade Airport** and
**Beckley Mine** (3 extractors each), and **Grafton Steel Yard**, **Federal Disposal
Field HZ-21**, **Converted Munitions Factory**, **Mount Blair**, and **Abandoned Bog
Town** (fewer extractors each, but Abandoned Bog Town also stacks food, water,
concrete, and silver).

## Note

Like other junk/workshop resources, respawns and deposit availability shift with
world content changes — check the sources above for the current picture if a listed
spot feels dead.
