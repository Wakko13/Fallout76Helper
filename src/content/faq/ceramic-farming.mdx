Ceramic comes entirely from scrapping pottery and dishware — there's no vein to mine
and no workshop deposit for it, so it's all about finding rooms packed with the right
clutter.

## Scrapping

Dozens of items scrap into Ceramic, including ashtrays, bowls, garden gnomes, vases,
plates, saucers, teacups, teapots, and flower pots of various types.

Dense spawn locations:

- **The Whitespring Resort** — the Whitespring Refuge dining area has vases, saucers,
  teacups, teapots, and ashtrays on the tables; the area left of the stairs has more
  ashtrays and vases. The **Whitespring Golf Club** is another good stop nearby.
- **Bloody Frank's** (north of Berkeley Springs) — the upper room with a bathtub in
  the spa salon building has red plates, yellow plates, sauce pans, saucers, cups, and
  vases. Blood Eagles and turrets guard the location, so come prepared.
- Other solid spots: **Lewis & Sons Farming Supply**, **Vault-Tec Agricultural
  Research Center**, **Red Rocket Mega Shop**, **Palace of the Winding Path**,
  **Sunday Brothers' Cabin**, and **Overseer's Home**.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts can shift with world content —
check FalloutBuilds' guide above for the current picture if a listed spot feels dead.
