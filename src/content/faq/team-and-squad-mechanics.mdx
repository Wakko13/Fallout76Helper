## Team size

Teams max out at **4 players** — yourself plus 3 others. This applies to both private
teams (invite-only) and public teams (open, joinable from the social/team-finder menu).

## Charisma and perk-card sharing

Each team member can share **one** perk card at a time with the rest of the team, and
you can swap which card you're sharing whenever you want. Sharing costs Charisma based
on the card's point/rank cost — the rule of thumb is **3 Charisma per rank** of the
card being shared:

| Card rank | Charisma required to share |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 9 |
| 4 | 12 |
| 5 | 15 |

A few caveats: shared cards from different teammates **don't stack** if they're
duplicates of the same card (you just get the one effect, not doubled), and Charisma
requirements are per-card — sharing multiple cards means paying the Charisma cost for
each one simultaneously, so most players build toward one specific high-value card to
share (a strong Luck or damage-resistance card, for instance) rather than trying to
share several at once.

## Team Goals (public team stat/XP bonuses)

Public teams pick a **Team Goal**, and every member gets a buff themed to it. The buff
starts small and **scales up as teammates "bond"** — bonding happens automatically
after being on the same team for about 5 minutes, and each bonded teammate adds another
stack, maxing out with a full 4-player bonded team. Goals include:

- **Casual** — flat Intelligence bonus (scales toward +4 with a full bonded team),
  which indirectly boosts everyone's XP gain (~2% XP per Intelligence point).
- **Hunting** — bonus XP for Legendary enemy kills (up to +100% fully bonded).
- **Events** — bonus XP for completing public events (up to +100% fully bonded).
- **Roleplay** — Charisma bonus (useful for vendor prices and perk-sharing headroom).
- **Exploration** — Endurance bonus.

The Team Leader sets and can change the Team Goal at any time from the Social Menu.

## What's actually shared vs. not

- **Free fast travel** to any teammate or their CAMP, from anywhere, regardless of
  distance — this alone is a major practical reason to team up.
- **Public event credit, tag/kill credit, and XP** are shared with nearby teammates —
  if a teammate tags or kills something, everyone in range gets credit and loot rights,
  and this works the same whether you're the leader or not.
- **Most main-story quest progress does not share automatically.** Only the **Team
  Leader** advances main quests that require dialogue/NPC choices while on a team —
  other members get no progress credit for those specific quest steps unless they
  either become leader themselves or complete that step solo later. This is a common
  gotcha for new groups expecting to co-op the entire story front-to-back.
- Side objectives that don't hinge on dialogue (killing a target, activating a
  terminal/switch, collecting an item) generally do count for whoever is nearby and
  participating, leader or not.

## Team Leader role

The Team Leader creates/disbands the team, invites and kicks/blocks members, and
converts the team between private and public via the Social Menu. If the leader
disconnects, the game typically disbands the team outright rather than promoting a new
leader — expect to be kicked back to solo (this can look like you were removed even
though nobody actively kicked you). During Raids specifically, a leader change mid-run
kicks the whole team out of that raid instance.
