Glass sits in your junk inventory as **Glass Shards** until you bulk it at a tinker's
workbench (it can't be sold to vendors before that) — worth knowing if you're
searching for it and only "glass" comes to mind.

## Scrapping

Well over 100 items yield Glass Shards, including beer, liquor, Nuka-Cola, and rum
bottles, mason jars and other glass jars, light bulbs, pre-war lamps, alarm clocks,
drinking glasses, magnifying glasses, and lab glassware like beakers, graduated
cylinders, and test tubes. Dense spots:

- **Sons of Dane Compound** — glass jars.
- **Fraternity Row** and **Black Bear Lodge** — beer bottles.
- **Tyler County Fairgrounds** and **Wavy Willard's Water Park** — mason jars.
- **The Whitespring Golf Club** — tall drinking glasses.

Nuka-Cola and beer bottles specifically are common enough that just looting them
off tables and shelves as you pass through settlements adds up fast.

## Workshop junk piles (no true ore vein)

Glass has no mineable vein, but junk piles at several workshops can contain Glass
Shards among their yield and can be worked with a Junk Extractor for a passive rate —
**Charleston Landfill** (three piles), **Dabney Homestead**, **Gorge Junkyard**,
**Red Rocket Mega Stop**, **Sunshine Meadows Industrial Farm**, and the **Converted
Munitions Factory** all have one or more.

## Note

Like other junk-based resources, exact item counts and workshop pile contents shift
with world content — check FalloutCounter's guide above for the current picture if a
listed spot feels dead.
