## Budget

- **Turrets, lights, and displays/decorations** eat CAMP budget far faster than
  structural pieces — you can build a surprising number of walls, floors, and
  foundations before you feel it, but a handful of turrets or a wall of lights will
  tank your remaining budget fast.
- **Crafting and repair benches** also cost more budget than most placeable objects
  (behind only prefabs and turrets). If budget is tight, park benches you don't need
  standing out in your stash/vault area rather than the main build.
- **Prefab structures** (e.g. Bungalow, Greenhouse, Pittsburgh Palace, Makeshift
  Mansion) are usually cheaper on budget than hand-building the equivalent space out
  of individual floor/wall/roof pieces, even though they look bigger.
- Items sitting in your **Storage** tab (built but not placed) still count against
  budget — clearing storage bloat is a real way to free up room, not just cosmetic
  cleanup.

## Blueprints

- **Blueprint your foundation separately from the build on top of it.** This lets
  you place the foundation first when relocating your CAMP, then drop the rest of
  the blueprint on top — much easier than trying to place one giant combined
  blueprint on uneven new terrain.
- Keep blueprints anchored to a **1x1 or 1x2 foundation module**. Large, irregular
  hand-built structures frequently fail to re-place cleanly elsewhere because the
  game is picky about the terrain matching the original footprint.
- When moving your CAMP, place blueprints from the **Stored** section, not the
  **Blueprint** section — using the Blueprint category re-crafts a fresh copy from
  raw materials instead of placing the one you already built.

## Placement tricks

- You can build structures over placed **power armor** without needing a floor/
  foundation under it first: build over it, exit build mode, then move the power
  armor away — the structure stays.
- **Flamethrower turrets/traps** can damage or destroy nearby CAMP objects when they
  go off — don't cluster them right next to walls or décor you care about.
- Building **vertically** is generally easier to secure and defend than sprawling
  horizontally, and uses less flat buildable footprint, which matters on cramped
  plots.

## General

- Junk collection matters more here than in most Fallout games — you'll have
  plenty of wood/steel later, but early on, under-collecting junk is what actually
  slows down CAMP expansion, not budget.
